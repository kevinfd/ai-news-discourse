# Google Energy Chief: Space Data Centers Cost Up to 100 Times More Than Those on Earth

The Information · Amir Efrati · 2026-11-01

Data centers in space could cost up to 100 times as much as those on Earth, Google's head of data center energy said this week, a reality check from a company that itself is testing AI server chips in orbit. “Let’s explore all the terrestrial options before we go extraterrestrial,” Amanda Peterson Corio said at The Information’s WTF summit,   a gathering of senior women in the technology and finance industries. The comments came as SpaceX’s orbital data center plans underpin part of its roughly $1.8 trillion market capitalization.
