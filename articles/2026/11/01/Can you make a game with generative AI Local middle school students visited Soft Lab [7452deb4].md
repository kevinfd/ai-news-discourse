# Can you make a game with generative AI? Local middle school students visited Soft Lab

note · ニューマネジメントシステム株式会社 · 2026-11-01

Can you make a game with generative AI? Local middle school students visited Soft Lab🍎
The second company visit✌
Six students from Hachinohe Municipal No. 2 Junior High School came to visit Hachinohe Soft Lab!
The purpose of this company visit was to help students understand the rewards and importance of work through interaction with adults who are actually working, and to deepen their understanding of the connection between what they learn at school and working.
On the day, we explained our company and IT work, and also conducted activities such as game creation using generative AI and a tour of the office where our employees actually work.
・Schedule for the day
- Company introduction & office tour
- Workflow
- App development
- About AI utilization at Newmane
- Q&A and exchange of opinions
・Office tour!
We guided them through the office where our employees usually work. What surprised the students was the number of computers and monitors each person uses!
Some people use as many as 5 PCs and 4 monitors!
They watched how we use multiple screens to carry out development and verification work. We were able to show them the work scene of a systems company, which is not something they usually have the opportunity to see.
・Making a game using generative AI🎮
We showed them how to make a game similar to "Suika Game" on the spot by utilizing generative AI. By giving a few lines of instructions that concisely summarize the specifications, the game is completed in no time.
The students seemed surprised to see a game being made in such a short time. When it came to actually writing the code, attention was also drawn to the speed of the employee's typing!
・How do you explain IT work to middle school students?
This time, the employee in charge thought hard about "how to explain things so that middle school students can understand."
There are many words in IT work that are not usually heard, such as systems, servers, and programming. So this time, we introduced the mechanisms and work of IT "without using technical terms," while using examples familiar to middle school students. For example, the story of how many servers are needed to keep "Pokémon GO" running constantly around the world.
When the conversation started with things familiar to them, the students seemed to find it easier to visualize, and they listened with sparkling eyes.
・We received many questions and comments from the students
・I was surprised that you need to write thousands of lines of code to create software.
・I was surprised that there is AI to support writing code.
・I was surprised that you can make games immediately with generative AI. It was a series of surprises for the students👀‼
The students who came this time were actually from the alma mater of our staff member, J. Seeing them visit Hachinohe Soft Lab in their nostalgic gym uniforms brought back memories for J, and there were moments that were truly heartwarming.
It was a somewhat special day for J as well, as they were able to share information about the company they work for and about IT jobs with their juniors from their alma mater.
・In conclusion
We hope that this corporate visit serves as an opportunity for the students to become interested in how IT works and what IT jobs are like.
A few days later, we received a wonderful message from their teacher!
“They returned to school with satisfied expressions. I believe that what they saw and heard at your company will surely be useful when they think about society and work in the future.”
Furthermore, we also received thank-you letters from the students who participated.
Wow, that makes us so happy!
Thank you to all the students who came to Hachinohe Soft Lab!
New Management System Co., Ltd. will continue to value our connection with the local community and engage in activities that convey the appeal of IT and manufacturing to local students.
