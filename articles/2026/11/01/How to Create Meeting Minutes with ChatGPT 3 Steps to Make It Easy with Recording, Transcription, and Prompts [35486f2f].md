# How to Create Meeting Minutes with ChatGPT | 3 Steps to Make It Easy with Recording, Transcription, and Prompts

How to Create Meeting Minutes with ChatGPT | 3 Steps to Make It Easy with Recording, Transcription, and Prompts
Hello, this is Ponpoko🍵
The phrase "Please take the minutes" that lingers after a meeting ends. 
Looking back at your notes, trying to remember who said what, organizing it into a document... before you know it, it has taken as much time as the meeting itself. Have you ever had that experience?
Now, if you use ChatGPT, you can leave the "organizing" part of the minutes largely to it. 
However, whether it works well or not is almost entirely determined by the "recording" and "transcription" done before handing it to ChatGPT.
What you will learn in this article
- The 3-step flow for creating meeting minutes with ChatGPT
- How to choose equipment to improve recording quality (in-person/online)
- How to choose a transcription method
- Copy-pasteable meeting minute prompts and points to keep in mind when using them
I have summarized methods that can be used on any platform, including Windows, Mac, and Android.
The flow of creating meeting minutes with ChatGPT consists of 3 steps
When creating meeting minutes with ChatGPT, it is helpful to think of the work in the following three stages.
- Record...Record the meeting audio clearly
- Transcribe...Transcribe the recording into text
- Organize...Hand the transcription to ChatGPT and have it summarized into the format of meeting minutes
What ChatGPT excels at is the third step, "Organizing." 
Rearranging remarks that jump around from topic to topic, or extracting decisions and action items (To-Dos), is exactly its area of expertise.
On the other hand, if the words are incorrect at the transcription stage, ChatGPT will create the document based on those errors. 
That is why getting the first two steps right is the biggest shortcut.
For those who want to confirm the basics of how to use ChatGPT, the book "Easy-to-Use Ex ChatGPT Work Techniques & Time-Saving Tips" by Gijutsu-Hyohron Co., Ltd. is a perfect entry point. It summarizes tips for prompts and how to use text processing for document creation and summarization chapter by chapter (released in February 2026).
Improve recording quality for in-person meetings with a speakerphone
If you place your smartphone in the middle of the desk in a meeting room to record, the voices of people nearby tend to be loud, while the voices of those further away are captured quietly. 
The sound of air conditioning and paper rustling also gets picked up, which causes the accuracy of the transcription to drop.
This is where a conference speakerphone (microphone speaker) comes in handy. 
Since it comes with a microphone that picks up voices from 360°, it makes it easier to record the voices of everyone around the table evenly.

- Auto-gain control that adjusts volume based on the speaker's voice volume and distance
- Can be connected via Bluetooth and USB (USB-C & USB-A cables included)

Deliver your voice clearly in online meetings with a headset
A surprisingly common issue with online meeting transcription is the pattern where "only your own voice is not transcribed well."
Built-in laptop microphones tend to pick up keyboard typing sounds and room echoes.
Using a headset with a microphone near your mouth allows your voice to be captured clearly, making it easier to reduce transcription errors.
It also makes it easier for the other party to hear you, making the meeting itself smoother.

Choose from 3 methods for transcription
Once you have the recording, the next step is transcription.
There are broadly three methods.
Use the transcription function of your meeting tool
Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet have built-in meeting transcription features.
Since speaker names are often recorded as well, this is a convenient method for gathering material for meeting minutes.
However, whether you can use it depends on your contract plan and company settings.
First, check your internal rules and the administrator's settings.
Using a recording app on your smartphone or PC
Some smartphone recording apps come with transcription features.
If you want to get started easily with in-person meetings, we recommend trying this method first.
Using an AI voice recorder
If you want to handle everything from recording to transcription and summarization with a single device, an AI voice recorder is another option.

Regardless of the method, don't forget to inform the participants and get their consent before recording.
Copy-and-paste meeting minutes prompt
Once you have the transcription, it is finally time for ChatGPT. 
The key is to decide and communicate "how you want the output to look" in advance. I have prepared a template for common meeting minute formats that you can copy and paste as is.
You are an assistant that creates meeting minutes. 
Please create meeting minutes based on the following transcription. 
[Output Format] 
・Meeting name, date and time, participants 
・Key points for each agenda item (bullet points) 
・Decisions made 
・ToDo (person in charge and deadline) 
・Items to confirm by next time 
[Conditions] 
・Do not write anything not in the transcription 
・Write "Needs confirmation" for parts that are inaudible or unclear 
・Summarize using polite Japanese (desu/masu style) 
[Transcription] 
(Paste here)
Please feel free to add or remove items from the [Output Format] to suit the type of meeting.
Including the two lines "Do not write anything not in the transcription" and "Write 'Needs confirmation' for unclear points" makes it easier to prevent the inclusion of plausible-sounding fabrications.
For those who want to understand the logic behind prompts more deeply, Nikkei BP's "Mastering ChatGPT! Fukatsu-style Prompt Reader" is also a helpful reference. It is a book that explains effective prompt patterns and risk management using business scenarios as examples.
Final checks should be done by human eyes
Always review the meeting minutes created by ChatGPT before distributing them.
- Proper nouns such as people's names, company names, and product names
- Numbers such as amounts, dates, and quantities
- The difference between decided items and items "under consideration"
Numbers and proper nouns, in particular, are prone to errors during the transcription stage.
Check company rules regarding confidential information
Meeting content may contain information that should not be shared outside the company.
Be sure to check in advance which AI tools are approved for use at your company and what scope of information can be entered.
Since more companies are subscribing to enterprise plans, it is best to ask your IT department first for peace of mind.
Summary
Let's review the flow for making meeting minutes easier with ChatGPT.
- Think of meeting minutes in 3 steps: "Record → Transcribe → Refine"
- For in-person meetings, use a speakerphone to pick up everyone's voice
- For online meetings, use a headset to keep your own voice clear
- Choose your transcription method from meeting tools, recording apps, or AI voice recorders
- For prompts, define the output format and also specify what "not to write"
If you only buy one tool, the most effective one is a speakerphone that improves recording quality. 
When the source audio is clear, both the transcription and the final ChatGPT output will improve together.
