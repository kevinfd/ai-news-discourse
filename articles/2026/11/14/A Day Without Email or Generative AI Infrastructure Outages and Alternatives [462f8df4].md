# A Day Without Email or Generative AI: Infrastructure Outages and Alternatives

note · Ak-Science · 2026-11-14

SYSTEM NOTICE
A Day Without Email or Generative AI: Infrastructure Outages and Alternatives
Yesterday, from the morning, I received a notification from my elementary school's communication system about a large-scale Microsoft outage. It stated that school email communications would be down starting that morning.
The same situation at the office
Company email was similarly not functioning properly, and the calendar was unstable... Furthermore, generative AI was also unavailable.
When things you usually take for granted all stop working at once, it is quite unsettling.
In-person meetings were fine
However, since yesterday happened to be a day I was scheduled to go into the office, my in-person meetings at work finished without any issues.
Meetings where you talk face-to-face are not affected by the state of email or systems. On a day like this, that was very helpful.
Switching generative AI tasks to manual
I switched the tasks I usually have generative AI do over to manual execution instead.
It took longer than usual, but since I wasn't completely dependent on it, I finished without any major impact.
That said, I realized once again that infrastructure failures can have a significant impact.
Alternative means for when the time comes
If my dependence on generative AI had been further along... the impact of the same outage might have been much greater.
Even if such a problem occurs, I should be able to handle it with a backup. For example, I felt that from now on, it will be necessary to always have some kind of alternative means prepared, such as setting up an AI agent using a local LLM system.
The more convenient something is, the more we should think about what happens when it stops working!
