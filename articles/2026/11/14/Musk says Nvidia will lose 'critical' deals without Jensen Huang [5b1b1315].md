# Musk says Nvidia will lose 'critical' deals without Jensen Huang

Channel NewsAsia · 2026-11-14

Musk says Nvidia will lose 'critical' deals without Jensen Huang
Nov 14 : Elon Musk said chipmaker Nvidia would lose "critical" deals without its now ousted CEO Jensen Huang, in a post on X on Saturday.
Musk said that while Huang drove supply chain "brilliantly" at Nvidia, many such deals were "a handshake with Jensen, not a written contract."
    Source: Reuters
