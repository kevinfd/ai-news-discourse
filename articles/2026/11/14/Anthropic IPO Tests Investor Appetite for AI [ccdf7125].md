# Anthropic IPO Tests Investor Appetite for AI

Bloomberg · 2026-11-14

Bloomberg Technology co-host Ed Ludlow tells Bloomberg This Weekend why Anthropic’s upcoming IPO could be a major test of investor appetite for AI, with the offering heavily oversubscribed and poised to follow SpaceX’s blockbuster debut. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Ludlow also explains how Nvidia and Jensen Huang’s departure loom over the offering, given Nvidia’s planned investment in Anthropic and Huang’s central role connecting the companies powering the AI boom. (Source: Bloomberg)
