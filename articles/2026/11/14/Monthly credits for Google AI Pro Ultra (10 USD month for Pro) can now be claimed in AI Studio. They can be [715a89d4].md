# Monthly credits for Google AI Pro/Ultra (10 USD/month for Pro) can now be claimed in AI Studio. They can be used for the Gemini API, but a prepayment of 5 USD or more is ...

note · メンサ会員のAi活用塾 · 2026-11-14

SYSTEM NOTICE
Monthly credits for Google AI Pro/Ultra (10 USD/month for Pro) can now be claimed in AI Studio. They can be used for the Gemini API, but a prepayment of 5 USD or more is required. How to claim and points to note
On November 13, 2026, Google announced a new way to claim credits. This applies to subscribers of "Google AI Pro" and "Google AI Ultra". You can now claim the Google Cloud credits you receive every month directly within Google AI Studio. Credits are a "free balance" that can be used instead of payment. For Pro, you get 10 USD per month, which can be applied to Gemini API usage fees. However, to start using them, a prepayment of 5 USD or more is required. This article summarizes the details, how to claim them, and points to note.
What happened
- Announcement. Google's official blog "Claim your Google Cloud Credits in Google AI Studio" (November 13, 2026) → https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/google-cloud-credits-gdp-ai-studio/
- Everything completed within AI Studio. According to the blog, they have eliminated the need to navigate between different sites to register billing information or claim credits. You can now create a profile, register billing information, claim credits, and check your balance all in one flow within AI Studio.
- Monthly amounts (AI Studio information page). Google AI Pro is 10 USD per month, Ultra 5x is 40 USD per month, and Ultra 20x is 100 USD per month. Google AI Plus is not eligible.
- Valid for 12 months. The monthly portion is valid for 12 months from the date it is issued. Unused portions will accumulate in subsequent months. Credits nearing their expiration date are used first.
- Conditions. A free "Google Developer Program" profile is required. If you do not have one, it will be created automatically during the claim process. Only the person who subscribed to the plan can use them; family sharing members cannot. Only one billing account can be assigned.
- Prepayment of $5. To use credits with the Gemini API, you need a minimum prepayment of $5 and that balance.
- Check your balance. https://aistudio.google.com/billing allows you to see the remaining amount and how much you have used.
Reference: Alphabet stock chart (as of writing)
Explanation
First, let's clarify the terminology. API (Application Programming Interface) is a "gateway" for calling AI from your own apps or programs. These credits will be applied to the fees incurred when using a paid API key. An API key is a string of characters like a "password" for using the API. To pay the fees, you need to set up a billing account (Cloud Billing).
In this account's view, those who are subscribed to Pro but have not been claiming their $10 monthly credit will benefit the most. The blog suggests that for those who have been subscribed for a while and have a profile, there may be unclaimed credits accumulated.
The following is also this account's perspective.
- It serves as a practice ground for small-scale testing. It is easy to test small tools you create yourself or apps built with AI Studio's "Build" feature within the free allowance.
- However, it is not "free and unlimited." Once the credits run out, charges will be deducted from the money you prepaid. You set the limit yourself.
- Google's goal. They likely want to provide an entry point for development to subscribers and increase the number of people using Gemini (this account's speculation).
How to use it for work and how to try it today
The following are the steps based on the official guide page and this account's usage suggestions.
- Open https://aistudio.google.com with the user's own Google account that is subscribed to Google AI Pro or Ultra.
- Click the "Claim your free API credits" card that appears in the left menu.
- Follow the instructions to select a project (a container for your work) and an API key (a string of characters acting as a password to use the API). If it is your first time, you can proceed with the automatically created "Default Gemini Project."
- Prepay $5 or more using a payment card to receive the credits. You can also choose to set up future monthly amounts to be automatically applied to your billing account.
- Connect a paid API key. This allows you to use credits in the AI Studio 'Playground' (testing screen) or 'Build'.
- Set a monthly spend cap for your project. Once the limit is reached, paid calls will stop.
- Check your balance in the bottom-left display or on the Billing page.
Once you are ready, try asking for the following on the AI Studio screen. The first is for 'Build' (app creation), and the second is an example for everyday tasks.

Points to note
- The $5 is not 'free' money. The $5 prepayment is your own money. It is used for API usage after your credits run out. When the prepaid balance reaches $0, credits will also stop being used (Google Developer Program FAQ). Note that the FAQ also states that whether a prepayment is required depends on the billing method (prepaid vs. postpaid).
- Be careful of overspending. Even if your credits run out, calls will not stop and will be deducted from your prepaid balance. It is safer to set a spend cap.
- For the account holder only. If a member of a family sharing group tries to receive them, an error will occur. You can receive them once per Google account, and they can be applied to one billing account.
- Different from other credits. This is a different system from Google One AI credits. Google One AI credits cannot be used in AI Studio.
- Do not show your API key to others. Your API key is the key to a paid gateway. Do not post it in chats or on social media.
- Countries and screens. The official page I checked does not list conditions by country. Please check your own account to see if the card appears on your screen.
