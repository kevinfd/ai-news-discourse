# Ask HN: Nobel Prizes for OpenAI and Anthropic: When?

Hacker News · LinaLauneBaer · 2026-11-25

In 2024, multiple Nobel Prizes were awarded for AI related accomplishments: > 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics: Awarded to John J. Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton for foundational discoveries and inventions enabling machine learning with artificial neural networks. (Source https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Hinton) > 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry: Awarded to Demis Hassabis and John M. Jumper for developing an AI algorithm (AlphaFold) that predicts complex three-dimensional protein structures (Source https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-025-02527-z) I would classify the work that got them the physics prize as basic research that played a big part in enabling AI. And the prize in Chemistry falls into a similar category I think… Now, both openAI + Anthropic are maybe less involved in basic research directly but the things they "produce" are now used to solve problems humanity tried to solve for many many years. If they both published all of their papers under a different name, a name that sounds like a real persons name, I am sure that person would be considered for multiple Nobel Prizes. It rarely happens but "organizations" can receive Nobel Prizes. Eg.: > The European Union (EU) won the 2012 Nobel Peace Prize for over six decades of advancing peace, reconciliation, democracy, and human rights in Europe So why not OpenAI + Anthropic? (Yes, its maybe still a bit early, given that some of the papers still are pending proper peer review - but overall the question remains: How would humanity deal with "that"? "That" being that the "products" of two companies made those scientific breakthroughs?
