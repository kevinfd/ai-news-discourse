# Neuberger Says 'Easy Wins' in AI Are Over, Stick to Hyperscalers

Bloomberg · 2026-11-25

Neuberger's multi-asset strategies co-CIO Maya Bhandari discusses the outlook for the artificial intelligence trade, explaining why she has stepped away from the "more vulnerable elements" of the industry. "What that really boils down to is wanting to own some of the hyperscalers that have moats," Bhandari tells Bloomberg Television. "They're both adopters and enablers." (Source: Bloomberg)
