# Prosus CEO ‘Very Bullish’ on Tencent's AI Assistant

Bloomberg · 2026-11-25

Prosus Chief Executive Officer Fabricio Bloisi says the firm is "very bullish" on its holding in Tencent. The Chinese company’s new AI assistant, known as Xiaowei, "has a potential of 1.4 billion customers inside WeChat," Bloisi explains. Speaking on Bloomberg Television, he also says M&A is "not our focus today." (Source: Bloomberg)
