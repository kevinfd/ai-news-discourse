# Austria's Fastest Growing Company Mulls US Listing, CEO Says

Bloomberg · 2026-11-25

Austrian circuit-board maker AT&S is weighing re-listing in the US or Asia, its CEO Michael Mertin tells Bloomberg TV's Anna Edwards and Guy Johnson on "The Opening Trade." The company, which supplies parts to AI chips used in data centers, has seen its stock soar this year. (Source: Bloomberg)
