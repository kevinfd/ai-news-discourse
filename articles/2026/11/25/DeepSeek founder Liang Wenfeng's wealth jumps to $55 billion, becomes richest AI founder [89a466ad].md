DeepSeek founder and CEO Liang Wenfeng is currently the richest among AI company founders. His wealth has jumped 45% to $55 billion after the Chinese AI startup raised around $15 billion in fresh funding. The company is now valued at around $86 billion.
DeepSeek raised nearly 100 billion yuan ($15 billion) from investors, including some of China's richest businessmen. According to the Bloomberg Billionaires Index, Liang's wealth has more than tripled since the beginning of 2026. His fortune has more than tripled since the beginning of 2026.
Interestingly, Liang's share in DeepSeek has actually come down after the latest funding round. He now owns 67.4% of the company, compared to 81.65% in June. However, the company's rising value has increased the worth of his remaining share.
Liang is now worth more than twice as much as OpenAI co-founder Greg Brockman, whose wealth is estimated at $25.5 billion. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has a fortune of around $21.6 billion.
Liang's wealth has increased because he still owns more than two-thirds of DeepSeek. This means he gets a large share of the company's value. Other AI company founders own smaller portions of their companies.
Some of China's richest businessmen have also invested in DeepSeek. These include Robin Zeng, who founded a battery-making company called Contemporary Amperex Technology, and Zhong Shanshan, who started bottled water company Nongfu Spring.
Zeng has acquired a 0.18% personal stake in DeepSeek, currently valued at around $154.6 million. Zhong, who had invested in the company's previous funding round, now holds a 0.08% stake worth around $70.6 million.
Liang started DeepSeek in 2023 using the resources of his investment business. Before that, he had set up an investment firm called High-Flyer with two college friends in 2015. DeepSeek is now planning to go public in 2027.
