# Huawei forms patent teams for chips and AI, eyeing ecosystem to rival Nvidia

South China Morning Post · Howard Liu · 2026-11-25

Huawei Technologies has established dedicated patent teams for semiconductors and artificial intelligence as it seeks to establish an overseas foothold for a computing ecosystem to rival Nvidia’s.
Extending an intellectual property (IP) operation rooted in telecommunications, the Shenzhen-based company had now created a semiconductor IP team and an AI-focused unit, with some engineers transferred from research and development departments, chief IP officer Alan Fan told South China Morning Post...
