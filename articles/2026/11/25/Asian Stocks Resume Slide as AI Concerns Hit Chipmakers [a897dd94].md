# Asian Stocks Resume Slide as AI Concerns Hit Chipmakers

Bloomberg · 2026-11-25

Stocks sold off as doubts over the durability of the artificial intelligence boom drove investors out of technology shares. MSCI’s gauge of Asia Pacific equities fell 2.1%, led by a 6.3% slide in South Korea and a 4.8% drop in Taiwan, the region’s two key barometers of the AI trade. Bloomberg's Winnie Hsu reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
