# ‘It’s been really a mess’: Meta insiders concerned over push to AI content moderation

The Guardian · Aisha Down Global technology reporter · 2026-11-25

Several employees say rapid speed at which algorithms work means mistakes can happen at greater scale Meta employees have expressed concern at the company’s push to replace human moderators with artificial intelligence, claiming the technology is leading to mistakes with the potential for sweeping political consequences. The social media company, which owns Facebook and Instagram, has been experimenting with AI-driven moderation for years, first introducing it in 2017 to filter out violent extremist content.
