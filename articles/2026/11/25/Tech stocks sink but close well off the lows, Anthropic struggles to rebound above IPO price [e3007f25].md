# Tech stocks sink but close well off the lows, Anthropic struggles to rebound above IPO price

CNBC · Samantha Subin · 2026-11-25

Technology stocks fell on Wednesday as excitement over Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's return faded and failed to calm concerns over the sustainability of the artificial intelligence trade.
The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite declined 0.6% and traded off its lows, recovering from steeper losses earlier in the session. Semiconductor stocks dragged on the index, and the VanEck Semiconductor ETF that tracks the sector edged down 1%.
"We just feel that this is panic right now," Venu Krishna, head of U.S. equity strategy at Barclays, told CNBC's "Squawk on the Street" on Wednesday.
"What the market is telling you is that the AI growth narrative has hit a wall and given the centrality of that theme at the economic level, the market is expecting some sort of a macro shock," he added.
After notching one of its best days on record Monday, Nvidia has fallen in the days since and slumped 4% Wednesday. Advanced Micro Devices, Marvell Technology and Intel all edged lower. Micron Technology declined 4%.
Huang's return to Nvidia on Monday briefly revived appetite for tech stocks but didn't fully calm investor concerns over the high costs tied to the AI build-out and worries of a potential slowdown.
Other stocks tied to the AI trade also plummeted. Oracle fell more than 5%. CoreWeave and Nebius moved lower.
AI lab Anthropic bucked the sell-off, however, and climbed more than 4% after shares dropped below last week's blockbuster IPO price.
Wednesday's declines also came as Wall Street digested a hot inflation reading that fueled concerns that higher interest rates could dent demand for growth stocks, including tech.
Amazon and Alphabet each declined about 2%. Microsoft and Tesla lost more than 1% and Meta traded 3% higher.
