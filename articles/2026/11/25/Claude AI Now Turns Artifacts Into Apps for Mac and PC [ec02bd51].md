# Claude AI Now Turns Artifacts Into Apps for Mac and PC

PCMag · James Peckham · 2026-11-25

Claude artifacts are an easy way to repeat regular tasks, and now Anthropic is making them easier to access on your computer. You can now turn a Claude artifact into a desktop app on both Mac and Windows, with the feature rolling out in beta across all plans, including free users.
This will let you take your favorite Claude functionality, turn it into a standalone piece of miniature software, and access it from your computer without booting the chatbot itself. You can build out your own tools, share them with others, or download ones shared with you.
To set up an existing artifact as an app, press the Share menu in Claude, then select Install as app. This will then be automatically ported to your desktop, and you can decide which level of permissions you want to grant the miniature software.
The default permissions mean the artifact can do whatever it normally would have in the browser, but if you want to, you can also give it the ability to run other functions, such as sending notifications or creating always-on-top windows. The final level is full computer access, which allows Claude to change or delete your files across your computer without asking you first.
Artifacts as apps let you access regularly used tools without having to boot up Claude, but there are caveats that mean they don’t work exactly like normal desktop software. You won’t be able to run these apps without Claude signed in, and you’ll also need a consistent internet connection. Unlike most other desktop software that runs away from a Wi-Fi or mobile connection, this severely limits how you can use these on the move.
Be aware of how this will also “count toward your plan’s usage limits,” so if you’re on a free version, it’s unlikely to go far. Anyone can use artifacts as apps across the Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, or Enterprise plans, but it's still in beta, which may mean you encounter issues.
Anthropic notes on a support page that it won’t be available on Linux, the web, or mobile. It’s unclear whether Anthropic intends to bring the tool to other platforms in the future.
