With Black Friday sales now underway at leading retailers, the timing couldn't be better to shop around for a new TV.
If you're in the market for a big upgrade this year, Amazon's sale has a substantial selection to browse, including a hefty price cut of over $400 on Sony's 85-inch Bravia 3 II LED 4K TV.
        
            The best tech deal this week
                            
                    
            
As of Nov. 25, this discount has brought the cost for this TV down to $1,198 from $1,599.99. More importantly, it's actually the lowest price we've seen it hit at the retailer.

If a new TV is on your Black Friday shopping list, don't miss out on this great deal on the Sony 85-inch Bravia 3 II LED 4K TV at Amazon.
