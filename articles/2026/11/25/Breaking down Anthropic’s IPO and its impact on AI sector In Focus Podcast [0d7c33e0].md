# Breaking down Anthropic’s IPO and its impact on AI sector | In Focus Podcast

The Hindu · Sahana Venugopal · 2026-11-25

Anthropic, the maker of Claude class of language models, made its public market debut on November 19 at $83 a piece. The stock nearly hit two trillion dollars in market capitalisation on the same, and was trading at $84.37 (as on Monday 23, 2026). The company is backed by Google and Amazon, and has totally raised $141.1 billion in private funding rounds.
How will Anthropic’s IPO affect the way its AI technology is governed? What will be the impact on AI users around the world? And what does this IPO signify for Anthropic’s rivals, such as OpenAI?
In this episode of The Hindu’s In Focus Podcast, Technology Editor John Xavier cuts through the noise and breaks down what news readers need to know about the Anthropic IPO breakout.
