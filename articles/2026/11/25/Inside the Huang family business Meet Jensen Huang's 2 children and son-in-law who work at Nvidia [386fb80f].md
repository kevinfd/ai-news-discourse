# Inside the Huang family business: Meet Jensen Huang's 2 children and son-in-law who work at Nvidia

Business Insider · Talia Lakritz, Talia Lakritz (tlakritz@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-25

From left to right: Spencer Huang, Jensen Huang, and Madison Huang. An Rong Xu/Bloomberg via Getty Images; Soeren Stache/picture alliance via Getty Images; Bloomberg/Getty Images Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang shares two children with his wife of 41 years, Lori Huang. Huang's son, daughter, and son-in-law all hold senior roles at Nvidia. Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia after admitting to extramarital affairs, but was reinstated. Nvidia is, in some ways, a family business. CEO Jensen Huang's two children, Spencer Huang and Madison Huang, whom he shares with his wife of over 40 years, Lori Huang , both hold senior roles at the company. His son-in-law, Nico Caprez, is also employed at the company as an executive. Jensen Huang's family life drew new attention after he admitted to having extramarital affairs with two women. The New York Times reported that one was Alice Huang, an executive at the Singaporean data center company Megaspeed, and that US intelligence officials believed she had passed information to Beijing. China has denied the espionage allegations. "I was unfaithful to my wife," the Nvidia CEO wrote in a message to employees on November 8. "My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry." When Jensen Huang resigned amid the scandal on November 12, speculation whirled that one of his children could succeed him as CEO . Nvidia's board voted "unanimously" to reinstate him on November 23. Board chair Harvey Jones said in a statement that Huang's return "is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers." Here's a closer look at Jensen Huang's son, daughter, and son-in-law, and their roles at Nvidia. Spencer Huang Spencer Huang. An Rong Xu/Bloomberg via Getty Images Spencer Huang, 36, is Jensen Huang's son and director of product management in robotics at Nvidia. He joined the company in 2022 as a product manager. He graduated from Columbia College in Chicago in 2012 with a double bachelor's degree in international marketing and cultural studies, and earned his MBA from New York University's Stern School of Business in 2022, per his LinkedIn profile . Before joining Nvidia, he ran R&D Cocktail Lab, a bar in Taipei City, Taiwan, which closed in 2021. He also consulted for Ommo Technologies, Ford Motor Company, and Pfizer. In the last fiscal year, Spencer Huang earned $1.32 million in his role at Nvidia, according to the company's annual filing . Madison Huang Madison Huang. Bloomberg/Getty Images Jensen Huang's daughter, 35-year-old Madison Huang , works as senior director of product marketing for physical AI at Nvidia. She joined the company in 2020 as an MBA intern before climbing the ladder, according to her LinkedIn . She earned a Bachelor's degree in business management from the Culinary Institute of America in 2012 and an MBA from London Business School in 2021. Before Nvidia, she worked as a chef and sommelier, then moved into hospitality marketing at LVMH, a luxury goods conglomerate. Madison Huang earned a salary of $1.23 million in her role at Nvidia in the last fiscal year. Nico Caprez Nico Caprez. Jacquelyn Martin/AP Photo Madison Huang's husband, Nico Caprez, 35, is Nvidia's vice president of global AI infrastructure growth. Caprez, who is from Switzerland, was a professional alpine skier from 2010 to 2013 before pivoting to the business world, according to his LinkedIn profile . He earned a bachelor's degree in banking and finance from the University of Zurich and an MBA from London Business School. He worked as an investment-banking intern at Nomura in 2015, then held roles at Boston Consulting Group, Moonfire Ventures, and PSquared Asset Management before joining Nvidia in 2024. Caprez and Madison Huang met while studying at London Business School. They married in Hawaii in September. Read the original article on Business Insider
