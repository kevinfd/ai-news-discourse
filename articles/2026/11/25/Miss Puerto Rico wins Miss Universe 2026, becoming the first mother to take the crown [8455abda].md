# Miss Puerto Rico wins Miss Universe 2026, becoming the first mother to take the crown

Business Insider · Anneta Konstantinides, Anneta Konstantinides (akonstantinides@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-25

Miss Universe Jennifer Barreto of Puerto Rico onstage during the 75th Miss Universe pageant. Jaydee Lee/Getty Images The 75th annual Miss Universe pageant took place in San Juan, Puerto Rico, on Tuesday. Over 110 women from around the world competed for the iconic crown. Miss Puerto Rico Jennifer Barreto won the Miss Universe title. Miss Puerto Rico is the new Miss Universe. Jennifer Barreto, 27, was named the winner at the 75th annual Miss Universe pageant in San Juan, Puerto Rico, on Tuesday night — much to the excitement of her home crowd. She was crowned by Fátima Bosch , who won Miss Universe 2025 after a drama-filled competition . Miss Dominican Republic won first runner-up, followed by Miss Venezuela. Miss Puerto Rico and first runner-up Miss Dominican Republic onstage during the 75th Miss Universe pageant. Jaydee Lee/Getty Images Barreto made history as the first mother and married woman to win the crown. Women with spouses or children weren't eligible to compete in the pageant until 2023. The governor of Puerto Rico, Jenniffer González-Colón, said in an Instagram post that she was "filled with pride" to see Barreto "demonstrate that motherhood is not a barrier to achieving our dreams." Barreto competed alongside 115 women from around the world in the competition, which kicked off last week with a closed-door interview and the national costume contest . Miss Puerto Rico 2026 during the national costume contest. Courtesy of Miss Universe The women then competed in the preliminary evening gown and swimsuit competition on Sunday. At the start of Tuesday's final, hosts Mario Lopez and former Miss Universe R'Bonney Gabriel revealed which of the 116 contestants had placed in the top 30. After the evening gown and swimsuit competitions, only five contestants remained for the interview round, where each was asked a different question to start. Barreto's question was about artificial intelligence. "As artificial intelligence grows, what is the most important human value we should never lose?" a Miss Universe judge asked her. "Artificial intelligence came here to stay, and I think the most important thing is to use it carefully," Barreto said in her response. Jennifer Barreto was crowned Miss Universe 2026. Jaydee Lee/Getty Images Each contestant was then asked to answer the same final question: What does the world need most from its leaders right now? Barreto said she believes the world needs "leaders that are proficient" and that it's "in our leaders' hands to stand up for themselves and also set the example and the bar." She added that she personally hoped to be a leader. "I hope that I can create a longitudinal impact by listening to the community needs," she said. "When we listen to the community needs, and we adjust the strategies to prevent harmful behaviors according to their needs, then that's when the impact happens." Barreto was preparing to commission as a US Army officer the summer she was crowned Miss Universe Puerto Rico. She now works as a prevention specialist and crisis responder for the US Department of War. Miss Puerto Rico 2026.
