# Google Gemini Notebook upgrades Flashcards with visual learning and study reminders

Digital Trends · Sudhanshu Kumar Mangalam · 2026-11-25

Google‘s Gemini Notebook is arguably one of the more useful AI tools for students, and the company has been adding new features to it at a pretty steady pace. The latest update is aimed at Flashcards, which can already turn your study material into little cards for memorizing information. Google is now giving the feature three new tricks, including pictures, a stopwatch, and reminders to help you keep up with your studies.
What’s changing with the flashcards?
Flashcards are quite handy for remembering facts, but staring at the same text over and over isn’t always the best way to learn. Google’s latest feature drop now allows Gemini Notebook to automatically pull relevant images from your uploaded study material and place them alongside the text on flashcards.
For example, someone studying biology could have diagrams of the human anatomy appear on their cards, while geography students could get maps and other visual references. The images are taken directly from the uploaded sources, so there’s no need to go hunting for them separately. I can see this being quite helpful for subjects where remembering what something looks like is just as important as knowing the answer.
And to make the process of preparing for exams a little more competitive, Google is adding a built-in stopwatch. Users can enable it during flashcard sessions to keep track of how long they’re taking to review the material. A useful perk for anyone preparing for timed examinations, or simply trying to improve their recall speed.
A little help with sticking to your study routine
The final addition is scheduled study reminders in the Gemini Notebook mobile app. You can now set notifications to revisit previously generated flashcards. I reckon this could come in handy for students who prepare a bunch of study material but have a hard time sticking to a regular revision schedule.
Google first introduced Flashcards and customizable quizzes back in September 2025. More recently, the company also added hundreds of free textbooks that users can turn into quizzes, flashcards, and study guides. All three new features are available now for personal Google accounts, Google Workspace customers, and Workspace Individual subscribers. No paid AI subscription required.
