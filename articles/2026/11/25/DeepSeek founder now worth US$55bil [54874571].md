# DeepSeek founder now worth US$55bil

The Star · 2026-11-25

SHANGHAI: DeepSeek founder Liang Wenfeng’s fortune has climbed to US$55bil after the Chinese artificial intelligence (AI) startup completed its latest fundraising, drawing investment from industrial tycoons and cementing his status as the world’s richest creator of AI models.
Liang’s stake in the Hangzhou-based firm fell to 67.4% from 81.65% in June, according to registry filings.
DeepSeek’s implied valuation is US$86bil, based on the roughly 100 billion yuan (US$15bil) that the firm recently raised, according to people familiar with the matter, and calculations by the Bloomberg Billionaires Index.
Liang’s net worth has more than tripled from earlier this year, according to the wealth index.
The latest numbers showed Liang’s growing financial power, even as cracks begin emerge in the AI boom that last Friday pushed the S&P 500 into a correction.
They also underscored how China’s richest are backing DeepSeek, which has evolved from a hedge fund side project into a centrepiece of the country’s AI ambitions.
The firm is preparing for a potential initial public offering next year.
The surge means that Liang, born in 1985, has put more distance between his own wealth and that of rival US-based founders. — Bloomberg
