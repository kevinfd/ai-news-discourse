# Seeking nominations for Business Insider's 2027 rising stars of brand marketing list

Business Insider · Lara O'Reilly, Lara O'Reilly (loreilly@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-25

It's time to submit your nominations for Business Insider's Rising Stars of Brand Marketing list. BlackJack3D/Getty Images Business Insider is recognizing up-and-coming marketers poised to become future brand leaders. We want your ideas for who should make the list. Submit your nominations through this form by 5 p.m. ET on January 7. Business Insider is seeking nominations for the 2027 list recognizing the rising stars of brand marketing. Submit your entries using this form . The list will feature early- and mid-career marketers who are driving growth for their companies and who show the potential to become future C-suite leaders. We want to highlight marketers who are harnessing technology like artificial intelligence to unlock data insights, boost productivity, and reengineer marketing workflows. We are also looking for the marketing pros who are breaking through on attention-scarce social feeds and leading the way with creator partnerships, IRL activations, and savvy media plans. These are the marketers inspiring others within their departments and across the profession. Some tips for your entry: Business Insider's Rising Stars of Brand Marketing is an annual list. Therefore, we only want you to highlight your nominee's achievements from the past 12 months. Details on their career history will be considered useful context but are unlikely to factor into the final choices for the list. Please support your entry with quantitative data, case studies, and links where possible. We want concrete examples that demonstrate how campaigns and projects drove impact for the business. We may use any of the information provided in the nomination form. Reporters may follow up with the nominee's communications department for additional information. We will require a headshot if a nominee is selected. Images must have a minimum width of 1,200 pixels. A photographer credit must also be supplied. The final list is determined by Business Insider's own reporting and the nominations we receive. Submit your nominations here by January 7, 2027. Need some inspiration?
