# Dan Ives Cuts Nvidia Price Target, But Stays Bullish Post-Huang

Bloomberg · 2026-11-13

Dan Ives, partner and senior managing director at Yorkville Ives & Co., says that Nvidia without Jensen Huang “evens the playing field” in artificial intelligence. Ives discusses Nvidia’s valuation without Huang, why he thinks the next CEO will be from outside the company and what challenges they may face. He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
