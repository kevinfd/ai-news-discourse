# People Have 'No Idea' How Much Nvidia Impacts Daily Life, Says Aaron Brown

Bloomberg · 2026-11-13

Aaron Brown, founder and CEO of Lumbra, said that the average person has 'no idea' how much Nvidia impacts their lives and that it remains a messaging issue the AI industry at large needs to solve. Brown, who is a former CIA officer, said that it is hard to know exactly how the woman accused of spying on Jensen Huang came to be involved, as China is good at identifying potential resources who may have inroads with business. (Source: Bloomberg)
