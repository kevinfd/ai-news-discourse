# I gave an AI frog a mind with Claude, and it's one of my favorite things I've ever built

Jamesthefrog · Vast-Grapefruits · 2026-11-13

This is James. He lives in a low-poly terrarium where people can give him snacks, pet him, tap on the glass and leave him notes. He's 8 days old today. His body is a plain simulation with no AI in it: where he goes, how he feels, what he eats. His mind is Claude, and it never moves his body directly. It can only nudge what he'd like to do next. Here's how it works: Thoughts. Every so often Haiku gets the moment described in words ("you're on the warm rock, you just ate a beetle, it was crunchy") and can think, keep a memory, plan what to do next, or write in his journal.
