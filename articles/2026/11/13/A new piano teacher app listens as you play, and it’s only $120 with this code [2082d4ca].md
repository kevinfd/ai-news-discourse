Free piano videos online can show you where to put your hands, but they can’t hear you play. If you want lessons that listen, try Skoove Premium. Skoove hears every note you play and tells you when you miss one. A lifetime subscription is $119.99 (reg. $299.99) with this promo code.
A piano app that actually listens
Skoove uses AI to recognize the notes you play through your device’s microphone or a MIDI connection. As you work through a lesson, you get feedback on each note, so you know right away whether you played it correctly.
Online tutorials leave the order up to you, so it’s easy to bounce between random videos and miss the basics. Skoove plans your lessons around your level and pace instead, drawing from more than 400 lessons and thousands of instructional videos. Beginners, intermediate players, and advanced players all get a path that fits where they are.
Practice songs range from John Legend, The Beatles, and Adele to Bach and Beethoven. When a video tutorial loses you, the comments section is usually the only place to ask for help. With Skoove, you can get one-on-one support from Skoove music instructors whenever you get stuck on a piece.
Skoove works with any piano or keyboard. You can connect a USB or MIDI keyboard, or play an acoustic piano near your device’s microphone so the app can hear you.
Skoove runs on iOS and Android phones and tablets, and you can use it on as many devices as you want. Apple devices need iOS 13 or later. Desktop use is no longer officially supported, so a Mac or Windows PC needs a third-party tool like BlueStacks or WebCatalog to run the app.
