# Nothing Ramps Up Screen-Free AI Gadget Teasers, Out Next Month

PCMag · James Peckham · 2026-11-13

Smartphone, audio, and accessory maker Nothing is expanding into AI-dedicated devices, and teasers are beginning to reveal what its first iteration will look like. A recent teaser from CEO Carl Pei confirmed a December launch for Nothing's screen-free, AI-powered gadget.
Pei recently called the product “a tool, but one built with the personality and care that have come to define a Nothing product." He also said he believes it wouldn't have worked as a smartphone app.
Nothing's latest teaser offers a brief glimpse, though it doesn’t reveal much about what the upcoming gadget can do. The video shows a rectangular item with a brushed silver metal design. There are two lights, green on the left and red on the right, above a larger recording button.

Pei’s accompanying message says, “Context doesn't live in a chat box." This appears to be a voice recorder that lets you talk directly to an AI, though we'll have to wait for confirmation from Nothing to know for sure.
Nothing confirmed the gadget will “ship next month,” meaning it’ll be in customers' hands by the end of December. It’s unclear what it will be called, how much it will cost, what it will do, and how much stock will be initially available. If this is a limited release, it may be difficult to secure your own.
Nothing first began talking about AI-native devices in September last year, when it launched its Essential Apps platform, software that lets you build personalized tools for its devices. It said then it would launch an AI-native product before the end of 2026.
This won’t be the first AI-dedicated gadget you can buy, but Nothing is among the first major tech companies to release one. The most talked-about rival is from OpenAI and designer Jony Ive, who are working together on a product expected to arrive in 2027.
