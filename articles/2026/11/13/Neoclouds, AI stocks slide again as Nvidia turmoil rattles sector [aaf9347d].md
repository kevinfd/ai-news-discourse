# Neoclouds, AI stocks slide again as Nvidia turmoil rattles sector

Channel NewsAsia · CNA · 2026-11-13

Neoclouds, AI stocks slide again as Nvidia turmoil rattles sector
Nov 13 : Shares of AI-related US companies dropped again on Friday, with neoclouds among the worst hit, fueled by fresh jitters over AI financing following Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's abrupt exit.
The tech-heavy Nasdaq-100 declined 2 per cent, on pace to erase about $800 billion in market value if the losses hold through the close. The Philadelphia SE Semiconductor index also shed 5.1 per cent, facing a more than $560 billion wipeout for the day.
CoreWeave and Nebius Group, neocloud firms backed by Nvidia, slumped 8.8 per cent and 9 per cent, respectively, extending their sharp declines from Thursday when longtime CEO Huang stepped down after revelations of extramarital affairs.
Nvidia, under Huang, has financed infrastructure built around its chips, a strategy that has driven demand but also raised questions about circular funding flows between the chipmaker and its customers.
"It's a short-term death blow to the industry because the amount of vendor financing and financing guarantees that Nvidia and Jensen Huang stepped into are now over," said Thomas Hayes, chairman of Great Hill Capital LLC.
Further, Nscale, a British cloud provider also backed by Nvidia, amended its IPO filing to say it expects to report a net loss of $1.7 billion in the nine months ended September 30, wider than the $433.3 million net loss a year ago.
A media report on Friday said Nvidia's board has approached Arm CEO Rene Haas in its search for a potential successor to Huang. Arm shares were last down 4.8 per cent.
Chip stocks, widely regarded as among the biggest winners of the AI boom, also dropped. Advanced Micro Devices fell 10.7 per cent and Broadcom lost 5.9 per cent after both gained for the last five sessions.
Memory chipmaker Micron Technology shed 5 per cent while data storage companies Western Digital and Sandisk dipped 6.6 per cent and 8.1 per cent, respectively.
Photonic product makers Lumentum and Coherent, along with AI server makers Dell and HPE, fell between 5.3 per cent and 9 per cent each.
Utilities and equipment suppliers exposed to the AI boom, including GE Vernova, Constellation Energy and Vistra, eased about 2.2 per cent to 3.5 per cent each.
AI hyperscalers were also mostly lower, barring Meta Platforms, which rose 1.2 per cent. Oracle, Alphabet, Amazon.com and Microsoft fell between 0.2 per cent and 2.7 per cent.
Nvidia's quarterly earnings and AI lab Anthropic's much-awaited public markets debut are set for next week and could prove to be catalysts for sentiment towards AI.
"You have no idea what to expect from Nvidia. That's the problem. This company is pure uncertainty right now," said Steve Sosnick, chief market analyst at Interactive Brokers.
