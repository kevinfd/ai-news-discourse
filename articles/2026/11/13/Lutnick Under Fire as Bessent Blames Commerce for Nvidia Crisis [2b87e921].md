# Lutnick Under Fire as Bessent Blames Commerce for Nvidia Crisis

Bloomberg · 2026-11-13

Denver Riggleman, CEO of RIIG Technology, said that it is 'mind boggling' that anyone would defend Jensen Huang's actions as he faces the personal, political and legal fallout from his affair with an alleged Chinese spy. Riggleman, who served in Congress as a representative from Virginia, said that lawmakers who want to get real answers from both Jensen Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick should call on intelligence experts to advise them on their line of questioning. (Source: Bloomberg)
