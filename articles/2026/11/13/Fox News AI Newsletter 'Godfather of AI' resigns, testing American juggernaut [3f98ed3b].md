UNCHARTED WATERS: The resignation of Jensen Huang as CEO of Nvidia on Thursday roiled the company's stock, as investors and analysts assessed the path forward for the AI giant.
SMARTER SKIES: The Trump administration's new AI-supported air traffic management tool has already helped reroute nearly 180 flights carrying approximately 24,000 passengers to avoid congestion, cutting flight times by more than 10 minutes on average, according to new Department of Transportation (DOT) data shared exclusively with Fox News Digital.
ELECTION RIPPLE: A new analysis from a conservative AI safety group found that deep-red counties with large data centers or proposed projects shifted further toward Democrats in last week's midterm elections than comparable counties without such projects.
EYES INSIDE: New York announces the launch of the Center for AI Responsibility and Research at Binghamton University, where researchers will scrutinize registered AI models. Billionaire Bloomberg cofounder Tom Secunda, a major Democratic Party donor, led a group that gave $30 million to launch the center.
PRICE TAGGED: You probably know that hackers want your passwords, credit card information and other personal details. But what if they could also figure out how much you're worth to them? That's exactly what researchers say a newly discovered malware strain is designed to do. It steals information from your computer, studies your digital life and then uses artificial intelligence to build a detailed profile of you. The goal? Help criminals decide which victims deserve more attention and which stolen accounts could bring in the most money.
TOO MUCH INFORMATION: You post a few photos from a concert on Instagram. The next day, an email arrives that appears to be from the venue, offering you access to exclusive photos from the show. It mentions the artist you saw and even references the night you attended. Sounds legitimate, right? You might be tempted to click without giving it a second thought. But what if that email came from a scammer who used artificial intelligence to study your social media posts?
BIG TECH, BIG BETS: Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company behind the Claude chatbot, is seeking a valuation of up to $1.02 trillion in a blockbuster initial public offering, falling short of earlier expectations as Wall Street grows increasingly cautious about the enormous costs fueling the AI boom.
NEXT LEVEL: Adobe and Google are announcing an AI-focused strategic partnership on Tuesday that will deepen the integration of the two companies' products.
HIDDEN AGENDAS: Nvidia’s reported spy scandal is shaking markets and rattling the tech world, raising urgent questions about how close foreign agents can get to America’s most powerful corporate leaders.
NOWHERE TO HIDE: My own niece has told me for years that she and her friends could get around age restrictions on Facebook and Instagram when they were younger. They knew how easy it was to make themselves appear older online. And I suspect plenty of parents have heard similar stories. So when I heard that Meta was expanding its AI technology to identify teens who lie about their age, my first thought was, it's about time.
NEXT CHAPTER: The abrupt resignation of Jensen Huang as CEO of Nvidia Thursday following reports he engaged in multiple extramarital affairs leaves the AI giant facing an uncertain future, and a longtime company executive is stepping in as interim CEO.
OPINION: With Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation on Thursday, an accused Chinese spy has taken out the No. 1 player on America’s AI roster. This is worse than a first-and-goal fumble. It’s a body blow that puts the U.S.-China AI race in flux.
