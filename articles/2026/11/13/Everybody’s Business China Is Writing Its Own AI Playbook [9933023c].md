# Everybody’s Business: China Is Writing Its Own AI Playbook

Bloomberg · 2026-11-13

Brad Stone joins Stacey Vanek Smith and Max Chafkin on the Everybody’s Business podcast to discuss his factory tour of EV maker Zeekr and how China is writing its own AI story. (Source: Bloomberg)
