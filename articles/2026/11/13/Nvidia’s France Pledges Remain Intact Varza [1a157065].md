# Nvidia’s France Pledges Remain Intact: Varza

Bloomberg · 2026-11-13

Station F Director Roxanne Varza says French founders remain broadly reassured after Jensen Huang’s departure, with no sign Nvidia’s recent investment commitments are being withdrawn. 
She says the immediate damage appears reputational rather than operational, while prompting founders to consider the risks of building a company too closely around one leader.
