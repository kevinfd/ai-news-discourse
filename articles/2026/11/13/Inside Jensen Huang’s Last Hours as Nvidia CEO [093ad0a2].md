# Inside Jensen Huang’s Last Hours as Nvidia CEO

Bloomberg · 2026-11-13

Bloomberg's Ian King said that situation Jensen Huang put himself in after he had multiple affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy, gave the former Nvidia CEO 'no choice' other than the resign from the company. King said that in his final days at the company Huang tried to 'cauterize the wounds' and accept responsibility, but that it ultimately wasn't enough. (Source: Bloomberg)
