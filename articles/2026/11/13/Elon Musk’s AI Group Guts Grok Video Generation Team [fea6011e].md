# Elon Musk’s AI Group Guts Grok Video Generation Team

The Information · Aaron Tilley, Claudia Chong, Juro Osawa · 2026-11-13

SpaceX’s artificial intelligence group has axed most of the team behind Grok Imagine, its AI tool for video and image generation, according to people familiar with the situation. 

 Around 60 people who worked on Grok Imagine were laid off in the past week from the SpaceX Super Intelligence group, leaving only a handful of employees to keep the product alive. The tool remains available to users, though its future is uncertain without a dedicated team working on it.
