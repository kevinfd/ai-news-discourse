# Banned Nvidia Chips Installed at China Data Center Linked to Alice Huang

Bloomberg · Andy Lin, Mackenzie Hawkins and Kari Lindberg · 2026-11-13

A Chinese company founded by Alice Huang, the woman at the center of a controversy dogging Nvidia Corp., procured smuggled AI chips that were installed at a Shanghai data center with links to Megaspeed International Pte, according to documents reviewed by Bloomberg News.
