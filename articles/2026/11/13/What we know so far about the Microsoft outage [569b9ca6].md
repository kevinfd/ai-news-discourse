# What we know so far about the Microsoft outage

BBC News · 2026-11-13

What we know so far about the Microsoft outage
A Microsoft outage is affecting services including Teams, Outlook and AI software Copilot.
Microsoft has said the problem was caused by a higher-than-usual stress on its servers but that systems were now showing "early signs of stabilisation" as work continued on a fix.
The BBC's technology reporter Shiona McCallum explains what we know.
