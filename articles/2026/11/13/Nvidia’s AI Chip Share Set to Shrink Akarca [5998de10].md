# Nvidia’s AI Chip Share Set to Shrink: Akarca

Bloomberg · 2026-11-13

Callosum co-founder Dan Akarca says Nvidia will remain dominant in the near term, but its market share is likely to decline as more specialized AI chips emerge. 
He says Callosum’s software can direct different AI tasks to the processors best suited to them, potentially cutting costs, improving performance and reducing energy use. 
Akarca was speaking to Tom Mackenzie on the latest episode of Bloomberg Tech: Europe which explored the risks and rewards for Europe following Jensen Huang's departure as Nvidia CEO. (Source: Bloomberg)
