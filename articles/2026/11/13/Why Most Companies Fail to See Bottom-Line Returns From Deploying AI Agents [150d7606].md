# Why Most Companies Fail to See Bottom-Line Returns From Deploying AI Agents

Forbes · Jonathan Palmer, Forbes Staff · 2026-11-13

On today’s episode of Iconoclast, 01.ai Founder and CEO Dr. Kai-Fu Lee sat down with Forbes to discuss his book ‘AI Native: The Mandate to Transform Your Company’. Lee talks about restructuring corporate hierarchies to manage AI agents through designated responsible individuals, pivoting his company away from building frontier models to focus on enterprise ontology and executive tools like Boss AI, and preparing for a potential Hong Kong listing.
