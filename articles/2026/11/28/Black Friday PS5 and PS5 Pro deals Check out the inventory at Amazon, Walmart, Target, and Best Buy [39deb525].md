# Black Friday PS5 and PS5 Pro deals: Check out the inventory at Amazon, Walmart, Target, and Best Buy

Mashable · Soumya Kumar · 2026-11-28

Black Friday PS5 and PS5 Pro deals: Check out the inventory at Amazon, Walmart, Target, and Best Buy
If you are trying to get your hands on a PlayStation 5 right now, the real win is not a discount — it is simply finding one in stock at standard retail price. Between a global RAM shortage driven by the AI boom, which pushed Sony to hike console prices twice in under a year, and the massive hype around GTA 6, standalone console markdowns are nowhere to be found.
Today, the PS5 Slim Digital Edition starts at $599.99, and the flagship PS5 Pro retails for $899.99. Depending on the model, that means PlayStation 5 hardware actually costs $150 to $200 more today than it did when the console originally launched in 2020.
For weeks, the hype around GTA 6 completely wiped out store shelves across the country — especially for the PS5 Pro. The good news is that major retailers like Walmart, Best Buy, Target, and Amazon are finally restocking standard PS5 models at regular list price.
We recommend paying full sticker price, because third-party marketplace listings are barely worth looking at anyway — the few that are up are mostly refurbished units marked up to nearly double the retail cost, so we left them off our list.
PS5 vs. PS5 Pro — which restock should you grab?
Even at full sticker price, picking up a PS5 or PS5 Pro makes a lot of sense if you are upgrading for GTA 6 or catching up on Sony's massive library of current-gen games.
The standard PlayStation 5 Digital Edition gives you the core current-gen experience in a slim, compact design that fits easily into a media console. You get an 825GB ultra-high-speed SSD that cuts load screens to seconds, 4K TV output, up to 120 fps gameplay on supported titles, and Tempest 3D AudioTech.
It also ships with Sony's DualSense wireless controller — featuring haptic feedback and adaptive triggers that add physical resistance when you pull a trigger or drive over rough terrain — and plays more than 8,500 backward-compatible PS4 games with smoother frame rates. Plus, if you ever decide you want to buy physical discs later, you can snap Sony's detachable disc drive right onto the side.

If you want the best possible visuals in Vice City, stepping up to the PlayStation 5 Pro unlocks PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) for sharper AI-upscaled clarity, upgraded GPU performance, and advanced ray tracing. While Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 targets 30 fps across all consoles at launch, the PS5 Pro is the only system that gets exclusive ray-traced shadows and cleaner image upscaling in the game.
Beyond GTA 6, dozens of major PS5 titles run at higher frame rates and sharper resolutions on the Pro, and its massive 2TB SSD easily swallows GTA 6's 212GB install size with plenty of storage left over for the rest of your library.
Where to find the PS5 and PS5 Pro right now
If you want to lock down a console while shelves still have stock, here is where the PS5 Digital Edition and PS5 Pro are currently available at standard retail price:
- Amazon — Along with the standalone PlayStation 5 Digital Edition and PlayStation 5 Pro 2TB available at regular retail price, Amazon also has the PlayStation 5 Digital Edition (825GB) + DualSense Wireless Controller Bundle discounted by roughly $22 — the only actual bundle discount floating around major stores right now.
- Best Buy — Both the PlayStation 5 Digital Edition and the PlayStation 5 Pro 2TB are available to order online right now at list price.
- Target — You can grab both the PlayStation 5 Digital Edition and the PlayStation 5 Pro 2TB at standard retail price while current restocks last.
- Walmart — The PlayStation 5 Digital Edition is back in stock directly from Walmart at regular retail price.
Where to actually save on PS5 hardware
If you don't want to pay 2026's inflated brand-new sticker prices, look straight at PlayStation Direct's certified refurbished lineup. Sony sells official refurbished PS5 and PS5 Pro consoles for up to $100 less than brand-new units ($499.99 for the Digital Edition, $549.99 for the Disc Edition, and $849 for the PS5 Pro), backed by a full one-year manufacturer's warranty. It is the most reliable way to shave real money off the console without gambling on sketchy third-party resellers.
You might also spot occasional game bundles at big-box stores, though availability is hit-or-miss. Even the overpriced bundles and double-the-price third-party listings floating around online are largely sold out, proving just how intense the demand still is.
After watching store shelves sit completely empty less than a week ago, seeing major retailers finally restock both the PS5 and PS5 Pro is a welcome surprise. If you are trying to jump into GTA 6 without paying double to a reseller, grab an official unit at retail price — or check PlayStation Direct's refurbished store — before this wave clears out too.
