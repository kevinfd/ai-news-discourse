# Weekly Funding Roundup: The startup deals you may have missed in week of November 29

Moneycontrol · Moneycontrol News · 2026-11-28

15
The week’s funding deals, beyond the usual headline-grabbers. From enterprise AI and digital lending to jet engines, robotics, nuclear technology and jewellery, here are some of the companies that raised fresh capital during the week of November 23–29.
  November 28, 2026 / 14:26 IST  
The week’s biggest funding rounds grabbed the spotlight, but they weren’t the only deals signed. Here’s a roundup of other startups that announced fresh funding over the past week.
Finnable
Funding: Rs 405 crore, Series C
Key investors in this round: Bharat Value Fund (BVF), which invested Rs 150 crore, along with Pratithi Investments, NK Squared, existing investors TVS Capital and MEMG Family Office
What it does: Bengaluru-based Finnable is a digital lending startup. It plans to use the fresh capital to expand its lending portfolio, strengthen its technology stack and build partnerships across the lending ecosystem.
AidenAI
Funding: $32 million (around Rs 297 crore), Series A
Key investors in this round: A91 Partners
What it does: AidenAI builds enterprise AI agents and technology modernisation solutions. Founded in 2023, the startup serves more than 30 enterprise clients across six countries and plans to expand its platform and engineering capabilities.
Green Aero
Funding: $14 million (around Rs 129.2 crore), Series A
Key investors in this round: Crane Ventures
What it does: Founded in 2023, Green Aero is developing jet propulsion systems, avionics and airframes for high-speed drones. The startup plans to develop engines in the four-kilonewton-plus category and build autonomous combat aircraft.
Matters.AI
Funding: Rs 115 crore, pre-Series A
Key investors in this round: Tanglin Venture Partners, IIFL Fintech Fund, Piper Serica's Bharat Tech Fund, Foster Ventures and Tetration Ventures, along with existing investors Kalaari Capital and Endiya Partners
What it does: Matters.AI is building an AI security platform for enterprise data. Its platform tracks where sensitive data is stored and moves, who or what accesses it, and whether that usage is intended. The round takes its total funding to around Rs 175 crore.
XEBO.ai
Funding: $10.5 million (around Rs 97.1 crore), Series A
Key investors in this round: Emirates Growth Fund
What it does: Dubai-headquartered XEBO.ai is an experience management company building an AI-native voice-of-customer platform. It plans to use the capital to expand its product portfolio, strengthen its presence in existing markets and enter North America.
Vegapay
Funding: $10 million (around Rs 92.3 crore), Series A
Key investors in this round: Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia and India and Prosus
What it does: Mumbai-based Vegapay is building credit-line infrastructure on UPI. It plans to use the capital to onboard more banks and introduce secured credit lines backed by fixed deposits, gold and mutual funds. The round includes primary and secondary transactions, allowing more than 40 employees to sell part of their vested ESOPs.
GIVA
Funding: Rs 90 crore in debt financing
Key investors in this round: BlackSoil
What it does: GIVA is an omnichannel fine jewellery brand offering products across silver, gold and lab-grown diamond categories. The capital will support store capital expenditure and working capital as it expands its retail network in India.
Pretxt
Funding: $5 million, seed round
Key investors in this round: Deciens Capital, Howdy Partners, NSV Wolf Capital and Service Provider Capital
What it does: Pretxt is developing an AI supervision platform for enterprises. Its runtime technology monitors AI agents as they work, evaluates their behaviour against business expectations and guides them back when they deviate from approved processes.
Zavya
Funding: Rs 22.6 crore, pre-Series A
Key investors in this round: Daksham Growth Ventures and existing investor Orios Venture Partners
What it does: Zavya is a budget jewellery brand offering everyday 925 sterling silver products, including rings, pendants, earrings and anklets. The round values the company at around Rs 191.5 crore post-money.
Cosine Robots
Funding: Rs 22.36 crore
Key investors in this round: Avaana Capital, WEH Ventures and angel investors
What it does: Bengaluru-based Cosine Robots develops high-performance actuators and advanced motion systems for robotics. It plans to use the capital to set up a dedicated research and production facility, accelerate development of its integrated servo actuator portfolio and expand its engineering team.
SUK-M
Funding: Undisclosed pre-seed round
Key investors in this round: Speciale Invest and Navam Capital as co-leads, along with BYT Capital and two angel investors
What it does: SUK-M, which describes itself as India's first thorium-based micro-modular nuclear reactor, is developing a compact reactor designed to supply electricity and high-temperature heat. The funding will support a testing facility and the recruitment of scientists and engineers in India.
