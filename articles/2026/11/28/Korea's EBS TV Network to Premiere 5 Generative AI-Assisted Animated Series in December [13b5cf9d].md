# Korea's EBS TV Network to Premiere 5 Generative AI-Assisted Animated Series in December

Anime News Network · Korean · 2026-11-28

News
Korea's EBS TV Network to Premiere 5 Generative AI-Assisted Animated Series in December
posted on  by Wonhee Cho
South Korea's public educational broadcaster Educational Broadcasting System (EBS) will premiere five animated series created using generative artificial intelligence (AI) technology, beginning December 1. The series were selected through the broadcaster's 2026 Generative AI Animation Series Co-Production Project.
The five projects were selected from 429 submissions, representing about 86 entries per selected project. The productions combine generative AI with traditional animation techniques, including stop-motion, 2D, 3D, clay animation, felt puppetry, and paper cutouts.
To showcase the selected works, EBS will establish a dedicated AI animation programming block on EBS 1TV, airing Tuesdays through Thursdays from 5:20 p.m. to 5:50 p.m. KST.
The first series, Coconini's Fairy Tale Adventure Team, will premiere on December 1 at 5:20 p.m. The musical fantasy follows a cheerful child named Coco and Nini, a magical stuffed toy, as they travel into fairy tale worlds, overcome challenges with their friends, and tell their stories through songs and dances.
Stories Told by the Night will follow at 5:35 p.m. The series follows Hyeon, a lonely child who feels overlooked by his busy parents, as he explores different story worlds with a small AI device named Bam and learns about emotions and relationships. Each episode explores themes such as understanding, patience, courage, and waiting, while incorporating different visual styles, including felt puppets, colored-pencil drawings, clay animation, and traditional Korean folk painting.
On December 2, Euphoria Quest will premiere at 5:20 p.m. The comedic fantasy follows four novice adventurers who become separated after a mysterious explosion in a dungeon. As they confront their troubled pasts and reunite, they work together to uncover the dungeon's secrets.
At 5:30 p.m., Monobooks Cat Bookstore will debut. The series follows Mono, a quiet cat who runs a bookstore and comforts animal customers through the books he recommends.
The fifth selected series, Yellow Socks Popo, is scheduled to premiere in March 2027. Set in a facility where discarded single socks end up, the story follows a protagonist who challenges an army of plain white socks and discovers the importance of individuality and uniqueness.
EBS also plans to rebroadcast Euphoria Quest every Thursday at 5:40 p.m.
According to EBS, the project aims to support emerging creators and explore how generative AI can complement traditional artistic techniques. Rather than relying solely on AI-generated visuals, the selected productions combine the technology with handmade aesthetics and creative storytelling.
