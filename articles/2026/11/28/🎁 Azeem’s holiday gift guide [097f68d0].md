# 🎁 Azeem’s holiday gift guide

Exponential View · Azeem Azhar · 2026-11-28

Readers have been emailing me to find out which paper and pen I use as I return to handwriting. So, that gave me an excuse to put together a gift guide for you all. Everything from my favorite stationery, tech and boozy ice cream. 20 items. Here you go:
For the person who thinks on paper
- Kolo Tino fountain pen. I use AI a lot, and handwriting is how I protect my thinking. I sometimes take the cartridge out of my fountain pen and dip the nib in ink. Every ten or fifteen words I have to stop, which gives me a moment to look at what I’ve just written.
- Paper Republic le trifold. I keep three inserts in my leather journal. One is for to-dos, and the other two are for thinking.
- An unlined Moleskine. You can’t go wrong with this one.
- Wispr Flow or Otter. Once I’ve worked through something by hand, I read it aloud into Wispr Flow. I tend to edit as I speak. Sometimes that’s when I hear that I’ve put two ideas in the wrong order. Otter is an older transcriber I’ve used for nearly a decade. It doesn’t have some of the fancy frills of Wispr Flow or Granola. But sometimes you just want good, old-fashioned transcription.
For music lovers
- The Saturday Night Fever soundtrack on vinyl. My first record, at seven, and still one of my favorite albums. I’ve had it on cassette, CD, MP3, and streaming. Eventually I bought it on vinyl again.
- DJ.Studio. This is how I got into making house mixes. It helped me work out where to bring in the vocals and how to get from one track to the next. I’ve since bought a pair of decks, but I still make mixes on my laptop during long flights.
- Roon. I use Roon to play music around the house.
- T+A Solitaire T headphones. These are the noise-canceling headphones I use at home when I’m listening and want to wander about. They have a built-in DAC, which gives a much higher-quality sound. A few tracks I use to try out headphones are Dire Straits’ “Sultans of Swing,” Bach’s Goldberg Variations, Yosi Horikawa’s “Letter,” and Energy 52’s “Café del Mar.”
For the person who spends too much time on planes
- Beats Fit Pro earbuds. These are the ones I use day to day, especially for music, workouts, and long flights. I’ve had less luck with the mic on calls. The Fit Pro is an older model, so check the listing. I love headphones and have about seven other pairs, including a couple of Apple wired Earpods in different bags as backups.
- Pzizz. I’ve used this sleep-audio app for about a decade. I put it on for naps, overnight flights, and jet lag. Paired with the Beats Fit Pros, it can almost give you a half-night’s sleep on a flight.
