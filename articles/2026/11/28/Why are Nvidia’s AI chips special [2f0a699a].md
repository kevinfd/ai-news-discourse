# Why are Nvidia’s AI chips special?

Fast Company · 2026-11-28

The most advanced AI chips, including Nvidia’s, are fabricated largely by one company, Taiwanese chip giant Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co., known as TSMC. Demand for AI hardware has also strained supplies of memory chips, creating shortages that reach gaming graphics cards and smartphones.
