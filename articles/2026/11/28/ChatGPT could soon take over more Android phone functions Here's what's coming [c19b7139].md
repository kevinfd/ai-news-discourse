# ChatGPT could soon take over more Android phone functions: Here's what's coming

The News International · Aqsa Qaddus Tahir · 2026-11-28

ChatGPT could soon take over more Android phone functions: Here's what's coming
At present ChatGPT is limited to voice conversations and text replies
OpenAI is planning to integrate ChatGPT deeper on your Android phone, allowing the AI assistant to make phone calls, send text messages and control media playback.
It is no mistake to say that OpenAI is following Google’s suit, making Gemini more accessible to Pixel phone users through task automation capabilities.
At the time of writing, ChatGPT cannot do much on your phone besides answering the questions.
An APK teardown of version 1.2026.328 of the ChatGPT Android app by Android Authority has revealed several references to upcoming device upgrades.
As per the report, ChatGPT could soon be able to access your contacts and call history, make phone calls, and read and send SMS messages after providing the necessary permissions.
Additionally, the AI model could also control music playback through Android’s notification access permission. Here is the catch. One can control existing playback sessions through ChatGPT rather than opening a music app and playing a specific song.
Another upgrade is how ChatGPT could answer questions based on the provision of information on your screen without making an effort of uploading a screenshot.
A "Use screen and app data" toggle suggests ChatGPT could read what is on your display to answer questions, refreshing its view of the screen for each follow-up.
None of these features have a confirmed release timeline or guaranteed rollout.
