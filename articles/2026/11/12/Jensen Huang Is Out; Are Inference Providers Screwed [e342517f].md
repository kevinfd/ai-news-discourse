# Jensen Huang Is Out; Are Inference Providers Screwed?

The Information · Stephanie Palazzolo · 2026-11-12

What a week. Jensen Huang , the CEO and cofounder of the world’s second-most-valuable company, resigned, Nvidia said Thursday morning , after reports of extramarital affairs both with a woman alleged to have spied for Beijing as well as a woman who reported to him at Nvidia . Nvidia chief financial officer Colette Kress will become interim CEO, the company said, while it searches for a permanent successor. Much remains uncertain, including implications for Nvidia’s business of selling advanced AI chips and for Nvidia’s aggressive investments in AI model developers and infrastructure startups…
