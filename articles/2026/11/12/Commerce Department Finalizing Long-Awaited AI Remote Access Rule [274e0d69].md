# Commerce Department Finalizing Long-Awaited AI Remote Access Rule

The Information · Leo Schwartz · 2026-11-12

The Commerce Department is finalizing a long-awaited rule that will prevent Chinese AI firms from remotely accessing advanced AI chips through data centers in other countries, according to two people familiar with the process. The move could help the department push back on criticism that it has acted too slowly to create policies to limit China’s access to advanced AI technologies—rules that have been opposed by influential companies such as Nvidia.
