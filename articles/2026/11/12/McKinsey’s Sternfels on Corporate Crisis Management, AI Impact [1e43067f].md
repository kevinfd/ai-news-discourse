# McKinsey’s Sternfels on Corporate Crisis Management, AI Impact

Bloomberg · 2026-11-12

McKinsey Global Managing Partner Bob Sternfels discusses how companies should prepare for corporate crises, from succession planning to strong and independent governance. Speaking exclusively on “The Pulse With Francine Lacqua” amid the leadership crisis at Nvidia, Sternfels also discusses how AI is reshaping McKinsey and its workforce, what CEOs are getting wrong about adopting the technology, and vulnerabilities in global trade.
