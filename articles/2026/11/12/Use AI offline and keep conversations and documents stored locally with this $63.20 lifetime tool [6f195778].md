AI tools can be incredibly useful, but there’s a trade-off worth thinking about: when you use a cloud-based assistant, your conversations and uploaded documents may be processed outside your own computer. If you’d rather keep more control over your information — and skip another monthly AI bill — Pansophy offers a different approach. Instead of sending your prompts and files to a cloud service, Pansophy runs its AI processing locally on your computer.
