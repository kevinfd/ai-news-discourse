# Google's Gemini Joins ChatGPT As an Option for AI Voice Chats Via CarPlay

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-11-12

Google has launched a dedicated Gemini app for Apple CarPlay, allowing iOS users to have back-and-forth conversations with the chatbot right from their infotainment screen, thanks to Gemini Live, Google says.
The chatbot was already available through Google Maps for location and travel queries, but this update brings the full-fledged version to cars that support CarPlay.
With your calendar and email connected to Gemini, you can ask it about meetings lined up for the day, catch up on unread emails, or get a quick summary of long email threads. Sample prompts shared by Google include “Help me prep for my 10 a.m. with the design team,” “Give me my daily brief for today,” and “Catch me up on my unread emails and summarize Sarah’s Q4 budget thread.”
Further, you can use Gemini as a sounding board for a presentation or negotiation you’re about to enter. Beyond work, you can use the tool for recommendations on nearby lunch places, the history of a local landmark you just crossed, or other subjects that intrigue you.

To use Gemini on Apple CarPlay, you must update the app to its latest version on your iPhone, give necessary microphone permissions, and use Gemini Live at least once. After that, the next time you connect your phone to your car, a Gemini logo should be visible in the app drawer.
Gemini is only available in voice mode on Apple CarPlay. Once you launch the app on your infotainment screen, tap New Conversation. Transcripts of all conversations will be automatically saved to the chat history.
If you have trouble finding the app on your car’s dashboard, go to Settings on your iPhone > General > CarPlay > My Car (select your car’s name) > Apps and confirm whether Gemini has been added to the list of Included Apps.
Android Auto and Google built-in, meanwhile, have already replaced the now-defunct Google Assistant with Gemini. In February, Apple announced that it would allow third-party voice-based conversational apps on CarPlay with iOS 26.4. The update began rolling out in March, and OpenAI was among the first to take advantage of the new capability with ChatGPT.
