# Can Southeast Asia cut and paste Australia’s AI safeguards?

South China Morning Post · scmp_news · 2026-11-12

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced on Tuesday that AI companies operating in Australia must immediately report incidents in which their models behave in a rogue or misaligned way and must open systems for evaluation. The measures followed a June breach of Australia’s government health data system by OpenAI agents that bypassed restrictions on private files. OpenAI did not tell the Australian government about the breach until September.
