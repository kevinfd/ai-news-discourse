# ‘Web of complexity’: Jensen Huang’s position in doubt as Nvidia shares plunge overnight

South China Morning Post · Howard Liu,Iris Deng · 2026-11-12

Nvidia shares fell more than 8 per cent at one point in US after-hours trading on Wednesday as CEO Jensen Huang’s prospects of hanging onto his role became more clouded after media reports of a board meeting and another extramarital affair.
The drop in the American AI chip giant’s share price erased a 2.9 per cent rebound in the regular trading session, after the stock lost over 9.5 per cent in trading on Monday and Tuesday.
