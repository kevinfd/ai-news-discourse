# Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO amid affairs scandal

South China Morning Post · Xiaofei Xu,Vincent Chow,Chong Ming Lee,Iris Deng · 2026-11-12

Jensen Huang has resigned as president, chief executive officer and board member of the AI chip giant Nvidia with immediate effect, the firm announced on Thursday, amid media reports of his alleged extramarital affairs.
The company’s executive vice-president and chief financial officer, Colette Kress, will take over as Nvidia’s interim CEO, according to the statement, while continuing in both her current roles.
