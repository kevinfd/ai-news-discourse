# Trump pitches Apple's Tim Cook as new Nvidia CEO

Axios · Avery Lotz · 2026-11-12

President Trump is backing Apple executive chairman Tim Cook to replace tech giant Jensen Huang after Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO, though it's unclear whether he meant it as a joke. The big picture: Trump didn't weigh in on the controversy surrounding Huang's departure following alleged affairs with a suspected Chinese spy and an Nvidia executive, instead calling Huang "a Legend" in his Truth Social post .
