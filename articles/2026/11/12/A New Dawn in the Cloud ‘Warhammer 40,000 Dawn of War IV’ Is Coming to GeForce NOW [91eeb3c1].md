# A New Dawn in the Cloud: ‘Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV’ Is Coming to GeForce NOW

NVIDIA Blog · GeForce NOW Community · 2026-11-12

Rally the ranks this GFN Thursday. Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV is coming to GeForce NOW, allowing players to experience the battle for Kronus through cloud gaming.
There’s more on the horizon, with NVIDIA DLSS 5 technology coming to War Thunder and Where Winds Meet. Meanwhile, Dragon’s Dogma 2: Dark Arisen brings new tales and encounters, alongside 10 games joining the GeForce NOW library this week.
Kronus Calls for Reinforcements
Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV, developed by KING Art Games and published by Deep Silver, is coming to GeForce NOW. Launching on Steam Thursday, Dec. 3, the real-time strategy game returns to the series’ roots with large-scale battles, base building and a renewed fight for planet Kronus.
Command the Space Marines, Orks, Necrons or the Adeptus Mechanicus, which makes their series debut. Build a base, secure resources and customize squads to the conflict across more than 70 campaign missions, playable solo or in co-op mode. The Last Stand, Skirmish and multiplayer modes offer more ways to put tactics to the test.
GeForce NOW will bring the action to supported PCs, Macs, handhelds and other devices, with game downloads and updates handled in the cloud. Save the storage space for everyday essentials and planning for the next offensive. Wishlist the game on Steam, and stay tuned to GFN Thursday to prepare for launch.
DLSS 5 Keeps on Coming
NVIDIA DLSS 5 continues to evolve, with Gaijin Entertainment’s War Thunder and Everstone Studio’s Where Winds Meet next to receive support for the technology with the 3D-Guided Neural Rendering feature.
Arriving in mid-November, War Thunder’s Eagle’s Shadow major update adds DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering, dozens of new planes, ships and tanks, along with cluster bombs for planes and visual-effects enhancements increasing the realism and dynamism of battle.
In Where Winds Meet, DLSS support arrives Monday, Nov. 23, enhancing the environments and characters of its wuxia open-world action role-playing game.
Both titles will use the latest DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering model, released in October, which doubles model performance compared with the previous version. Since its initial announcement in March, model performance has improved tenfold, bringing the technology to more gamers, including GeForce NOW Ultimate members streaming with GeForce RTX 5080 cloud rigs.
Get a closer look at the DLSS updates and find more details.
A New Tale for the Arisen
Dragon’s Dogma 2: Dark Arisen is streaming on GeForce NOW, expanding the deep, explorable fantasy world of Dragon’s Dogma 2 with new tales and encounters. Travel to the long-abandoned region of Norgan, join the enigmatic Eir and uncover the secrets of the undying Fallen Dragon.
Unearth forgotten relics to unlock powerful weapons and skills, take on Lost Rites’ 12 dungeon challenges and explore new hairstyles and tattoos for Arisen and Pawn customization.
Delicious in Dungeon also arrives in the world of Dragon’s Dogma 2: Dark Arisen with a special collaboration.
GeForce NOW keeps all the new downloadable content available across supported devices — without a single download claiming local storage on a gamer’s hardware of choice.
In addition, members can look for the following games joining the GeForce NOW library this week:
- Worst Class Ever (New release on Steam, Nov. 9)
- Ashes of the Singularity II (New release on Steam, Nov. 10)
- Cyberpunk 2077 (Xbox Play Anywhere, new release on Battle.net, Nov. 10)
- DreamWorks Shrek 2 Remastered (New release on Steam, Nov. 10)
- Warhammer Survivors (New release on Steam and Xbox, available on Game Pass, Nov. 10)
- Main Sequence (New release on Steam, Nov. 12)
- Deadly Days: Roadtrip (Steam)
- GeForce 8800 GTX – 20 Years of a Legend (Steam, Ultimate members)
- Graveyard Keeper (Steam)
- Tape to Tape (Steam)
Also this week, Ultimate members can take a trip back to 2006 with GeForce 8800 GTX – 20 Years of a Legend, a playable demo celebrating the iconic GPU’s 20th anniversary with DLSS 4.5 and path tracing in the cloud.
Dates listed above reflect when games are released on their respective stores. GeForce NOW availability may vary, as games are onboarded after release and added throughout the week. Keep an eye on GeForce NOW channels and GFN Thursdays for availability updates on announced titles.
