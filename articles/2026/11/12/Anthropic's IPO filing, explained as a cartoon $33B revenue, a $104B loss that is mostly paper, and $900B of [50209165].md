# Anthropic's IPO filing, explained as a cartoon: $33B revenue, a $104B loss that is mostly paper, and $900B of compute commitments

Youtu · No-Birthday-5559 · 2026-11-12

Short version of what the filing says (Amendment No. 2, filed Nov 10): - Revenue: $41M in 2023, $33.2B in the first nine months of 2026 - Net loss: $104.0B over those nine months, but $94.3B of it is the change in fair value of convertible notes. Operating loss was about $1.3B, and cash from operations was +$6.6B - The number I think matters more: about $900B in purchase commitments, almost all compute - One Class F share carries 50.1% of the votes Happy to be corrected if I misread anything.
