# Nscale, Firmus Deals Hinge on Nvidia Extending Investment Sprint

The Information · Dakin Campbell, Phoebe Liu, Stephanie Palazzolo, Valida Pau, Julia Hornstein · 2026-11-12

In the last three years, Nvidia has emerged as one of the most active backers of AI startups, small cloud providers and frontier AI labs—largely thanks to CEO Jensen Huang’s unrelenting focus on ensuring continued, strong demand for its specialized AI chips. With his sudden resignation late Wednesday, that source of cash now looks at risk.
