# Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns after tech giant rocked by second affair allegation

BBC News · twoloafbread · 2026-11-12

Huang willing to appear before Congress after resigning from Nvidia over affair allegationspublished at 23:36 GMT 12 November
Jacob Phillips
Live reporter
Tech billionaire Jensen Huang has indicated he is willing to appear before Congress to discuss whether US national security was compromised, following a major scandal at one of the most valuable companies in the world.
Huang resigned as the CEO of Nvidia, the enormously successful AI chip company, after it was reported he had two affairs: one with suspected Chinese spy Alice Huang and another with senior employee Kimberly Powell.
The company's stocks plummeted as US markets opened shortly after Huang's resignation, and while it recovered some ground, it closed more than 4% down, with the firm shedding more than half a trillion in market value.
Huang has been replaced for now by the company's Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress who will look to steady the ship at the company.
Dubbed the "Taylor Swift of tech", Huang transformed Nvidia from a video game chip business to develop semiconductors critical for developing AI.
Access to advanced computer chips has become a key battleground between Beijing and Washington, writes our China correspondent. Nvidia's powerful B200 and B300 chips cannot be legally exported to China. Beijing has called allegations of spying a "smear".
