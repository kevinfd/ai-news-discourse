# Character.AI Plans to Auction Off User Chats, 42 State AGs Move to Stop It

PCMag · James Peckham · 2026-11-26

A majority of US state attorneys general have worked together to oppose the sale of the data of millions of Americans during an AI company's bankruptcy proceedings.
The coalition of 42 state AGs issued a series of similar press releases earlier this week, accusing Character.AI of attempting to sell a dataset containing the chat histories of more than 21 million US users.
Each state AG is asking the court to reject the sale after Character.AI first filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy last month. The press releases claim that the sale of its user data would include chat history with the AI tool, as well as other personal data, such as IP addresses and what it calls "other metadata."
It says the information would include 2.1 million accounts registered in minors' names. The AI brand was often used for making AI-powered children’s characters, although it limited functionality for minors in 2025 after one of its models allegedly encouraged a teen to take his own life.
Character.AI previously reassured its customers that their personal information would not be sold as part of the sale process. In announcing its bankruptcy, the company said in a blog post, “Your information continues to be protected under our Privacy Policy, and our data privacy and security practices are unchanged.”
That's no longer the case, with bidding for the data now underway ahead of an auction set for Dec. 1. The press releases from state AGs note that the first bidder was a company called Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC (Smirks). Little is known about its owners or investors, and it was first set up on Nov. 13, 2026.
The case from state AGs notes that the new buyer would not have to follow the original privacy policy and that it would take away existing users' ability to delete their data. It would also allow the auction winner to resell the data without notifying any users.
If you are affected, you may find it worthwhile to export your data from Character.AI and delete your own account. However, it’s unclear whether doing so at this stage, less than a week before the auction, will mean your data is removed ahead of a potential sale. Character.AI has yet to confirm whether that’s the case.
The state AGs opposing the sale include Alaska, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, District of Columbia, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Utah, Virginia, Vermont, Washington, Wisconsin, and West Virginia.
