# AI agents used in five cyber attacks in Switzerland, government says

Channel NewsAsia · CNA · 2026-11-26

AI agents used in five cyber attacks in Switzerland, government says
ZURICH, Nov 26 : Five cyber attacks in the last few days in Switzerland used AI agents to target companies and operators of critical infrastructure, the Federal Office for Cybersecurity (BACS) said on Thursday.
• In four of the five cases, the attacks were successful and systems were compromised, BACS said in a statement
• Three operators of critical infrastructure were among those targeted, BACS said
• These included providers of electricity, water, and telecommunications, Swiss public broadcaster SRF said
• BACS did not give details of who could be behind the attacks
• Companies are being advised to establish protection measures, including regular cyber tests, it said
