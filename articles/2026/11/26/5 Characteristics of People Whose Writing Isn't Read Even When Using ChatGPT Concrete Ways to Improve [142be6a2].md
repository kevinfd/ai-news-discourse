5 Characteristics of People Whose Writing Isn't Read Even When Using ChatGPT | Concrete Ways to Improve AI-Sounding Text and Practical Prompts
"I write articles using ChatGPT, but hardly anyone reads them."
"The writing is neatly organized, but something feels off."
"I want to make AI-generated writing sound more like myself."
Do you find yourself struggling with these issues?
I also create blog and note articles using ChatGPT.
While it has shortened the time it takes to produce text, I have experienced firsthand the difficulty of turning merely more articles into traffic or sales.
Text generated by ChatGPT can be grammatically correct and neatly organized.
However, neat writing is not the same as writing that resonates with readers.
Even if what you write is accurate, if it lacks specificity or sounds like something anyone could have written, the reader's reason to keep reading until the end weakens.
In this article, I will introduce 5 common characteristics of people whose ChatGPT-written texts go unread, along with concrete ways to improve them.
I will compare texts before and after improvement, while also providing practical prompts you can use right away.

1. Writing Content That Applies to Anyone
If you prompt ChatGPT with "Write an article about side gigs," it often ends up focusing on generic explanations.
For example, something like this:
Before Improvement
Starting a side gig is an effective way to increase your income.
It is important to find a side gig that suits you and pursue it consistently.
There is nothing wrong with this content.
However, it doesn't give readers a clear idea of what they should actually do.
After Improvement
On weekdays, you are exhausted from work and have only 30 minutes to spare for a side hustle.
For someone in that position, suddenly starting a side hustle that requires two hours of work every day may be difficult to sustain.
Start by trying tasks you can do in 30 minutes, such as outlining an article or drafting social media posts.
Another approach is to see if you can keep it up for a week before increasing your workload.
In the improved text, the reader's daily life and struggles are envisioned with concrete details.
It is essential to aim for writing that makes readers feel, "This is relevant to me."
Points for Improvement
• Clarify the target audience
• Consider the situation the reader is facing
• Propose concrete actions
• Do not stop at abstract explanations
Practical Prompt 1
Please revise the following text so that the target audience feels it directly speaks to them.
Target Audience: [Audience]
Reader's Problem/Concern: [Problem/Concern]
Reader's Situation: [Situation]
Text to Revise: [Text]
Do not stop at generalities; add concrete scenarios and actionable steps.
Do not fabricate personal experiences or numerical data that cannot be factually verified.
2. Lack of Concrete Examples and Real-Life Experiences
Even if text created with AI is neatly explained and summarized, it often lacks concrete experiences and verified results.
Especially on blogs and platforms like note, the writer's own personal experiences can be what gives an article its originality.
Before Improvement
To earn an income on note, it is important to continuously publish high-quality articles.
In addition, leveraging social media to attract readers can lead to monetization.
After Improvement
Using ChatGPT, I published 44 note articles over the past month.
As of November 26, 2026, my page views over the previous 28 days were 202.
Meanwhile, my revenue checked on November 25 was 0 yen.
I have come to realize firsthand that merely increasing the number of articles does not lead to monetization.
Therefore, I am currently working on guiding readers from free articles to paid ones, as well as refining my profile.
The improved text includes actual operational data.
Even if it isn't a track record of success, the things you tried and the results that didn't go well provide unique value to your articles.
Original Information You Can Add to Articles
Category / Concrete Examples: Actual experiences: Methods you tried yourself, failure stories, reasons things didn't work out; Verification results: Changes before and after improvements; Numerical figures: Traffic metrics, working hours; Comparisons: Impressions from trying multiple approaches; Insights: Challenges discovered through hands-on practice
Just because you lack proven achievements doesn't mean you need to write fictional success stories.
Another approach is simply to honestly share what you are currently working on and the challenges you have yet to solve.
Practical Prompt ②
Please use the following real-life experiences to concretely improve the writing of this article.
Original text: [Text]
Actions actually taken: [Content]
Confirmed results: [Results]
What did not go well: [Content]
Current challenges: [Challenges]
Please use only the information I have provided.
Do not add fictional anecdotes, track records of success, numerical figures, or impressions.
Separate unverified information into a list of items requiring confirmation.
3. Repeating the same phrasing
When generating long-form content with ChatGPT, similar phrasing is sometimes repeated.
For example, expressions such as the following:
・〜することが重要です (It is important to...)
・〜することが大切です (It is essential to...)
・〜することができます (You can...)
・〜することで効率化につながります (Doing ... leads to increased efficiency.)
・〜を意識しましょう (Let's keep ... in mind.)
・〜が期待できます (... can be expected.)
There is nothing inherently wrong with these expressions.
However, when the same sentence endings or structures appear repeatedly, the writing can come across as monotonous.
Before Improvement
By utilizing ChatGPT, you can make text creation more efficient.
Additionally, using appropriate prompts is important.
Furthermore, checking the generated text is crucial.
After Improvement
Using ChatGPT can reduce the time it takes to draft your writing.
However, if your questions are vague, you might not get the answers you were hoping for.
First, try clearly communicating your purpose and criteria.
Once you receive a response, review the content rather than using it as-is.
In the improved version, variation has been added to sentence lengths and endings.
By combining explanations, caveats, and concrete actions, the goal is to create writing that is easy and engaging to read through.
Phrasing to Reconsider
Examples Before and After Improvement: "活用することが重要です" (It is important to utilize) → "活用してみましょう" (Let's try using it); "実施することができます" (It is possible to implement) → "実施できます" (You can implement it); "確認することが必要です" (It is necessary to check) → "確認してください" (Please check); "さまざまなメリットがあります" (There are various benefits) → "主なメリットは3つです" (There are three main benefits); "効率化につながります" (It leads to greater efficiency) → "作業時間を短縮できます" (You can reduce your work time); "適切な方法を選択しましょう" (Let us select an appropriate method) → "自分に合う方法を選びましょう" (Choose the method that suits you).
However, there is no need to force a rephrasing.
The goal is simply to polish the writing into natural phrasing while preserving its meaning and accuracy.
Practical Prompt 3
Please revise the following text into natural, easy-to-read Japanese.
[Paste text here]
Revision conditions:
• Reduce repetitive consecutive sentence endings
• Cut redundant phrasing
• Split sentences that are too long
• Explain technical terms as needed
• Vary the length of your sentences
• Do not alter the original meaning or facts
• Do not use overly casual expressions
Please provide the revised text along with the main points of improvement.
4. Delaying the Answers Readers Are Looking For
Articles created with AI sometimes tend to have lengthy introductions.
For example, when readers want to know "how to create Excel formulas using ChatGPT," an article might start with a lengthy explanation of the history of AI or the basics of Excel.
Readers likely want to access the information they need as quickly as possible.
Before Improvement
In recent years, along with the development of AI technology, artificial intelligence is being utilized across various operations.
Among these tools, ChatGPT is used for a wide range of applications, such as writing texts and organizing information.
Furthermore, Excel is a spreadsheet software used by many companies.
In this article, we will explain ChatGPT and Excel.
After Improvement
When you're not sure which Excel formula to use, try telling ChatGPT the structure of your table and what you want to achieve.
For example, if you have product names in Column A and sales figures in Column B and want to calculate the total sales, you can ask like this:
"Please tell me the Excel formula to sum the sales amounts from B2 to B100."
Sample Response:
=SUM(B2:B100)
In this way, by clearly specifying the cell range and your objective, you can identify the necessary function.
Please verify that the generated function works properly in the actual Excel software.
After the revision, the answer the reader wants to know and a concrete example are presented right at the beginning.
Example of an Easy-to-Read Article Structure
- Present the reader's problem
- State the conclusion
- Provide concrete examples
- Explain reasons and precautions
- Suggest the next action to take
You don't need to use the same structure for every article, but for practical explanatory pieces, presenting the answer early on is effective.
Practical Prompt (4)
Please revise the following article into a structure that allows readers to quickly understand the information they need.
Article Title: [Title]
What Readers Want to Know: [Content]
Article Body: [Body]
Revision Conditions:
• Clarify the reader's problem at the beginning
• Place key conclusions in the first half
• Cut unnecessary introductory remarks
• Indicate places where concrete examples can be added
• Make the content understandable from the headings alone
• Do not alter the original factual relationships
Please provide the revised structure and body text.
5. Publishing AI-generated text without reviewing it
Using ChatGPT can sometimes allow you to create text in a short amount of time.
However, the generated text is not necessarily of a quality that can be published as is.
AI is an auxiliary tool for creating text.
Ultimately, it is the person publishing the article who must verify its content.
Issues to check before publishing
• Inaccurate explanations
• Non-existent services or features
• Outdated information
• Figures without basis
• Fabricated personal experiences
• Unnatural phrasing
• Repetition of the same content
• Discrepancies between the title and body text
Before improvement
By utilizing ChatGPT, even beginners can easily earn 50,000 yen a month.
After Improvement
ChatGPT is a tool that assists with tasks necessary for side businesses, such as writing and organizing information.
However, earnings vary depending on factors such as service demand, quality, customer acquisition, and sales activities.
Simply using ChatGPT does not guarantee a fixed income.
The text before improvement made unsubstantiated assertions about earnings.
Even if your goal is to attract readers' attention, you should avoid expressions that guarantee unverified results.

Checklist Items What to Check Accuracy Does it match the facts? Timeliness Is the information outdated? Specificity Is the content actionable for readers? Originality Does it include your own experiences and thoughts? Readability Is the writing natural? Credibility Are there any unsubstantiated assertions? Practical Prompt ⑤
You are a demanding third-party editor.
Please evaluate the following article before publication.
[Article Body]
Evaluation Criteria:
- Alignment between title and body
- Readability of the opening
- Answers to reader concerns
- Specificity of content
- Originality
- Natural flow of writing
- Parts requiring fact-checking
- Unnecessary explanations and redundancies
- Unsubstantiated claims
- Reasons for readers to read to the end
Please point out the issues with each item and present concrete suggestions for improvement.
Do not force compliments; prioritize parts that should be revised before publishing.
Do not fill in unknown information with guesswork.

5 Steps to Improve Your Writing with ChatGPT
The workflow I keep in mind when creating articles can be broken down as follows.
Step 1 | Define the Purpose of the Article
Clarify what message the article conveys and who you want to read it.
Step 2 | Create a Draft with ChatGPT
Get assistance with crafting article structure and explanatory text.
Step 3 | Add Your Own Experiences and Data
Incorporate what you actually worked on and the results you confirmed.
Step 4 | Evaluate from a Third-Party Perspective
Rigorously check whether the content is something readers actually want to read.
Step 5 | Review Personally and Publish
Verify factual details, phrasing, links, images, and other elements.
After publishing, make improvements as needed based on traffic and reader reactions.
Rather than leaving everything to AI, it is crucial to divide roles between AI and humans.

Body & Readability
- Specific examples are included
- The writing does not end with just abstract explanations
- The same sentence endings do not repeat consecutively
- Sentences that are too long have been split up

Originality and Credibility
- Your own experiences and thoughts are reflected
- No fabricated personal anecdotes are included
- Figures and track records are backed by evidence
- Sections requiring up-to-date information have been verified

Final Checklist Before Publishing
- Typos and omissions have been checked
- Links are accurate
- There are no copyright or personal information issues
- It is easy to read on smartphones as well
- The next action readers should take is clear
Not every article requires original personal anecdotes or figures.
Please review the relevant items according to the purpose of your article.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q. What kind of writing is considered "AI-like"?
It often refers to text that is heavy on generalities, repeats the same expressions, or lacks concrete examples and unique perspectives.
However, it is not possible to accurately determine whether text was generated by AI based solely on its stylistic characteristics.
Q. Will articles written with ChatGPT not be read?
Whether an article gets read is not determined solely by the fact that ChatGPT was used.
Factors such as article content, reader demand, title, traffic generation methods, and accuracy of information also play a role.
Q. Are external tools necessary to make AI-generated writing sound natural?
They are not strictly necessary.
You can make improvements simply by giving revision prompts to ChatGPT or by proofreading the text yourself.
Q. Can I write original articles even if I don't have a proven track record?
Methods you actually tried, experiences of failure, comparison results, and questions you wondered about also lead to originality.
There is no need to force yourself to fabricate successful results.
Q. Is it enough to just have ChatGPT evaluate my articles?
Evaluations by ChatGPT serve as helpful references for finding areas to improve.
However, they cannot substitute for actual reader reactions or fact-checking.
It is also important to check traffic and engagement after publishing.
