# Read the pitch decks of 23 startups looking to disrupt advertising and marketing with AI

Business Insider · Sydney Bradley,Lara O'Reilly,Lucia Moses, Sydney Bradley,Lara O'Reilly,Lucia Moses (sbradley@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-26

Tano cofounders Sagar Shah and Will Caplan recently raised $4 million in investment for their startup, which uses AI to match brands with creators. Tano AI is transforming the advertising and marketing industries. Startups are developing AI tools to help marketers create ads and streamline workflows. Take a look at the pitch decks of 23 media and advertising AI startups shared with Business Insider. AI is reshaping the media and marketing industries at warp speed. Adtech and martech startups are raising millions of dollars from venture capital firms on the back of the AI wave. Many of these companies are developing under-the-hood tech, such as agentic AI tools designed to streamline marketers' workflows and boost productivity. Others are working on creative platforms that let marketers create ads and even virtual influencers using generative AI . Some are working in new fields such as " generative engine optimization" (GEO), helping brands optimize their visibility in AI search results and to AI shopping agents . The AI boom has also sparked a rush of mergers and acquisitions in the advertising space. "Adtech has been innovating with real-time decisioning, identity, and all these component parts that in a much more automated world are very relevant," Sparrow Advisers cofounder Ana Milicevic told CMO Insider. Business Insider has interviewed the founders of startups building tools to disrupt advertising and marketing with AI. These companies shared the pitch decks they used to impress investors and raise venture capital funding.
