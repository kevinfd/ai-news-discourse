# Jensen Huang's Nvidia return just gave Denny's a viral marketing moment

Business Insider · Thibault Spirlet, Thibault Spirlet (tspirlet@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-26

Jensen Huang donned a Denny's apron at Nvidia GTC in 2025. Emma Cosgrove/Business Insider Denny's used Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia to reshare its role in the company's origin story. The chain said it reinstalled a plaque at the booth where Nvidia's three founders first met. Huang, a former dishwasher at Denny's, has repeatedly called the diner brand his "alma mater." Denny's turned Jensen Huang 's return into a very public serving of nostalgia. In social media posts on Wednesday, the diner chain used the Nvidia CEO's comeback to remind the internet that Huang once washed dishes, bussed tables, and served customers at Denny's — before he and his cofounders discussed the idea for Nvidia over coffee at one of its San Jose locations in 1993. "Some things you just don't take off the menu," Denny's wrote. "Jensen Huang is one of them." The posts said Denny's had reinstalled a plaque marking the booth where Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem talked through the idea that became Nvidia after learning earlier this month that it had been taken down. "Jensen, your booth is waiting and the coffee's on," the chain added in the post, which racked up over 3.9 million views on X and garnered thousands of comments across Instagram and Facebook. Every story starts somewhere. We’re proud to be part of Jensen’s. pic.twitter.com/WpWJdLFmIs — Denny’s (@DennysDiner) November 25, 2026 

 The marketing move came two days after Nvidia reinstated Huang as CEO following his 11-day resignation. Huang stepped down after admitting he had been unfaithful to his wife and as the company launched an independent investigation into reports that he had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. Nvidia's board reversed course on Monday after saying it had received input from senior levels of the US government and was satisfied that Huang's return posed no national-security risk. Denny's nostalgia play got a mixed reception Denny's has embraced its link to Huang before, including with its limited-edition Nvidia Breakfast Bytes , which launched around Nvidia's 2025 GTC conference. But the post's timing on Wednesday drew a range of responses split between disbelief, jokes, and criticism. "Taking it down and putting it back up as soon as he gets rehired is crazy work," Diana Regan, an Oakland-based content creator and comedian, commented on Denny's post on Instagram. Some users said the post was a bold choice given the circumstances of Huang's resignation and reinstatement . "Why celebrate a guy who had multiple affairs, in which it had a direct effect on the global economy through the stock market and his ties to the AI bubble?" one commenter wrote in a reply that received more than 6,000 likes. Another summed up their mood more simply: "Genuinely cannot tell if this is a bit or not." Still, the chain's connection to Huang is not manufactured. Huang has repeatedly described Denny's as his first "alma mater," saying the job taught him that no task was too small to do well.
