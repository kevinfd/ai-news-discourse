# Japan's JERA enters talks with Anthropic, Meta to build data centres, Nikkei reports

Channel NewsAsia · CNA · 2026-11-26

Japan's JERA enters talks with Anthropic, Meta to build data centres, Nikkei reports
Nov 27 : Japan's largest power generator, JERA, has entered into talks with Anthropic, Meta, and other US tech giants to build data centers near power plants in the Tokyo area, the Nikkei newspaper reported on Friday.
JERA Global CEO and Chair Yukio Kani said in a recent interview that the energy group is also in discussions with Amazon, Microsoft and Google, Nikkei reported.
JERA announced a partnership with Dell Technologies and UK-based AI infrastructure developer RHAELM in October to accelerate the development of artificial intelligence infrastructure in Japan.
They signed a memorandum of understanding to develop a standardised model for building AI infrastructure on a national scale, starting with a $15 billion hyperscale data centre in Chiba, near Tokyo.
A JERA spokesperson said RHAELM is in charge of marketing and the company had been told that RHAELM is in talks with multiple hyperscalers. RHAELM was not immediately available for comment.
Anthropic, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft and Google did not immediately respond to Reuters' requests for comment.
