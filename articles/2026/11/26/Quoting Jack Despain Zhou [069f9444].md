# Quoting Jack Despain Zhou

Simon Willison · Simon Willison · 2026-11-26

26th November 2026
Among the many already existing effects of AI has been a great de-individualization. I open a YouTube video, and I hear Claude. I sit in a conference presentation, and I hear Claude. I open my email inbox, and I find pitches and articles from ostensibly serious people with ostensibly serious credentials attached to ostensibly serious institutions who want to publish things attached to their reputations and mine, and I see Claude.
— Jack Despain Zhou, Spitting in the Face of Glaude
