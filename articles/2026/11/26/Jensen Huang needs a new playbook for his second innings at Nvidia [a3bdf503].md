# Jensen Huang needs a new playbook for his second innings at Nvidia

The Hindu · John Xavier · 2026-11-26

Within two weeks, Jensen Huang, Nvidia’s co-founder, went from being an AI rainmaker, whose chips and business ingenuity kick-started the AI revolution, to a sex-and-spy scandal-hit CEO, who quit the company he built from scratch and helmed for over three decades, and then returned as its boss once again after a band of tech billionaires intensely lobbied the Trump administration for his reinstatement.
Returning to Nvidia’s Santa Clara headquarters in California on Monday (November 23, 2026), and standing on the ground floor of the atrium, Mr. Huang apologised to employees and vowed to be a better CEO as staff cheered to see their chief back, whose exit wiped out close to a quarter of the company’s market value. Mr. Huang’s return buoyed the market as Nvidia’s stock soared, adding nearly a trillion dollars in market value the day he was back at Nvidia.
