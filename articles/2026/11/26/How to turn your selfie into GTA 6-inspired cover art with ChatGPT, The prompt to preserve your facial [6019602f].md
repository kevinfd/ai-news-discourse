# How to turn your selfie into GTA 6-inspired cover art with ChatGPT, The prompt to preserve your facial features

Revista Merca2.0 · Javier Pérez Ramírez · 2026-11-26

Imagine you have a selfie you love and want to see what it would look like if you were the main character on a video game cover. You can upload that same photo to ChatGPT and ask it to preserve your face, hairstyle, and expression while transforming your surroundings. That’s how the GTA 6-style photo trend works.
Why Vice City? It’s one of the settings of Grand Theft Auto VI, the game Rockstar Games released on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. If you want to explore the colors, lighting, or composition before creating your portrait, Rockstar has made the game’s trailers, image gallery, and official poster available online.
How to create a GTA 6-style photo in ChatGPT
OpenAI allows users to upload a photo to ChatGPT and request changes directly within the conversation. The image generation tool is available across all plans, including the free version. For this exercise, start with a selfie that clearly shows your facial features.
Once you’ve uploaded your photo, consider two things.
1- First, decide what you want to preserve. If your glasses, beard, or hairstyle are part of your signature look, you can mention them.
2- Next, decide what you want to change. For example, you can ask ChatGPT to keep your outfit and place you on a coastal boulevard lined with palm trees and neon lights.
You can also specify the image format. If you’re planning to share it as an Instagram Story, request a vertical image. If you want a landscape-oriented cover image, specify that from the start.
The prompt to turn your selfie into a GTA 6-inspired portrait
Upload your photo to ChatGPT and paste the following prompt. You can customize the setting or colors depending on the look you’re going for:
“Use my photo as a reference. Preserve my facial features, skin tone, hairstyle, and expression. Transform it into an original illustration that looks like an open-world video game cover. Place me in the foreground on a coastal boulevard lined with palm trees, Art Deco buildings, and neon lights at sunset. Use shades of pink, orange, and purple, with bold shadows and a digital painting finish. Keep my face well-lit and the background slightly blurred. Generate the image in a vertical format, without text or logos.”
Can you ask ChatGPT to create a GTA 6-style photo?
Yes, you can mention GTA 6 in your prompt to give ChatGPT a visual reference. However, if you’re looking to transform your selfie, it’s better to describe the specific elements you like about that aesthetic: the palm trees, Art Deco buildings, pink sunset skies, or bold shadows.
This gives the tool clear, specific instructions for creating an original illustration featuring your face. Most importantly, keep in mind that copyright law protects Rockstar’s artwork, including the game’s official cover art, while the game’s name and logo are protected as trademarks.
If you like the first version of your ChatGPT-generated photo but notice that it has changed your smile or left your face in shadow, for example, you can request a specific adjustment within the same conversation.
In that case, try writing something like: “Keep the background and colors, but restore the facial expression from my original photo.” ChatGPT also allows you to select a specific area of an image to edit.
