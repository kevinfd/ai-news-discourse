# Weekly Startup Funding Roundup: Sierra Raises USD 1.75 Billion, Marlowe USD 277 Million

News
Weekly Startup Funding Roundup: Sierra Raises USD 1.75 Billion, Marlowe USD 277 Million
Five startups raised over USD 2.1 billion during the week of November 23, led by Sierra's USD 1.75 billion funding. Marlowe, Avicena, Agon, and AidenAI also secured investments across AI and defense technology.
Artificial intelligence continues to attract big-ticket investments, with startups across enterprise software, computing infrastructure, defense technology, and semiconductor innovation drawing fresh capital. During the week of November 23, 2026, five companies announced funding rounds, led by AI customer experience startup Sierra, which raised USD 1.75 billion and reached a USD 30 billion valuation.
Marlowe followed with USD 277 million, while Avicena secured more than USD 100 million. Defense AI startup Agon raised USD 70 million, and enterprise AI company AidenAI attracted USD 32 million. The investments reflect growing interest in businesses developing AI infrastructure, specialized hardware and enterprise-focused applications.
AI customer experience startup Sierra raised USD 1.75 billion on November 23, valuing it at USD 30 billion. The funding attracted investors including Greenoaks, Lightspeed Venture Partners, Benchmark, Sequoia, GV and Tiger Global.
Sierra plans to use the capital to strengthen its technology stack, expand its AI-powered customer service agents, and hire additional sales personnel. The company will also focus on accelerating enterprise customer acquisition and improving contract management as businesses increasingly adopt AI-driven customer experience solutions.

Marlowe raised USD 277 million on November 25, reaching a USD 1.15 billion valuation. Its investors include Accel, Eclipse, Pitango, Bessemer Venture Partners, Altimeter, Atreides, Journey, Valor, Emergence Capital and Village Global. Individual investors include Assaf Rappaport, Henry Kravis, Dara Khosrowshahi, Aaron Levie, Ryan Roslansky, Patrick Collison and John Collison.
The company is developing integrated AI computing infrastructure that combines processors, networking equipment, data center architecture and software. The investment will support chip and networking equipment procurement, infrastructure development, data center operations and hiring. Its objective is to make AI inference faster, cheaper and more energy-efficient.
Avicena secured more than USD 100 million on November 24, with participation from InterVest, Tiger Global, Cerberus Capital Management, SK hynix Ventures, Samsung Catalyst Fund, AMD Ventures, Prosperity7 Ventures, Diamond Edge Ventures, Hitachi Ventures and other investors.
The company will use the funding to accelerate the commercialization of its LightBundle microLED-based optical interconnect platform for AI infrastructure. The capital will also support product development, engineering capabilities and team expansion. Avicena is targeting the launch of its first commercial product in 2027.
Defense AI startup Agon raised USD 70 million on November 23 at a USD 450 million valuation. Its investors include Northzone, Lakestar, XYZ, Lux Capital, Global Founders Capital, Speedinvest, the UK Sovereign AI Fund, the National Security Strategic Investment Fund (NSSIF), David Helgason and Roelof Botha.
Agon plans to develop Europe's sovereign defense AI infrastructure and foundational AI models for autonomous defense systems. The investment will support research and engineering expansion, platform development and collaboration with government and industry customers.
Enterprise AI startup AidenAI secured USD 32 million on November 24, with A91 Partners leading the investment.
The company plans to expand its enterprise AI platform, strengthen engineering capabilities, and scale its global business. It also intends to build a team of more than 1,000 frontier AI engineers through its AI Builder Academy.
The week's funding activity shows investor interest extending beyond conventional AI software to the infrastructure needed to develop and deploy advanced AI systems. Sierra and AidenAI focus on enterprise applications, while Marlowe and Avicena address computing and connectivity challenges.
Agon's funding, meanwhile, reflects growing investment in sovereign AI capabilities and defense applications.
With these five companies collectively attracting more than USD 2.1 billion, the latest funding announcements underline the scale of capital flowing into AI-related businesses. Their next challenge will be to convert these investments into commercial products, stronger infrastructure, and sustainable business growth.
