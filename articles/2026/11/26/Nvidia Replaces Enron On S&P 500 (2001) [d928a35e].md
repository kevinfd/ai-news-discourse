Nvidia Replaces Enron On S&P 500
The demise of energy firm Enron is good news for one company game developers are familiar with: Nvidia.

Standard and Poors today announced that Nvidia will replace Enron Corporation in the prestigious Standard & Poors 500 Composite Index (S&P 500 Index) after the close of trading today. Battered energy trader Enron Corporation is on the brink of bankruptcy, as creditors assess their exposure to one of the world's biggest-ever corporate collapses.
