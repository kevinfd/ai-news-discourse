# HP launches OmniBook Ultra 16 AI laptop for creators

The Hindu · The Hindu Bureau · 2026-11-26

HP on Thursday (November 26, 2026) launched the OmniBook Ultra 16 AI laptop in India for developers, creators and professionals.
HP OmniBook Ultra 16 comes with Nvidia RTX Spark N1X platform, with Nvidia Blackwell RTX integrated graphics. It claims to deliver up to 1 petaflop of FP4 AI performance and supports up to 128GB of unified memory.
HP OmniBook Ultra 16 has a 3K OLED touch display with up to a 120Hz variable refresh rate, 100% DCI-P3 colour coverage.
HP OmniBook Ultra 16 ships with a 99Wh battery, a 140W GaN charger, supports Wi-Fi 7, a 5MP IR camera, two USB Type-C ports, two USB Type-A ports, one HDMI 2.1 port and a microSD card reader.
HP OmniBook Ultra 16 is available exclusively on HP Online Store at a starting price of ₹5,49,999 in Eclipse Gray colour.
