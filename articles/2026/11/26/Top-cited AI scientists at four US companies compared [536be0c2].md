# Top-cited AI scientists at four US companies compared

Top-Cited Scientists · kkk4seis · 2026-11-26

What it shows
The chart has four panels, for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta. Each dot is one of the company’s scientists in the list who works in the US and whose main subfield is Artificial Intelligence & Image Processing. The grey behind them is all 2,531 US scientists in the list in this subfield.
The typical scientist at Anthropic received 4,830 citations in 2025. At OpenAI it was 2,150, at Google 1,481 and at Meta 1,295. The typical US scientist in this subfield received 968, with 88 papers. At each of the four companies the typical scientist has 34 to 44 papers, at most half as many, and more citations.
Anthropic has 9 scientists in the list in this subfield in the US, OpenAI 18, Google 169 and Meta 122.
How it was made
The figure uses the 2026 Stanford–Elsevier top 2% list, which ranks scientists by their citations in 2025. A company includes every name the list gives it. Google includes Google DeepMind. Alphabet’s other companies, like Waymo and Verily, are left out. Only scientists in the United States are counted.
The horizontal axis is the number of papers each scientist has published in all years. The vertical axis is the citations their work received in 2025, not counting citations from their own papers. Both axes are logarithmic, so each grid line is ten times the one before. The typical scientist is the median.
