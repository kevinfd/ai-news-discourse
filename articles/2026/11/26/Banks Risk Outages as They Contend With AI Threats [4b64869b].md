# Banks Risk Outages as They Contend With AI Threats

Bloomberg · 2026-11-26

The risk of cyberattacks is forcing banks to contend with a growing number of technical hiccups as they race to adapt aging infrastructure and find vulnerabilities before they’re exploited. Artificial intelligence is advancing so fast that IT teams patch first and ask questions later, which can risk outages. Speaking to Bloomberg last week, Citigroup Inc. Chief Executive Officer Jane Fraser warned of a “tsunami” of patches. Bella Farr reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
