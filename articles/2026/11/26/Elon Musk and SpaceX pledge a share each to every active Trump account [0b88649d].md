# Elon Musk and SpaceX pledge a share each to every active Trump account

Business Insider · Natalie Musumeci,Theron Mohamed, Natalie Musumeci,Theron Mohamed (nmusumeci@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-26

Elon Musk and SpaceX are jumping into Trump Accounts. Kevin Dietsch/Getty Images SpaceX says it will give one share of its stock to every active Trump Account. Elon Musk says he'll personally match the number of shares donated as part of a broader commitment. SpaceX's COO has separately pledged more than 2 million of the rocket maker's shares. SpaceX said Wednesday that it will give one share of its stock to every active Trump Account . CEO Elon Musk will personally match the number of shares donated by the AI and rocket company, as "part of his pledge to donate one SpaceX share to every kid in America ," the company said. Gwynne Shotwell, SpaceX's president and chief operating officer, has also pledged more than 2 million SpaceX shares. "SpaceX is building the infrastructure of the future and working to make life multiplanetary," the company behind Starlink, Starship, Grok, and X said in a press release. "We believe ownership of the company creating these systems should extend to the next generation of Americans for their long-term benefit." Musk — the world's wealthiest person with an estimated $820 billion net worth, per the Bloomberg Billionaires Index — confirmed his commitment in a pinned post on X. "Every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from me personally," he wrote. Musk said the share from SpaceX "will be transferred next month," while the share from him "will be transferred when my lockup expires in June next year." The share from SpaceX will be transferred next month. 

The share directly from me will be transferred when my lockup expires in June next year. — Elon Musk (@elonmusk) November 26, 2026 
 A SpaceX share was worth about $127 as of Wednesday's market close, down 44% from its peak price of around $226 in mid-June, shortly after the company went public. Trump Accounts are tax-advantaged investment accounts for American children born between 2025 and 2028. They were launched in July in an effort to broaden participation in the stock market and help families build long-term financial security. The US Treasury has been seeding each account opened with a one-time, $1,000 contribution. The White House said in early October that 70 million Trump Accounts had been created. It's unclear how many are considered "active," but if Musk and SpaceX were to transfer 70 million shares each, those gifts would be worth $8.9 billion separately or nearly $18 billion combined, based on Wednesday's closing price. SpaceX joins a growing number of companies that have decided to invest in Trump Accounts.
