# Minecraft’s reclusive creator has a proposition for Anthropic

Business Insider · Natalie Musumeci, Natalie Musumeci (nmusumeci@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-26

Markus Persson created Minecraft. Yui Mok/PA Images via Getty Images Minecraft creator Markus Persson asked Anthropic for more AI access and speed. "I've got the money to burn, and I'd love to exchange it for better goods and services," he wrote on X. Persson is an AI convert who previously called AI coding an "incredibly bad idea." Minecraft creator Markus Persson has a request for Anthropic : He wants more AI power — and he's willing to pay. "@AnthropicAI Yo, any chance I could get more unlimited token usage and perhaps even more speed?" the billionaire video game developer, commonly known as "Notch," wrote in a Monday X post. "I've got the money to burn, and I'd love to exchange it for better goods and services. And I'm building new shit that I've never seen before," Persson said. Persson also expressed support for the AI giant's approach to the rapidly advancing technology, writing, "I agree with your AI manifesto, btw."  Persson's appeal to Anthropic, the newly public company behind the Claude AI chatbot, marks a turnabout for the Minecraft creator. Persson was once fiercely opposed to using AI for coding. At the beginning of the year, he called it an "incredibly bad idea still" and said, "anyone advocating for it is either incompetent or evil." By July, Persson said he was trying out coding with AI, and a month later he said it might be time to "eat some crow." "One of the reasons I don't want to embrace this AI thing too much is that I'd prefer to stay a developer and not become middle management," Persson wrote in an August post. "Feels related somehow." Anthropic did not respond to a request for comment about Persson's proposition. Read the original article on Business Insider
