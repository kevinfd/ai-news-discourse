# US Chip Curbs Stall Asia Data Center Financing

Bloomberg · 2026-11-26

US chip curbs are putting the brakes on billions of dollars in AI data center financing across Asia, with lenders reassessing deals tied to Chinese tech firms including ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent. Bloomberg’s Annabelle Droules says legal and compliance risks are weighing on banks as new rules on chip access take effect. (Source: Bloomberg)
