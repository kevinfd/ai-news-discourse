# Your Family Might Not Be Able to Use Copilot on Microsoft 365!? | AI Is for the Owner Only: 7 Things to Check

note · MONO · 2026-11-26

Your Family Might Not Be Able to Use Copilot on Microsoft 365!? | AI Is for the Owner Only: 7 Things to Check
Microsoft 365 Family can be used by up to six people, but only the person who signed up (the subscription owner) can use the Copilot benefits. Microsoft's official help page states, "AI benefits are only available to the subscription owner and cannot be shared with other users."
In other words, family members cannot use Copilot in apps like Word or Excel. They can only use the web version of Copilot Chat. If you chose Family thinking, "Now the whole family can use AI," it is a good idea to check who can use what to be certain.

What can and cannot be shared on Family
First, let's look at what "can be shared" on Family. On Microsoft's pricing page, Family is listed as being for "1 to 6 people." People invited as family members can use Office apps and cloud storage with their own accounts. This has not changed from before.
What changed is the AI portion. In the Family column on the same page, a parenthetical note adds, "AI is available only to the subscription owner." This is also true for the higher-tier Premium plan. Even on a plan that can be used by six people, the AI benefits are only for one person—the owner.
Specifically, it breaks down as follows:
- What only the owner can use: Copilot included in desktop apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and OneNote. Higher usage quotas for Copilot Chat. Usage quotas for AI features such as image generation.
- What shared family members can also use: The web version of Copilot Chat. Official help states that shared users can use Chat by signing in to microsoft365.com (or copilot.com) with their own Microsoft accounts.
- What anyone can use: The free version of Microsoft Copilot. You can use this without any subscription.
Even if family members open Word, they cannot have Copilot draft text for them the way the owner can. If you run into situations like "the Copilot button doesn't appear on my family member's computer" or "it appears, but it doesn't work," please remember this mechanism first.
