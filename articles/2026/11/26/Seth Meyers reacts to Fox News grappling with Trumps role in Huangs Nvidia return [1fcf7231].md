# Seth Meyers reacts to Fox News grappling with Trumps role in Huangs Nvidia return

Mashable · Shannon Connellan · 2026-11-26

Donald Trump's admitted role in getting Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reinstated has Democrats and some Republicans in Congress promising to investigate. ICYMI Huang resigned after reports of multiple affairs, the CEO admitting one with an alleged Chinese spy. After Trump intervened with Nvidia's board, the CEO was reinstated, and Nvidia's stock (which the president owns) then surged.
Taking "A Closer Look" in his Late Night deep dive segment, Seth Meyers examined Trump's role in Huang getting his job back, notably in the same week he pardoned two turkeys ahead of Thanksgiving Day at the White House.
"Trump got around to granting clemency to Freedom and Feathers, but it turns out those turkeys aren't the only ones he's thinking of pardoning," said Meyers. (Trump told the New York Post that pardoning Huang for any potential crimes was "always an option," a day after telling CNBC the CEO "did nothing wrong.")
But Meyers particularly responded to Fox News anchors struggling to defend Trump's intervention with Nvidia and Huang's subsequent rehiring, running through a series of clips in which hosts including Jesse Watters and Laura Ingraham, according to Meyers, "know how bad this looks."
"Even the most hardcore Trump supporters can't defend the obvious corruption and self-dealing we can all see with our own eyes in the Nvidia scandal," said Meyers. "Trump won't help Americans impacted by high prices or tech outages or bomb-making chemicals in Iowa. But if you're Jensen Huang or Elon Musk, you get special treatment. The president will bend over backwards to help them out."
