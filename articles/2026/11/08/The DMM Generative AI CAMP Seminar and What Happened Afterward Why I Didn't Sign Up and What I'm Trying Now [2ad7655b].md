# The DMM Generative AI CAMP Seminar and What Happened Afterward | Why I Didn't Sign Up and What I'm Trying Now

note · いーさん＠Aiオンチだった凡人-今はChatgptで時短中 · 2026-11-08

The DMM Generative AI CAMP Seminar and What Happened Afterward | Why I Didn't Sign Up and What I'm Trying Now
I previously wrote about my experience taking the free DMM Generative AI CAMP seminar. 
It has been about two months since then. If you ask me, "Did you end up signing up for the paid course?", I will answer honestly.
I haven't signed up for anything yet.
In this post, I will write about the reason for that and what I am doing instead.
*This article contains affiliate links.
The main reason I didn't sign up
The truth is, I would prefer to do it for free if possible.
The second half of the seminar focused on product introductions, and there was also information about the course. 
It is true that at the time, I thought, "It seems good to be able to learn in a structured way." 
However, I am someone who has originally been finding ways to make do with the free plan of ChatGPT. 
I was left with the feeling that "I haven't fully tested how much I can do for free before paying money."
Therefore, I decided that I would only move on to the paid version "once I hit a wall with the free version."
Instead, I am trying out what I learned in the seminar
What I wrote as the "biggest takeaway" in my previous article was the method of "having AI grade my output and improve it."
It's not just about having the AI create text and being done with it, but 
having it grade it by asking, "How do you think this would be received by the other party?", correcting it, and showing it again. 
I am trying this extra step when polishing work emails and the like.
After trying it, I think that this was a method I could use starting today just by taking away what I learned from the free seminar, without having to join a paid course.
In my case, the value of taking the seminar was primarily in this.
When would I move on to the paid version?
As of now, it is "not yet," but I don't intend to claim that free is always enough. 
I think I would move on if these situations occur.
・When I am trying things for free and absolutely cannot move forward anymore
・When I want to relearn in a structured way because self-study is disorganized
・When I judge for myself that I won't continue if I am alone
Right now, there are still things I can try within the free scope, so this is an interim report.
Conclusion: To those who are hesitant
It was not the case that "if you don't sign up immediately after taking the seminar, there is no point." 
I don't think it's too late to do what I did: take away what you can learn from the free seminar first, and then decide after trying it for free.
If you are interested, I think it is fine to just listen to the free seminar first without being too guarded.
