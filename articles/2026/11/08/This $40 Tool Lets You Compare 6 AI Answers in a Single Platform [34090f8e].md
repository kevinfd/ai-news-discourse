# This $40 Tool Lets You Compare 6 AI Answers in a Single Platform

PCMag · StackCommerce Team · 2026-11-08

TL;DR: AskAnyModel AI Pro lifetime access is on sale for $39.99 (MSRP $499) through Nov. 8, including six-model comparisons, 30 unlimited standard models, and 500 monthly premium credits.
AskAnyModel AI Pro brings multiple AI models into one browser-based workspace. Its lifetime subscription is just $39.99 (MSRP $499) through Nov. 8, a savings of $459.01.
The main feature is side-by-side comparison. Select two to six models, enter one prompt, and review their answers together. Response times and token counts provide additional context when deciding which output suits your task.
The catalog includes GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Llama, and Mistral options. You can switch models during a conversation without losing context, making it easier to try different approaches to writing, coding, research, or brainstorming.
Usage allowances differ by model. The plan includes unlimited chats with 30 standard models and 500 monthly credits shared across 19 premium models. A separate Gemini Pro model with Google Search grounding is limited to 300 chats per week. Lifetime access therefore does not mean unlimited use of every model.
Image generation across supported models is unlimited, though, and does not consume premium credits. The platform requires no downloads or installation, and its model catalog is regularly refreshed.
