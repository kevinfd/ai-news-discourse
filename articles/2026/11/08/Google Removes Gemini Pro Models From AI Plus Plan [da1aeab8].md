# Google Removes Gemini Pro Models From AI Plus Plan

Economy.pk · Rohaan · 2026-11-08

Google Removes Gemini Pro Models From AI Plus Plan
From November 13, subscribers to Google’s cheapest paid Gemini plan will keep Flash models, while Pro models move to AI Pro and above.
From November 13, Google AI Plus subscribers will lose Gemini Pro access and keep Flash models; Pro stays with AI Pro and above.
Google is removing access to Gemini Pro models from its AI Plus subscription plan, effective November 13, 2026. According to an email sent to subscribers, Pro models in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Google Search will be “only available to Google AI Pro subscribers and above.”
What AI Plus Subscribers Keep
AI Plus subscribers will keep access to the latest Flash-Lite and Flash models. Google’s subscription page now lists only the Flash model for AI Plus. Previous chats that used Pro models can be continued with a different model.
Plan Differences
- Usage limits: AI Plus offers 2x standard limits, while AI Pro offers 4x.
- Context window: AI Plus has 128,000 tokens, while AI Pro has 1 million.
Pricing
In the United States, AI Plus costs $4.99 per month and AI Pro $19.99. In Germany, AI Plus costs €4.99 and AI Pro €21.99.
Background
The email to subscribers was first shared by 9to5Google in mid-October. Google has not given a reason for the change, and no compensation for current subscribers has been announced.
