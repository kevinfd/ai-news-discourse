# How to Get an HP Copilot AI Laptop for Less Than Apple’s Cheapest iPad, With Microsoft Office 365 Sweetening the Deal

Kotaku · Brittany Vincent · 2026-11-08

HP’s 2027 Everyday Laptop is a budget-friendly option for doing homework, browsing, streaming, and video calls, and it comes with Microsoft 365 and 1TB of cloud storage. A quad-core Intel N150 processor can run up to 3.6GHz and handles almost everything you need to do every day. Windows 11 with Copilot AI features helps you complete tasks faster, and the laptop’s Honey Lavender color adds flair to your desktop or backpack.
A 14-inch Windows 11 laptop with an anti-glare screen and Copilot
The HP 2027 Everyday Laptop is ideal for students, families, and anyone looking for a simple, portable laptop for day-to-day use. This HP model is equipped with an Intel N150 processor, 4GB of LPDDR5 RAM, 128GB of UFS internal storage, 1TB of cloud storage, a 14-inch HD anti-glare display, an HP True Vision 720p HD camera, Wi-Fi 6, Windows 11, Copilot AI, and a Microsoft 365 subscription, all housed within a very thin, lightweight design.
This N150 chip has four processing cores and four threads, with Intel Turbo Boost reaching up to 3.6GHz and 6MB of cache. So the HP 2027 Everyday Laptop can easily handle common computing tasks, such as browsing the internet, checking email, creating documents, streaming content, and making video calls. The laptop’s 4GB of LPDDR5 memory also provides good responsiveness for most daily multitasking.
The 14-inch HD display features a micro-edge design, an anti-glare layer, and up to 250 nits of brightness. As a result, you get fewer distractions from glare, whether you’re working indoors or outdoors. Plus, with a high screen-to-body ratio of 79%, the viewing area looks larger than expected, even though the overall physical dimensions remain relatively compact. This makes it suitable for watching movies and TV shows and reading books, magazines, and websites.
The HP 2027 Everyday Laptop comes with 128GB of UFS internal storage, so you should see relatively quick start-up times and fast file access. The laptop also offers 1TB of cloud storage space. As a result, you can store many personal files, documents, photos, and projects in the cloud and access them from your cell phone or any other computer from anywhere. Microsoft 365 allows you to use programs like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
For video conferencing, HP uses temporal noise reduction in its HP True Vision 720p HD webcam for better-quality video chat sessions, and dual-array digital microphones improve audio reception for both parties during conversations. Wi-Fi 6 provides a strong, fast, and stable internet connection, and rapid charging lets you recharge quickly between class changes. The full-size keyboard sits atop a natural silver deck that matches the laptop’s Honey Lavender body.
The HP 2027 Everyday Laptop is a reliable budget-priced notebook that comes with Microsoft 365 and has attractive styling and portability. It’s an excellent purchase if you’re a student needing a laptop for school assignments, a home office worker wanting a reliable, low-cost notebook, or someone who needs nothing more than a functional laptop.
