Best budget QLED TV
Why we like it
The Toshiba 50-inch M450 is a great fit if you want richer QLED color on a budget for a bedroom, apartment, or secondary living space. Powered by Toshiba's REGZA AI Engine, it pairs a quantum dot QLED panel with Dolby Vision and HDR10+ Adaptive support, giving everyday shows and movies punchier color and better contrast than standard entry-level LED screens.
You also get dual clear-direct speakers with Dolby Atmos support, built-in Fire TV with an Alexa voice remote for quick hands-free searches, and Apple AirPlay compatibility. Best art TV
Why we like it
If you prefer your living room decor to look like an actual gallery, the Toshiba 65-inch G700 ArtVision doubles as framed wall art without giving up fast gaming or movie specs.
At just 1.2 inches thick with a zero-gap wall-fit design and an included art frame, it mounts nearly flush against the wall and uses Ambient Mode to cycle through more than 3,000 free artworks on a matte, paper-like anti-glare screen. When you sit down to watch or play, its QLED display delivers vibrant color alongside a native 165Hz refresh rate for ultra-smooth console gaming and live sports, plus Dolby Vision IQ, DTS Virtual:X audio, and built-in Fire TV with Alexa.
Best big-screen Mini-LED TV
If you want a massive screen that still looks great during sunny afternoon games and dark movie nights, the Toshiba 85-inch Z670R brings solid living room upgrades without complicated menus. Its Mini-LED backlight uses local dimming to keep dark scenes deep and bright highlights punchy, while a built-in light sensor automatically adjusts the picture to match your room's lighting.
Gamers and sports fans get a fast 144Hz refresh rate with AMD FreeSync Premium for smooth, blur-free motion, and a built-in bass woofer gives movies and live broadcasts a fuller sound right out of the box — tied together with Fire TV and an Alexa voice remote.
