# Trump says 'it's probably not too late' for the US to take a stake in Nvidia

Business Insider · Betelbuddy · 2026-11-27

President Donald Trump may want a piece of the Nvidia pie.
After saying he intervened to support the reinstatement of CEO Jensen Huang, Trump said he has also considered seeking out an equity stake in the chipmaker for the US government.
"I thought about it," Trump told the New York Post on Tuesday. "I said, 'You know, maybe I should do it,' but I didn't bring it up at this point. It's probably not too late."
Huang was reinstated as CEO of Nvidia on Monday, less than two weeks after resigning from the company he founded when reports emerged of extramarital affairs with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy and an executive who reported to him.
Huang's return came days after Nvidia's board said they had no plans to bring him back. Trump, who has repeatedly praised Huang, played up his role in the CEO's return and said he spoke directly to the board.
The president's suggestion of the government taking an equity stake in Nvidia would align with similar moves made under the current administration. In August 2025, the US government reached a deal to invest $8.9 billion in Intel in exchange for a nearly 10% stake, a move aimed at supporting the semiconductor industry.
Prior to Intel, the administration said it took a "golden share" in US Steel in exchange for approving its acquisition by Nippon Steel, a Japanese company. The government has also invested in critical minerals firms MP Materials, Lithium Americas, and Trilogy Metals, as well as established a strategic partnership with the nuclear energy company Westinghouse.
Nvidia did not respond to a request for comment.
