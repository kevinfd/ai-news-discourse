And it's not just designed for small or dainty yards. This Mammotion device can tackle uneven ground, thanks to its all-wheel drive and the ability to climb slopes of up to 80% (38 degrees). Its UltraSense AI Vision and RTK navigation system also help it find its way around your yard on its own, even in shaded areas where satellite signals may be blocked.
Mammotion says it can cover up to 13,000 square feet on a single charge, so you don't need to worry about constantly recharging it to finish your yard.
Other features include up to 50 mowing areas, no-go zones around pools or flower beds, and different mowing patterns such as parallel lines or a checkerboard, all controlled through the Mammotion app.
Amazon's Black Friday sale is live until Nov. 30, so this deal could be around for only a few days.
