# Black Friday TV deals: Get an OLED TV for $799 and save 50% on TCL, LG, and Hisense

Black Friday TV deals: Get an OLED TV for $799 and save 50% on TCL, LG, and Hisense
Table of Contents
Savvy TV shoppers wait all year to buy TVs during Black Friday, and the best Black Friday TV deals are now live.
Amazon and Best Buy launched their official Black Friday events on Nov. 19, which means the best TV deals of the year are now online. The days of post-Thanksgiving doorbusters are dwindling. Sure, these retailers may hold back a few surprise deals, and prices may still fluctuate a little, but we don't expect any more big price drops on the TVs in this shopping guide.
This is also a great Christmas gift idea the whole family will appreciate, and we've found huge 50% discounts on some of the best TVs of 2026, from budget sets to flagship OLED and art TVs to Micro RGB models.
Our tech experts will be closely monitoring TV prices throughout Black Friday to find the best deals from top brands such as TCL, Samsung, Hisense, Amazon Fire TV, LG, and more. We'll also be comparing prices at the most popular retailers to make sure you truly are getting the best possible price.
Need some guidance? Check out Mashable's guide to TV specs for a quick refresh on the difference between QLED, OLED, Mini LED, and RGB displays.
Roku 65-inch OLED Pro Series 4K Smart RokuTV
This fall, Roku's new OLED TVs have been selling out at Amazon, and that was before they got a $400 price drop. This TV isn't as bright or sleek as Samsung or LG's best OLED TVs, but it's right in the sweet spot for affordability, letting you enjoy deep blacks and dazzling colors without spending over $1,000.
Hisense 65-inch E7 Mini-LED Hi-QLED 4K TV
Even as every other tech product gets more expensive, new TVs are seemingly immune to the price hikes we've seen in recent years. Case in point: You can get the 65-inch Hisense E7 QLED TV for $499.99 at Amazon, a full 50% discount from its retail price. This is the lowest price ever for this TV, according to our research. Get fast access to your favorite streaming apps via the Fire TV OS, and take advantage of the 144Hz refresh rate for watching movies and playing video games.
The bottom line: While this discount is available, this smart TV offers high-end features at a budget price tag.
Amazon 4-Series Fire TVs
If you're already plugged into the Amazon product ecosystem with Alexa, then Amazon's 4-Series Fire TVs are an affordable way to upgrade your living room. These budget TV sets essentially double as Alexa+ smart speakers, and despite the low price, you can still enjoy 4K content on an HDR10+ display. Both the 43-inch and 55-inch models are 39% off now that Amazon's Black Friday TV deals are live.
LG 65-inch C6 Series OLED 4K Smart TV
LG's C-Series is one of the most award-winning TV series ever made. Since the C1 debuted in 2021, these popular LG TVs have set the standard for OLED displays, picking up countless TV of the year awards. The best Black Friday TV deals always include discounts on the latest model, and the new C6 offers incredible peak brightness, dark blacks, and expansive colors, now with an AI processor for smoother motion and upscaling. Pull out a microscope, and you'll find 8.3 million self-lit pixels.
Our favorite OLED TV for most people just keeps getting cheaper. We saw the 65-inch LG C6 OLED TV drop to just $1,499.99 during Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days event, but now it's $100 less and at a record-low price at both Amazon and Best Buy.
Samsung 65-inch S90H OLED 4K Smart TV
If you prefer Samsung to LG, then you can't go wrong with Samsung's answer to the LG C6. The premium S90H from Samsung has also gotten a Black Friday price drop, and this 2026 model has built-in technology to reduce glare from nearby windows. And, yes, it's also at a record-low price, according to our research.
Amazon Ember 55-inch Mini-LED Fire TV
Looking for the best Black Friday TV deal on a 55-inch TV? We have to give it up to another Amazon Ember TV. This time, it's the Amazon Ember 55-inch Mini-LED Fire TV. Mini-LED is a type of QLED display, and this mid-range model has over 500 local dimming zones for strong contrast and darker blacks. It also achieves up to 1,400 nits, on par with the LG C6.
With a 45% discount, this deal is Amazon's version of old-fashioned Black Friday TV deals.
TCL 75-inch QM8L SQD-Mini-LED QLED 4K TV
TCL has made remarkable strides in making TVs brighter, and the QM8L offers a remarkable 6,000 nits of peak brightness and more than 4,000 dimming zones. The result is a TV with a picture that jumps off the screen and colors that pop even in a bright living room. For those who want a 75-inch TV that can be the centerpiece of a home theater, this QLED TV has remarkable blacks and bright colors, with brightness that puts OLED competitors to shame.

TCL 40-inch Q3CR QLED TV on white background
Best Buy has this 40-inch QLED TV from TCL for $150, which is an absolute steal, especially if you're looking to outfit one or more of your rooms with another display. TCL’s Q3CR Series pairs a quantum-dot panel with a direct-lit backlight, so colors come through saturated, with Full HD resolution that's plenty sharp at 40 inches. Plus, its built-in Roku streaming functionality means you can toss the streaming stick and use just the TV to watch all your favorite shows and movies, hassle-free.
Sony 65-inch Bravia 7 II True RGB 4K HDR Google Gemini TV
If you stay up to date on the latest TV specs, then you know that Micro RGB (or is it Mini RGB, RGB Mini-LED, or Micro RGB evo?) is the latest trend in high-end displays. Whatever you call them, RGB TVs use red, green, and blue miniature LED backlights (as opposed to just white and blue) for more accurate color and contrast. And Sony’s True RGB technology, introduced in May, sets a very high standard.
If you want a future-proofed TV that combines the benefits of OLED and QLED, then grab Sony's new Bravia 7 II True RGB flagship TV while it's more than $1,000 off at Best Buy. Besides the gorgeous display, this TV has a sleek design with thin bezels, and for AI power users, it brings Google Gemini right into your living room.

Samsung 32-inch Movingstyle Essential 4K Smart Monitor
Can't decide between a new monitor or new TV this Black Friday? No problem: Samsung's Movingstyle line launched last November (with a showing earlier this year at CES) and it covers both use cases. The 32-inch monitor can be wheeled from room to room, and can be used without a computer (like a regular TV). Though the stand is heavy when setting up, the wheeled setup and 13 feet of power cord make it easy enough to move from space to space. Reviewers on Amazon cited uses like watching TV outside, in the garage while doing housework, or in a college apartment.
According to PCMag (also owned by Ziff Davis), the 4K picture is perfectly clear at multiple angles, though the color spectrum is slightly lacking and the 60Hz refresh rate probably counts this out for gamers. It's a specific kind of product, but if you're the intended audience, a $215 discount is pretty sweet.
Insignia 40-inch F40 Series LED HD Smart Fire TV
Not every TV needs to be a stunner. If you're looking for a bedroom or kitchen TV, you can pick up this 1080p LED TV for under $100 at Amazon right now. It offers Amazon's Fire TV OS, so you don't need a streaming stick, and it even comes with an Alexa+ voice remote.
onn 32-inch LCD HD Smart TV

Vizio 50-inch Mini LED 4K QLED Smart TV
One of the best balances between budget and quality you'll find this Black Friday is in the Vizio 50-inch VQM50C TV. Mashable's sister site CNET (also owned by Ziff Davis) named it the best budget TV of 2026. (PCMag also named it a budget favorite). Why? According to CNET, this TV offers the best contrast and color, with the "inkiest blacks," resulting in an experience that is "head and shoulders above its competition." The stand is complicated to attach, but if you can get over that, you get a gorgeous 50-inch picture for less than $200.
Hisense 43-inch S5 Deco QLED 4K TV
Buying a TV for the bedroom is a delicate art. You want it big enough to be enjoyable to actually use, but not so big that it dominates the entire room. An art TV seems like the fix to keep design in mind (i.e., not having an ugly black box on the wall), but that can be a bigger investment for what's most folks' secondary TV. That's why former Mashable Senior Shopping Reporter Leah Stodart loved the Hisense H5 Deco TV and used it in her own bedroom. She wrote the TV "matches my room's color scheme and level of whimsy," both of which are crucial for maintaining a great vibe in your space.
That doesn't mean you sacrifice on quality. At $299.99, you'll be hard-pressed to find another TV that meshes this picture quality with an aesthetically pleasing design. For now, you can grab it on sale both at Amazon and Best Buy.
Hisense 50-inch 4K QLED S7 CanvasTV
Speaking of art TVs, Hisense also makes the most popular dupe for Samsung's famous Frame TV. Though the 50-inch models of Hisense and Samsung's TVs are both down to $647.99, Hisense has the advantage in a couple areas. For one, getting anything other than the standard black frame on Samsung's model will run you at least an additional $200. So if the teak look is what you're going for, Hisense is an easy get.
Secondly, Hisense may have a smaller art library than Samsung's expansive one, but it also requires no additional subscription to use. The same, unfortunately, can not be said for Samsung. In other words, if you prefer to buy your product up front and have it perform to its fullest ability without an additional monthly payment, you'd likely prefer the Hisense TV.
The ultimate splurge: Samsung 65-inch S95H OLED 4K Smart TV
We have one more Black Friday TV deal we need to highlight, if only to tease you. Our tech editor says this is the TV he would buy if money was no object, and it's one of the best TVs ever made. Unfortunately, even with an $1,100 price drop at Best Buy, this flagship OLED TV still requires a hefty investment.
What makes this TV so special? It takes everything we loved about Samsung's famous The Frame Art TVs and brings them into a flagship OLED TV. The S95H borrows the matte, anti-glare display that made The Frame so legendary, as well as the ultra-thin design that almost disappears onto your living room wall. Crucially, it also has Art Mode, letting it double as an art TV when not in use.
We're not done. This TV can also support up to 8 HDMI devices at once, wirelessly. And with a peak refresh rate of up to 165Hz, video games and movies have never looked better. It's truly a drool-worthy TV.
