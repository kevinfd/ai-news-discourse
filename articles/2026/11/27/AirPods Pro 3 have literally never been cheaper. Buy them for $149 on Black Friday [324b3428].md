# AirPods Pro 3 have literally never been cheaper. Buy them for $149 on Black Friday.

Mashable · Tabitha Britt · 2026-11-27

AirPods Pro 3 have literally never been cheaper. Buy them for $149 on Black Friday.
TL;DR: As of Nov. 27, you can get the Apple AirPods Pro 3 for $149, down from $249, at Amazon. With a 40% discount, this is the lowest sale price we've ever seen for these popular earbuds
Black Friday is here, and Amazon isn't playing around. We're seeing major price drops across tons of categories, particularly on Apple products. The MacBook Air is finally back under $1,000, Apple Watch discounts have finally landed, and the AirPods Max 2 headphones are down to just $419. However, the best Black Friday Apple deal so far has to be on the AirPods Pro 3.
Right now, you can get the Apple AirPods Pro 3 for $149, down from $249. That's a 40% discount, or $100 in savings, and the lowest price we've ever seen on these premium buds. In the past, we've seen Amazon discount the AirPods Pro 3 to $179 or even $169, but the new $149 price puts them on par with the regular AirPods 5. That's right, the AirPods Pro 3 are now the same price as the base-level AirPods 5, at least, at Amazon. (As of this writing, Walmart has price matched the $149 price at Amazon.)
