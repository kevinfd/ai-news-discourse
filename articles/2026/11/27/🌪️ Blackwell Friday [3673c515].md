# 🌪️ Blackwell Friday

Exponential View · Azeem Azhar · 2026-11-27

Washington has made it harder for Chinese firms to remotely access Nvidia’s most powerful chips, including thousands of B300s. Some operators in Asia are looking for American customers to take computing power they had set aside for Chinese companies.
SpaceX reportedly cut its asking price for small Blackwell clusters to $10-$12 per chip-hour, down from about $15-$15.50. That gets smaller customers closer to the rate it had offered Google and Anthropic for large clusters.
In Shenzhen, traders are asking more — at least $3 million for a server with eight B300s. Kyle Chan estimates that the price for an equivalent server in the US would be $500,000 to $600,000.
