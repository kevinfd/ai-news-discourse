# Natural England objected to a datacentre. Weeks later ministers threatened to abolish the agency

The Guardian · Sandra Laville Environment correspondent · 2026-11-27

Body urged Bristol city council to reject plan because of risk to nearby protected wildlife and habitats Natural England objected to the building of a large AI datacentre near Bristol weeks before the government threatened to abolish the independent environmental adviser. The nature adviser made a significant intervention to urge Bristol city council not to grant planning permission for a 40MW datacentre at Avonmouth, because of the threat to protected wildlife and habitats at one of the UK’s most important sites for migratory birds, which is within 600 metres of the site.
