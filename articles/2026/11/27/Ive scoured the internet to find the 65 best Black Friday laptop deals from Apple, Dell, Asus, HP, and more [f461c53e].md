# Ive scoured the internet to find the 65 best Black Friday laptop deals from Apple, Dell, Asus, HP, and more

Mashable · Haley Henschel · 2026-11-27

I've scoured the internet to find the 65 best Black Friday laptop deals from Apple, Dell, Asus, HP, and more
Table of Contents
Black Friday has always been the best time to buy a laptop, and that's still holding true in 2026 despite RAMageddon. While the holiday season's top discounts on MacBooks and Windows PCs may not look as great as usual due to industry-wide price hikes, they're still shaping up to be retailers' most competitive offers of the year.
In fact, some of the latest Black Friday laptop deals are even better than I expected. (I monitor laptop prices year-round for Mashable.) MacBook Air deals maxed out at $250 last year, but right now, some configurations are up to $300 off. And the MacBook Neo is finally seeing its first proper price cut for non-students.
On the PC side, I'm impressed by the number of budget Windows laptops that are currently on sale for $700 or less, and by the number of gaming laptops that are close to $1,000 off. For those with deeper pockets, note that some of this fall's new Nvidia RTX Spark laptops are on sale for the first time ever.
Read on for my running master list of the best early Black Friday laptop deals below. I'll continue to update it with fresh picks as holiday sales continue, so check back if you don't see any models with the specs you need.
Best MacBook deals
The Mashable Choice Award-winning M5 MacBook Air boasts "a premium design, great speakers, a bright and sharp display," and incredibly impressive performance, said Mashable Tech Editor Timothy Beck Werth. He called it "a damn near-perfect machine" — so suffice it to say, it's our top MacBook for most people.
