# Creating Google Forms by Consulting Gemini: Starting from "Here's What I Want to Do"

note · みっちゃん｜Ai活用でNote記事を執筆 · 2026-11-27

Creating Google Forms by Consulting Gemini: Starting from "Here's What I Want to Do"
I always thought Google Forms looked really handy, but 
having to think up every single question on my own, 
as well as configuring answer formats and options—
just thinking about doing all that 
made me feel like it was completely beyond me.
Then I found out that AI is now built into Google Forms.
If you tell the AI the purpose of your form and the questions, it builds it for you, 
and it even comes with prompt templates and the like.
"Maybe even I can make one with this!"
That's what I thought, but 
when I actually sat down to create one, 
I froze up all over again.
To begin with, I couldn't even think of what questions to ask.
Even if AI can generate the form for you, having to figure out the prompt instructions yourself 
is still a huge hassle after all.
So, I decided to consult Gemini right from the stage of coming up with the questions.
Overall Step-by-Step Process
Consulting Gemini Starting from Use Cases for Google Forms
This time, 
because I wanted to see how far you can go using AI to create a Google Form 
and put it to the test, 
I had Gemini propose potential use cases first.
After getting several suggestions, I also asked about ways to use them in daily life and personal projects.
Since this was an experiment,
if I chose a common business event as the theme, 
anyone could easily guess most of the questions just from the event name.
If I was going to test it, 
I wanted to try something where the questions would vary depending on the specifics of the plan.
So, for this test, I decided to go with a BBQ meetup.
Consulting on the Plan Rather than Brainstorming Questions
First, I had Gemini create a sample plan for the BBQ meetup.
Based on that proposal, I consult further.
In response, it suggested questions that do more than just check attendance—they actually help with event preparations.
- Participation time slots, such as full participation, late arrival, or early departure
- Drink preferences to help determine the shopping list and budget
- Equipment attendees can bring, such as chairs or cooler boxes
- Willingness to assist with preparations on the day of the event
I see. Asking about these things in advance certainly makes preparations much easier.
I didn't come up with the questions entirely from scratch myself.
By consulting Gemini about the plan, 
what to ask 
and how to use the collected responses all became clear.
Summarizing the Consulted Content into a Google Doc
The AI within Google Forms has a feature that lets you attach materials from Google Drive.
That means 
if I summarize the discussion with Gemini in a Google Doc, 
couldn't it generate the form just by having that document handed to it?
So, I had Gemini compile the discussed details 
into a single Google Doc.
The document consists of the following three parts (I simply asked Gemini to do it):
- Part 1: Event Plan and Overview
- Part 2: Pre-Event Google Form Design Proposal
- Part 3: Operational Workflow
Part 2 contains 
• Form purpose 
• Question text 
• Response format 
• Options, etc.,
written out in concrete detail.
Once you have prepared this much, 
there is no need to explain each question one by one 
to the AI inside Google Forms.
Just attach the document and say, "Make it"
I opened Google Forms and, clicking the "+" button in the AI input field, 
attached the Google Doc from earlier.
And here is the prompt I entered:
That's really all it took.
The edit screen generated after creation contained the form title and description, along with 11 questions and their options.
Questions decided through consultation with Gemini—such as participation time slots, drink plans, and gear attendees could bring—were all reflected.
A point to keep in mind is that 
certain settings, such as required fields and branching logic based on answers, 
could not be configured by the AI, 
so you have to set them manually. 
Final checks and adjustments are also necessary.
Even so, I was able to build this much of a Google Form—a tool I had previously avoided because even coming up with questions felt like a chore.
This is so convenient!
Not sure what to instruct the AI? You can consult the AI about that too.
What was interesting about trying this out wasn't just that you can build Google Forms with AI.
Consulting on the plan with Gemini, 
having it brainstorm the questions, 
compiling that content into a Google Doc, 
and feeding it to the form's built-in AI to generate the form.
By combining each of these features, 
I was able to reduce the burden of thinking everything up on my own 
and writing out all the instructions.
Above all, the entire process from initial consultation to completed form took only about 20 minutes.
Even when you want to use AI, there are times when you just don't know what prompts to give it, right?
However, you can even consult the AI on formulating those instructions.
"Here's what I want to accomplish—how should I go about it?"
By first bouncing ideas off the AI like that, 
I believe your own inspiration will start to flow.
Even if you haven't used Google Forms before because it seemed too difficult, 
working alongside AI might just open up a whole new range of possibilities.
