# Fox News AI Newsletter: Locals 'never seen nothing like' hulking Meta data center

Fox News · Fox News Staff · 2026-11-27

Welcome to Fox News’ Artificial Intelligence newsletter with the latest AI technology advancements.
IN TODAY’S NEWSLETTER:
- Louisiana locals fear ‘losing ground’ as Meta’s data center moves in
- Trump doubles down on support for Nvidia CEO amid national security concerns
- ‘Data centers in space’: FCC clears SpaceX for 1M satellites to ‘extend America’s lead in superintelligence’
‘LOSING GROUND’: A data center is transforming rural Louisiana, bringing thousands of workers and new opportunities while raising concerns over farmland, traffic, and the future of small-town life. Locals and Meta site workers discuss living through the change.
SECOND TERM: President Donald Trump doubled down Thursday on his support for reinstated Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, defending the tech executive amid national security concerns and predicting he would be even more successful during his second stint leading the chip-making giant.
FINAL FRONTIER: The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) has authorized Elon Musk’s SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million satellites to build artificial intelligence data centers to orbit the Earth.
SMART MONEY: Corporate leaders' discussions about artificial intelligence are shifting, with the leader of a top professional services firm telling FOX Business that AI deployment across the workforce and the cyber risks from the technology are top of mind.
OPEN SECRETS: An independent researcher has raised concerns about how Gemini handles information retrieved from connected Gmail and Google Drive accounts. The findings prompted questions about whether some private content could end up in conversations reviewed by humans.
PIXEL PERFECT LIE: Housefishing happens when property photos create an unrealistic impression of a home. AI can make an outdated property appear freshly renovated without anyone doing the actual work.
CHILD SAFETY ALARM: Apple has yanked an AI chatbot app with roughly 11 million monthly users from its App Store worldwide after reports alleged the platform’s virtual characters sent sexually inappropriate messages to children.
CYBER EQUALIZER: While the recent string of headline-grabbing service outages across critical U.S. sectors may not be the result of a coordinated cyberattack, governments and companies across the globe still face a reality in which individuals have the same power to disrupt that was once only held by large nation-states, cybersecurity expert John Walsh explained.
GROUNDED: Wouldn't it be nice to tell an AI assistant to find the cheapest flight to your destination and book your favorite seat while you do something else? That kind of convenience is getting closer. But United Airlines wants to make sure an unauthorized AI assistant never gets that far.
COMMAND AND CONTROL: Ever download an app on your Mac and click through the permission requests without giving them much thought? You're certainly not alone. But what if one of those approvals gave an AI assistant access to your private messages, financial documents or even files you thought were protected?
POWER VACUUM: America’s AI industry is about to find out how much of its financing depended on Jensen Huang’s word.
DATA DRIVEN OUT: Affordability and data centers were on the ballot this November. Voters made it clear that they are concerned about both issues: prices are too high and the neighborliness of data centers is in doubt. In a time of economic turbulence, cities and counties are struggling to pay for schools, roads and public safety without the tax revenue data centers generate. Where can part of the solution to affordability and concerns with data centers be found? Vacant lots.
