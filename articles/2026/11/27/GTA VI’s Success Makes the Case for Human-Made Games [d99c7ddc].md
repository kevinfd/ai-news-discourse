# GTA VI’s Success Makes the Case for Human-Made Games

Bloomberg · 2026-11-27

Grand Theft Auto VI arrived after 13 years in development and it was made without using any generative AI according to Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick. It smashed all kinds of records. Gordon Bellamy, Director of Ethics at the USC School of Cinematic Arts, discusses what Rockstar’s blockbuster says about the value of human-made content in the AI era, why its extraordinary launch could reshape holiday spending across the gaming industry, and what smaller studios can learn from GTA VI’s success. (Source: Bloomberg)
