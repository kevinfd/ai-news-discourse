# 'A golden age for builders': Here's what business leaders are thankful for this year

Business Insider · Sarah E. Needleman,Amanda Goh, Sarah E. Needleman,Amanda Goh (sneedleman@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-27

Sam Altman, Fei-Fei Li, and Bill Ackman posted about what they were thankful for this Thanksgiving. Getty Images CEOs, VCs, and other business leaders are feeling reflective. Sam Altman said being a dad never gets old. Bill Ackman thanked the healthcare workers who have helped his daughter. Fei-Fei Li and Brian Armstrong took different approaches to expressing their gratitude. It's not just your neighbor or family friend firing off a Thanksgiving post. Business leaders can't seem to resist either. Sam Altman took the opportunity to reflect on fatherhood. "I am thankful for a lot of things in life, but my family is the best thing that has ever happened to me," the OpenAI CEO wrote on X. "Most good things get a little less satisfying the 100th time you do them," Altman added. "A quiet evening hanging out with your kids, even doing very mundane stuff, is in the rare category of something repetitive just getting better and better." Altman is among a number of tech leaders, investors, and AI researchers who took to the internet to share what they're thankful for this year. Healthcare workers got a shoutout from billionaire investor Bill Ackman, who expressed appreciation for all the doctors, nurses, and others who helped his daughter with her recovery after she suffered a massive brain hemorrhage in February. She spent weeks in a coma and six months in the hospital. "Healthcare is a field, perhaps more so than any other, where people make a massive personal sacrifice of their lives, their time, their sleep, and their energy to help others," Ackman wrote on X. Investor and Bridgewater founder Ray Dalio, who has warned of a looming US debt crisis , emphasized the importance of connection amid turmoil in a post on LinkedIn. "I hope that we can keep in mind that despite all the challenges and conflicts in the world today, the most important thing is how we choose to be with each other," wrote Dalio. In a post on X, "Godmother of AI" Fei-Fei Li thanked the people in her life, including her students, colleagues, mentors, friends, and family, after noting that people often talk more about machines than each other. Li recently sold a startup she cofounded to AMD in an all-stock deal valued at roughly $8.2 billion. Meanwhile, AMD CEO Lisa Su shared a photo of herself with her Thanksgiving turkey on X, saying she was "very grateful" for the company's team, partners, and friends around the world.  Elon Musk , the world's richest man and CEO of Tesla, also marked Thanksgiving with a message of gratitude on X. "Super appreciate your support. Tesla wouldn't be where it is without our awesome customers," Musk wrote. Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong leaned into the machine talk. "Today I'm thankful for technology, and living in a golden age for builders," he wrote, also on X. Former Intel CEO Pat Gelsinger had technology, people, and religion on his mind. He said he was thankful for the teams he works with, the opportunity to build AI that serves humanity, his family, and his faith. Similarly, Apple executive chairman Tim Cook said he was grateful for family, friends, and the tech giant's employees and customers. He wasn't the only representative of the iPhone maker.
