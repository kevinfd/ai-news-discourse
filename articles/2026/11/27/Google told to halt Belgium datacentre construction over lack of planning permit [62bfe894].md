# Google told to halt Belgium datacentre construction over lack of planning permit

The Guardian · Jon Henley · 2026-11-27

Greenpeace publishes report showing tech firm is continuing to build despite planning appeal still unresolved Belgian authorities have ordered Google to halt construction work on a large datacentre in the south of the country because the US tech firm’s requests to modify the original terms of the planning permission did not have approval. “Inspectors visited the site and observed that the building was almost finished, with the structural work completed, even though the permit had been refused,” Raphaël Stokis, a Wallonia regional official, told the public broadcaster RTBF .
