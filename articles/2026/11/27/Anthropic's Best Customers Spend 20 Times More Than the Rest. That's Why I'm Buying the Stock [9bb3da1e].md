# Anthropic's Best Customers Spend 20 Times More Than the Rest. That's Why I'm Buying the Stock.

The Motley Fool · John Ballard · 2026-11-27

Anthropic's Best Customers Spend 20 Times More Than the Rest. That's Why I'm Buying the Stock.
A quiet line in Anthropic's IPO filing reveals which customers spend the most, and why that matters for the stock.
By John Ballard –
Key Points
- Anthropic's biggest growth opportunity may rest on adoption of its agentic AI models like Claude Code.
- Claude Code turns a chat assistant into an agent that does the work, and its revenue grows bigger as customers give it more work.
- The valuation looks expensive, but Anthropic's annualized revenue has soared 16x since August 2025.
Anthropic (ANT +10.98%), the company behind the Claude family of artificial intelligence (AI) models, is officially a public company. Its shares, priced at $83 on Nov. 18, began trading the next day and closed trading on Friday, Nov. 20, at $88.91.

Many investors view Anthropic as a chatbot company charging everyone a similar monthly fee. However, its IPO filing tells a different story. Some business customers spend roughly 20 times as much as others, and the gap comes down to how they use the product. That difference may be the best clue to where Anthropic's next growth will come from.
Top agentic users are the real growth engine
Claude Enterprise is the plan Anthropic sells to businesses. It includes Claude.ai, the company's chat assistant, where you ask a question and get an answer.
The bigger opportunity for Anthropic is to grow usage of its agentic AI models, such as Claude Code. This is Anthropic's coding agent, an AI that takes on a multistep task and carries it out. Anthropic calls it the product "we believe has most concretely defined what autonomous work at the frontier looks like."
In the three months ended Sept. 30, 2026, Claude Enterprise users who didn't use Claude Code spent less than $75 per month on average. The top 5% of Enterprise users who used Code spent about 20 times that amount.
Image source: Getty Images.
That matters because most of Anthropic's revenue is based on usage, rather than subscription fees. When a customer switches from chatting to doing agentic work with Code, the bill grows without Anthropic having to win a new customer. The company says clients often start with one use case, then spread Claude across more departments. That's a growth engine that can compound for years.
Risks: Losses and competing models
Anthropic has no public track record yet, and it's still losing money. It also competes with Alphabet's Google and OpenAI, and its filing says many rivals have greater resources. If one of them offered an equally good coding agent at a lower price, customers could shift work elsewhere, slowing the spending growth that supports the stock's price.
Still, Anthropic's operating loss shrank in the first nine months of 2026 compared to a year earlier. Customers who hand Claude real work keep finding more for it to do. The growth from agentic models could be explosive.
Why I'm buying before 2027
The stock is more reasonably priced than its roughly $1.2 trillion market cap suggests. At Nov. 20’s price, it trades for about 14 times its run rate revenue, the company's measure of what its current sales would add up to over a full year. That looks expensive, but it's not considering how fast it is growing. Anthropic's annualized revenue jumped from $5 billion in August 2025 to $81 billion by Oct. 31, 2026.
That is extraordinary. Enterprise spending could multiply quickly as Claude's agentic AI models take on more work, allowing revenue to grow faster than the customer count.
I believe that makes Anthropic worth a starter position today, with more added on dips. As long as usage keeps climbing and its operating loss narrows toward profitability, I'm interested in holding shares.
