# Mark Zuckerberg's Thanksgiving menu: 'GTA 6,' smoked meat, and Meta's AI gadget

Business Insider · Georgia Hennessy, Georgia Hennessy (ghennessy@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-27

Mark Zuckerberg didn't miss an opportunity to promote Muse Charm. Bloomberg/Getty Images Mark Zuckerberg shared a Thanksgiving video showing him playing "GTA 6" as a turkey smoked. He asked Meta's Muse AI agent for an update on the bird using a Muse Charm. Zuckerberg said his AI-assisted turkey won his family's annual turkey smoking competition. Mark Zuckerberg found time for a very Mark Zuckerberg "GTA 6" side quest this Thanksgiving: promoting Meta's AI hardware. In a video posted Thursday by Zuckerberg, his wife, Priscilla Chan, enters the room and reminds him that guests will arrive soon. "You're supposed to be making the turkey," Chan says. Zuckerberg is sitting on the sofa playing "GTA 6." Instead of answering her directly, Zuckerberg asks his Muse Charm — a pocket-sized device for speaking with Muse, Meta's AI agent — for an update. Muse says the turkey is on track, its breast temperature is rising steadily, and the smoker is holding near the target. Pulling the turkey around 4 p.m. should leave enough time for the guests, the agent says. Zuckerberg nods and gets back to the game. 

 "Tried a new strategy and got Muse to smoke my turkey this year," Zuckerberg wrote in the Instagram post's caption. He said the result was so good that Chan conceded their annual turkey-smoking competition and asked Muse to smoke hers, too. He also shared a photo of the finished bird on his Instagram story. The holiday promo comes as Muse has proven a hit for Meta. It quickly soared straight to the top of the App Store following its September launch and has been used by more than 10 million people. The technology promises to handle tasks on users' behalf, so they have more time for other pursuits — in Zuck's case, cruising around "GTA 6's" Vice City. Meta unveiled Muse Charm in late September, but it's not yet available for purchase. The standalone gadget gives users voice access to Muse, which Meta says can carry out tasks across connected apps and websites. Zuckerberg is among the millions getting in on the "GTA 6" hype. The game generated an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide sales on its first day, while players logged 683 million hours on PlayStation 5 in its first four days .
