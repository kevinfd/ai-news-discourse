# Nvidia Blackwell Chips Turn Up at China Site Tied to Alice Huang

Bloomberg · 2026-11-27

A pair of Chinese firms installed Nvidia Corp.’s powerful Blackwell semiconductors in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, the woman at the heart of a controversy surrounding the US chipmaker. 
The gear installed at the Yangtze River Delta Project included a GB300 NVL72 rack, among Nvidia’s most powerful products, according to a serial number specified in documents submitted to Chinese regulators in November.
