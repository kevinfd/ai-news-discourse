# Dunzo founder Kabeer Biswas' AI startup M raises $9 million in follow-on round led by RTP

Moneycontrol · Aryaman Gupta · 2026-11-27

Dunzo founder Kabeer Biswas' AI startup M raises $9 million in follow-on round led by RTP
The round takes the startup's total funding to $20 million. Moneycontrol wrote in April that M raised $11 million from Peak XV Partners, Blume Ventures and CRED
Kabeer Biswas-founded M, an AI-powered household concierge startup, has raised $9 million (around Rs 83 crore) in a follow-on funding round, taking its total capital raised to $20 million (roughly Rs 186 crore), sources told Moneycontrol.
US-based RTP Global invested $5 million (about Rs 46 crore) in the latest round, while existing investors contributed the remaining $4 million (around Rs 37 crore), the sources said.
The startup is attempting to move beyond grocery delivery and build an AI concierge that can understand household preferences, plan meals, coordinate with domestic staff and manage grocery requirements.
The company is building a platform that can integrate with existing consumer services, including quick-commerce and food delivery platforms, to execute tasks on behalf of households.
M's approach is different from that of consumer internet companies, which primarily focus on facilitating transactions. Instead, it wants to automate the decisions and coordination that lead to those transactions.
The startup is building on Biswas' experience at Dunzo, the hyperlocal delivery company he co-founded, which initially began as a concierge service before expanding into logistics and quick commerce.
He also briefly led Flipkart's quick-commerce business, Flipkart Minutes, before stepping down.
The company said earlier it was targeting 10,000 households. Its early product focus is on households that employ cooks and domestic staff, with the platform designed to coordinate meal planning and grocery procurement.
