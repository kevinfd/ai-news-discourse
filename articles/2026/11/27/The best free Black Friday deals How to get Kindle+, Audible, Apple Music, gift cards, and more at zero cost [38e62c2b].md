# The best free Black Friday deals: How to get Kindle+, Audible, Apple Music, gift cards, and more at zero cost

Mashable · Tabitha Britt · 2026-11-27

The best free Black Friday deals: How to get Kindle+, Audible, Apple Music, gift cards, and more at zero cost
Table of Contents
Black Friday sales are live, and while everyone loves a good discount, nothing beats an unexpected freebie — especially when you're buying gifts for other people.
Of course, “free” usually comes with a few conditions. You might need to buy a certain product first, show up to a store, be a new subscriber, remember to cancel a plan by a certain date. Below, I've rounded up the best free Black Friday deals and trials that are worth the effort.
Best free gift card deal
Why we like it
What's better than free money? Personally, I can't think of anything. Right now, anyone who buys select $100 Best Buy gift cards will earn a free $15 Best Buy voucher they can put toward a future purchase there. Choose from Regal, ChatGPT, Google Play, or Apple gift cards.
Heads up: As part of the Apple Store's Black Friday Event, you'll soon be able to score a free gift card worth up to $300 there when you buy select Apple devices. The offer goes live on Friday, Nov. 27 and runs through Nov. 30.
Best BOGO deal
Why we like it
The Home Depot's BOGO Black Friday tool deal was a hit with Mashable readers last holiday season, so I'm pleased to report that it's back again. Just add certain on-sale cordless tools and kits to your cart to earn another free cordless tool worth up to $249. You can take your pick from impact drivers, saws, hammer drills, sanders, and more from popular brands like DeWalt, Makita, Milwaukee, Ridgid, and Ryobi.
Best in-store freebie deal
Why we like it
Most of the best Black Friday deals are available online, so retailers have to pull out the stops to get people into their stores. And Lowe's is going hard with its free Black Friday Bucket giveaway for the second year in a row.
Here's how it works: The first 50 MyLowe’s Rewards members in line at every store will receive a bucket containing at least 10 free products and offers. Four of those 50 buckets will include a red, green, gold, or silver ticket that earns their possessor a grand prize: either a free $2,000 major appliance, an EGO outdoor power tool, a Craftsman tools package, or a Blackstone grilling package. If you can't show up right away, fret not: The next 100 people in line will get a miniature bucket containing $5 in MyLowe’s Money and other rewards.
The festivities kick off at 6 a.m. local time at more than 1,700 Lowe's stores. If you're not a MyLowe's Rewards member yet, you can sign up for free.
More in-store freebie deals on Nov. 27
- Bass Pro Shops and Cabela’s — The first 250 customers (ages 16 and older) at every store will receive a free mystery gift card worth $10 to $500, which will be valid from Dec. 6 to 24.
- JCPenney — Select stores are giving away four prizes throughout the day, including a miniature Harry Potter snow globe, a Ninja air fryer, diamond earrings, and a Samsonite suitcase.
- Michaels — Every store is giving away 500 "Countdown to Craftsmas" advent calendars. One calendar in every store contains a $100 gift card, and one recipient will win a $10,000 grand prize.
- Sephora at Kohl's — The first 200 customers at select stores will receive a free Kohl’s Cash voucher worth $5 to $100 to spend on beauty purchases that day.
- Target — The first 100 customers at every store will receive a free Target gift card worth $25, $50, or $100. One person per store will get a $1,000 gift card.
Best free membership deal
Why we like it
Paying for a membership just to shop a sale feels a little counterproductive. Luckily, eligible customers can try Amazon Prime free for 30 days. Starting your trial now gives you time to shop early deals and still have it for Black Friday and Cyber Monday. After the trial, Prime costs $14.99 per month or $139 per year, depending on your selected plan.
Prime comes with more than free shipping, though that’s probably the perk you’ll use most while shopping for gifts. You also get Prime Video, Amazon Music’s ad-free listening options, access to a selection of books through Prime Reading, and member-exclusive deals. The newest addition is $0 delivery fees on eligible Uber Eats restaurant orders of $25 or more placed through Amazon — a useful extra for the nights you’d rather order dinner than make it.
Best free reading deal
Why we like it
Books are one of those things I don’t particularly enjoy putting a spending limit on. A subscription at least gives you a way to read more without paying for every title separately. Kindle+, formerly Kindle Unlimited, offers a 30-day free trial with access to a catalog of ebooks, audiobooks, magazines, and comics. It automatically renews at $11.99 per month unless you cancel.
You might also see an offer for two months for $0.99 total. That’s not free, but it’s worth comparing with the trial before you choose.

Best free music deal
Why we like it
A music subscription is pretty easy to test for a month. You’ll have time to find your favorite artists, try a few playlists, and decide whether you like it enough to pay for it. New subscribers can get one month of Apple Music free, including ad-free listening and downloads for when you don’t have an internet connection. The Individual plan costs $11.99 per month afterward.
And despite the name, you don’t need an iPhone to use it — Apple Music also works on Android and Windows.
More free music deals
- Spotify Premium Individual — One month free for people who haven’t tried Premium before, then $12.99 per month. Includes ad-free music and offline downloads.
- Amazon Music Unlimited Individual — 30 days free for new subscribers, then $11.99 per month for Prime members or $12.99 per month for nonmembers, plus applicable tax.
- SoundCloud Go — 7 days free for new subscribers, then $4.99 per month, plus applicable tax. Subscription automatically renews unless cancelled.
- Deezer Family — One month free, then $19.99 per month. Cancel anytime.
Best free audiobook deal
Why we like it
Not all reading has to involve sitting down with a book. Audible’s 30-day free trial lets new customers choose one audiobook from its collection, including bestsellers and new releases, and listen to an included catalog of podcasts. After the trial, it automatically renews at $8.99 per month unless you cancel.
There’s also a separate promotion for three months at $0.99 per month, plus a $20 Audible bonus. I wouldn’t call that free, but paying $2.97 for three months might make more sense than taking one free month and then paying the regular rate.
More free audiobook offers
- Hoopla — Free through participating public libraries. Check whether your library offers access before signing up; this is library borrowing, not a subscription trial that turns into a monthly bill.
- Kobo Plus Listen — 14 days free, then $7.99 per month. The Read & Listen plan includes both ebooks and audiobooks for $9.99 per month after the trial. You can use the Kobo Books app on your phone or tablet, so there’s no need to buy an e-reader.
- Everand — 30 days free, with plans starting at $11.99 per month afterward. The trial includes one premium-title unlock, plus unlimited access to a separate catalog of more than 20,000 audiobooks, ebooks, and podcasts.
- All You Can Books — 30 days free, then $24.99 per month. The service advertises unlimited downloads from its catalog of audiobooks, ebooks, language courses, and other content, and says downloaded files remain yours even after cancellation.
Best free magazine and news deal
Why we like it
Apple News+ gives new subscribers one month to try its collection of newspapers and magazines, making it a different option from the book-focused subscriptions above. The subscription costs $12.99 per month after the trial.
I’d use the free month to check whether the publications you actually want to read are included. A long list of magazines sounds nice, but it doesn’t mean much if you’re only opening one of them.
Best free streaming deal
Why we like it
If you've had an Apple original on your watchlist, Apple TV's free trial is a good excuse to finally get around to watching it. New subscribers can try the streaming service free for seven days, giving you access to hundreds of exclusive shows and movies, with new releases added every week.
After the trial, the subscription costs $14.99 per month, so it's worth setting a reminder to cancel if you don't plan on keeping it.
More free streaming deals
- YouTube Premium — One month free for eligible new subscribers, then $15.99 per month for the Individual plan, $26.99 per month for Family, or $8.99 per month for eligible students. Includes ad-free YouTube and YouTube Music, offline downloads, and background playback. Automatically renews unless canceled.
- AMC+ — 7 days free for eligible new subscribers, then $7.99 per month with ads or $10.99 per month for the ad-free Premium plan. An annual Premium subscription costs $109.99.
- Hulu + Live TV — 3 days free for eligible subscribers, then $99.99 per month unless canceled. Includes Hulu and Disney+ with ads, ESPN, more than 100 live TV channels, and unlimited DVR.
Best free phone plan deal
Why we like it
If you've been thinking about switching phone providers, Verizon is also offering a 30-day free trial, giving you a chance to test its network without switching providers. You can keep your existing phone number and continue using your current network while trying Verizon through an eSIM. There's no credit card required, no contract, and no need to remember to cancel, either. The trial automatically expires after 30 days, so you won't be charged if you decide not to switch.
More free phone plan deals
- T-Mobile — 30 days free for eligible non-T-Mobile customers with a compatible, unlocked device. Includes access to T-Mobile's network, satellite coverage, and member benefits, with no credit card, credit check, or commitment required.
- Visible — 15 days free with unlimited data, talk, text, and hotspot access on Verizon's network. No credit card, contract, or automatic renewal required.
- AT&T Wireless — 30 days free with unlimited talk and text, plus 100GB of data. Keep your current phone, number, and service while testing the network. Compatible iPhone or Android required.
Best free fitness app deal
Why we like it
If you're planning to get a head start on your New Year's fitness goals, Apple Fitness+ is offering new subscribers one month free. The service gives you access to a range of guided workouts, from strength training and HIIT to yoga, Pilates, and meditation, so there's plenty to try before deciding whether it's worth paying for. After the trial, it costs $9.99 per month or $79.99 per year.
And if you're planning to pick up a new Apple device during the Black Friday sales, you could get an even better deal, with three months free when you purchase an eligible device.
More free fitness app deals
- Glo — 7 days free, with access to more than 12,000 on-demand yoga, Pilates, meditation, and strength classes led by over 60 instructors.
- Peloton App — Free trial for new members, with access to thousands of workout classes across more than 15 exercise types, including options that don't require equipment. Cancel anytime.
- Ladder — 7 days free, with access to all training plans, personalized workout recommendations, introductory classes, and fitness communities. No payment information required to start the trial.
- Nike Training Club — Completely free, with access to guided workouts, multi-week training programs, and fitness advice. No subscription or trial period required.
Best free meditation and mindfulness deal
Why we like it
If you're looking for a way to unwind after a busy day, Calm is offering a seven-day free trial of its Premium membership. You'll get access to more than 50,000 minutes of content, including guided meditations, Sleep Stories, relaxing music, and nature sounds. After the trial, the subscription automatically renews at $79.99 per year, plus applicable taxes, so make sure you cancel before the seven days are up if you don't want to continue.
