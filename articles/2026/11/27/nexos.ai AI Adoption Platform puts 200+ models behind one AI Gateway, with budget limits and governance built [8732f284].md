# nexos.ai AI Adoption Platform puts 200+ models behind one AI Gateway, with budget limits and governance built in

MarkTechPost · Asif Razzaq · 2026-11-27

nexos.ai is an all-in-one AI platform for enterprise AI adoption . It brings full control and governance to every team. The AI gateway at its core routes every LLM request through one unified layer where budgets, policies, and audit trails apply.  

 Here’s how it governs usage, optimizes AI spend, and meets the compliance requirements EU companies set. 

 TL;DR 

 Governance. Adopt AI with control. Set who can use which models, apply budgets and usage limits, and manage permissions from one place. 

 Compliance. SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001 certified, and fully GDPR compliant. 

 Cost control. Smart Router and LLM caching allow AI spend optimization. Cost tracking attributes every dollar to a team, project, or user. 

 European hosting. The platform and most available models are hosted in Europe, with zero data retention and no training on your data. 

 Visibility. LLM observability shows usage, cost, and routing decisions across every team. 

 Why does AI adoption need a control layer? 

 Teams often start using AI on their own. Marketing picks one tool, engineering picks another, and each team signs up for its own API keys. Spend grows faster than anyone’s view of it, and leadership sees the total on the monthly invoice. 

 The EU AI Act adds a second pressure. It moves AI governance and accountability from policy documents into daily operations. CTOs and CISOs now need shared answers on which models are used, what data goes into them, and what each team spends. 

 What does the nexos.ai AI gateway do? 

 An AI gateway sits between your applications and the model providers they call. Developers keep one API and their existing SDKs. Leadership gets one place to set and enforce rules. The nexos.ai AI gateway handles three jobs. 

 Governance and control. Admins set which models each team can access and enforce policies centrally. 

 Spend optimization. Requests are routed, cached, and compressed so routine work uses cheaper models and fewer tokens. 

 Reliability. When a provider goes down or slows, automatic fallbacks reroute requests to another model or provider. 

 The same endpoint reaches 200+ leading AI models, so teams can adopt new models without locking into one vendor. 

 How does the gateway govern AI usage? 

 Admins decide which teams and users can reach which models. They set budgets and usage limits per team or user. Policy enforcement applies those rules to every request, and audit logs and traces record each one. 

 Usage is visible by team and project. When finance or an auditor asks who used what, the answer is in the logs. 

 The gateway works alongside Chat & Agents . Employees adopt AI in daily work there, chatting with leading AI models and building no-code agents under the same permissions, budgets, and audit trails. 

 How does it optimize AI spend? 

 The gateway reduces spend in four ways. None of them require changes to your applications. 

 Smart Router. Each request goes to a model chosen on cost, quality, and latency. Routine workloads stop using premium models. 

 LLM caching. Identical requests aren’t paid for twice. Caching lowers both latency and token spend. 

 Context compression. The gateway sends fewer tokens to the model, which lowers cost before the request reaches the LLM. 

 Cost tracking and controls. Spend is tracked in real time and attributed to a team, project, or user, down to the request. 

 Teams see where money goes before the invoice arrives. They can also measure the savings from routing, caching, and request optimization as they happen. 

 What compliance and security does it cover? 

 This list covers what nexos.ai provides for security and compliance reviews. 

 Certifications. nexos.ai is SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001 certified. 

 Regulation. The platform is fully GDPR compliant. 

 Hosting. The platform and most available models are hosted in Europe. 

 Data handling. Zero data retention is available for supported models, and your data isn’t used to train AI models unless you allow it. 

 Access. SSO and RBAC protect sensitive information. 

 Certifications, European hosting, and zero data retention give security and compliance teams a clear basis for approval. Teams can still pick the LLM that fits their own preferences and compliance requirements. 

 What do teams and leadership each get? 

 The AI gateway serves two groups at once. Teams get tools to build with, and leadership gets the controls to scale AI adoption safely. 

 Teams get one API for every leading LLM, access to 200+ models, production-ready infrastructure, and no vendor lock-in. 

 Leadership gets visibility into AI usage and costs, one invoice with budget and spend controls, policy enforcement, and audit logs and traces.
