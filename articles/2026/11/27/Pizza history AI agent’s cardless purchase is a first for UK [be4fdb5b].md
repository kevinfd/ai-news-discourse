# Pizza history: AI agent’s cardless purchase is a first for UK

The Guardian · Mark Sweney · 2026-11-27

Papa John’s order was made without sharing bank security details – tech that could revolutionise shopping Retailers enter the unknown with AI shopping assistants on Black Friday An order of Papa John’s margherita pizzas has become the first purchase in the UK to be made using an AI agent using cardless payment technology. The order of nine mini pizzas was made as part of the Financial Conduct Authority’s (FCA) regulated sandbox trials to test the technology, using Lloyds Bank and payment specialist TrueLayer.
