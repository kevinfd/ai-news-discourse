It may not be explicitly listed as a "Black Friday Deal" at the retailer, but it's an offer we certainly still think is worth jumping on while it's still live.

This new Mac mini is quite a performance upgrade over its predecessor, moving from the M4 to the M6 chip. With this boost, the desktop offers up to 4.8x faster AI performance, thanks to a Neural Accelerator in each GPU core, faster unified memory, and a Dual 16-core Neural Engine. Overall, this means a far more responsive workflow than the previous model.
The Mac mini remains a wonderfully compact piece of hardware, too, measuring just five by five inches. For connecting other devices, like an external monitor, it also comes with a variety of ports, including three Thunderbolt 4 ports, an HDMI port, a 2.5 Gbps Ethernet port on the back, plus two USB-C ports and a headphone jack on the front.
