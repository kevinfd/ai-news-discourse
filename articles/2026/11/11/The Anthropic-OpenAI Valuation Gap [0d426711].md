# The Anthropic-OpenAI Valuation Gap

The Information · Valida Pau · 2026-11-11

If you want proof of the widening gap in sentiment between public and private investors, take a look at the valuations of Anthropic and OpenAI. On Tuesday, Anthropic disclosed it was targeting a valuation of between $1.26 trillion and $1.4 trillion in its upcoming initial public offering. The prospectus update comes on the heels of OpenAI’s most recent fundraising, which valued the company at $1.25 trillion .  That means Anthropic expects public investors to value it at only 12% more than OpenAI, assuming it achieves the valuation at the high end of its range.
