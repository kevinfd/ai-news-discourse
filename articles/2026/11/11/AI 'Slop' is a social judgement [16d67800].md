# AI 'Slop' is a social judgement

Nature · learning_by_looking · 2026-11-11

A new paper argues that AI Slop isn't something you can find in the content. It’s a social judgement, made when a community decides that some AI-generated content is unwanted. If we study 'slop' that way, we can actually measure it and govern it.
