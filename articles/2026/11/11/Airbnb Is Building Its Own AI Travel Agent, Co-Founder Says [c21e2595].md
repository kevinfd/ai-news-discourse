# Airbnb Is Building Its Own AI Travel Agent, Co-Founder Says

Bloomberg · 2026-11-11

Airbnb Co-founder and Chief Strategy Officer Nathan Blecharczyk says the company is developing its own specialized AI travel agent that will eventually be able to book both on and off Airbnb’s platform. In an interview with Bloomberg’s Francine Lacqua on “The Pulse”, he spoke about how AI is transforming Airbnb, the company’s return to growth, regulation and housing in Europe, and the shift in travel beyond major cities. (Source: Bloomberg)
