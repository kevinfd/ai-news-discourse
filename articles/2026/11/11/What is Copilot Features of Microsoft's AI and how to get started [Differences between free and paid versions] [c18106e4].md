# What is Copilot? Features of Microsoft's AI and how to get started [Differences between free and paid versions]

note · まる吉｜Ai仕事術 · 2026-11-11

SYSTEM NOTICE
What is Copilot? Features of Microsoft's AI and how to get started [Differences between free and paid versions]
You use Word and Excel, but you have to open AI separately. 
Copilot is an ally for people like that. 
Copilot is Microsoft's AI. You can use it within Word and Excel.
What can Copilot do?
Copilot is an AI created by Microsoft. You can use it in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams. 
To use it, just talk to Copilot within the app. There is no need to open a separate screen.
Three things you can do for work
The first is drafting in Word. If you provide the key points you want to convey, it will create a rough draft for you. 
The second is organizing tables in Excel. If you tell it what you want to summarize, it will show you how to create the table. 
The third is summarizing emails in Outlook. You can read just the key points of long emails.
Three features of Copilot
The biggest feature of Copilot is that it can be used within apps. You can ask the AI for help while keeping Word or Excel open. 
The second is that it connects to your work data. It helps you based on meeting content and email exchanges. 
The third is that you can start for free. You can try the free version of Copilot first.
What is the difference between the free and paid versions?
The free Copilot and the work-oriented Microsoft 365 Copilot are different things. 
The free Copilot can be used with a Microsoft account. You can ask questions in chat or create text. 
The work-oriented Microsoft 365 Copilot requires a work or school account and a qualifying subscription. You can use it within Word and Excel, connected to your work data. 
Microsoft 365 Personal is 2,130 yen per month (as of November 2026. Prices are subject to change, so please check the official website). 
Try the free Copilot first, and if you decide you want to use it for work, you can consider the work-oriented version.
Who is Copilot for?
Copilot is suitable for the following people.
- People who use Word and Excel every day
- People who spend time organizing meetings and emails
- People who find it troublesome to open AI separately
If even one of these applies to you, it is worth trying.
Steps to get started in 30 minutes a day
First, choose just one document you want to create in Word. It could even be part of a report. 
There are only three steps.
- Write the key points you want to convey in a bulleted list
- Pass it to Copilot and have it create a draft
- Read it yourself and make any necessary corrections
You don't need to aim for a perfect draft from the start. If you have a rough outline, it's fast to make corrections.
You can use this just by changing the points section to your own content.
Differences from ChatGPT and Claude
ChatGPT is an all-purpose type that you can consult about anything. You can handle emails, documents, and brainstorming with a single AI.
Claude is a specialized type that is strong with text. It excels at polishing and summarizing long documents in natural Japanese.
Copilot is a type used within applications. You can use it while keeping Word or Excel open, connected to your work data.
There is no single correct answer; knowing how to use them differently is important. It's good to remember: use ChatGPT when you want to do various things quickly, Claude when you want to finish a document, and Copilot when you want to use it within Word or Excel.
Things to be careful about (2 points)
The first is to check your company's rules when inputting company information. Whether or not you can put internal information into an AI depends on the company.
The second is not to use the answers as they are. Please check numbers and proper nouns yourself.
Start with one draft in Word
Copilot is an AI that can be used within Word and Excel. Try having Copilot help you with just one draft in Word.
Once you get used to it, try using it for organizing Excel tables or summarizing Outlook emails. I have written about how to use it for work in another article.
