# 8 Examples of Generative AI Use in Local Governments: Comparing Backgrounds, Usage, and Results

8 Examples of Generative AI Use in Local Governments: Comparing Backgrounds, Usage, and Results
As the population declines and the number of staff decreases, more local governments are adopting generative AI to reduce the burden of document creation and responding to inquiries. On the other hand, there are many voices expressing concerns such as "we introduced it but it's not being used" or "I'm worried about how personal information is handled."
In this article, we compare eight local government cases interviewed by Reborn, looking at the services introduced, how they are used, and the results achieved, and summarize the key points for a "successful" implementation that actually gets used.
What you will learn in this article
- Generative AI in local governments is expanding from document creation, summarization, and translation to public relations images, macro creation, and resident support
- Examples of results: Yokosuka City reduced work by approximately 4,000 hours in six months, and Miyakonojo City reduced work by several thousand hours per month
- The keys to adoption are "guidelines," "small-group and proficiency-based training," and "sharing how to use it"
How far has the introduction of generative AI in local governments progressed?
According to the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications' "Status of Generative AI Introduction in Local Governments (FY2025)" as of October 31, 2025, 100% of prefectures and designated cities have introduced generative AI, and 45.6% of other municipalities have done so. Including those currently conducting trials, planning to introduce it, or considering it, about 90% of organizations are working toward implementation.
On the other hand, 647 local governments have "established" generative AI usage guidelines, while 1,004 have "not yet established" them (Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications' "Report of the Working Group on AI Usage in Local Governments," July 2025). Even as implementation progresses, many local governments still need to create rules for safe usage and find ways to encourage staff to use it.
Comparison of 8 cases
Iga City, Mie Prefecture
- Generative AI introduced: GaiXer (FIXER)
- Main usage: Document creation, material summarization, translation into foreign languages
- Results/Features: Distributed to almost all staff. Small-group training for up to 3 people and ongoing support
Nisshin City, Aichi Prefecture
- Generative AI introduced: QommonsAI (Polimill)
- Main usage: From assembly support to daily tasks
- Results/Features: Over 70% of the 38 staff members who received training felt an improvement in work efficiency
Shimotsuma City, Ibaraki Prefecture
- Generative AI introduced: ChatSense, QommonsAI
- Main usage: Meeting minutes search, document creation, flyer image generation
- Results/Features: One account per employee for all staff. Training sessions led by staff members
Miyakonojo City, Miyazaki Prefecture
- Generative AI introduced: Local Government AI zevo (co-developed with Shift Plus)
- Main usage: Document creation, inquiry responses using uploaded manuals
- Results/Features: The first service in the country available on LGWAN. Reduces thousands of hours of work per month
Yokosuka City, Kanagawa Prefecture (Health Insurance Division)
- Generative AI introduced: Macros created with generative AI
- Main usage: Input and listing of deposit inquiries
- Results/Features: Approximately 4,000 hours saved in six months, number of seizures doubled from the previous year
Shizuoka Prefecture
- Generative AI introduced: AI x Designer banner generation service
- Main usage: Public relations materials such as flyers and banners
- Results/Features: Used by over 130 divisions and for over 500 items annually, with a satisfaction rate of over 98%
Beppu City, Oita Prefecture
- Generative AI introduced: LINE generative AI chatbot
- Main usage: Guidance on waste sorting and procedures (English support)
- Results/Features: 24-hour inquiry availability. Approximately 3,000 uses per month for waste sorting, etc.
Yokosuka City, Kanagawa Prefecture (Dementia Prevention)
- Generative AI introduced: Cotomo (Starley)
- Main usage: Voice conversations with the elderly
- Results/Features: A demonstration project aiming to maintain cognitive function through conversations that evoke memories

Key points for each case study
Iga City, Mie Prefecture
The challenges were the disparity in staff digital literacy and the use of "shadow AI"—generative AI contracted individually by staff for work. The generative AI "GaiXer" was distributed to almost all staff, and regional revitalization entrepreneurs dispatched from FIXER are providing small-group training for up to three people and hands-on support for business improvement. In addition to document creation and summarizing government materials, it is also used for translation for foreign residents.
Nisshin City, Aichi Prefecture
We started by testing the generative AI "QommonsAI" for parliamentary correspondence. After establishing guidelines for information that must not be entered, we expanded training tailored to staff, such as beginner and intermediate levels, and over 70% of the 38 staff members who received the training felt an improvement in work efficiency.
Shimotsuma City, Ibaraki Prefecture
We introduced "ChatSense" and "QommonsAI" and provided one account per person for all staff. Through training where staff from the DX Promotion Division serve as instructors, we are expanding usage from searching meeting minutes and creating documents to generating flyer images.
Miyakonojo City, Miyazaki Prefecture
In collaboration with Shift Plus, we developed "Local Government AI zevo," a generative AI that can be used in an LGWAN environment. We share task-specific prompts with local governments nationwide and use it for inquiry responses by loading financial accounting manuals and for policy making by referencing comprehensive plans. This has led to a reduction in work on the scale of several thousand hours per month.
Yokosuka City, Kanagawa Prefecture (Health Insurance Division)
We used generative AI to create macros that automate the input and listing of deposit inquiries without entering personal information. We are now able to create inquiry data for 3,000 people at once, reducing work by about 4,000 hours in six months, equivalent to about two staff members. The number of seizures has doubled from the previous year.
Shizuoka Prefecture
Because many respondents in the prefectural public opinion survey stated that "prefectural politics and administration are difficult to understand," the prefecture introduced a banner generation service where generative AI collaborates with designers. Public relations materials can be created in as little as 20 minutes, and the service is used by over 130 departments for more than 500 productions annually.
Beppu City, Oita Prefecture
By incorporating a generative AI chatbot into their official LINE account, which has approximately 27,000 registered users, the city has enabled foreign residents and dual-income households to look up garbage sorting rules and procedures in English without having to visit the municipal office.
Yokosuka City, Kanagawa Prefecture (Dementia Prevention)
Using the voice-based generative AI "Cotomo," the city is conducting a pilot program to create opportunities for conversation for the elderly by drawing out nostalgic topics and memories. The initiative aims to maintain cognitive function and reduce feelings of isolation.
Related: While not generative AI, please also see the case of Himeji City, Hyogo Prefecture, which reduced overtime related to creating child consultation records by approximately 40% using AI voice recognition.
Key Points for Implementing "Used" Generative AI Seen from Case Studies
- Decide what information can be input through rules: Iga City and Nisshin City distributed the tools after establishing guidelines. Miyakonojo City selected a service that can be used in an LGWAN environment.
- Try it in one task first: Nisshin City started with assembly support, and Yokosuka City started with tasks like deposit inquiries, focusing on areas where effects are easily visible.
- Conduct training in small groups and by proficiency level: Iga City limits training to a maximum of 3 people, Nisshin City separates training for beginners and intermediate users, and Shimotsuma City uses staff members as instructors.
- Share how to use it: Miyakonojo City shares task-specific prompts with local governments nationwide.
Frequently Asked Questions
Q. What tasks do local governments use generative AI for?
It is mainly used for document creation, summarizing materials, and translation. It is also expanding to include creating public relations images (Shizuoka Prefecture), creating macros (Yokosuka City), and responding to inquiries from residents (Beppu City).
Q. How is personal information handled?
Methods include defining information that must not be input in guidelines (Iga City, Nisshin City), selecting services that can be used in an LGWAN environment (Miyakonojo City), and using it only for macro creation without inputting personal information (Yokosuka City).
Q. What should we do if staff do not use it even after implementation?
Small-group training tailored to proficiency levels and hands-on support for consultation are effective. In Iga City, they switched from annual group training to small-group training that staff can take whenever they wish.
Q. How many local governments have implemented generative AI?
According to a survey by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (as of October 31, 2025), 100% of prefectures and designated cities have implemented it, while 45.6% of other municipalities have done so.
Summary
Generative AI is a tool that is harder to "integrate" than it is to "implement." The eight examples show a common approach: creating rules, starting with small tasks, and conducting small-group training. Other examples of AI use in administration are introduced in AI x Administration (Local Government DX).
