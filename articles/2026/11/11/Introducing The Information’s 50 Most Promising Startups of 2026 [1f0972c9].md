# Introducing The Information’s 50 Most Promising Startups of 2026

The Information · Amir Efrati · 2026-11-11

As artificial intelligence changes how companies get hacked, investors are paying up for startups that promise to keep defenders ahead. 

 That dynamic shaped this year’s The Information 50 , our annual list of the most promising startups. For the first time, our list includes a category devoted solely to security, and it holds some of the list’s biggest bets.
