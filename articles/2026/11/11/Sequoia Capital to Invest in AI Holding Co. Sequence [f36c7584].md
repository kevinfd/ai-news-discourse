# Sequoia Capital to Invest in AI Holding Co. Sequence

The Information · Stephanie Palazzolo, Valida Pau · 2026-11-11

 This will be Sequoia’s second known investment in a new category of holding companies. These aim to buy businesses in established industries such as accounting, insurance and HOA management and then replace or complement some human work with AI.
