# roomDJ: School Safe Spotify Jukebox

Instagram · DaltonJFowler · 2026-11-07

Hey everyone! Dalton J Fowler again. Wanted to share an update as I make roomDJ fully public and open source. This is a project I've made over the last 1.5 months with Claude code. It's made the High School I teach in better I think. NJ enacted a law banning phones bell to bell and headphones are also not allowed. I've learned so much about my students' music taste over the last few weeks it's awesome. I used Claude to make a jukebox style app that uses a low cost cloudflare ai call to check lyrics for appropriateness. We already have almost 6k songs checked with 3,141 being pre-approved…
