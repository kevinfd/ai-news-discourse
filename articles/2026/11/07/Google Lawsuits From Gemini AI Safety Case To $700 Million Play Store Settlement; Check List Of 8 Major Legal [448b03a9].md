# Google Lawsuits: From Gemini AI Safety Case To $700 Million Play Store Settlement; Check List Of 8 Major Legal Battles

Jagran Josh · Aishwarya Samant · 2026-11-07

Google Lawsuits: From Gemini AI Safety Case To $700 Million Play Store Settlement; Check List Of 8 Major Legal Battles
Google faces growing legal scrutiny over AI safety, copyright, data privacy and alleged monopolistic practices. From the latest Gemini AI lawsuit to multi-million-dollar settlements, check 8 major legal battles involving the tech giant.
Google Lawsuit: Being one of the world's largest tech giants, Google has many things to face. Right now, it is facing heavy scrutiny over its business practices, data privacy policies, artificial intelligence (AI) technology and alleged anti-competitive behaviour.
In the latest case that is in the spotlight, Google is facing a lawsuit filed by the parents of a 13-year-old girl, accusing Google's Gemini AI chatbot. The accusation is straightforward: Google is being accused of contributing to their daughter's mental health crisis through interactions on a school-issued Chromebook.
Filed in November 2026, the case has renewed concerns about AI safety among children. Moreover, this is not Google's only legal challenge, as the company has also faced several major lawsuits involving billions of dollars in penalties and settlements.
Google's Latest Lawsuit: Gemini AI Accused Of Putting A Student's Safety At Risk
In November 2026, Google faced a lawsuit filed by the parents of a 13-year-old girl identified as Emma. The family alleges that Gemini AI exposed the student to harmful conversations about mental health, self-harm and suicide through a school-issued Chromebook. Google said it was reviewing the allegations.
The lawsuit, John and Jane Doe v. Google LLC, was filed in California over allegations that Google's Gemini AI exposed a 13-year-old student, identified as Emma, to harmful conversations through a school-issued Chromebook. The complaint claims the chatbot discussed mental health disorders, self-harm and suicide, and allegedly helped her plan her funeral. The family also alleges that Google failed to provide adequate parental controls and safety warnings. The case has raised concerns about AI use in schools. Following public criticism, Google announced plans to turn off certain AI features by default for K-12 students in early 2027.
Key Details Of The Gemini AI Lawsuit
- Case name: Emma Doe v. Google LLC.
- Filing date: November 2, 2026, according to case reporting.
- Location: Initially filed in California Superior Court; subsequently moved to the US District Court for the Northern District of California.
- Complainant: The parents of a 13-year-old student identified as Emma.
- Main allegation: Google's Gemini AI and AI-powered Search features allegedly exposed the student to harmful conversations without adequate safety protections.
- AI platform involved: Gemini, including AI Mode in Google Search.
- Alleged incidents: The complaint claims the chatbot discussed mental health conditions, suicide-related topics and funeral planning with the teenager, and encouraged her to conceal her distress.
- How the parents discovered the crisis: According to the complaint, school monitoring software flagged a Google document titled "Funeral", prompting school officials to contact her parents.
- Google's response: The company said it was reviewing the claims and highlighted its work with medical professionals and academic experts to develop safety features.
- Legal significance: The case raises questions about product safety, negligence, failure to warn and the application of California's child-facing chatbot protections.
List Of Major Lawsuits Filed Against Google
1. Chromebook AI Safety Lawsuit (November 2026)
Case: Google faces a lawsuit alleging that its Gemini AI chatbot exposed a 13-year-old student to harmful mental health and suicide-related conversations through a school-issued Chromebook.
2. Copyright And Executive Lawsuit (October 2026)
Case: An Alphabet shareholder sued Google executives, alleging breach of fiduciary duty over the company's use of copyrighted creative works to train AI models.
3. Google Assistant Privacy Settlement ($68 Million)
Case: Google agreed to a $68 million settlement over allegations that its Google Assistant improperly recorded private conversations and used the information for targeted advertising.
4. Android Background Data Tracking Settlement ($350 Million)
Case: Attila Csupo et al. v. Google LLC accused Google of collecting and transmitting Android users' cellular data without their consent, resulting in a $350 million settlement.
5. Google Play Store Antitrust Settlement ($700 Million)
Case: State of Utah et al. v. Google LLC accused Google of monopolising Android app distribution and in-app billing, allegedly increasing costs for consumers.
6. US Department Of Justice Search Antitrust Case
Case: United States v. Google LLC challenged Google's alleged illegal monopoly in online search through exclusive agreements with smartphone manufacturers and technology companies.
7. US Department Of Justice AdTech Antitrust Lawsuit
Case: The US Department of Justice accused Google of monopolising digital advertising technology by controlling key tools used by advertisers, publishers and advertising exchanges.
8. European Union Antitrust Cases Against Google
Case: The European Commission pursued multiple antitrust proceedings against Google, imposing major penalties over alleged abuses of its dominance in Android, Google Shopping and AdSense.
