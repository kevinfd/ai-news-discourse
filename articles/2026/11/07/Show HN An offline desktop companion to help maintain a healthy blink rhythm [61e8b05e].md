# Show HN: An offline desktop companion to help maintain a healthy blink rhythm

EyesRhythm · prnysoni · 2026-11-07

EyesRhythm uses your webcam to score how often and how steadily you blink
on long screen days. Poise shows which way you turned your head, so the
neck and shoulders get a change of direction. No account, and no video
leaving your machine.
See the last 5 minutes of your blink rhythm on a live graph.
Stays beside the work. Counts only while you are at the screen.
Why it exists
Your eyes work hard for you. Screens make them work worse.
Focused computer use often cuts blink rate in half. Sparse, uneven
blinking is a known driver of digital eye strain, dryness, grit,
fatigue, blurred vision, and it is easy not to notice until the eyes
already hurt. EyesRhythm is a
blink rate detector that sits
beside that work.
What rhythm means
Rate and regularity, together.
Rate is how often. Regularity is how steady. Enough blinks can still
arrive in bursts and droughts; a slow but even pattern still leaves
the surface dry. EyesRhythm folds both into the
Ocular Rhythm Index (0 to 100). Maximize your healthy
desk ORI, use practice daily, it helps to build muscle memory.
The companion
It watches, scores, and, if you ask, gently nudges.
ORI on a compact window. Alert is always on: Visual by default, a
quiet orange pulse, or Sound if you click A. Optional beep on each
blink. Practice is a two-minute coach so the habit is easier to feel.
Poise is the neck’s half of the same sit. The dial turns green where
you looked and stays red where you did not, so a still pose gets a
turn up, down, left, or right, and the neck and shoulders can relax
before you face the work again. A wellness companion, not a medical
device. Lasting discomfort belongs with an eye-care professional.
Get the highlights

Built for the desk

FAQ
Questions, answered.
What is a blink rate detector?
A blink rate detector uses a camera to notice each blink and measure how often they happen, usually as blinks per minute. EyesRhythm does that on your Mac or Windows computer, then also looks at how evenly those blinks are spaced.
Does EyesRhythm work with a laptop webcam?
Yes. It uses the built-in camera on a Mac or Windows laptop, or a USB webcam. Sit as you usually work so the camera can see your eyes. The setup guide in the journal walks through the few things that help.
Does any video leave my computer?
No. Frames are read on this machine and discarded. There is no account, no license key, no cloud, and no analytics. Camera frames are not uploaded or kept as a film of your face.
How is this different from a blink counter?
A counter only totals blinks. Six blinks can still mean two very different minutes if they arrive in clumps with a long dry wait in the middle. EyesRhythm folds rate and regularity into one Ocular Rhythm Index, from 0 to 100.
What is Poise for?
Poise shows which way you turned your head today, in 30° slices. Green is where you looked most. Red is a direction you barely used. Long computer work parks the neck in one pose. Poise is there so you move it up, down, left, and right from time to time, which lets the neck and shoulders relax, then you look back at the screen. It is a reminder, not a treatment.
Is EyesRhythm a medical device?
No. It is a wellness companion for desk work, not a diagnosis or a treatment. Lasting discomfort or a change in vision belongs with an eye-care professional.
