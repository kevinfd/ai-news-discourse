# PLaMo 3 Translate - a pfnet Collection

Hugging Face · pmttyji · 2026-11-07

Overview We have released the weights for PLaMo 3 Translate , our translation-specialized LLM, on Hugging Face. The models come in four sizes—0.8B, 2B, 8B, and 31B. Even the smallest 0.8B model outperforms existing translation-specialized LLMs, including models with more than 30 times as many parameters, in Japanese–English translation and translation between Japanese and other languages . The key was meticulous data refinement through techniques such as tournament-based data synthesis, back-translation, and iterative self-distillation, combined with distillation into smaller models.
