# Turning 1 hour of meeting minutes into 1 minute. How AI I learned for my side hustle made my main job incredibly easy

note · すきまAiママ｜会社員×子育て×Ai副業 · 2026-11-07

Turning 1 hour of meeting minutes into 1 minute. How AI I learned for my side hustle made my main job incredibly easy
Hello, I'm a part-time AI mom.
I work as a company employee, raise children, and do AI side work in my spare time.
I started using AI as a side hustle, but the area where it has actually helped me the most is my main job.
Today, I will honestly write about what I delegate to AI in my main job and how I started using it.
What I delegate to AI in my main job
Right now, I have AI helping me with these tasks in my main job.
- Meeting minutes
- Weekly report summaries
- Report creation
- Document creation
- Research
- Image generation
It includes almost all of those "tasks that quietly take up time" that occur almost every day.
The biggest change was "meeting minutes"
The effect was most clear with meeting minutes.
Before I used AI, it took at least 1 hour just to create meeting minutes. Now, I just hand the transcript to AI. It can be done in 
1 minute.
After that, I just check with my own eyes to see if the content is correct.
- Delegating the "summarizing work"
- Doing the "checking if the content is correct" myself
Since I started dividing it this way, meeting minutes are no longer a heavy task.
The reason I started using it was two "troubles"
There were two triggers for me starting to use AI in my main job.
1. I couldn't ask for help with color-changing tasks
The designer was so busy that I couldn't get them to take on color-changing tasks. 
I wanted to ask, but I couldn't. Yet, the work had to move forward.
So, I decided to try image generation.
2. Meeting minutes were taking too much time and squeezing my work
Meeting minutes were taking over an hour each time, crushing the time I had for the work I actually wanted to do. 
Throwing the transcription to AI was my first step in using AI for my main job.
Honestly, neither was something I started because I wanted to; I tried them because I was in a bind. That is the truth.
Image generation didn't go well at first
I'll share a slightly bitter story too.
Image generation didn't go as I wanted at first. 
But as AI models became newer, I gradually became able to create things that looked good.
Even if it doesn't work well now, it might be usable in a few months. 
I recommend not giving up just because you tried it before and it failed, but trying it again from time to time.
Do not put confidential information into AI
There is one thing I always strictly follow.
Do not put anything that could be confidential information into AI.
Precisely because it is convenient, I have made this a rule. 
If in doubt, don't put it in. Just by doing this, I can continue to use it with peace of mind.
Summary
- I entrust AI with meeting minutes, weekly reports, reports, document creation, research, and image generation in my main job as well
- Meeting minutes that took at least an hour now take 1 minute
- Entrusting AI with the 'summarizing work' and doing the 'content check' myself
- The triggers were not being able to ask the designer for color changes and my work being squeezed by meeting minutes
- Even if image generation doesn't go well at first, it becomes usable as the models evolve
- Do not input anything that could be confidential information into AI
What I learned for my side hustle ended up creating time for my main job as well. 
Try delegating tasks that you find "a little troublesome" to AI.
