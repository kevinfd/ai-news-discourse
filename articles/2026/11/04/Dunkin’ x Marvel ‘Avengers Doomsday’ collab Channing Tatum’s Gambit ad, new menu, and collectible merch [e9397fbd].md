# Dunkin’ x Marvel ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ collab: Channing Tatum’s Gambit ad, new menu, and collectible merch

Mashable · Joseph Green · 2026-11-04

Dunkin’ x Marvel ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ collab: Channing Tatum’s Gambit ad, new menu, and collectible merch
With Avengers: Doomsday landing in theaters on Dec. 18, Dunkin’ has officially launched a nationwide campaign starring Channing Tatum as Gambit.
To celebrate the nationwide launch, Dunkin’ is debuting “MUNCHKIN Incursion,” a new ad featuring Channing Tatum reprising his role as Gambit alongside H.E.R.B.I.E., the Fantastic Four’s beloved robotic companion.
But that's far from all. The ad campaign accompanies an extensive in-store rollout: a multi-item themed menu, four flagship store takeovers, and a merchandise collection headlined by a $14.99 collectible Doctor Doom 25-count Munchkins tin.

Store takeovers
From Nov. 17, four Dunkin’ restaurants will transform into immersive destinations inspired by the Marvel Cinematic Universe:
- Los Angeles (Nov. 17): A Fantastic Four-themed restaurant at 6201 Hollywood Blvd.
- Chicago (Nov. 17): An X-Men-themed experience at 10 West Chicago Ave.
- New York City (Nov. 18): An Avengers-inspired takeover at 102 8th Ave.
- Boston (Nov. 18): A Doctor Doom takeover at 209 N Harvard St. in Allston.
Avengers: Doomsday menu and collectible merch
You've got a little wait for the store takeovers to go live, but the Dunkin’ Avengers: Doomsday lineup is available now at participating restaurants nationwide. The limited-edition menu features beverages, treats, and limited-edition collectibles inspired by the film’s heroes and villains:
Superpowered Sips
- Iced Captain’ccino: Espresso, caramel flavor, and coffee milk, lightly blended and served over ice.
- Clobbered Cloud Latte: Espresso, whole milk, and toasted white chocolate flavor topped with Cookie Butter Cold Foam and crumbles.
- Wakandan Daydream Refresher: Berry Clementine Refresher, raspberry flavor, and oat milk, topped with Purple Marshmallow Cold Foam.
- Optic Blast Lemonade: Sweet-tart lemonade charged with blue raspberry flavor.
- Doom’s Orders Energy: Green Apple Dunkin' Zero, sparkling water, and Purple Marshmallow Cold Foam.
- Doom Cloud Latte: Espresso, whole milk, mocha swirl, and vanilla flavors topped with Sweet Cold Foam and cocoa powder.
Heroic Treats
- Doomsday Donut: Brownie Batter Donut returns with chocolate icing and green and chocolate sprinkles.
- Avenging Donut: A cotton candy-filled donut topped with vanilla-flavored icing and red, yellow, and blue sprinkles.
- Doomsday & Avenging MUNCHKINS: Glazed Chocolate and Buttermilk MUNCHKINS get villain- and hero-inspired treatments.
Collectibles
- Doctor Doom 25-Count MUNCHKINS Tin ($14.99): Collectible tin is available with any 25-count MUNCHKINS purchase, while supplies last.
- Avengers: Doomsday-Themed Tumblers ($24.99): Designs inspired by Doctor Doom, the Avengers, X-Men, and Fantastic Four.
- Avengers: Doomsday-Themed Color-Changing Mug ($14.99): A black mug that transforms to reveal film and Dunkin’ branding.
- Avengers: Doomsday-Themed Glove Cup Sleeves ($19.99): Collectible designs inspired by Doctor Doom, Black Panther, Mister Fantastic, and Captain America.
- Avengers: Doomsday-Themed Character Cup Sleeves ($8.99): Artwork inspired by Doctor Doom, the Avengers, X-Men, and Fantastic Four.
Pumped for Doomsday to hit theatres? Dunkin’ is giving Marvel fans plenty of ways to celebrate the upcoming release.
