# Show HN: I built a tool that prices a pre-built PC part by part

pcdealcheck.com · adlep · 2026-11-04

Is that computer
a good deal?
PC Deal Check makes checking a computer’s asking price quick and easy, whether you understand hardware or not.
Check US retailer links from anywhere. Photos and pasted text require a US connection. US market prices. No account needed.
A clear second opinion. No prompts or hardware expertise needed.
Start with a listing
Paste a retailer’s product link or upload a photo of the price and specs. AI helps read the listing and identify the main parts.
Understand the price
Our custom pricing engine compares the asking price with market-based component estimates to determine the deal’s grade.
Get a clear report
Review the grade, dated component estimates and price-check links. Share, email or print your report before deciding.
A real check · November 1, 2026
See what a report looks like.
A PowerSpec G531 listed at $1,399.99 received an A against a parts estimate of about $1,569. See the identified specs, price dates and comparison.
