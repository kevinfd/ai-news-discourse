# Ricursive Intelligence released their first LLM: Ripple-65B Preview, a 65B dense model.

X · xyzrt · 2026-11-04

Ripple sets a new state-of-the-art for U.S. open-weight models in agentic coding tasks, surpassing significantly larger models including Muse Spark Open-816B, Beam-501B, Inkling-975B, and Nemotron 3 Ultra-550B. Ripple-65B Preview was trained on 12 trillion tokens, supports up to 1M context length, and achieves: - 80.5% on TerminalBench 2.1 - 79.1% on SWE-Bench Pro v2.0-Hard - 44.3% on DeepSWE v1.1
