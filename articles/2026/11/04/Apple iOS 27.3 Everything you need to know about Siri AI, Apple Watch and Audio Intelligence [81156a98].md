# Apple iOS 27.3: Everything you need to know about Siri AI, Apple Watch and Audio Intelligence

The Hindu · John Xavier · 2026-11-04

Apple has begun rolling out the 27.3 updates for testing, and the update leans heavily on Siri AI, which now gets deeper access to the company’s own apps. The release also brings new features for Safari, StandBy, Health and Fitness+, along with a few device-specific additions.
With this update, Siri AI is poised to do more complex tasks across a range of apps, including Calendar, Mail, Notes, and Photos. The company claims that Siri can now help users create playlists based on a specific suggestion in the Music app. Users can also ask Siri to bring tabs together in Safari browser, and schedule a message to friends.
