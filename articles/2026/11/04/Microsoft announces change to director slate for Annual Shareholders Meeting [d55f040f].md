# Microsoft announces change to director slate for Annual Shareholders Meeting

Microsoft Source · Samantha Kubota · 2026-11-04

Microsoft Corporation (NASDAQ: MSFT) announced that Emma N. Walmsley has notified the board of directors of her decision not to stand for re-election at the company’s annual shareholders meeting on Dec. 8, 2026. This follows the announcement earlier today by HP Inc. that she will become their CEO, effective Jan. 4, 2027.
“We are grateful to Emma for her contributions to Microsoft and the industry expertise, thoughtful judgment, and global perspective she brought to our board,” said Satya Nadella, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Microsoft. “We look forward to continuing our partnership with Emma and with HP.”
Sandi Peterson, the company’s lead independent director, said, “Emma brought a valuable and distinct perspective to our board, combining her global business expertise with a balanced perspective and sound judgment. It has been a privilege to serve with her.”
Ms. Walmsley has served as a Microsoft director since 2019, currently sitting on the Compensation and Environmental, Social, and Public Policy committees. Her experience and engagement contributed to the success of the company on a broad range of issues throughout her board service.

The technology company is committed to making AI available broadly and doing so responsibly, with a mission to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more.
