# Marvell to buy Lumentum in $115 billion deal in AI data-center bet

Moneycontrol · Reuters · 2026-11-02

Marvell to buy Lumentum in $115 billion deal in AI data-center bet
Marvell Technology has agreed to acquire optical components maker Lumentum in a $115 billion all-stock deal, including debt, as it seeks to expand its AI data centre connectivity business and compete with rival Broadcom.
Reuters
November 02, 2026 / 18:23 IST
Marvell Technology will acquire Lumentum in a $115 billion all-stock deal, including debt, as it expands its AI data centre and optical connectivity business. (Image: Reuters)
Marvell is buying Lumentum in a $115B all-stock deal.
The acquisition aims to boost Marvell's AI optical tech.
The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2028.
