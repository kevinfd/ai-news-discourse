# Marvell Reaches $115 Billion Deal for Lumentum Holdings

Bloomberg · Ryan Gould, Michelle F. Davis and David Carnevali · 2026-11-02

Marvell Technology Inc. has reached a deal to buy data center optics maker Lumentum Holdings Inc. at an enterprise value of $115 billion, one of the biggest tech tie-ups ever, in a race to capitalize on the artificial intelligence boom.
