# DeepSeek aims to rival Anthropic with new coding agent

Silicon Republic · Laura Varley · 2026-11-02

The Chinese AI company has released what is reportedly a more autonomous coding agent in a bid to keep pace with Anthropic’s Claude model.
Chinese AI company DeepSeek has issued a prototype of a new autonomous coding agent named Super-Agent, reportedly in a bid to keep pace with its global rivals, such as Claude creator Anthropic.
According to Bloomberg, the DeepSeek Harness team explained in a post on social media platform WeChat that the open-source agent is capable of monitoring group chats among developers for feedback on coding issues and suggesting solutions without being prompted.
The DeepSeek Harness team was created earlier this year, and was reportedly designed to focus on building AI agents capable of competing with those offered by rival organisations in the AI space.
China’s AI companies are locked in a battle with global counterparts to dominate the AI landscape and be at the forefront of creation when it comes to advanced, affordable and accessible AI tools.
Companies and investors are funneling more and more resources into AI capabilities and innovation, as a means of being the frontrunner.
In late October, it was reported that DeepSeek had raised around $15bn in one of the country’s largest-ever private fundraising rounds. Among the investors was technology company Tencent and battery manufacturer Contemporary Amperex Technology.
Reportedly, the fundraising occurred ahead of DeepSeek’s initial public offering (IPO) preparations. The company could potentially file by the end of the year or early next year in 2027, according to some anonymous sources speaking to various publications.
Rival Anthropic filed for an IPO in June of this year, as did OpenAI one week later.
OpenAI was forced to halt its plans, however, amid AI safety concerns. CEO Sam Altman said that 2026 was no longer the right time to take the company public and that challenges relating to safety and how the industry interacts with governments needed to be addressed.
Meanwhile, Anthropic is nearing its IPO, which is expected to raise $100bn or more in one of the largest listings in history.
