# Corning opens new optical connectivity facility in Pune, commences operations

The Hindu · The Hindu Bureau · 2026-11-02

Corning on Tuesday (November 3, 2026), announced that its new optical connectivity manufacturing facility in Pune, India, has officially commenced operations. Co-located with Corning’s existing optical fiber plant, which was established in 2012, this new facility significantly expands the company’s manufacturing presence across the country.
The facility is dedicated to manufacturing high-performance optical connectivity products tailored specifically for data center and telecommunications network applications. The operational launch enhances localised supply chain capabilities and strengthens Corning’s overall network capacity.
Corning also outlined its Springboard Plan, under which, it targets to achieve an annualised sales run rate of $20 billion by the end of 2026, $30 billion by the end of 2028, and $40 billion by the end of 2030. To capitalise on rapid market shifts, Corning is focusing on major technological trends which include AI data infrastructure.
Corning is also engineering ultra-thin bendable glass capable of withstanding hundreds of thousands of folds. Currently, it claims to power over 8 billion devices worldwide with Gorilla Glass. It aims to grow on automotive sector as well by expanding its AutoGrade Gorilla Glass and optical in-vehicle networks for modern vehicles.
Corning has completed its 175th anniversary, and over 60 years of operations in India, where it has a domestic footprints including an optical fiber plant in Pune, a new Gorilla Glass finishing facility in Tamil Nadu, which will be operational in couple of months, and a pharmaceutical packaging in Telangana.
“With this facility now operational and additional capacity expected to come online in 2027, Corning is building on its established presence in India and enhancing its ability to serve customers,” said Sudhir Pillai, MD, India, Middle East, and Africa, Corning. “This announcement reflects our long-term commitment to India and to supporting the continued development of its digital infrastructure.”
