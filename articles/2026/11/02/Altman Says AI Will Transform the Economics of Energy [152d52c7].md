# Altman Says AI Will Transform the Economics of Energy

Bloomberg · 2026-11-02

AI “is going to transform the economics of the energy world,” OpenAI Chief Executive Officer Sam Altman said Monday at the Adipec conference in Abu Dhabi. The ChatGPT-maker’s signed deals to deploy its technology across energy companies, investment firms and government agencies across the United Arab Emirates, deepening ties as it seeks billions of dollars in funding from the country’s state-backed investors. (Source: Bloomberg)
