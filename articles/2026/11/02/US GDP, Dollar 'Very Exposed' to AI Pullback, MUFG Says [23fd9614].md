# US GDP, Dollar 'Very Exposed' to AI Pullback, MUFG Says

Bloomberg · 2026-11-02

Derek Halpenny, MUFG global markets research head, tells Bloomberg Television artificial intelligence is "everywhere" in GDP data in the United States. "The US is very exposed, very dependent to a continuation of the very positive story that we have on AI," he says, adding that the dollar is also vulnerable. (Source: Bloomberg)
