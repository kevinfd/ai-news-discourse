# Are you using generative AI to help decide who to vote for this election season?

WSLS 10 News · Miles Reese · 2026-11-02

Are you using generative AI to help decide who to vote for this election season?
AI has been a big conversation for quite some time now. It has even sparked a national conversation about its use when researching information about midterm election candidates. 
Some people have taken to social media and said that they are using ChatGPT to figure out who to vote for. Carole Tarrant, who’s the Communications Director for The League of Women Voters of the Greater Roanoke Valley, cautions against the use of ChatGPT in this way. 
 “Midterms are just too important to just spend five minutes and ask a chat: who should I vote for? The chats are not developed to really give you accurate information.”
Carole Tarrant, Communications Director for The League of Women Voters of the Greater Roanoke Valley
