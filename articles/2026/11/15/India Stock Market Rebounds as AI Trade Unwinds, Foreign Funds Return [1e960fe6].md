Bloomberg · Abhishek Vishnoi and Faseeh Mangi · 2026-11-15

Indian stocks suffered bruising losses in the first nine months of the year as the artificial intelligence trade hoovered up global investor capital. The unwinding of that strategy has turned the Asian nation’s equities into world beaters.
