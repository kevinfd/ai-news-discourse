# Firmus Plans to Raise $3 Billion After IPO Collapse, AFR Says

Bloomberg · Ishika Mookerjee · 2026-11-15

Firmus Grid Ltd. is planning a $3 billion private fundraising effort that will include new equity and debt issuance, as well as locking in pre-payments from customers including its backer Nvidia Corp. and Meta Platforms Inc., the Australian Financial Review reported.
