# How a Chinese Energy Company Is Outpacing the U.S. on AI Power Technology

The Information · Claudia Chong, Qianer Liu · 2026-11-15

Ulanqab, a city in China’s Inner Mongolia region, used to be known for its potatoes. Now it’s where a Chinese renewable energy firm is trying out a new way to plug in AI chips that shows China’s edge in the power systems that data centers need. Envision Energy has started using an 800-volt direct-current system to power computing workloads at its data center there, the Shanghai-based company said earlier this month.
