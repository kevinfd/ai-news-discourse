# [2026 Latest] Google's Proprietary 'TPU' Chip is Transforming the AI Semiconductor Market | Scenarios for the Collapse of Nvidia's Monopoly and the Intentions of Tech Giants_927

note · Whereverjp · 2026-11-05

[2026 Latest] Google's Proprietary 'TPU' Chip is Transforming the AI Semiconductor Market | Scenarios for the Collapse of Nvidia's Monopoly and the Intentions of Tech Giants_927
Until now, the star of the generative AI boom has been the GPU from Nvidia, which competes for the world's top market capitalization. However, Google's proprietary AI processor, the 'TPU (Tensor Processing Unit),' which it has been refining behind the scenes for over a decade, is now rapidly expanding its external sales and commercialization.
Surprisingly, even Nvidia's largest customers—OpenAI, Meta, and the rapidly growing Anthropic—are moving toward adopting Google's TPU.
In this article, we will explain in an easy-to-understand way why Google's TPU is being chosen so much right now, the movements of each tech giant, future massive revenue forecasts, and Nvidia's fierce defensive measures.
1. What is Google TPU? A Paradigm Shift from 10 Years of In-house Development to 'Accelerated External Sales'
From internal search and Gemini to public cloud and third-party data centers
Google's TPU is a dedicated ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) originally developed around 2015 to speed up and save power for Google's internal deep learning processes (search, voice recognition, image search, etc.). Since 2018, it has been opened to external users through 'Google Cloud,' but it was positioned strictly as something to be used within Google's cloud environment.
However, the tide has changed dramatically. By partnering with third-party data center operators like Fluidstack and Oracle, an unusual commercial expansion where 'TPU infrastructure operates and is provided outside of Google Cloud' is accelerating.
Why open up TPU to the outside now?
The biggest factors are the 'soaring costs of AI inference and training' and the 'semiconductor supply shortage.' As AI companies around the world scramble to secure computing resources, Google has shifted its internal infrastructure from a 'weapon for its own services' to an 'external sales business that generates massive revenue.'
2. Adopted by OpenAI and Meta? Reasons why major AI players choose TPU
Currently, the top runners in the AI industry are all moving to secure TPUs.
List of trends for Oracle, OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic
- Oracle 
  - As the first major hyperscaler other than Google, it has formed an alliance to provide Google TPU to customers through its own cloud and data center infrastructure.
- OpenAI 
  - Although OpenAI is Nvidia's largest customer and the core of the Microsoft alliance, it is proceeding with plans to purchase and use Google TPU to diversify its sources of computing resources.
- Meta 
  - Meta has signed a large-scale rental contract for TPUs from Google and is clearly moving to incorporate them into its model development, including the Llama series, and its inference infrastructure.
- Anthropic (Developer of Claude) 
  - In its IPO prospectus, it was revealed that in addition to a $114.5 billion spending plan for Google Cloud, it is estimating an additional $125 billion to $150 billion in spending for TPUs. Furthermore, it has committed $161.2 billion in spending to its co-development partner Broadcom, making it a leading member of the TPU camp.
3. Google's Revenue Explosion Brought by TPU | Toward a $138 Billion Market by 2028
Google's custom silicon strategy, which has invested massive research and development costs (CapEx) until now, is finally entering a full-scale recovery phase.
Revenue Forecast Timeline for 2026-2028
According to Anat Ashkenazi, CFO of Google (Alphabet), revenue from external TPU sales has begun to be recorded as of the most recent quarter, and she has indicated that a full-scale explosion in revenue is expected to occur from 2027 onwards.
Forecast data presented by John Blackledge, a senior analyst at major U.S. investment bank TD Cowen, has sent shockwaves through the market.
- Second half of 2026: 
  - Standalone TPU sales will reach $9.4 billion, accounting for approximately 13% of the total projected revenue for Google Cloud (approximately $70 billion).
- 2028: 
  - The TPU business will surge to a scale of $138 billion. It is expected to evolve into a monster business generating approximately 34% (more than one-third) of Google Cloud's revenue.
In Google's business model, which has relied on search-linked advertising, the external sale of cloud and hardware (proprietary silicon) is being established as its "second and third massive revenue pillars."
4. Nvidia's Counterattack | The "$3 Billion" Customer Lock-in Strategy Orchestrated by the Absolute King
In response to Google's offensive, the absolute king Nvidia, which holds approximately 80% of the AI accelerator market, is not sitting idly by.
Nvidia's Defensive Strategy: $3 Billion Investment and Capital Alliance with Meta
Nvidia's annual revenue forecast for the fiscal year ending January 2028 is expected to reach $692 billion, and it remains overwhelming in terms of market share.
However, to prevent customers from defecting to Google TPUs, AMD, and other third-party chips, Nvidia has launched a powerful counter. Most recently, it announced that Nvidia will invest $3 billion in the $97.5 billion massive stock offering being pursued by Meta. By not only acting as a chip supplier but also deeply involving itself in the customer's capital policy, it is deploying a lock-in strategy to strongly secure the continued adoption of its next-generation GPUs.
Summary: AI Infrastructure is Moving from "Single GPU Dependency" to the "Multi-Architecture Era"
The rapid growth of Google TPUs is not a passive choice made because "Nvidia GPUs are unavailable," but is beginning to be chosen by major tech companies as a result of "pursuing cost performance and power efficiency for specific workloads."
- Google: Creating $138 billion in massive hardware revenue by 2028 through the expansion of external TPU offerings
- Major AI Companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta): Actively using TPUs in combination to reduce costs and diversify GPU procurement risks
- Nvidia: Building a $690 billion defensive network armed with capital alliances and a robust software ecosystem (CUDA)
The competitive axis of AI development has now shifted from algorithm performance to "how low-cost and sustainable computing infrastructure can be secured." The battle for hegemony between Google TPUs and Nvidia is a critical theme that will directly impact future technology stock markets and the overall usage costs of AI services.
