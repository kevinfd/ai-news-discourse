# Nvidia’s Answer to Data Center Backlash: Retrofitting Apollo’s Old Phone Buildings

The Information · Kevin McLaughlin, Dakin Campbell, Phoebe Liu · 2026-11-05

Nvidia is looking for a faster way to get its chips turned on: old telecom company buildings. 

 The chip designer has approached private equity firm Apollo Global Management about creating a network of small AI data centers potentially spanning 20 states across the U.S., using existing buildings of a struggling telecom firm, according to two people with direct knowledge of the effort.
