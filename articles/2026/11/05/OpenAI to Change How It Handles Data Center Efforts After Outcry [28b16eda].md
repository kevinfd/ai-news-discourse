# OpenAI to Change How It Handles Data Center Efforts After Outcry

Bloomberg · Dina Bass · 2026-11-05

OpenAI said it will avoid nondisclosure agreements when it negotiates data center deals and ensure residents don’t pay for the electricity to power those projects, part of a framework the company believes should be adopted into laws governing the expansion of artificial intelligence.
