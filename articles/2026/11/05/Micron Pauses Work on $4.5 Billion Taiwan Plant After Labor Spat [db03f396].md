# Micron Pauses Work on $4.5 Billion Taiwan Plant After Labor Spat

Bloomberg · Debby Wu and Dina Bass · 2026-11-05

Micron Technology Inc. has suspended plans for a memory chip plant in Taiwan due to a protracted labor dispute, according to people familiar with the matter, throwing into question the fate of a major production base for crucial AI components.
