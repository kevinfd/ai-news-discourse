# Airbnb Founder Chesky Is Self-Funding His New AI Startup

The Information · Stephanie Palazzolo, Aaron Tilley, Jyoti Mann · 2026-11-05

Airbnb on Thursday shed new light on a secretive AI startup that founder and CEO Brian Chesky controls, saying the company has reached an agreement that could allow it to invest in the startup and license its technology. Chesky has so far self-funded the AI lab and plans to continue financing it, according to a person with knowledge of his plans.
