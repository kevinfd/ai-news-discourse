# California Sends AI Safety Lawmaker to Congress

The Information · Rocket Drew · 2026-11-05

Scott Wiener , the California politician who led the successful effort to pass AI safety legislation in the state’s legislature last year, is headed to Washington. The state senator won Tuesday’s election to succeed long-time House Speaker Nancy Pelosi , after his opponent conceded on Wednesday.  When Wiener arrives in D.C., he will be among the few members of Congress to have authored significant AI legislation.
