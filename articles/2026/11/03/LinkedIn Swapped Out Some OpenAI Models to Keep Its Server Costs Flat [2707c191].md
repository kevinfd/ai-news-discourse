# LinkedIn Swapped Out Some OpenAI Models to Keep Its Server Costs Flat

The Information · Aaron Holmes · 2026-11-03

The more people use AI features in apps, the more the companies selling those apps have to spend on servers to run them , potentially squeezing their profit margins. But some firms have found ways to keep costs in check. For instance, Microsoft-owned LinkedIn says it has flattened the cost of running its AI-powered Hiring Assistant, which uses large language models to automate candidate search and outreach for recruiters, even as usage of the tool has steadily increased in the past year.
