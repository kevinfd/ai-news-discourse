# ElevenLabs CEO Says He'd 'Love to IPO'

Bloomberg · 2026-11-03

"IPO of course is not an end in itself," Staniszewski says during an interview with Tom Mackenzie at Bloomberg Tech in London. "It's a step in the journey." (Source: Bloomberg)
