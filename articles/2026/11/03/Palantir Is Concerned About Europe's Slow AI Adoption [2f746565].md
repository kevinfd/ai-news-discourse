# Palantir Is Concerned About Europe's Slow AI Adoption

Bloomberg · 2026-11-03

Louis Mosley, who oversees Palantir's business in the UK and Europe, says, “blue chips on this side of the Atlantic are not yet adopting AI." He made the comments in a conversation with Tom Giles at Bloomberg Tech in London. "That worries me because we’re starting to see extraordinary results from those customers, those big American blue chips,” he adds. (Source: Bloomberg)
