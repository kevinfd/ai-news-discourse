# Quili AI

A TOWN BECOMES AI FOR A DAY IN A BID TO SAVE ITS WATER.
				
AN INITATIVE BY FUNDACIÓN AVINA AND CORPORACIÓN NGEN
			
			In the early 2010s, Quilicura began to host one of the largest
				concentrations of data centers dedicated to artificial intelligence.
				These use the region's water as a cooling method.
			
		
				
					The initiative helped dimensionalize a complex problem, and helped
					show the world the real people affected by the the irresponsible use
					of A.I. and the hidden cost it has on local water resources.
				
				
			
On JAN 31st from 8 AM to 8 PM: more than 40 people from Quilicura managed to answer more than 25,000 prompts.
Why are hot dog buns sold in packs of 8 and sausages in packs of 12? please make an image of a sloth playing in the snow. what is your best cake recipe? Hi! This is my dog, he's a good boy. Could you please draw a picture oh him? How do I keep my hand from falling asleep while playing VIDEO games?
Each chat lasted an average of 11’ 4
Chatting with more than 68 countries around the world that supported the project.
WAS THE GLOBAL REACH IN JUST ONE DAY.
The initiative managed to reach more than 1 billion people in the world.
Thank you for helping us save our water.
