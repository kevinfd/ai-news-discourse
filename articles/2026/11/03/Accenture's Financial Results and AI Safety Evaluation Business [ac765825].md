# Accenture's Financial Results and AI Safety Evaluation Business

note · 世界経済ノート｜仕事に活きる海外ニュース · 2026-11-03

Accenture's Financial Results and AI Safety Evaluation Business
As of October 5, 2026 / This article is based on public materials available at the time. The outlook and future schedules mentioned in the text are also based on information from that time.
Three Key Points
- Accenture's annual revenue is approximately $74.2 billion. Distinguish between figures with and without currency adjustments.
- The projected investment in AI evaluation is not guaranteed revenue.
- Consider the efficiency of implementation alongside the independence and accountability of evaluation.
As AI spreads throughout companies, how will the work of the firms supporting its implementation change? The overseas company feature for the morning of 2026-10-05 focuses on Accenture. We will examine the current state of the business based on the fiscal 2026 results announced on 2026-10-01 and the AI safety evaluation partnership announced on 2026-09-18.
Understanding the Company's Business
The company supports corporate system upgrades, business process reviews, and daily operations. According to its financial reports, it has approximately 814,000 employees. Its clients include those in the finance, manufacturing, retail, and public sectors. To make AI usable, it is necessary to organize data, connect it to existing systems, and change on-site workflows. Taking on the execution of these tasks is the entry point to understanding the company's business. [S26][S28]
Starting with the Financial Figures
First, let's look at the confirmed figures. For the full fiscal year 2026, the 12 months ending 2026-08-31, revenue was approximately $74.2 billion, a 6% increase from the previous year. On a local currency basis, excluding the impact of exchange rate fluctuations, it was a 5% increase. The operating margin based on U.S. GAAP for the same period was 15.4%. The company's own adjusted metric, which excludes business optimization costs, was 15.8%. Since the calculation premises for these two differ, they must be read separately. [S26]
For the most recent quarter, June to August 2026, revenue was approximately $18.7 billion, and new bookings were approximately $22.2 billion. [S26] Bookings are a clue to thinking about future work. However, the contracted amount does not all become revenue in that quarter. Even if large contracts increase, it is important to verify the timing of the work and how it translates into earnings.
Customer Demand as Described by the Company
At the earnings briefing, the company explained that much of its growth is coming from building data foundations and enterprise AI mechanisms for clients. On the other hand, it also stated that there has been no major change in the overall demand environment, which includes discretionary spending by clients. This explanation suggests that rather than corporate IT spending accelerating across the board, the company is winning projects from clients who are selectively investing in necessary areas. This is a perspective based on the company's explanation. [S28]
Work Expanding into AI Safety Evaluation
Another development is the partnership with AI development company Anthropic. Led by Accenture's subsidiary Faculty, the plan is to proceed with model evaluation, stress testing to find weaknesses, and verification of safety measures. Both companies indicated that they expect to invest at least $1 billion each in building capabilities in this area over the next five years. This is a future investment plan and does not mean that the same amount of revenue is guaranteed to Accenture. [S27]
Anthropic has described a concept of giving evaluators access similar to that of employees. At the same time, it has also specified that common standards for information that can be viewed and how results are reported have not yet been established. For the time being, Anthropic will directly bear the costs of the evaluation work. In addition to the effectiveness of the evaluation, how to ensure independence is a point we will want to confirm in the future. [S27]
Two Points to Verify Next
From here on, these are considerations for reading the business. As AI usage spreads, there may be a demand for post-implementation inspections and safety verification. For a company that knows the corporate front lines, there is room to expand the scope of its support. On the other hand, if AI speeds up work, the revenue structure of businesses based on human labor hours could change. It will likely become important not just to increase bookings, but to reflect the value provided in the pricing.
The company's revenue growth forecast for fiscal 2027 is 3-6% on a local currency basis. [S26] This is a projection, not a result. Moving forward, we will track whether bookings translate into revenue and profit, and whether the safety evaluation mechanism takes concrete shape. Following these two points will make it easier to grasp how the spread of AI is changing the business of service companies.
Things to Check at Work
For AI implementation projects, determine the evaluators, the scope of information access, cost allocation, and the reporting destination for results. Investment plans and order/sales performance should be verified separately.
