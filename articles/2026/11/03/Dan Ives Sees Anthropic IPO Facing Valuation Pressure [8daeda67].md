# Dan Ives Sees Anthropic IPO Facing Valuation Pressure

Bloomberg · 2026-11-03

He examines capital raised by hyperscalers, calling it the “start of winners and losers” in the AI trade and says chip stocks have about another 10% - 12% to fall. He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
