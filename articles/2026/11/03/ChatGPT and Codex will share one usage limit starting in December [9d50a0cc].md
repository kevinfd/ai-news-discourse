# ChatGPT and Codex will share one usage limit starting in December

Notebookcheck · 2026-11-03

Starting in December, ChatGPT and Codex will draw from a single usage allowance on Plus, Pro and Business plans. Usage is measured in tokens, so advanced models and agent tasks cost more. Regular chats with Instant do not count.
