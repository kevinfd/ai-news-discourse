# Uber’s Jill Hazelbaker to Join OpenAI as Chief Corporate Affairs Officer

The Information · Jemima McEvoy, Jessica E. Lessin · 2026-11-03

Uber ’s President Jill Hazelbaker is joining OpenAI as its chief corporate affairs officer, filling a role that has been empty since the start of the year. 

 Hazelbaker, who is also a veteran of Google and Snap, will be responsible for global communications and brand strategy. She will attempt to stabilize a team that has had significant turnover in its senior management in recent years and a time when market jitters ahead of a potential IPO are mounting.
