# AI Could Transform How Drones Identify Sharks At Australian Beaches

Forbes · Melissa Cristina Márquez, Contributor · 2026-11-03

Drones are giving beach managers a powerful, non-lethal way to watch for sharks, but a new study from New South Wales reveals a surprising problem: when pilots identify sharks in real time, they can mistake other sharks and even fish for the species considered most concerning.
