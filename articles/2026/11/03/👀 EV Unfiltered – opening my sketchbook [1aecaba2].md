# 👀 EV Unfiltered – opening my sketchbook

Exponential View · Azeem Azhar · 2026-11-03

Most of my ideas turn into sketches, research notes or brief videos for the team. Some of them finally make it into or . But most — because of the time it takes to polish them — simply remain internal as tools to push our thinking forward. We’ve been wondering just how valuable it would be for readers to peek at the artist’s sketchbook, even in its raw, unfiltered form. The advantage is that you’d get to see things I think are important but, for whatever reason, we don’t finish or publish. Maybe they go out late, much, much later, or they don’t survive the cut.
