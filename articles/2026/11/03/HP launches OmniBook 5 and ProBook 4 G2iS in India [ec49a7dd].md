# HP launches OmniBook 5 and ProBook 4 G2iS in India

The Hindu · The Hindu Bureau · 2026-11-03

HP on Tuesday (November 3, 2026) launched the OmniBook 5 and HP ProBook 4 G2iS laptops in India. Designed for flexible learning and hybrid working, the AI-ready devices feature Intel Core 7 processors, integrated NPU capabilities, and OLED displays.
The student-focused HP OmniBook 5 features an 11.77mm aluminum chassis weighing 1.16kg. Powered by up to an Intel Core 7 processor with a 16 TOPS NPU, it offers a cinematic OLED display with a 90 percent screen-to-body ratio and up to 42 hours of local video playback battery life.
Aimed at business professionals, the HP ProBook 4 G2iS features a 12mm thin profile weighing 1.18kg. It packs up to an Intel Core 7 processor with a 17 TOPS NPU, 32GB RAM, and 1TB storage. It sports a 14-inch 2.8K OLED screen, 70Whr battery, AI Noise Reduction, and HP Drive Lock.
The HP OmniBook 5 14 is open for pre-booking until November 10 on the HP Online and HP World Stores starting at ₹1,09,999. It comes in Smoke Silver, Starlit Blue, Peony Pink, and Soft Honeydew options. Meanwhile, the HP ProBook 4 G2iS 14 starts at ₹86,000 plus taxes, available starting November 6.
