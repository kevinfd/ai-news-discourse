# China's DeepSeek Adds Multiple Industrial, Financial Investors in New Fundraiser

Yicai Global · Liu Xiaojie · 2026-11-20

China’s DeepSeek Swells Investor Base in Second Fundraiser
DATE:  4 hours ago
/ SOURCE:  Yicai
(Yicai) Nov. 20 -- DeepSeek’s long-awaited second external fundraiser has greatly expanded the Chinese artificial intelligence developer’s investor base, bringing in more major technology companies, industrial groups, and venture capital firms as its backers.
Investors linked with Tencent, Contemporary Amperex Technology, China Mobile, China Telecom, and Xiaohongshu, as well as financiers like Monolith Capital, Boyu Capital, IDG Capital, Gaorong Capital, and Legend Capital have backed Hangzhou Chengyan Enterprise Management Consulting Partnership, the investment vehicle for the Series B fundraiser with a 17.3 percent stake in DeepSeek after the round, according to data from corporate information site Qichacha.
In addition, the registered capital of Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research, the AI startup’s operating entity, rose to CNY19.9 million from around CNY16.5 million (USD3 million from USD2.5 million).
The corporate information changes do not provide information on the amount raised. Media reports have said DeepSeek initially targeted CNY50 billion (USD7.5 billion), then doubled that to roughly CNY100 billion on strong investor demand. The round reportedly closed at about CNY105.5 billion, giving DeepSeek a post-money valuation of around CNY615.5 billion (USD92 billion), making it the largest venture financing ever secured by a Chinese AI firm.
Hangzhou-based DeepSeek has yet to announce any details about the funding round, including the amount raised.
DeepSeek's Series A fundraiser closed in June, raising roughly CNY50 billion. Its backers then included founder Liang Wenfeng, major tech companies such as Tencent, CATL, and NetEase, along with IDG Capital and China’s national AI fund.
In less than a year, DeepSeek has gone from being a geek team driven by tech idealism into an AI unicorn potentially valued at more than CNY600 billion and backed by a broad cross-section of Chinese businesses and investors.
Entities affiliated with Tencent and three investment funds backed by CATL each pitched in about 9.6 percent to the funding round, ranking as the largest contributors among Hangzhou Chengyan's shareholders. The pair were also key external investors in the Series A fundraiser.
The third- to fifth-largest contributors were China Mobile and an entity under Citic Securities, each adding around 7.7percent, followed by Monolith at 7.2 percent.
The funding round also drew participation from entities linked to internet giants such as Meituan, NetEase, and Trip.Com; businesses in the telecoms, devices, and manufacturing sectors like Vivo, Midea, Geely, Luxshare Precision, and GigaDevice; as well as VC firms CPE Yuanfeng, Loyal Valley Capital, and Shixiang Capital.
The privately held company stormed onto the global AI stage in January last year with the release of its ChatGPT-style R1 large language model, which paired strong reasoning performance with dramatically lower running costs, in what Silicon Valley venture capitalist Marc Andreessen famously described as an “AI Sputnik moment.”
With capital and resources now largely in place, the market’s attention is turning to how DeepSeek can maintain its technological edge while delivering commercial results for its investors that are commensurate with its valuation.
