# Former SEC Chairman Gary Gensler Says AI Is “Already a Bubble,” Set to Pop and Cause Economic Catastrophe

Futurism · Joe Wilkins · 2026-11-20

A year ago, you’d be hard pressed to get any major financial authority to admit that AI investments were a bubble, let alone one who’d use the “R” word: “recession.” Yet late in 2026, it seems the writing’s on the wall, as even former chairman of the US Securities and Exchange Commission Gary Gensler is now warning of impending financial catastrophe.
In an unsettling interview with CNN, the former Biden administration official said that AI is “already a bubble.”
“We’ve had many transformative technologies over centuries, and what our capital markets do is they fund the infrastructure in anticipation of revenues, but the revenues come later,” Gensler explained. “The 1920s with electricity, the 1990s with the internet… you come to a point where it plateaus, it retrenches.”
While some commentators maintain that the tech industry can handle the runaway investments in AI, Gensler sees some kind of crash as the only way out.
“The only question is if it’s a soft recession or something more dramatic,” the former SEC chair explained. “I think that’s going to start to happen before the election in 2028.”
Asked what regular people should be doing with this information, Gensler likened the coming market downturn to a distant storm. Though he can’t say for certain when the storm will arrive or how severe it will be when it does, Gensler said he “would be battening down the hatches and making sure you have some proper rain gear.”
Gensler is far from alone in forecasting some rough weather ahead. Earlier this week, managing director of the International Monetary Fund Kristalina Georgieva likened the world economy to something that is “standing on one leg, on the AI leg.”
“If this leg wobbles, that can lead to financial stability troubles,” Georgieva warned.
