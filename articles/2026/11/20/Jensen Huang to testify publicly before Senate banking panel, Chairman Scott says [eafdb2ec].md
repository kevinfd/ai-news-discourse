# Jensen Huang to testify publicly before Senate banking panel, Chairman Scott says

CNBC · Justin Papp · 2026-11-20

Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang will testify publicly when he appears before the Senate banking panel, a spokesperson for Committee Chair Tim Scott said Friday.
The South Carolina Republican confirmed earlier this week that Huang had agreed to appear, but the details of that appearance remained unclear. Huang, who resigned from the company he co-founded earlier this month over a series of extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy, also reached an agreement with three House committees to appear for a closed-door, transcribed interview on Dec. 1.
The date of Huang's public testimony in the Senate has not yet been announced, though Scott initially requested he appear on Dec. 3.
His office did not respond when asked when Huang would appear. Scott told CNBC on Wednesday he hopes to hear testimony from Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick "before Christmas." The new Congress, when Sen. Elizabeth Warren, D-Mass., is expected to take over as Senate banking chair, convenes Jan. 3.
