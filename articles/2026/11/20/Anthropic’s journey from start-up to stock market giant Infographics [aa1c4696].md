# Anthropic’s journey from start-up to stock market giant | Infographics

The Hindu · The Hindu Bureau · 2026-11-20

Anthropic shares surged 17% in their record Nasdaq debut on Thursday (November 19, 2026), defying a broader Wall Street sell-off as investors backed the artificial intelligence (AI) giant despite concerns over the Nvidia spy scandal and a potential tech bubble.
The shares closed at around $97, up from the IPO price of $83 set on Wednesday (November 18), which was already above the company’s expected price range. They opened at $111.50, briefly valuing the maker of the Claude chatbot at nearly $2 trillion.
The strong debut came on a turbulent day for U.S. markets, with both the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite and the broader S&P 500 falling more than 2%.
Anthropic raised a record $81.3 billion through the offering. Its IPO price valued the company at approximately $1.5 trillion, including employee stock options and restricted shares. Its market valuation rose to about $1.75 trillion at the close.
