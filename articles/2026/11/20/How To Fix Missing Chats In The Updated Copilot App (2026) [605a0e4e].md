# How To Fix Missing Chats In The Updated Copilot App (2026)

The Droid Guy · The Droid Guy · 2026-11-20

How To Fix Missing Chats In The Updated Copilot App (2026)
If your old chats or projects are missing after the Copilot app updated, Microsoft says the move to the updated app may still be in progress.
Microsoft has set a deadline: you must sign in to the updated Copilot app by December 31, 2026 to keep eligible chats and content.
This guide follows Microsoft’s troubleshooting page for each situation, from missing chats to sign-in problems.
Quick answer: why content is missing
Sign in before December 31, 2026
Microsoft says content that has not been migrated by December 31, 2026 will be permanently deleted.
That makes signing in to the updated app the single most important step if you still want your history.
Accounts linked with several personal emails are merged into the updated app through your Microsoft account, and Microsoft says no action is needed for that.
Sign in with the same account you used before
The first check Microsoft gives is to confirm you are signed in with the same personal Microsoft, Google or Apple account.
If you used Google or Apple before, select the matching Continue with button on the sign-in page.
Signing in with a different method can look like an empty history.
Restart Copilot and check again
- Quit the Copilot app completely and open it again.
- On the web, refresh the page.
- Follow any message in the app, because it may link to your existing chats.
- Wait and check again later, since migration time depends on how much content you have.
Microsoft says migration time varies with the volume and type of content.
Only some of your content is missing
Microsoft says certain retired features do not carry over to the updated app.
Microsoft’s page gives a garbled end date for Podcasts and Deep Research, so check the page for the exact date.
Find your projects under Notebooks
Projects were renamed Notebooks in the updated app.
Open the left navigation pane in the Copilot app to find them.
Files you shared or generated in Copilot are stored in OneDrive, and you can manage them in the OneDrive app or by signing in to your Microsoft account.
Microsoft notes that more storage is available with a Microsoft 365 subscription.
Different chats on each device
- Confirm the same personal account is used on every device.
- Close and reopen Copilot on each device, or refresh the web page.
- Give the move time to finish and sync, then check again.
A “coming soon” message means Microsoft says chats are still being moved, so the same waiting advice applies.
You have more than one personal account
Microsoft says accounts may move at different times.
Microsoft gives a way to check where an account stands.
- Open a new private browsing session.
- Go to copilot.microsoft.com and sign in with the account you want to check.
- If you are sent to copilot.com, the account has moved or is finishing the move.
- If you stay on copilot.microsoft.com, keep using Copilot there, and the account will move later.
Personal and work or school accounts stay separate, and you can use the account switcher to change between them.
Why you are asked to install a new app
Microsoft says that on some devices the updated experience is a new app rather than an update.
You may see a prompt to install the Copilot app, or the store may show Get or Install instead of Update.
If Copilot is not on your Windows PC at all, Microsoft says to get it from the Microsoft Store, and new Windows 11 PCs typically include it already.
Ways to open Copilot on Windows
Microsoft says the key and shortcut can open the full app or a smaller quick view.
To change that, go to Account > Settings > Copilot Keyboard Shortcuts inside the app.
If Copilot will not connect
Microsoft offers a connectivity troubleshooter in the Get Help app for people who have difficulty connecting.
- Open the Get Help app.
- Search for Copilot connectivity troubleshooter.
- Follow the steps it gives you.
It checks for firewall rules or other blockers to your connection.
Report a problem that stays unresolved
Select the ellipsis in the upper-right corner of the app, then Send feedback, then Report a problem.
Microsoft asks you to include when the problem started, your device and operating system, and a screenshot.
If you would rather clear what Copilot stores, see how to delete Copilot history and turn off memory.
For the Windows side of Copilot, our guide to removing or disabling Copilot on Windows 11 may help.
