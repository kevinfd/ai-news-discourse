# Wall Street Reels as AI Fears Erupt; Nasdaq 100 Near Bear Market

Bloomberg · Judy Lagrou and Geoffrey Morgan · 2026-11-20

Wall Street reeled on Friday as nagging concerns about the AI trade, which propelled US stocks from one high to the next for much of the year, turned into a full-blown, market-wide selloff. Anthropic PBC slumped after soaring in its public-market debut, while Nvidia Corp. sank 14%, pushing the Nasdaq 100 to the brink of a bear market.
