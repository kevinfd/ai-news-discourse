Is Anthropic really worth $1.3 trillion? - The Latest
The AI firm and Claude chatbot maker Anthropic has made a historic stock market debut, with a peak valuation of $1.3tn on its first day of trading. It marks a remarkable ascent for a start-up launched in just 2021 by a group of ex-OpenAI staffers. Anthropic’s CEO, Dario Amodei, said the record-breaking IPO had ‘strengthened’ the company’s resolve to ‘do the right thing’.
