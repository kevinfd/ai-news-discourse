# US Credit-Fear Hits 8-Month High as Tech Angst Roils Markets

Bloomberg · Caleb Mutua and Emily Graffeo · 2026-11-20

Measures of perceived US credit risk reached levels not seen in nearly eight months and the cost of protecting the debt of some of the biggest tech firms against default jumped on Friday, as the wobbling AI trade spurred volatility in global markets and sent investors piling into safe havens.
