# Hindlian Advisory CEO on AI & Anthropic Market Sell-off

Bloomberg · 2026-11-20

Amanda Hindlian, Founder and CEO of Hindlian Advisory and Former Global Head of Capital Markets at the New York Stock Exchange, says Anthropic’s IPO should be judged separately from a volatile week for markets. She notes that investors had been uncertain about the pricing and overall execution just two weeks earlier, but the offering priced well, drew a significantly oversubscribed book—estimated at five to seven times—and traded strongly on its first day. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
