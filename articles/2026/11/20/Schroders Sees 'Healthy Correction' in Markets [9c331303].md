# Schroders Sees 'Healthy Correction' in Markets

Bloomberg · 2026-11-20

Remi Olu-Pitan, multi-asset growth and income head at Schroders, says the selloff in equities is a “healthy correction” as a gauge of stocks heads for its longest losing streak in more than three years. The market is reassessing how far a lot of the artificial intelligence companies can go, Olu-Pitan tells Bloomberg Television. “European equities are actually in solid position,” she adds. (Source: Bloomberg)
