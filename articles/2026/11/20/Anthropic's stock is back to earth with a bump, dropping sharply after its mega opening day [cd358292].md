# Anthropic's stock is back to earth with a bump, dropping sharply after its mega opening day

Business Insider · Pete Syme, Pete Syme (psyme@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-20

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei arrives at the Nasdaq on Thursday. Adam Gray/Bloomberg via Getty Images Anthropic shares are back to earth with a bump on Friday. As of 7:55 a.m. ET, the stock was around 9% lower in premarket trading. After going public, it soared 34% to $111.50 a share when trading began on Thursday. Anthropic stock is set to open sharply lower the day after its blockbuster initial public offering. As of 7:55 a.m. ET, the stock was at $88.21 in premarket trading, down roughly 9% from its $97.17 Thursday closing price. That would wipe more than $100 billion from the AI lab's valuation. As the first AI lab to go public, Anthropic is widely seen as a bellwether for the broader industry. Anthropic's IPO was hyped for months and only grew in stature as it carried hopes of reviving the bull market. The tech industry has been shaken due to concerns about the scale of AI investment and Nvidia's leadership crisis. Anthropic's share price soared on Thursday as investors piled in for a piece of the biggest ever IPO. It went public at $83 a share — above its previously provided range — before soaring 34% to $111.50 a share when trading began. That meant it debuted with a valuation around $2 trillion, temporarily making Anthropic the world's sixth-most valuable company. The share price fell in the afternoon but still closed 17% higher on the day. With a valuation of $1.76 trillion on a fully diluted basis, it dropped below SpaceX to become the seventh-most valuable company. That's set to drop further when the market opens Friday morning, falling to around $1.6 trillion, or roughly $1.15 trillion on an undiluted basis. Read the original article on Business Insider
