# No spy. No temptress. The Nvidia love affair is called RICO

Greg Palast · MilnerRoute · 2026-11-20

Oh, pa-lease. The so-called “sex-pionage” scandal is a brilliant cover-up.
Until last week, Jensen Huang was CEO of Nvidia, sole maker of Blackwell, the advanced AI chip the planet is salivating for. He was forced to resign, over a dalliance with a woman he supposedly didn’t know was a spy for Chinese intelligence.
If you swallow that, I’ve got a bridge in Brooklyn to sell you.
Huang’s publicly acknowledged playtime with his Chinese unfortunate cookie, for which he loudly apologized to his wife and co-workers, was a fake-out to hide their real relationship: all business, not monkey business. And their business was criminal, on a scale Al Capone could never imagine.
Let this old gumshoe lay out the only story that makes sense. Huang’s “femme fatale” is no “spy.” She’s something more dangerous: evidence suggests she’s Huang’s business conduit to the Chinese military industrial complex. Doubt me? Then tell me, where are the chips?
Memo to AG Letitia James: Secret Sex is a great cover for racketeering
RICO stands for Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations Act (18 U.S.C. §§ 1961–1968). The law was clearly written for the Huang and Huang hook-up.
Many centuries ago, or so it seems, I advised my client, the Attorney General of New York, on corporate crimes. I wrote one memo recommending a RICO (racketeering) action that ultimately won a $4.8 billion jury verdict for the public.
So, despite my insistence on a quiet retirement, I am today writing the Attorney General, now an office held by Letitia James, because she has the legal jurisdiction, and the cojones, to bring a RICO action against Jensen Huang, the Nvidia Board and their enabler, co-conspirator Donald Trump.
So, here I am again. This time recommending to New York Attorney General Letitia James that she file both civil and criminal RICO complaints against Huang, the Nvidia Board of Directors and their enablers in the Executive Branch.
Here are the facts as I present them in the action memo to the AG:
- In 2023, Huang’s Chinese “girlfriend,” Alice Huang (no family connection) took over a dinky Singapore cloud computing company called Megaspeed. Megaspeed wasn’t very mega; its assets totaled only $33 million. Yet Nvidia sold it a breathtaking $7.3 billion in chips. Say what? How did Nvidia expect teeny Megaspeed to pay? And why was Megaspeed prioritized over AI bigs like Sam Altman’s Open AI and Elon Musk’s Grok, giants pleading for the chips?
- The Wall Street Journal’s stellar reporting found that, in 2025, Nvidia reported Singapore bought 22% of its worldwide output. Why flood little Singapore with one in five of the planet’s super-chips?
- And why, in 2026, did Nvidia simply stop reporting sales to Singapore. If there’s nothing wrong with sales, apparently almost all to Megaspeed, then why cover it up?
Here is the likely story: Alice Huang was not spying on him. Jensen Huang was working with her as a conduit to China’s leadership, arranging the secret handoff of roughly one in five Nvidia chips.
- We begin with this: Alice was introduced to Jensen by — ready for this? — HER HUSBAND, who weirdly appeared to publicly condone their trysts. Maybe “husband” deserves quote marks: “Husband” Kang Wei received $9 billion from the Chinese government to build data centers, and his “wife” then purchased $7.5 billion in Nvidia chips.
Did wify’s chips end up in China? The cover story: some ended up in a yet-to-be completed data center in Malaysia. Even that smells of violating US law by using an offshore data center under the apparent control of Chinese users. That would be a violation of U.S. Export Administration regulations. That’s a felonious workaround and, if Jensen knew it, a criminal denial of KYC (Know Your Customer) regulations.
Why did the two Huang’s make the affair so obvious? If she’s a spy, why flaunt it before TV cameras and paparazzi? Because Huang and Huang wanted you to know. I suspect their time alone was spent on how to move the contraband processors and get Nvidia paid — a lot of accounting, leaving little time for tickle and spank.
Think about this. If Jensen and this guy’s wife are conspiring to move hot chips to China, they can’t exactly talk about it over the phone or in emails. So, they agree to meet-up in various hotel rooms during business conferences, doors locked for the — wink, wink, “discussions.”
Then, as inevitable, they’re busted. Turns out, last year, both the US Treasury and State Department wanted Megaspeed put on the “entity” list which would bar Nvidia from slipping their processors to the company.
The pair of lovebird-racketeers assume that, sooner or later, the Feds will kick in their hotel room door, figuratively if not literally. And there the Feds find the Chinese operative and billionaire greedster together under the (spread)sheets. What do you do? You jump into bed and yell, “WE WEREN’T COMMITTING A BLACK-MARKET DEAL THAT THREATENS ALL LIFE ON OUR PLANET, WE WERE JUST CHEATING ON OUR SPOUSES!”
Sure. Huang’s billionaire tech-bro’s bought it, saying the poor man was “ensnared” by the wily Oriental temptress. And that douche-y leather jacket Jensen wears makes it all-the-more believable.
The media ate it up like buttered popcorn. Look, I’ve been in newsrooms and I can tell you that there’s nothing a news chief loves more than a story of a celebrity penis gone astray. Why spend network cash on an investigation?
Bored of Directing?
And may we talk about Nvidia’s Board of Directors? A very uncurious lot. Did they not wonder where one in five dollars of revenue was coming from? In fact, in a blaring admission of guilty minds, the company blocked all sales to Megaspeed last year after it became known that the US Treasury was getting suspicious.
Yet, did Nvidia’s board even once ask Jensen about the Singapore chips? And, are we to believe that the man called, “the genius of a generation” couldn’t figure out where a fifth of his chips were going?
- Uh, oh. This screams of another federal crime: “Willful blindness” or “conscious avoidance” of knowing of a crime. This applies to Jensen’s pillow talk. Did he never ask, “Honey, my company wants to know where $7.5 billion of our chips are going and who is giving you the money to pay for them”?
Jensen can’t say, “I never asked.” Vinnie the Chin Gigante, mob boss, tried pleading ignorance to the bullets the Chin’s lieutenants were putting into troublesome enemies. Gigante pleaded what became known as the “Ostrich Defense.” Gigante still ended up in prison.
And that explains, despite all the moaning and groaning from Huang aficionados, why the Nvidia board fired Jensen’s ass. I mean, he made billionaires of these ungrateful fools.
- Maybe it was because, if they did not fire Huang, the Board members themselves face RICO charges under the “willful blindness” doctrine. Civil penalties would require each, personally, to cough up their ill-gotten gain from the China conspiracy. Billionaires no more.
The Nvidia board threw Jensen overboard but carefully avoided the missing chip problem by citing his penile intrusions into an employee and a supplier. The board thinks you’ll buy that, right? The US media did.
Justice Department?
In days of yore, I directed a major racketeering investigation for the US Justice Department because, I assumed, Justice and their G-men went after bad guys.
So where is the Justice Department today; where is the FBI, Mr. Kash Patel?
Let us recall that, in 2025, President Trump called on Nvidia to remove Jensen because of his China ties. But then, the crafty CEO slithered into the Oval Office with a check for $25 million for Trump’s ballroom and a still-hidden deal to kick back to the US 15% to 25% on sales to China. To get that slice of the China pie, Trump would, of course, have to lift the restriction on sales to China.
Trump did that, allowing the sale to China of Nvidia’s H200 chips, kind of like Blackwell Lite processors…
- …but only after Trump bought Nvidia stock, which then shot up. His holdings in Nvidia are now worth about $62 million.
Then there’s Trump’s little boys with their “1789” investment fund. They’ve put roughly a third of the fund’s billions into projects financially connected to Nvidia, investments now juiced by Trump’s pro-Nvidia diktats.
This is what RICO Act was designed for, conspiracies between Racketeer Influenced Corrupt Organizations: Nvidia, Megaspeed, the Oval Office.
Madame Attorney General, remind your agents not to forget to read the President his rights.
