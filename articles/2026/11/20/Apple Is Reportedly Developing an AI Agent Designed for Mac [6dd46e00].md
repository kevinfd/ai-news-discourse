# Apple Is Reportedly Developing an AI Agent Designed for Mac

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-11-20

Apple may soon have an AI agent of its own. Plans for such a product haven’t been officially announced, but Bloomberg recently spotted a job listing that all but confirmed its development.
“People already do their most valuable work by moving fluidly between tools: a browser, a document, a terminal, an editor, a service. We’re building AI agents that can do the same,” Apple wrote in the now-deleted job listing for a senior engineering manager.
Apple computers are popular among those experimenting with AI agents, but the company currently doesn’t have a competing product of its own. According to the listing, Apple hopes to create a tool that “browses the web, works across apps, runs commands, writes and debugs code, and calls services,” while choosing “the right tool for each step.”
Meta’s Muse, OpenAI’s Dots, and Anthropic’s Personal Computer can already handle a few basic tasks on Macs. Google also rolled out a computer-use feature in the Gemini app for Mac this week, while Anthropic is testing Claude Duet, a proactive agent that runs in the background for users on mobile.
News about Apple’s job listing arrives a couple of weeks after Bloomberg reported that CEO John Ternus was growing frustrated with the company’s lack of urgency toward AI development and asked Craig Federighi, Apple’s software chief, to accelerate projects and make Siri more agentic next year.
The listing also comes after the company announced it would roll out new guardrails in macOS 27.3 to warn users about the risks of granting full-disk access to AI apps. With the same update, Apple will also let third-party developers integrate Siri AI into their apps.
