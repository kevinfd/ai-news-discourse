- At $1.8 trillion, Anthropic looks priced for perfection. But its customer economics suggest the story may be more interesting than the price tag.
- Claude's roughly 900% net revenue retention suggests that landing a customer may be just the beginning of a much bigger revenue opportunity.
- More than half the Fortune 500 already uses Claude, yet spends less than 0.1% of total expenses on it, on average. Adoption and saturation are two very different things.
- My projections suggest Anthropic could approach $275 billion in revenue by 2028 even with customer expansion slowing dramatically from today's extraordinary levels.
- At roughly 6.4x projected 2028 sales, Anthropic's valuation starts looking less absurd. I remain bullish that its existing customers may have barely scratched the surface of their spending potential.
