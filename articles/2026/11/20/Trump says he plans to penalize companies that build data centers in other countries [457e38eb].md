# Trump says he plans to penalize companies that build data centers in other countries

ABC News · ABC News, The Associated Press · 2026-11-20

Trump says he plans to penalize companies that build data centers in other countries
President Donald Trump says U.S. tech companies will face “a tremendous tax” if they build artificial intelligence data centers in other countries
WASHINGTON -- President Donald Trump says U.S. tech companies will face “a tremendous tax” if they build artificial intelligence data centers in other countries.
Trump did not provide details Friday and he said he hadn’t yet told his own officials about his plan. The Republican president urged Americans to welcome data center construction in their communities and extract concessions from the developers, repeating a point he made before the midterms.
Trump says, “You ought to take advantage of the data centers. Make them pay you, make them pay your community, but don’t shut them off.”
With host communities across the U.S. turning against their gargantuan data centers, America’s technology giants are promising to take fewer taxpayer handouts, keep fewer secrets or shower more money onto the communities willing to allow the server warehouses.
The new raft of promises from the world’s richest companies came as anti-data center sentiment was a potent issue in the midterm elections, in which Democrats took control of Congress.
Despite backing from Trump, the data centers powering the artificial intelligence boom have become politically toxic. Community opposition that blocks municipal permits has become a bottleneck, and tech giants could face even more challenges if voters next month elect more public officials who are skeptical of data centers.
