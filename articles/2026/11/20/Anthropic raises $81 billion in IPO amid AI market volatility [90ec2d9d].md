# Anthropic raises $81 billion in IPO amid AI market volatility

The Hindu · Balasubramanyam C P · 2026-11-20

Anthropic, the maker of artificial intelligence (AI) chatbot Claude, has priced its initial public offering (IPO) at $83 a share, valuing the offering at approximately $81.34 billion, according to an announcement by the company on November 18.
The company said its shares were expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19 under the ticker symbol ANT. The offering is expected to close on November 20, subject to customary closing conditions.
According to Anthropic’s announcement, the offering comprises 980 million shares of Class A common stock. Of these, 956.35 million shares are being sold by the company and 23.65 million by existing shareholders. Anthropic said it would not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling shareholders.
At the IPO price of $83 a share, the company’s portion of the offering is valued at approximately $79.38 billion, while the shares sold by existing shareholders are valued at approximately $1.96 billion. These figures are before underwriting discounts and expenses.
At $83 a share, Anthropic’s overall equity value works out to approximately $1.03 trillion, based on the total number of shares outstanding after the IPO.
Anthropic has also given the banks (underwriters) managing the IPO the option to purchase an additional 147 million shares within 30 days, potentially increasing the size of the offering.
Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are the lead underwriters for the IPO, with JPMorgan, Citigroup, Barclays and other investment banks also participating, according to the SEC filing.
The IPO comes amid growing investor concerns over the sustainability of the AI investment boom and whether technology companies can generate sufficient returns from their infrastructure spending.
On November 5, the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.5%, wiping out trillions of dollars in market value, while NVIDIA shares declined 9% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 12%.
Earlier, on October 29, Alphabet shares plunged 12.6%, wiping out approximately $500 billion in market value after its quarterly results raised concerns over its advertising business and the cash-flow impact of rising AI investments. The sell-off reflected broader concerns over the valuations of AI-linked companies and the returns from their heavy spending on data centres and computing infrastructure.
The broader U.S. market opened higher on Thursday, with the Nasdaq Composite gaining 1.55% at the opening bell at 9.30 am U.S. Eastern Time (8 pm IST), according to Reuters.
Anthropic’s shares had yet to begin trading as of 10.45 am US ET (9.15 pm IST), with The Wall Street Journal reporting that early indications pointed to an opening price of around $104, about 25% above the IPO price of $83. Reuters had earlier reported an indicative price of $99.82 at 9.50 a.m. U.S. ET (8.20 p.m. IST).
