The Information · Julia Hornstein, Tiffany Li · 2026-11-20

Earlier this week I reported that at least four venture firms were in line to make at least 10 times their investment in Anthropic, whose shares closed up 17% on the first day of trading Thursday. For many venture capitalists and the endowments and foundations that invest in VC funds, the offering is the first step to turning private shares into cash.  But some VCs want even more of the company: Sequoia Capital and Iconiq had planned to buy more shares at the IPO, a person familiar with the firms’ thinking said.
