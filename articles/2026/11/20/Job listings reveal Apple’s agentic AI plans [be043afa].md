# Job listings reveal Apple’s agentic AI plans

heise online · Heise Online, Malte Kirchner · 2026-11-20

Job listings reveal Apple’s agentic AI plans
Job listings show Apple is working on an autonomous AI agent that writes code, browses the web and controls apps — launch no earlier than late 2027.
Apple is developing its own autonomous AI agent that can write code for users, browse the web, control apps and operate devices. That’s according to job listings the company has posted on its website. Among other roles, Apple is looking for a senior engineering manager for a “proactive, agentic AI assistant.”
The U.S. news agency Bloomberg first reported on the listings, including the job posting for the senior engineering manager, which has since been deleted. Bloomberg had already reported earlier this month on Apple’s alleged plans for agentic AI. According to the report, a launch is planned no earlier than late 2027. Development is still in its early stages, as the job listings also suggest.
Early signs in iOS 27.3
“We don’t talk about future products.” Anyone who deals with Apple and tries to get the iPhone maker to say what’s next for something will quickly get this standard response. But Apple had already laid the groundwork for agentic AI in 2024 with the introduction of Apple Intelligence. At a time when AI for many people still revolved mostly around chatbots, the idea of proactive AI was seen as a step forward. But Apple Intelligence’s rocky launch quickly caused the company’s big idea to fade from memory.
The release of the AI voice assistant Siri AI this fall changed the picture. Apple is more restrained in public, but work is apparently underway behind the scenes to significantly expand its AI efforts. iOS 27.3 is being prepared for deep integration between Siri and third-party apps. At the same time, macOS 27.3 introduces stricter access controls for apps from other developers — for security reasons, Apple says. But over time, Apple would naturally benefit if its own agentic AI faced fewer hurdles when using apps.
What the job listing says is planned
The job listing describes the planned agents as software that operates computers like a human: opening websites, working across apps, using the command line, and writing and debugging code. Apple’s development environment, Xcode, already offers the latter in collaboration with outside providers. Developers can use Anthropic’s Claude Agent and OpenAI’s Codex; Apple has not had its own solution until now. Swift Assist, announced in 2024 and potentially a foundation for such a tool, never arrived in this form. Apple’s agentic AI is also expected to put a strong emphasis on security and privacy.
In the consumer market, Apple would compete with services such as OpenAI Dots, Meta’s Muse and Anthropic’s Claude Duet. From a European perspective, it remains unclear whether users there will get any of it, and how much. There’s currently no sign that the European Commission and Apple have reached an agreement on anything. It’s therefore still unclear whether Siri AI will also come to iPhones and iPads in the EU. Apple most recently said the talks were constructive.
