# The Funniest GTA 6 Memes Are Running Full Throttle

CNET · Gael Cooper · 2026-11-20

Even if you haven’t played a video game since Frogger, it’s tough to ignore Thursday’s launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, the most preordered video game of all time. Some companies gave employees a day off to play. (CNET bosses, I’d like the same for the next Animal Crossing game, OK?)
McDonald’s launched a promotion that lets GTA 6 players order burgers and fries via Discord without stopping the game. And CNET’s Andrew Lanxon played it for 24 hours straight at his Edinburgh home, much to his ignored cat’s dismay.
Goodbye to ‘We got X before GTA 6‘
Many of the memes play off a common complaint about how long it took the much-anticipated GTA 6 to arrive: 13 years.
“We got [fill in the blank] before GTA 6” was the usual caption.
The Marvel Mania account went one step further and listed as many things as possible that “we got” before GTA 6, including COVID-19, a new English king, foldable phones, a second Donald Trump presidency, an American pope, McDonald’s Grimace shake, the Hawk Tuah meme, the Roman Empire trend and many more.
People immediately started looking for a new long-awaited item to fill GTA 6’s place in the meme. George R.R. Martin’s eternal Game of Thrones sequel, yer up!
Let’s be careful out there
GTA 6 might be fun for players, but not for the fictional cops in the game. Minnesota’s South St. Paul Police Department was one of several actual law-enforcement agencies that got in on the release hype, posting: “Our thoughts are with the Vice City Police Department tonight as they prepare for an influx of visitors.”
Touch my cat, and it’s on like John Wick
GTA 6 includes 34 types of cats and dogs you can pet, as well as other animals. If you spend any time on social media, you know cats are a favorite pick. This post jokes about coaxing the little furballs with the “pspsps” sounds.
GTA grocery grab
Sure, you can do wild, crazy things in GTA 6, but you can also… grocery shop? Numerous memes have poked fun at a screenshot noting that Lucia “will appreciate” it if you bring home groceries.
Wrote one Bluesky user, “I think I’m going to spend all my time in GTA6 fixing up the house, buying groceries, raiding gang hideouts, feeding my pet. Total escapism from life where I can barely afford any of that.”
Apparently, you can also pull a Tom Sawyer’s pals and do a little painting.
Check that cholesterol
The American Heart Association got in on the release hype by telling veteran players they’re old.
“If you’ve played GTA on more than one console generation, it’s time to get your cholesterol checked,” one post read. Thanks a lot, Mom.
From Nvidia to Vice City
Some joked that Jensen Huang’s resignation from Nvidia might not have had anything to do with extramarital affairs or backdoor business deals with China. Maybe he just wanted unlimited time to play GTA 6.
Grand What Auto, now?
Some players complained that you can’t actually commit “grand theft auto” in the game. In reality, you can steal most cars by carjacking drivers or breaking into standard vehicles — only keyless and luxury models require a Key Cloner. Still, the fact that you can’t effortlessly steal every car on sight sparked a wave of memes mocking the game for not living up to its name.
What about us?
And many memes had a sad tinge, focusing on PC gamers who were left out of the GTA 6 launch. There’s still no release date for the game’s PC version.
