# Anthropic Slumps as AI Selloff Deepens

Bloomberg · 2026-11-20

Anthropic shares fell in premarket trading as a broad market selloff and doubts about the AI trade overshadowed the optimism that had fueled a rally on its record-setting debut.

Apple is developing its own autonomous AI agent that can get things done on behalf of users, looking to enter a fast-growing market already being pursued by OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and others.

A gauge of global stocks is heading for its longest losing streak in three years as the AI trade undergoes a sharp unravelling, putting this year’s broader equity gains at risk and roiling metal markets.
