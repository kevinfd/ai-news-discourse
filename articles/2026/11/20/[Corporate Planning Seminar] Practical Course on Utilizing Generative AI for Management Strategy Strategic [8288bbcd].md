# [Corporate Planning Seminar] Practical Course on Utilizing Generative AI for Management Strategy | Strategic Planning and Business Advancement Combining Corporate Planning ...

[Corporate Planning Seminar] Practical Course on Utilizing Generative AI for Management Strategy | Strategic Planning and Business Advancement Combining Corporate Planning Frameworks with Generative AI
The Japan Management Association (Headquarters: Shibuya-ku, Tokyo) will be holding a "Practical Course on Utilizing Generative AI for Management Strategy."
With the evolution of generative AI and AI agents, new possibilities are expanding for information gathering, market analysis, and the formulation of management strategies within corporate planning departments. On the other hand, a challenge remains in how to integrate generative AI into corporate planning tasks to improve the speed and accuracy of decision-making.
In this seminar, we will organize essential frameworks for corporate planning, such as PEST analysis, SWOT analysis, and 3C analysis, while also learning about the latest trends in major generative AI and methods for practical application.

● Recommended for:
・Corporate planning staff who create decision-making materials for management or materials for management meetings.
・Planning staff and managers responsible for medium-term management plans and the planning/promotion of new businesses.
・Staff who want to conduct external environment analysis and market research to apply to strategic planning.
● Goals of this seminar
① Grasp changes in modern society and the business environment, reaffirm the role of the corporate planning department, and build a foundation capable of adapting to the AI era.
② Organize essential frameworks for corporate planning and visualize the advancement of analysis and planning tasks by combining them with generative AI.
③ Understand risks such as hallucinations and prompt injection, and take home a generative AI utilization policy suitable for your company.
● Purpose of the seminar
The corporate planning department is a core function that builds the future of a company by connecting management and the field, analyzing the external environment, and promoting medium-term management plans and new businesses. Due to the rapid evolution of generative AI and AI agents, the social environment surrounding management is changing at an unprecedented speed, making it difficult to guarantee the speed and accuracy of decision-making using only conventional information gathering and analysis methods.
In this seminar, we will learn about both the corporate planning frameworks that remain unshakable even as times change and the latest trends in generative AI that evolve day by day. By learning from a practical perspective how to incorporate major generative AI into planning tasks and how to respond to new risks such as prompt injection, we aim for participants to outline the optimal utilization policy for their own companies.
● Program Overview
1. Understanding Modern Society: The Rapidly Changing Business Environment and the AI Shift
(1) From VUCA to BANI: Changes in the social and business environment in 2026 (DX / Labor shortages / Geopolitical risks)
(2) The impact of generative AI and AI agent trends on management (Market size / Penetration rate / Implementation cases)
(3) The five roles of the corporate planning department (New business / Environmental analysis / Medium-term plans / Information dissemination / Productivity improvement)
(4) The value of the corporate planning department as a "mediator between management and the field"
2. Representative Frameworks Supporting Corporate Planning
(1) Using "unchanging theories" as a foundation in an era of change
(2) External environment analysis: PEST analysis / Five Forces / 7S
(3) Internal and competitive analysis: 3C analysis / SWOT analysis / VRIO analysis
(4) Growth strategy and business portfolio: Ansoff Growth Matrix / PPM
(5) Business concept design: Business Model Canvas
3. Basics of Generative AI and the Latest AI Situation
(1) Positioning of generative AI and major tools (ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini, etc.)
(2) The unchanging mechanisms of generative AI (LLM / Tokens / Context / Inference models)
(3) Latest trends in 2026 (AI agents / MCP / Practical video generation, etc.)
(4) Strengths and limitations of generative AI (Tasks it is suited for / Tasks it is not suited for)
4. Corporate Planning Tasks Utilizing Generative AI
(1) Basics of prompting (4 elements / Markdown notation / Points to note for persona setting)
(2) AI utilization for framework analysis (Practical examples of PEST analysis x SWOT analysis)
(3) Market and competitor research using Deep Research
(4) Redesigning planning tasks through AI agent and MCP integration
(5) Changes in the human role: From "executor" to "manager (orchestrator)"
(6) Medium-term management plans and human resources: Personnel planning and reskilling in the AI era
5. Risk Management in Generative AI
(1) Hallucination risks and countermeasures
(2) Prompt injection, information leakage, copyright risks, and countermeasures
(3) Risks and limitations of AI dependency in management decision-making
6. Summary and Future Outlook
*Please note that the program content may change in order to incorporate the latest case studies.
