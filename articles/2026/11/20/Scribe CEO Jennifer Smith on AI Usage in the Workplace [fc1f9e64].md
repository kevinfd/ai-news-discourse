# Scribe CEO Jennifer Smith on AI Usage in the Workplace

Bloomberg · 2026-11-20

Jennifer Smith, Co-Founder and CEO of Scribe and a former Consultant at McKinsey, discusses AI valuations to adoption, noting research that puts AI use for workplace tasks below 4%. She says the findings contrast with the widespread chatbot and AI use they observe in their own circle. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on “The Close.” (Source: Bloomberg)
