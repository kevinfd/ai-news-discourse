# An artificial intelligence robot replaces the cooks in a nursing home: it feeds 100 people and personalizes their meals

MARCA · Juan Carlos Diaz · 2026-11-20

The presence of technology in human life is becoming ever greater, and this is causing certain changes that can be somewhat unexpected, especially due to artificial intelligence (AI). A nursing home in Valleyfield (Canada) has replaced its cooks with an AI robot that is capable of feeding 100 people and personalizing all meals.
This has been made possible thanks to the investment of more than 2 billion dollars that the governments of Quebec and Canada have allocated to the company responsible for developing the AI robot, called Chef Jasper.
The arrival of this device has caused quite a stir among the nursing home workers who, although they admit that it cooks well and offers some advantages, say that its presence has brought about some changes and layoffs that have not gone down well with the staff.
The advantages of chef Jasper in the nursing home
Chef Jasper has a mechanized arm and is capable of weighing, mixing, cooking, and customizing the residents' recipes. It prepares lunch and dinner, although its work is supervised at all times by an employee of the Chef Jasper company, whose salary is included in the contractual agreement.
The employee also helps the robot with the plating of the dishes and the filling of the food cylinders. However, this has only increased the complaints from the residence’s cooks, now angered by the change: “Jasper is an artificial intelligence robot that has taken the place of a human being. My place,” one wrote on social media, as reported by Radio-Canada.
“For many, this represents the future. For others, it is the loss of employment and a hard blow. [...] Are you prepared for the future? Because that is what is coming,” continues the complaint about this robot, whose presence has led to the dismissal of one cook position and two assistant positions.
