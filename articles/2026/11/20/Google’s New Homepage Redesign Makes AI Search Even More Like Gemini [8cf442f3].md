# Google’s New Homepage Redesign Makes AI Search Even More Like Gemini

PCMag · James Peckham · 2026-11-20

Google’s AI Mode is becoming a more integral part of its search engine, and a new collapsible sidebar makes it feel even more like using the brand’s Gemini AI chatbot.
A new update rolling out starting Thursday will give those proactively using AI Mode in Google Search a left-hand sidebar to access previous conversations. It will let you create new threads, search existing ones, start a research-focused Notebook, or browse your recent results.
PCMag found the feature working, but you need to actively use AI Mode for it to pop up. If you make a search through Google and then head to the Ask anything box within an AI Overview, you’ll then see the sidebar appear. It's there by default if you head straight to Google AI Mode.
Robby Stein, vice president of product for Google Search, first announced the feature on X, saying, “People are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done – and in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress & continuing to explore the web.”

He noted this is designed to be a “much simpler way to pick up where you left off,” which is likely why Google is using its tried-and-tested design from Gemini.
This follows an upgrade earlier this week where AI Mode adopted temporary threads, similar to how they work in Gemini. Head to the top-right corner in AI Mode to find a small pen-and-paper icon with dotted lines next to your profile picture and the Products menu. Press this to open a temporary thread that works similarly to how Incognito would in search.
“Because your session is completely detached from your account, your responses won’t be informed by past preferences, affect future personalization, or be saved to your history,” says Google’s blog post. “Once you close the thread, it’s gone, letting you go back to searching as usual.”
This option wasn’t available for PCMag during testing, suggesting it may be part of a slower rollout than the collapsible sidebar.
