# Exclusive CNBC Transcript: See how Trump 'made the deal' to reinstate Huang as Nvidia CEO

CNBC · Luke Fountain · 2026-11-23

watch now
President Donald Trump said Monday that he personally intervened to secure Jensen Huang’s return as Nvidia CEO, telling CNBC that he spoke with the chipmaker’s board before it reversed course and reinstated its co-founder.
“I made the deal,” Trump told CNBC’s Eamon Javers during an interview on “Squawk on the Street.”
Trump described his involvement in the decision, which came less than two weeks after Huang resigned, and addressed questions about national security concerns surrounding the Nvidia executive's return.
