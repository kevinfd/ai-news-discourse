# Chaince Digital Subsidiary Signs Joint Venture Framework Agreement to Develop an AI Data Center with Potential for Up to 25 MW of Power Capacity in Big Lake, Texas

The Manila Times · GlobeNewswire · 2026-11-23

Chaince Compute expected to hold 60% of the proposed joint venture; Big Lake would be its first announced project, with a planned 5 MW first phase subject to utility confirmation for a separate project parcel
NEW YORK, NY, Nov. 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Chaince Digital Holdings Inc. (Nasdaq: CD) ("Chaince Digital” or the "Company”), a digital finance and technology company focused on tokenization, on-chain innovation, and regulated brokerage services, today announced that on November 19, 2026, its wholly owned subsidiary, Chaince Compute Inc. ("Chaince Compute”), entered into a binding Joint Venture Framework Agreement (the "Framework Agreement”) with Bitlakedata Inc. ("Bit Lake”) to form a joint venture to develop an artificial intelligence and high-performance computing ("AI/HPC”) data center in Big Lake, Texas.
Bit Lake operates a bitcoin mining site in Big Lake, Texas, with 15 MW of grid-connected power in service. The proposed joint venture, which the parties expect to be owned 60% by Chaince Compute and 40% by Bit Lake, plans to develop the data center in phases.
Under the Framework Agreement, 5 MW is currently available to contribute to the joint venture for Phase 1, subject to written confirmation from AEP Texas Inc. ("AEP Texas”), the electric transmission and distribution utility serving the area, that the separate parcel can be served by that capacity, with room to expand in later phases. The parties will arrange Phase 1 power supply separately, including a joint-venture meter under Bit Lake’s existing Peridot Power supply agreement. The joint venture may expand to up to 25 MW by purchasing 10 MW of the existing mining load from the existing shareholders, at a negotiated price once a Phase 2 anchor tenant and schedule are confirmed, and a further 10 MW from BitRealm Inc. at a negotiated price once AEP Texas approves the pending interconnection application.
The parties plan to form the joint venture and sign definitive documents, including an operating agreement, within 90 days after signing the Framework Agreement. If the joint venture is not formed or the operating agreement is not signed within that period, either party may terminate the Framework Agreement.
Project Highlights
- 5 MW available for Phase 1, subject to AEP Texas confirmation. In the Company’s view, access to power has become a key constraint on new AI data center development. The first phase is planned around 5 MW of power capacity that is currently available to contribute to the joint venture for Phase 1 under the Framework Agreement. If AEP Texas confirms that the separate parcel can be served by that capacity, the Company believes this can shorten the path to a first operating facility compared with projects that must first secure new grid capacity. The joint venture plans to buy land near the existing site for the data center, subject to AEP Texas’s written confirmation that the parcel can be served by the first-phase capacity, with room to expand in later phases, and to clearance from Texas counsel under state law.
- Potential to scale. The joint venture plans to add capacity in step with tenant demand: the 10 MW currently used for mining, to be acquired from the existing shareholders at a price to be negotiated once a Phase 2 anchor tenant and schedule are confirmed, and a further 10 MW to be acquired from BitRealm Inc., also at a price to be negotiated, once AEP Texas approves a pending interconnection application. If all three phases are completed, the project would have up to 25 MW of power capacity.
- From bitcoin mining to AI computing. In its second phase, the project plans to purchase the 10 MW of existing mining load once a Phase 2 anchor tenant and schedule are confirmed, with Bit Lake phasing out mining and switching the power to the data center. The joint venture intends to lease data center capacity to AI/HPC tenants and to seek long-term agreements with anchor tenants. Chaince Compute will lead project financing and customer development. Each party will fund the joint venture in phases, in proportion to its ownership, as the project requires.
- First announced project, with plans for more sites. Big Lake is Chaince Compute’s first announced project. Chaince Compute plans to focus on small and mid-sized AI data centers in the United States and intends to use modular construction and diversified power sources, targeting shorter development timelines. The Company intends to apply the same model, partnering with holders of power capacity, to additional sites.
"Power is the key constraint in AI infrastructure today, and Bit Lake’s existing mining site has 15 MW of grid-connected power in service. AEP Texas must confirm that the separate project parcel can be served by the Phase 1 capacity,” said Shi Qiu, Chief Executive Officer of Chaince Digital and Chaince Compute. "This agreement gives Chaince Compute its first announced project, with a planned first phase of 5 MW subject to AEP Texas confirmation and a path to grow to up to 25 MW as tenants and approvals are secured. We believe partnering with holders of existing power capacity is a practical way to bring AI computing capacity to market.”
