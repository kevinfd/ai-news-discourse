# Anthropic, Nvidia shares rise in premarket trading after major tech sell-off

CNBC · Kai Nicol-Schwarz, Arjun Kharpal · 2026-11-23

Anthropic and Nvidia shares rose in premarket trading Monday after artificial intelligence stocks fell in the Nasdaq's worst week since 2020.
The AI lab was up 4.38% at 6.45 am ET, ahead of its third day of trading following its initial public offering on Thursday. The chip giant rose 8.76%.
Anthropic raised a record $93.5 billion in its IPO and an additional $26 billion from tech giants that bought at the offer price. The stock gained 17% in its debut on Thursday, before closing 8.5% down on Friday.
The Nasdaq slid 10% last week.
Tech stocks remain rocky as investors remain nervous about the valuation of some AI companies and business models.
Nvidia's premarket rebound follows a more than 40% slump in the stock over the past month, as Jensen Huang was ousted as CEO of the company he founded after he admitted to having an extramarital affair with a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly believes to be a Chinese spy.
Other Big Tech companies also saw stocks buoyed on Monday.
Alphabet stock rose 6%, while Meta added 4.29%, Amazon was up 3.92% and Microsoft jumped 3.66%. SpaceX was up 3.18% and AMD added 4.84%.
Neoclouds, which have seen a particularly choppy few months, also showed signs of recovery.
