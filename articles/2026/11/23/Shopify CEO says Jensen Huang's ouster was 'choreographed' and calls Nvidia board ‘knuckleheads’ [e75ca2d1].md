# Shopify CEO says Jensen Huang's ouster was 'choreographed' and calls Nvidia board ‘knuckleheads’

Business Insider · Shubhangi Goel, Shubhangi Goel (sgoel@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-23

Lütke blasted Nvidia board for Jensen Huang's rushed ouster. Kevin Dietsch/Getty Images Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke said that Nvidia's Jensen Huang scandal felt choreographed. Lütke blasted Nvidia board for rushed CEO ouster that risks turning Nvidia into a legacy. The Shopify founder said there was no record of custodial CEOs running companies as hot as Nvidia. Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke had some strong words about Nvidia's board over its handling of Jensen Huang's resignation. In an episode of David Senra's podcast released Sunday, Lütke questioned the sequence of allegations that preceded Huang's departure, saying it appeared too neatly arranged to be incidental. "Let's be honest, this was surgical: affair, Chinese spy, another affair, another affair," Lütke said. "This is, like, choreographed. It's too organized. This is exactly how you write it in a book as a takedown story." Huang stepped down as Nvidia CEO this month after an affair scandal and an investigation into concerns that he may have been targeted by a foreign intelligence operation. Nvidia has said Huang voluntarily resigned after disclosures of conduct inconsistent with the company's code of conduct, standards, and cultural norms. The board is not considering reinstating him, Business Insider reported last week. On the podcast, Lütke said he could be wrong and that Huang may have had the alleged affairs. Still, Lütke said there was a "pretty large list" of people who stood to benefit from Huang no longer having his job. He did not name any people or organizations. Lütke also called Nvidia's board "knuckleheads" and said that the company was at risk of becoming a legacy company because of what he described as the board's snap decision during the scandal. He added that there was no track record of "custodian" CEOs successfully managing companies as "red hot" as Nvidia. "You can put him on administrative leave or figure out something, but he built this company," he said. He added, "The burden of proof needs to be much, much, much higher for saying that he's not the right person to run this company." The German-Canadian entrepreneur co-founded Shopify in September 2004, became its CEO in 2008, and has led the company through its evolution into one of the world's largest e-commerce platforms. Lütke, like several tech leaders , has consistently supported Huang since the scandal broke. On November 12, he called Huang's departure a "generational blunder" based on the information available at the time, writing that "there is no replacing a founder like Jensen" and that Nvidia would become a company "cosplaying Nvidia" under custodial management.
