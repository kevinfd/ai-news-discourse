# Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is back after a stunning reversal

ZDNet · Radhika Rajkumar · 2026-11-23

ZDNET’s key takeaways
- Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO on Monday, Nov. 23.
- Support for Huang’s reinstatement grew, including from President Trump.
- Investigations into Nvidia remain ongoing.
Nvidia announced on Monday, Nov. 23, that it had reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO, and board member “effective immediately,” just 11 days after his departure. The decision comes despite ongoing investigations into Nvidia.

“The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead Nvidia,” the release said. “In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.”
The move is a quick 180. After Huang was removed as CEO on Nov 12 in response to numerous affairs with employees and a possible connection to a foreign intelligence official, Nvidia’s board said during last week’s earnings call that it would not reinstate him.

Both President Donald J. Trump and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent also reversed earlier criticism of Huang over the weekend and aligned with efforts to reinstate him, though Trump had been more supportive of the move earlier on than Bessent.
What investigations into Nvidia are still ongoing?
The SEC opened an investigation into Nvidia after Huang’s departure, which appears to remain ongoing now that he is back. The SEC investigation is separate from national security concerns surrounding Alice Huang, the suspected foreign intelligence operative Jensen Huang admitted having an extramarital affair with.

“In light of recent reporting that Mr. Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, the Board formed a committee comprised solely of independent directors to investigate, and the committee retained independent counsel to assist it in that investigation,” Nvidia’s announcement continued. “The Board reiterated that the committee’s investigation will proceed to conclusion. Mr. Huang fully supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it.”
Huang confirmed the first of several extramarital affairs on Nov. 8 and was removed as CEO just a few days later. The timeline is reminiscent of when OpenAI CEO Sam Altman was briefly removed from his position on Nov. 17, 2023, and reinstated five days later.
