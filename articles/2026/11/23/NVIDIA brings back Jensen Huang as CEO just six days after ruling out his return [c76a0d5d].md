# NVIDIA brings back Jensen Huang as CEO just six days after ruling out his return

VentureBeat · 2026-11-23

NVIDIA announced Monday that Jensen Huang had been reinstated as CEO, 11 days after announcing his resignation and six days after its board publicly ruled out his return. The board said it concluded that his reinstatement poses no national security risk after receiving input from senior U.S. government officials, even as it continues its independent investigation into reports of a foreign intelligence effort targeting Huang.
