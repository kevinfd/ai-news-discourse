# Nvidia-Backed AI Company Firmus Mulls $1.5 Billion Bond Offering

Bloomberg · Janice Huang, Sheryl Tian Tong Lee and Megawati Wijaya · 2026-11-23

Artificial intelligence company Firmus Grid Ltd. is considering seeking around $1.5 billion in a bond offering that would be part of a larger debt financing deal, according to people familiar with the matter, after it shelved plans for an initial public offering last month.
