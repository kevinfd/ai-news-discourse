# Apples new iPad mini is $100 off in Amazons Black Friday sale — shop now for $599

Mashable · Hannah Hoolihan · 2026-11-23

Apple's new iPad mini is $100 off in Amazon's Black Friday sale — shop now for $599
SAVE $100: As of Nov. 23, Apple's new iPad mini (A20 Pro, 128GB, WiFi) is on sale for $599 at Amazon. That's $100 off its full cost of $699.
Apple's new iPad mini with the A20 Pro chip — which launched just a few weeks ago — has already received a nice discount at Amazon during Black Friday week. What a sweet seasonal sale surprise.
As of Nov. 23, the 128GB, WiFi version of this tablet has dropped to $599 at Amazon.
        
            The best tech deal this week
                            
                    
            
If you've been eager to grab Apple's latest tiny tablet, this is a solid deal compared to paying the full $699. Both the midnight and starlight colorways are on sale at this price, so you can score a sweet discount on your preferred model. We certainly think it's worth the investment, with Mashable's Timothy Beck Werth calling it "the best Apple tablet" in his review.
The exciting new addition to the iPad mini is the A20 Pro chip, which offers a significant performance boost. Werth's review highlighted that it "compares favorably to the iPad Air and iPad Pro, coming somewhere between the M4 chip in the latest iPad Air and the M5 chip in the iPad Pro." It also has a gorgeous 8.5-inch OLED display, so you can enjoy deep contrast and vibrant colors.
Its thin, portable design is also a bonus if you want a smaller tablet to fit in a bag, and it comes with Apple Intelligence and Siri AI features. After spending time with it, we've named it the best pick for entertainment in our roundup of the best iPads.
Don't miss out on $100 off Apple's new iPad mini at Amazon.
