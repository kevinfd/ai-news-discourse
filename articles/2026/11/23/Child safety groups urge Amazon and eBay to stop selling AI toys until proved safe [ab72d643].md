# Child safety groups urge Amazon and eBay to stop selling AI toys until proved safe

The Guardian · Priya Bharadia · 2026-11-23

Advocates from across the world voice concern over privacy issues and inappropriate communication to children More than 150 child safety groups and experts across six continents have urged Amazon and eBay to stop selling AI toys this Christmas until they are proved to be safe. In a letter sent to Amazon’s president, Andy Jassy, and eBay’s president, Jamie Iannone, the coalition called for the online retailers to stop the sale of AI toys and offer refunds to customers who have already bought one.
