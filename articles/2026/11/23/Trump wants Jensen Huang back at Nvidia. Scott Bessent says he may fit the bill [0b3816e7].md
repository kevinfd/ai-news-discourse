# Trump wants Jensen Huang back at Nvidia. Scott Bessent says he may fit the bill.

Business Insider · Aditi Bharade, Aditi Bharade (abharade@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-23

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Jensen Huang is now an outsider at Nvidia, making him a potential CEO candidate. China Pool/Getty Images Donald Trump and Scott Bessent both seem in favor of Jensen Huang returning to Nvidia as CEO. Bessent previously said Nvidia should find an external hire, but later added Jensen Huang is an outsider. Other tech leaders, including Elon Musk, and Nvidia employees are backing calls for Huang's return. Both President Donald Trump and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent are warming up to the idea of Jensen Huang leading Nvidia again. "I hope the Board of Nvidia rehires Jensen Huang," Trump wrote in a Truth Social post on Sunday, adding that it would be best for the company and the US. Bessent didn't directly call for Huang's reinstatement, but he seemed to have shifted his stance on the matter compared to a week ago. In a November 15 interview on Fox's "Sunday Morning Futures," Bessent said Nvidia's board should consider candidates " outside the company " to replace Huang. "They need a dramatic shake-up," Bessent said in the interview. But on Sunday, speaking on CBS's "Face the Nation," Bessent said Huang can now be considered an outsider. Less than two weeks have passed since the executive resigned from the company. "I said they should go to the outside, but he's on the outside now," Bessent said. Nvidia announced on November 12 that Huang had resigned following reports of extramarital affairs, including one with Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed whom US officials have suspected may have been a Chinese spy. He was also accused of having an affair with a direct report at Nvidia. Nvidia CFO Colette Kress took over as interim CEO. The company's board has yet to decide on a permanent replacement and has confirmed that it has no plans to reinstate Huang . On "Face the Nation," Bessent added that Huang's affair with Alice Huang could distract from questions about Nvidia's culture of compliance. "I think that is kind of a canard," Bessent said, referring to the distinction between questions about the company's compliance culture and "one person's foibles." It's not just Trump; calls for Huang's reinstatement have poured in from his allies, such as Elon Musk and Replit CEO Amjad Masad, as well as Nvidia shareholders and current and former employees. The petition, "Stand with Jensen," had received over 200,000 signatures as of early Monday, according to its website.
