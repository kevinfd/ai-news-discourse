Why we like it
The AirPods Pro 3 are $149 at Walmart, down from $249, which matches Amazon's record-low price. That's $100 off, or 40%, and before this we'd only seen them as low as $169. They come with five ear-tip sizes for a comfortable fit and last up to eight hours on a single charge with noise cancellation on. Beyond audio, they can track your heart rate and calories burned across 50 workout types, translate conversations in real time using Apple Intelligence, and switch automatically between your Apple devices.
