The Information · Anita Ramaswamy · 2026-11-23

While some on Wall Street think the recent turmoil at Nvidia could be an opportunity for AMD to sell more of its AI chips to customers and steal market share, its valuation is already predicated on sky-high growth expectations and doesn’t reflect the geopolitical, financing and execution risks the company faces.
