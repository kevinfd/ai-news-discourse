# AI Security Startup Matters.AI Raises $12 Million, Targets North America

CIOL · CIOL Bureau · 2026-11-23

Bengaluru-based cybersecurity startup Matters.AI has raised $12 million (around ₹115 crore) in a pre-Series A funding round led by Tanglin Venture Partners, as it looks to expand its data security platform and strengthen its presence in North America. The latest round takes the company's total funding to $18.25 million (around ₹175 crore).
The round also saw participation from IIFL Fintech Fund, Piper Serica's Bharat Tech Fund, Foster Ventures and Tetration Ventures, along with existing investors Kalaari Capital and Endiya Partners.
Founded by Keshava Murthy and Harsh Sahu, Matters.AI develops security technology to track how sensitive enterprise data is accessed, used and transferred across business systems. Its platform addresses a growing concern for organisations adopting AI agents, which can access and move company data using the permissions granted to employees.
Traditional data security tools typically identify where sensitive information is stored or flag potential exposure. However, tracking who or what accesses the information, how it moves across systems and whether that activity is authorised can require security teams to correlate alerts from multiple tools.
Matters.AI's Data Detection and Response (DDR) platform uses artificial intelligence to identify sensitive information, trace its movement across cloud infrastructure, on-premises systems, software-as-a-service applications, endpoints and browsers, and investigate potential security risks.
The platform also analyses data sensitivity, system misconfigurations, access permissions and user intent to identify insider and compliance risks. It can investigate incidents and initiate remediation within the policies established by an organisation's security team.
The company said its platform is currently deployed across more than 50 organisations, including financial institutions, stockbroking and asset management firms, retailers, and digital media and entertainment companies.
Keshava Murthy, co-founder and CEO of Matters.AI, said enterprise security teams needed systems that could investigate potential threats and take action rather than simply generate alerts. He added that the growing use of AI agents to access and move sensitive information was changing how organisations needed to approach data protection.
Matters.AI will use the fresh capital to expand research and development in data lineage, autonomous investigation, endpoint and browser intelligence, and preventing sensitive data misuse by AI agents. It also plans to build its North American sales organisation, beginning with senior leadership appointments.
The company has teams in Bengaluru and the San Francisco Bay Area. Its funding comes as enterprises increasingly deploy AI systems across business operations, creating additional requirements to monitor how employees and automated agents access proprietary information.
