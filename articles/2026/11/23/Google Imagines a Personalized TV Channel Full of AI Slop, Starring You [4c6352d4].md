# Google Imagines a Personalized TV Channel Full of AI Slop, Starring You

CNET · Ty Pendlebury · 2026-11-23

If you’ve ever dreamed of starring in your own TV show, this might be for you. Google’s latest patent application is for an AI-powered version of your personalized TV channel, using data advertisers have gathered about you. The AI-created shows can also insert you as the main character.
According to the website Patentlyze, the application is for a system designed to compile your video or TV watch preferences using genres and titles you watch on services such as YouTube, plus context from your IP address and demographic data such as your age. Although Patentlyze says this particular Google patent application was rejected, the company can amend the application and resubmit.
A Google representative didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.
More from CNET
The system would include an option to make you the hero by building an avatar from your photos and replicating your voice for the lead character’s lines.
The program could create a personalized feed like “John’s Channel,” stocked with AI-made crime-comedy capers, rom-coms and sci-fi adventures, if those are the shows John enjoys. The service could make new videos based on voice prompts or create new ones on its own, the report said.
Building a channel of AI slop isn’t a new idea. Roku recently unveiled FairgroundTV, the first free, all-AI streaming channel.
Generative AI continues to draw widespread skepticism from both local communities and everyday internet users. New data centers built to power AI models have met growing grassroots resistance over their heavy energy and water footprints, while synthetic content faces mounting cultural pushback, especially from younger audiences, who have largely rejected AI-generated media as inauthentic “slop.”
