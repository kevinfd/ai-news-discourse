# What a Town of Vibecoders Says About the Chinese Economy

Bloomberg · 2026-11-23

According to Stanford's AI Index, 84% of people surveyed in China say they are optimistic about AI. In the US, by contrast, 38% of those surveyed say they are optimistic. That's a big gulf in enthusiasm, and you can see it borne out in the stories you read: While in the US we hear about AI doomsday scenarios, in China, you'll hear about grandmas who are obsessed with using OpenClaw.
