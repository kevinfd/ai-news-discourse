# US Treasury Secretary leaves door open for Jensen Huang’s return to Nvidia

South China Morning Post · Ann Cao · 2026-11-23

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has opened the door to former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang returning to the chipmaker, softening his stance in the latest twist to the company’s leadership turmoil.
Speaking with American broadcaster CBS on Sunday, Bessent sidestepped the apparent friction between his earlier call for Nvidia’s board to recruit an external leader and US President Donald Trump’s recent public backing of Huang.
