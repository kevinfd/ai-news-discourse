# Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; Trump praises ‘correct’ decision; shares surge

South China Morning Post · Xiaofei Xu,Howard Liu,Vincent Chow · 2026-11-23

Nvidia has reinstated Jensen Huang as its CEO, president and board director with immediate effect, after the company determined following input from US officials that the move would not pose a national security risk.
The firm’s board of directors voted unanimously to reinstate Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with him, after which the board was satisfied with Huang’s candour and ability to lead the firm, the company said in a statement on Monday.
