# Jensen Huang says he'll be a 'better CEO' as staff applauds his return

The Verge · baal80spam · 2026-11-23

Jensen Huang returned to Nvidia’s office as CEO today to a crowd of applauding employees who stood and filmed the cofounder’s first moments back.
Jensen Huang says he’ll be a ‘better CEO’ as staff applauds his return
Nvidia’s reinstated CEO thanked employees for the ‘second chances.’
“Thank you all for the second chances. I’m gonna be a better CEO the second time around,” Huang said, as shown in a video of his return.
Huang resigned as CEO on November 12th following reports of affairs, including with an alleged Chinese spy as well as a direct report at Nvidia. Huang admitted two days before that he was “unfaithful to my wife.”
President Trump, who had advocated for Huang to be reinstated ahead of it actually happening, was “totally involved” in his return, the White House said. In its press release about Huang’s reinstatement, the board of directors said it “received input from senior levels of the United States government” that made it “confident” that Huang’s reinstatement “poses no national security risk.” Trump asked Vice President JD Vance to help with the reinstatement over the weekend, including assisting the board to get “the assurances it needed,” The New York Times reported.
“This is my first day at work,” Huang told staff today, according to a video reported on by Business Insider. “I’m terribly sorry for putting all of you through all of this.”
