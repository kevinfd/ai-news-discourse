# Arm's Haas extends contract to lead chip firm through 2033 amid Nvidia top job chatter

Channel NewsAsia · CNA · 2026-11-23

Arm's Haas extends contract to lead chip firm through 2033 amid Nvidia top job chatter
Nov 23 : Arm Holdings CEO Rene Haas has extended his contract with the chip designer to lead the company through 2033, confirming his leadership role at the firm amid speculation that Nvidia could tap him for the top job after Jensen Huang's resignation.
Nvidia's board had approached Haas in its search for a successor to Huang, the Financial Times reported earlier this month, following Huang's exit amid revelations of extramarital affairs including one with an alleged Chinese spy.
Analysts had also pointed to Haas as a solid candidate for the CEO role at Nvidia, given he spent his formative years at the company, rising to vice president and general manager of its computing products business, before joining Arm in 2013.
Haas became the British chipmaker's chief executive in 2022 and oversaw its return to public markets through a $65 billion Nasdaq listing the following year.
Arm licenses processor designs used across smartphones, PCs, servers and connected devices.
"I’ve never been more confident about Arm's future ... I’m wholly committed to realizing Arm’s full potential for many years to come," Haas said in a statement on Monday.
Haas will also assume the role of vice chairman of SoftBank Group, effective December 1, Arm Holdings said on Monday.
