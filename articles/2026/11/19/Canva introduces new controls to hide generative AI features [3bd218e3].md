# Canva introduces new controls to hide generative AI features

9to5Mac · Marcus Mendes · 2026-11-19

In light of growing pushback from users who want more control over how AI is integrated into their apps (if at all), Canva is introducing new settings that let them hide generative AI features and see less AI-generated content. Here are the details.
Canva adds an off switch for generative AI
Canva today announced new controls that will let Free and Pro users choose whether to see generative AI features across the app, as well as how much AI-generated content appears in search results and in recommendations for templates and design elements.
The company notes that both controls are available at no additional cost and can be turned on or off at any time.
According to Canva, “the future of creativity isn’t a choice between AI and no AI.” For this reason, it is introducing the following controls:
- Generative AI feature control. A new switch to turn supported generative AI features on or off. When it’s off, those features are hidden across Canva, and can be switched back on any time. It can be found under Profile > Settings > Permissions > then switch Enable generative AI features on or off.
- AI-generated content control. A new option to see less AI-generated content while browsing the Canva library, without changing any existing designs. It can be found Profile > Your account > AI personalization, and enable showing less AI-generated content.
The company says that, while it knows that there is an ongoing debate between users with opposing views on whether AI has a place in creative work, it thinks “both views are valid”:
“It’s not our place to tell anyone how to create, so today we’re launching two new controls that put that decision firmly with our community […].”
Importantly, the company noted that select features that use AI without generating new content will remain available even when generative AI features are turned off:
“Some of Canva’s everyday editing tools use AI to make simple tasks faster, without generating anything new. Background Remover, one of Canva’s most popular tools, lifts a subject cleanly out of a photo in a single click, and Magic Grab lets creators select an object in an image and move it somewhere else in the design. Both remain available when the generative AI switch is off, because they work only with what’s already in a design.”
Canva added that these tools also won’t add an AI label to a design’s metadata, since that label is reserved for AI-generated content.
As for the company’s design content library, Canva said that while “a small portion of the library is AI-generated content made by Canva, including some abstract backgrounds and illustrated graphics,” its community has told it “they want an easier way to find design assets made by real people, so the content control reduces the number of AI-generated templates and elements that appear in search results and recommendations.”
With today’s announcement, Canva is essentially expanding on the AI permissions already available to administrators of Business, Enterprise, Teams, and Education accounts, which let organizations control how their teams use AI.
