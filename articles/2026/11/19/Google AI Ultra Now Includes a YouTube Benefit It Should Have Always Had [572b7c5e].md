# Google AI Ultra Now Includes a YouTube Benefit It Should Have Always Had

PCMag · James Peckham · 2026-11-19

Google’s top AI Ultra plans now come with a long-sought-after benefit: YouTube Premium Family, rather than just an individual subscription.
The $99.99-a-month plan is designed to be the go-to option for those who want everything from Google, with higher Gemini usage limits, extended access to models like Gemini 4 Neon and Gemini 4 Carbon, and various other benefits such as Google Health and Home Premium subscriptions.
YouTube Premium has always been part of the package, alongside 20TB of cloud storage, but previously its YouTube subscription was limited to an individual user. Now, all accounts on the plan can add up to five more users through Google's family system at no extra cost.
Head of AI subscriptions at Google, Shimrit Ben-Yair, posted on X to say, “This is one of the features you all have been pinging me the most about.” Google also announced the change on its support forum, confirming that all active subscribers have immediate access.

To access the new subscription, the AI Ultra user needs to be the manager of your family plan. If you’re not the family plan owner, you’ll need to manually set up a new group by going to Settings > Manage family settings, as there’s no way to transfer ownership once a group is set up.
You may find this switch saves your family money, especially if you’re spread across multiple streaming services, and you're all willing to switch to YouTube Music.
The benefit is available through the $99.99-a-month subscription, and it’s also included in Google’s top $199.99-a-month tier. That gives you even more Gemini usage alongside 30TB of storage, but it's reserved for those who need the absolute most AI prowess.
Google’s AI Pro plan continues to cost $19.99 per month and includes access to YouTube Premium Lite. That limits the amount of ads you see when watching videos, but it doesn’t unlock access to ad-free YouTube Music.
