# STAT+: Merck, Isomorphic Labs partner to develop oncology drug candidates

STAT News · Katherine MacPhail · 2026-11-19

Merck and Isomorphic Labs, the artificial intelligence drug designer built on a Nobel winning technology that can model how molecules will bind to proteins, said Thursday that they are entering into a research collaboration to discover small molecule candidates for an undisclosed oncology target of “strategic importance.”
The partnership — only the latest such deal between a pharma giant and Isomorphic — will bring together the latter’s proprietary drug design engine and existing wet lab data from Merck, officials from both companies said. Merck will handle the preclinical and clinical development, manufacturing, and commercializing of any products.
“It is a target of high interest to us, a target where we have deployed internal efforts, and we think the pairing of those datasets with the Isomorphic drug discovery engine technology could be a very powerful pairing,” said George Addona, global head of discovery, preclinical development, and translational medicine at Merck.
