# Nvidia Nears Acquisition of Diffusion AI Developer for More Than $500 Million

The Information · Amir Efrati, Phoebe Liu, Stephanie Palazzolo · 2026-11-19

Nvidia is in advanced talks to acquire Inception, a two-year-old startup that develops a type of AI that aims to perform faster than the one that powers products such as ChatGPT and Claude, according to three people with knowledge of the discussions. The proposed deal is worth at least $500 million, plus “earnout” awards for the new employees, depending on whether they hit certain goals, one of these people said.
