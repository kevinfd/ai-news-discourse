# Meta Bets on $300 Muse Charm to Boost AI Hardware Business

The Information · Jyoti Mann · 2026-11-19

Meta Platforms plans to charge $300 for its forthcoming Muse Charm device and offer user subsidies, according to a person familiar with the matter, betting that the pocket-sized AI gadget will help energize its hardware business and draw users to its new personal assistant. 

 To lure buyers, Meta plans to offer a free 5G data plan from Verizon for up to 12 months that would enable faster responses from Muse, its personal AI agent, wherever users go, the person said.
