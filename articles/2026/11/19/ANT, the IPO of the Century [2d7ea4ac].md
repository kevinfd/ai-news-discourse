# ANT, the IPO of the Century

AI Supremacy · Michael Spencer · 2026-11-19

Hey Everyone, So Anthropic’s IPO represents a major turning point in the history of Generative AI. Anthropic was founded on January 26, 2021, just five years and nine months ago. Anthropic was basically an ideologically walkout of talent from OpenAI. Together just these two companies have raised $428.4 Billion to date not counting proceeds from the IPO. Over the past five years (2021 to 2026), the BigAI duopoly, just these two startups have raised around 27% (more than a quarter) of all AI funding to startups. Along with the backing of BigTech, this is textbook unfair advantages. Anthropic could possibly be not just the technology IPO of the decade or our generation, but of the century. Anthropic has thus managed to attract and retain top AI researcher and now executive talent. Expected to go public on Thursday, November 19th in the afternoon perhaps around 1:30 pm, Anthropic has priced its IPO at $83 per share, and an IPO where retail investors themselves are likely to be far more bullish about Anthropic than they were about SpaceX. CNBC. The split of Anthropic with OpenAI stemmed from disagreements over OpenAI's direction—specifically concerns among the departing team that OpenAI was prioritizing rapid commercialization (after the Microsoft deal), a position Anthropic now finds itself in itself not even six years from its founding. Torn between a mandate of AI alignment while radically pitching recursive self-improvement as a selling point, and needing so much (almost too much) compute, it’s hard to say where Anthropic will land in the 2030s, or even in the decade ahead. How the IPO is received could shape much of Nvidia’s fall. Anthropic is the singular frontier lab and product company to watch in 2027 and takes over being the focal point of the AI boom. I no longer consider OpenAI or Google a peer in LLMs with Anthropic. However my opinion is not yet unanimous consensus. According to Epoch AI , Anthropic’s compute spending is increasing by 4x per year, outpacing OpenAI’s 3x. For all of Anthropic’s antics about trust & saftey, we’ve never seen BigTech spawn a child company quite like this! An IPO where Alphabet (Google) and Amazon will own a 28% equity stake in the company tied to hyperscaler (Cloud computing) incentives and TPUs, the AI chips of Google . Epoch AI. How do you justify a young startup with a market valuation of roughly $1.49 trillion on a fully diluted basis, one that makes the world’s best frontier AI models? With Nvidia seemingly no longer the poster child of the AI trade, can Anthropic take its place for the IPO boom period? We’ll know more in the next few weeks. It may ultimately depend on how well Anthropic transitions to a product-company for Enterprise. While Anthropic’s revenue ramp is indeed historic, margins and compute costs will be something to watch. Anthropic should raise in the area of $94.48 billion in gross proceeds significantly boosting its cash position while entering a shaky AI correction market where both AI related semiconductor and compute related Neo Clouds have been under significant pressure over the last few weeks. It remains to be seen how Anthropic’s identity will morph as a public company - which could move as fast as its products. In theory, Anthropic is an AI research and products company based in San Francisco that builds Claude, and that family of large language models supposedly treating AI safety as a core part of its mission. Anthropic, the fastest revenue growth startup of tech history. How will they deal with that level of success and those expectations? If this is the dominant Enterprise AI company of our generation, we should not judge it too hastily or too soon.
