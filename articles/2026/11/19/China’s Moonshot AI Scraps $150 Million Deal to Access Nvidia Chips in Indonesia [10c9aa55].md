# China’s Moonshot AI Scraps $150 Million Deal to Access Nvidia Chips in Indonesia

The Information · Qianer Liu, Claudia Chong · 2026-11-19

Moonshot AI, the Beijing-based developer of the Kimi models, has scrapped a $150 million deal to access Nvidia Blackwell chips through a data center in Indonesia, after the U.S. this week announced a rule to restrict Chinese companies from accessing advanced chips remotely, according to two people with direct knowledge of the matter.
