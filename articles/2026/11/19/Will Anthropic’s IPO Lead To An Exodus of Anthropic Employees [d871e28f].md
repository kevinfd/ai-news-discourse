# Will Anthropic’s IPO Lead To An Exodus of Anthropic Employees?

The Information · Stephanie Palazzolo · 2026-11-19

Today is a major milestone for Anthropic . The five-year-old maker of Claude beat its archrival OpenAI to the public markets, and many of its employees are going to become millionaires, if not billionaires, when the company starts to trade later today. The big question is what today means for Anthropic’s future. Before we get to that, let’s set the scene. Anthropic priced the offering at $83 a share , well  above its preliminary pricing range, valuing the company at about $1.5 trillion. Chances are the stock will trade significantly higher when it opens later Thursday. For employees looking to cash in, they’ll need the stock to stay higher for a while. While they can sell up to 10% of their shares on the first day of trading, they can’t sell more until the company reports its full-year 2026 earnings early next year. Assuming the stock doesn’t tank, the sudden influx of money will help Anthropic employees buy houses and donate a big chunk of their newfound wealth to causes like shrimp welfare . Counterintuitively, though, it may also lead them to leave the very company that brought them the wealth in the first place. You may think that employees at a company as philosophically driven as Anthropic are as bought in as the cofounders and in it for the long haul. That’s not necessarily true. At the end of the day, many of the employees, especially those who have joined more recently from big tech companies, might not be drinking the Kool-Aid as much as just trying to make a quick buck.
