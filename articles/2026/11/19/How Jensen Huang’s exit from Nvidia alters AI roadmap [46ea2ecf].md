# How Jensen Huang’s exit from Nvidia alters AI roadmap

The Hindu · John Xavier · 2026-11-19

The past few weeks have been anything but ordinary in the world of technology as Nvidia, key AI chip supplier to the world’s data centres, was shaken by a scandal involving its co-founder Jensen Huang and Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed.
Ms. Huang is believed to be a spy to China’s Ministry of State Security, recruited to gather intelligence on the Nvidia chief and secure high-end AI chips. Beijing has dismissed the allegation, calling it baseless and nothing but a smear campaign against China.
