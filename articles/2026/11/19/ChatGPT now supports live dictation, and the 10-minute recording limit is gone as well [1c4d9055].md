# ChatGPT now supports live dictation, and the 10-minute recording limit is gone as well

Digital Trends · Shimon Das · 2026-11-19

OpenAI has rolled out an upgraded Live Dictation feature in ChatGPT, delivering faster, more reliable speech-to-text transcription and removing the previous 10-minute recording limit. The update is rolling out globally across all ChatGPT plans on iOS, Android, the web, and desktop apps.
Previously, users dictating longer prompts or notes in ChatGPT had to speak without seeing real-time text appear until processing finished, while running up against a hard 10-minute ceiling. Live Dictation changes that workflow by showing words on the screen as the user speaks. Seeing live transcription unfold makes it easier to catch misheard words on the fly, adjust phrasing mid-thought, or speak uninterrupted during extended brainstorming sessions, drafting, or multi-step instructions.
The freedom to speak your mind is here
While built-in operating system dictation tools offer basic voice entry, Live Dictation works directly inside the ChatGPT interface, tightening the loop between speaking, reviewing text, and sending prompts.
For writers, developers, and everyday users who prefer talking through complex ideas rather than typing them out, Live Dictation turns voice input into a continuous, real-time editing experience. By pairing faster transcription with unlimited session length, OpenAI makes hands-free interaction with ChatGPT noticeably smoother across every supported device.
Is this what cool kids call yapping?
The update also introduces a subtle interface control for those who prefer less visual distraction while speaking. Users can tap or click the audio waveform during dictation to toggle the live transcript visibility on or off on the fly.
OpenAI has not published specific latency benchmarks, accuracy metrics, or a newly stated maximum recording duration alongside the announcement, noting only that the feature is faster and more reliable than prior dictation. For users concerned about data privacy, OpenAI notes that dictation audio sharing can be managed through data controls in ChatGPT settings by disabling the option to include audio recordings or turning off model improvement sharing.
Live Dictation is rolling out globally across all ChatGPT subscription tiers, including free and paid plans on iOS, Android, web, and desktop applications on your desired platform. As the rollout reaches individual accounts, users will be able to access the upgraded experience by tapping the microphone icon and starting to speak.
