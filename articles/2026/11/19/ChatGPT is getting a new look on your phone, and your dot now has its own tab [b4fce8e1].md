# ChatGPT is getting a new look on your phone, and your dot now has its own tab

Digital Trends · Anvinraj Valiyathara · 2026-11-19

OpenAI is rolling out a new mobile experience for ChatGPT designed to make your favorite features easier to reach and quicker to discover. OpenAI’s Thibault Sottiaux (Tibo), who works on Codex and ChatGPT, shared a demo of the redesign on November 19.
The first wave is for ChatGPT Pro users who already have one of OpenAI’s always-on dots or are eligible to create one, and some will see the new layout by default. If “New ChatGPT Design” appears in Settings, you can turn it on or off there.
Your dot gets its own tab, and you can pin the tools you use most
The official preview shows a bottom tab bar with a Home screen, a dedicated Dot tab, and a grid for the rest of ChatGPT’s tools. Home greets you with a “Good morning” message and an “Ask anything” field. Open the Dot tab and you move into a conversation with your dot, with a “Message dot” composer and a notice that Dots are in early preview.
A third tab opens a grid of features under the instruction “Tap and hold an item to pin it to the tab bar.” In the preview, that grid includes Space, Projects, Images, Scheduled, Codex, Plugins, Finance, Health, and Sites. A long press on Codex offers “Reorder” and “Pin to tab bar,” and once you pin it the Codex icon appears in the tab bar beside Home and your dot.
OpenAI is starting small, and dots are reaching more ChatGPT plans
OpenAI says the redesign starts with ChatGPT Pro users who are 18 or older in regions where dots are available. The rollout excludes the European Economic Area (including the EU), the UK, Switzerland, and South Korea. A small group of eligible Plus users will also see the new experience.
Tibo also said the same day that OpenAI is starting to test dots with a small number of ChatGPT Plus users and is bringing dots to all ChatGPT Business Standard users. OpenAI’s help pages describe that Business Standard expansion as reaching eligible members in the United States. Pro users in the US can already text their ChatGPT dot in a beta, and he added that he is excited to hear feedback before the new mobile design rolls out more widely.
