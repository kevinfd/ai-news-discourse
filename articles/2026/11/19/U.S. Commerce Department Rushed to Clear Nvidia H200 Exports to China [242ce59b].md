# U.S. Commerce Department Rushed to Clear Nvidia H200 Exports to China

The Information · Leo Schwartz, Qianer Liu · 2026-11-19

In recent months, Jeffrey Kessler, the official overseeing the U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security, has pressured staff to expedite the approval of sales of Nvidia ’s H200 graphics processing units to China, according to three people with knowledge of the matter. In some cases, Kessler directed staff at BIS —an agency under the Commerce Department—to approve the exports over objections from other U.S. government departments, two of those people said.
