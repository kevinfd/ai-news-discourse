# Solomonoff induction with reflective oracles

Alignment Forum · Cole Wyeth · 2026-11-19

Linkpost for https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14454v1 Our PIBBSS fellow Damini Kusum wrote an excellent paper on prediction with reflective oracle Solomonoff Induction (rOSI). Reflective oracles are best known for their game-theoretic applications, where they are often dismissed because "why would all players choose the same reflective oracle?"
