# DeepSeek Closes 100 Billion Yuan Funding Round

Caixin Global · Yang Zirui Published Nov · 2026-11-19

DeepSeek Closes 100 Billion Yuan Funding Round
Listen to the full version
Chinese AI developer DeepSeek has completed a second external funding round of about 100 billion yuan ($15 billion) as it prepares to pursue an initial public offering on Shanghai’s STAR Market.
Business registration changes completed on Tuesday show that a partnership representing the round’s new investors took a roughly 17.3% stake in DeepSeek. The company’s first major external funding round raised 50 billion yuan at a post-investment valuation of 350 billion yuan, with registration changes completed in July.
