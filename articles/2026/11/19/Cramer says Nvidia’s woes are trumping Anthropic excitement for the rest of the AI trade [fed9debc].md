# Cramer says Nvidia’s woes are trumping Anthropic excitement for the rest of the AI trade

CNBC · Alexa LoMonaco · 2026-11-19

Nvidia's deepening sell-off following last week's leadership shake-up is overwhelming the optimism surrounding Anthropic's stock market debut and hurting the rest of the artificial intelligence trade, CNBC's Jim Cramer argued Thursday.
The Claude creator raised a record-breaking $81.3 billion, and shares surged as high as $115 in their trading debut Thursday. While off the highs of the session, the stock closed up 17% from its IPO price of $83. But the excitement did little to lift the broader market, with the Nasdaq Composite and S&P 500 both falling roughly 2%. Nvidia, once the beating heart of the AI trade, dropped 3.3% for its sixth straight day of losses — a skid that began Nov. 12, the day longtime CEO Jensen Huang's abrupt resignation was announced amid a widening affair scandal.
Cramer argued that Nvidia's continued decline is dragging down other technology stocks, wiping out wealth faster than Anthropic's blockbuster debut can create it.
"Nvidia is casting a pall on the market that's tainting everything," the "Mad Money" host said.
Despite reporting blockbuster earnings earlier this week, with revenue more than doubling from a year earlier and beating expectations, Nvidia has struggled to regain investors' confidence in the wake of Huang's exit. Shares have fallen 16% since his resignation.
Cramer argued that Huang's desire to return could further complicate Nvidia's search for a permanent CEO. Any successor would face the challenge of leading a company still closely associated with its founder, raising questions about whether a new chief executive could establish authority while Huang remains influential. Nvidia's board said it is not considering bringing back Huang.
The continued chatter about a potential Huang return "makes it so that this still-giant company is sinking like a stone, destroying the savings of millionaires and regular index investors alike," Cramer said.
The damage extends well beyond Nvidia itself. As the longtime leader of the AI trade, the chipmaker has helped drive investor enthusiasm for everything from semiconductor manufacturers to data center infrastructure companies. Its continued decline is now weighing on those same stocks, Cramer said, cutting against the bullish hope that Anthropic's IPO would revive the rest of the AI trade.
