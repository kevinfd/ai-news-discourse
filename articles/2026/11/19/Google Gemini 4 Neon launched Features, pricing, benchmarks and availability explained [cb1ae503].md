# Google Gemini 4 Neon launched: Features, pricing, benchmarks and availability explained

The Hindu BusinessLine · ANI · 2026-11-19

Updated - November 20, 2026  at 04:57 PM.
| 
         Washington DC [US]
     Google has announced Gemini 4 Neon, a new mid-tier artificial intelligence (AI) model designed to deliver frontier-level intelligence while balancing performance, speed and cost across software engineering, knowledge work and scientific workflows.
As per GSM Arena, the latest addition to Google's Gemini 4 family is positioned between the company's flagship Gemini 4 Carbon and the faster, more affordable Gemini 4 Hydrogen.
According to Google's benchmark comparisons, Neon outperforms Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 and OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Sol in most listed tests.In the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, Gemini 4 Neon scored 61, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 at 59 and GPT-6.2 Sol at 57.
It also recorded a score of 70.9 per cent in the Vals Index, compared with 70.1 per cent for Opus 5.5 and 66.1 per cent for GPT-6.2 Sol. However, Claude Opus 5.5 performed better than Neon in two benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 4.1 and FrontierCode 2.0.Google also claims that Gemini 4 Neon has its lowest rate of lies yet in the company's internal testing.
Gemini 4 Neon is designed to balance the capabilities of Google's more powerful Carbon model with the efficiency of Hydrogen. While Carbon targets complex, long-horizon projects and deep research, Hydrogen focuses on speed and affordability for everyday coding and agentic workflows.
Neon aims to offer a middle ground, delivering advanced capabilities at a lower cost than Carbon. The model is priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. By comparison, Gemini 4 Carbon costs $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, while Gemini 4 Hydrogen costs $1.50 and $7.50, respectively
.Neon also features a 1-million-token context window and four thinking levels, with xhigh being the highest setting.Google claims that Gemini 4 Neon, when operating at its default high thinking setting, costs 32 USD less per task than Carbon. Another capability allows Neon to function as an orchestrator, directing multiple parallel Hydrogen subagents within Google Antigravity.
Google is rolling out Gemini 4 Neon across several of its platforms, including the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Gemini for business and Google Antigravity. The model is also available in the Gemini app through the model selection drop-down for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.
Additionally, Google plans to introduce a dedicated Gemini 4 Neon for the cyber version in the coming days. It will be made available to vetted security defenders through the company's Fairwind Program.
