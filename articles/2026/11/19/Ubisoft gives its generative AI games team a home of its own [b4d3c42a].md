# Ubisoft gives its generative AI games team a home of its own

Tbreak · Tbreak Admin · 2026-11-19

Ubisoft has put the team behind its first playable generative AI prototype into a dedicated subsidiary called Motherbrain.
Teammates, the prototype Ubisoft revealed in late 2025, was designed to let players talk to AI-powered characters. In its first-half earnings release, Ubisoft says Motherbrain is developing characters with memory and AI game masters that can manage large, complex game worlds.
It’s still unclear whether players will see Motherbrain’s work in a standalone release or in other Ubisoft games.
VGC reported that the name surfaced in a September 2026 trademark filing that pointed to a generative AI studio. Ubisoft confirmed the subsidiary as part of the restructure it began in January 2026 with the cancellation of six games. The new structure is now fully in place, with each Creative House responsible for its own profits and losses.
We have also established Motherbrain, the team behind our first playable generative AI experience Teammates, as a dedicated subsidiary developing proprietary technologies for gameplay to empower creators and open up new possibilities for richer, more immersive and engaging player experiences.
Yves Guillemot, Ubisoft CEO
Motherbrain aims to work with game creators worldwide, both inside Ubisoft and outside it, and plans to partner with selected technology companies.
The company set up Motherbrain while cutting jobs elsewhere. Shortly before the earnings release, Ubisoft confirmed it was cutting 166 roles in Montréal and Québec City. Its headcount fell by close to 1,100 over the half. Its cost-cutting plan targets a fixed cost base of €1.25bn on a run-rate basis by March 2028.
Ubisoft posted first-half net bookings of €644.7m, down 16.5% year on year. Second-quarter net bookings were €389m, beating guidance of around €370m.
Giving the Teammates team its own subsidiary makes the work more than an internal experiment: Ubisoft wants outside creators to use the technology too.
