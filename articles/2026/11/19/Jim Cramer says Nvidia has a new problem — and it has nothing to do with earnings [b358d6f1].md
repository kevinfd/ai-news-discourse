# Jim Cramer says Nvidia has a new problem — and it has nothing to do with earnings

CNBC · Alexa LoMonaco · 2026-11-19

CNBC's Jim Cramer said Wednesday that he sees another headwind for Nvidia following the abrupt resignation of longtime CEO Jensen Huang.
"You don't need to own Nvidia," the "Mad Money" host said. "Sure, the Interim CEO, Colette Kress, was brilliant on 'Mad Money' last night. The numbers weren't good; they were fabulous. But it doesn't matter. Because the market, like the tribe in Survivor, has voted Nvidia off the portfolio island."
Nvidia delivered blowout quarterly earnings Tuesday, with revenue more than doubling from a year earlier and guidance exceeding Wall Street's expectations. Complicating matters is Anthropic's blockbuster IPO, Cramer said, which is drawing investor enthusiasm further away from the chipmaker. Anthropic is expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Thursday.
To illustrate the dynamic, Cramer compared the situation to visiting a favorite restaurant and being tempted by an exciting new special. Even if customers have ordered the same beloved dish for years, the prospect of something new can make them reconsider their usual choice.
In this case, Nvidia is the tried-and-true favorite, while Anthropic is the hot new offering. The AI startup's explosive growth and highly anticipated market debut are giving investors another way to capitalize on the artificial intelligence boom, potentially reducing their appetite for Nvidia shares.
Cramer argued that the leadership uncertainty makes that decision easier. Despite Nvidia's impressive results and his praise for Kress, investors remain concerned about the company's future without Huang, the visionary founder who built it into the dominant supplier of AI chips.
"People want a company with a permanent CEO, and they want Jensen Huang," Cramer said. "They don't want to wait for Colette Kress and the board as they complete a search for a new one. ... They would rather just get some Anthropic."
