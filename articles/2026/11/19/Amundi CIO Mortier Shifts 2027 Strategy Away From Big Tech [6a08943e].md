# Amundi CIO Mortier Shifts 2027 Strategy Away From Big Tech

Bloomberg · 2026-11-19

Amundi Chief Investment Officer Vincent Mortier says the firm is reshaping its risk exposure in 2027 by moving away from capitalization-weighted benchmarks, adding commodities and gold, diversifying into emerging-markets and value stocks, and running an underweight position in broadly-defined tech amid doubts over long-term AI monetization. (Source: Bloomberg)
