# Meta introduces Muse Ember AI model, claims higher intelligence at lower cost

Moneycontrol · MC Tech Desk · 2026-11-09

15
The new model is designed for repetitive, token-intensive tasks and supports text, images, audio and video. Meta claims it can outperform competing models while costing less to operate.
  November 10, 2026 / 10:39 IST  
Meta has introduced Muse Ember, a new small AI model designed to handle high-volume tasks performed by AI agents. Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg says the model offers higher intelligence per dollar than other models in its category, making it suitable for businesses and developers looking to reduce the cost of running AI-powered workflows.
Announcing the model on X, Zuckerberg said Muse Ember was developed for token-intensive workloads that AI agents handle throughout the day. These include coding subtasks, data extraction, task routing and processing lengthy documents.
What is Meta Muse Ember and what can it do?
Unlike larger frontier AI models, Muse Ember is designed to complement more capable models rather than replace them. Meta expects developers to use it alongside models such as Muse Wonder, which can plan complex assignments before delegating repetitive tasks to Ember.
For instance, in a customer support workflow, a larger model could establish how customer complaints should be handled, while Ember processes individual cases using those instructions.
Alexandr Wang, head of Meta Superintelligence Labs, said Muse Ember is the company's first publicly released model trained using Muse Wonder Full.
The model supports text, images, audio and video inputs. This allows it to process screenshots, charts, scanned documents, voice recordings and videos without requiring separate models for different formats. It also features a context window of one million tokens and supports up to 512,000 output tokens.
Meta claims Ember outperforms competing models, including Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 and OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna, across several benchmarks. On Terminal-Bench 4.0, it scored 40.9%, compared with 39.2% for Claude Haiku 5.5 and 16.4% for GPT-6 Luna.
Muse Ember is priced at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens. Meta says it matches Claude Haiku 5.5 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at approximately one-third of the cost.
Meta brings Muse AI assistant to smartwatches
Separately, Meta has expanded its Muse personal AI assistant to Apple Watch and Wear OS devices.
Users can interact with the assistant through voice dictation or text to manage schedules, find information and make reservations. The feature requires the Muse smartphone app and a compatible smartwatch.
Muse is currently available in select markets, including the US, Canada, Singapore and Japan. Meta has not announced an India launch date.
