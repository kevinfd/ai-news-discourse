# Meta Plans Sales Team, Consulting Partnerships to Win Over Big Companies

The Information · Jyoti Mann · 2026-11-09

Meta Platforms is taking the next steps in a plan to build a new business selling its AI products to companies. 

 The company has begun forming a sales organization to sell those products to small and midsize businesses and also forge relationships with consulting firms and systems integrators, including Accenture, Deloitte and IBM, to help it sell and deploy AI products to corporate customers, according to a person familiar with the matter and job postings reviewed by The Information.
