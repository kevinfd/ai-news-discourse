# WIPO: China Leads the World in Generative AI Patents; Published Applications in 2024-2025 Exceed the Total of the Previous Decade

note · 知財エミリ · 2026-11-09

WIPO: China Leads the World in Generative AI Patents; Published Applications in 2024-2025 Exceed the Total of the Previous Decade
According to the latest data recently released by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), the landscape of patents related to generative AI (GenAI) has reached a new milestone. The number of GenAI-related patent families published in 2024 and 2025 exceeded the cumulative total of the previous ten years.
The number of published GenAI-related patent families (a common metric for measuring the scale of new inventions) increased from approximately 14,000 in 2023 to over 37,800 in 2025.
Over 56,000 new GenAI-related patent families were published in the two-year period of 2024 and 2025, surpassing the cumulative total from 2014 to 2023. GenAI-related patent families account for 8.7% of all published AI-related patent families, up from 6.1% in 2023 and 4.2% in 2017.
Of the top 10 global GenAI-related patent applicants, six are companies headquartered in China. China continues to be the world's largest country of origin for published GenAI-related patents by a wide margin.
In 2024 and 2025, Chinese inventors published over 43,000 GenAI-related patent families. The compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of GenAI-related patent families in China from 2023 to 2025 reached 64%.
In contrast, the CAGR for the United States during the same period was 92%. As the commercialization of generative AI by U.S. companies gains momentum, this activity is also leading to an acceleration in patent activity.
Japan recorded the highest relative growth rate among major inventor countries, with a CAGR of 210%. As a result, Japan's global ranking rose from fourth to third.
