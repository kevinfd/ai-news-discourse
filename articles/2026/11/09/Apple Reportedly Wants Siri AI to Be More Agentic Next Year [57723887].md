# Apple Reportedly Wants Siri AI to Be More Agentic Next Year

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-11-09

Earlier this year, Apple updated Siri AI with personal context understanding, on-screen awareness, and the ability to take some basic actions within the company’s apps. Deeper third-party integration for Siri AI is still to arrive on iOS 27, but a new report says Apple wants its assistant to be “more like an agent as early as next year.”
Even after its recent overhaul, Siri largely remains a request-based AI assistant. An AI agent, however, operates differently by tracking multiple past and ongoing activities, proactively suggesting actions, and completing them upon approval. OpenAI’s Dots and Meta’s Muse already promise similar agentic capabilities, and sources tell Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman that “Apple ultimately wants to go in this direction.”
Gurman also reports that Apple CEO John Ternus has been frustrated by the company's lack of urgency in AI development and recently held a closed-door meeting with software chief Craig Federighi to accelerate Siri’s growth.
Apple has long lagged behind competitors in integrating AI into its products. Siri’s personal context awareness and ability to take cross-app actions were first promised with the iPhone 16, but the company failed to deliver them on time and faced multiple lawsuits over the delay. After a personnel change and a new AI partnership with Google, Apple finally announced the long-overdue features at WWDC 2026.
With iOS 27, Siri can now access your photos, emails, messages, calendar, and more to answer questions about your flight or hotel bookings. Using Visual Intelligence, it can read content on your screen or even in your camera’s viewfinder to answer questions.
With the iOS 27.3 beta last week, Apple also announced that third-party developers could begin integrating Siri’s new capabilities, meaning the assistant may soon be able to take cross-app actions, such as making a restaurant reservation or ordering food online.
