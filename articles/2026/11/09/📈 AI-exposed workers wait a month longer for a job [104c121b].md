# 📈 AI-exposed workers wait a month longer for a job

Exponential View · Azeem Azhar · 2026-11-09

In the AI Investment Brief today: The funding quality is still a concern even as hyperscalers move into long‑dated, investment‑grade debt.
Hi,
Here’s the latest roundup of data signals across AI, energy & markets from Exponential View.
- Anthropic’s place in the queue. Anthropic paid $3.4 billion to take over a supplier’s two-year-old order for 1.7GW of gas generators – the largest known power-equipment purchase by an AI lab.
- VC in 2026 is about two giants. Take a16z out, and US fundraising looks much like 2025. Without SpaceX, this year’s exits are close to last year’s.
- Memory hoarders. Apple’s memory inventory grew 2.5x year on year, helping build protection against memory cost increases.
- Re-application slowdown. Workers let go of jobs in AI-exposed roles in 2025 took about a month longer than peers in other roles to get a new job.
