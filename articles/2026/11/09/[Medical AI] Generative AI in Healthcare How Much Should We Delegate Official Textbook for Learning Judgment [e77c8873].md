# [Medical AI] Generative AI in Healthcare: How Much Should We Delegate? Official Textbook for Learning Judgment Skills Now Available for Pre-order

note · 猫 · 2026-11-09

[Medical AI] Generative AI in Healthcare: How Much Should We Delegate? Official Textbook for Learning Judgment Skills Now Available for Pre-order
Hello, this is Neko @ Medical Information Technologist ᐕ)ﾉ
I want to get help from AI for patient explanations and documents used within the hospital. However, there are times when I hesitate, wondering, "Is it okay to input this information?" or "To what extent can I trust the generated text?"
The official textbook for the "Medical Generative AI Specialist Examination," which includes such judgment skills in its learning scope, has begun accepting pre-orders. As a teaching material that covers not only AI operation but also the handling of patient information and verification of responses, I will organize the official information on what it covers and who can use it.
An examination to verify the knowledge and judgment of those using AI in healthcare
On November 6, 2026, GENSHI AI announced that it has been entrusted with the operation of the "Medical Generative AI Specialist Examination," hosted by the Medical Generative AI Promotion Association, and has begun accepting pre-orders for the official textbook.
The association is responsible for hosting and certification, while GENSHI AI is in charge of exam operations and textbook production. Daichi Nagashima, Representative Director of GENSHI AI, also serves as the Representative Director of the association.
This examination targets the basic knowledge required to use generative AI in the medical field and the ability to judge safe and ethical usage. In addition to doctors, nurses, and pharmacists, medical office staff, administrative department employees, and students can also take the exam, regardless of age, educational background, occupation, or qualifications held.
Learning both "whether it is okay to input" and "whether the output can be used"
Even if you can ask AI questions, it does not necessarily mean you can use them directly in your work. When handling text containing patient information, you must discern the input content, and when using the generated text, you must exercise judgment to ensure a human verifies it for errors or biases.
The learning scope of the exam covers the following five areas:
- Basics of Generative AI: Understanding the mechanisms, strengths and weaknesses, and "hallucinations" that create plausible errors.
- Utilization in Healthcare: Learning how to use it for document creation, summarization, patient explanation, education, research, etc., and human verification.
- Medical Information and Personal Information Protection: Handling patient information and knowledge for judging what information to input.
- Legal and Ethical Issues: Dealing with copyright, accountability, bias, and fairness.
- Risk Management: Learning workplace usage rules, usage records, output verification, and how to respond when problems occur.
For example, responding to "Shadow AI," where AI is used outside of workplace approval and management, is also part of the background of this learning. The structure connects everything from how to give convenient instructions to what humans should judge before and after using it. is the focus.
Official textbook is 3,480 yen, scheduled to be shipped sequentially from December
The official name of the teaching material is "Medical AI Specialist Official Textbook 2026 Edition." Edited and published by the association, it summarizes everything from AI mechanisms to medical utilization, information protection, and laws and ethics, following the five areas of the exam.
In addition to how to give instructions and verify answers, it also covers the mechanisms of AI agents that carry out multiple tasks, as well as the scope that humans should judge and verify. The book's specifications are as follows:
- Format: B5 size.
- Price: 3,480 yen per copy (tax included); shipping for a single copy is 550 yen (tax included).
- Shipping: Scheduled to ship sequentially starting December 2026.
The 2026 edition is for exams held by March 2027. As of November 9, 2026, it is in the pre-order stage, and you can preview pages regarding AI mechanisms and data input risks.
The exam is taken on a PC with a camera, with the next one scheduled for spring 2027
The exam is taken via the internet. The currently published guidelines specify a 60-minute, 50-question multiple-choice format, and for identity verification and proctoring, a Windows or macOS computer with a camera is required.
Taking the exam also involves preparing the dedicated browser 'Safe Exam Browser'; smartphones and tablets are not supported. Even though it is a format that can be taken from home, you must prepare your device and testing environment.
The second exam is scheduled for spring 2027, with the application and testing periods yet to be determined. The currently published exam fee of 9,800 yen (tax included) and other details are for the first exam, so please treat them separately from the next recruitment conditions. Pre-ordering the textbook and registering for the exam are separate procedures.
