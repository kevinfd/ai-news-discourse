# Trump’s 60 Minutes interview touched on AI safety, threat to arrest an AI exec

The Verge · Stevie Bonifield · 2026-11-09

Asked about AI executives’ warnings about their models’ safety on 60 Minutes, Trump said, “I think that the risk is not nearly as real as it– as they talk, and I think the risk can be managed, and it’s managed.” He claimed the DOJ is managing that risk, saying, “We had a situation where somebody was reported to us to be doing something bad with AI. We called them very strongly and said, ‘Get ready to go to jail. Please give your wife a kiss good night.’”

Trump claims the DOJ told a top AI executive, ‘get ready to go to jail.’
Trump refused to name the person or company he was referring to, but said it was the leader of an AI company, “One of the big ones.” He claimed that after the threat of arrest, “this person totally changed everything.” Despite previously claiming that “artificial intelligence” is a term used by people he considers the enemy, Trump said AI rather than SI or super intelligence multiple times during the interview. Eventually he said China’s Xi is “…all in to SI, as I call it, because it really is super intelligence. It’s a much better name, I think, because it’s not artificial.”
60 Minutes’s Ross Douthat went on to ask if Trump would work with Democrats on AI safety, and Trump said he “absolutely would,” adding, “I don’t mind guardrails, but I don’t want to put us in a deep second position. We’re leading number one. And China has no guardrails, by the way. [...] They have the guardrails that whatever President Xi says goes. With me, it’s pretty strong, but it’s not quite that strong.”
