# What to Know About Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s Affair With Suspected Chinese Spy

Bloomberg · Edwin Chan · 2026-11-09

Nvidia Corp., the world’s second-most valuable company and the US chipmaker at the heart of the global AI industry, is facing a crisis like none other in its three-decade history. On Nov. 8, Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang admitted he had been unfaithful in his marriage, effectively confirming a series of reports linking him romantically with a woman who has been identified as a suspected Chinese spy and whose company bought chips from Nvidia.
