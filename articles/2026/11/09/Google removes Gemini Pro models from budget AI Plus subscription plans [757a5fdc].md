# Google removes Gemini Pro models from budget AI Plus subscription plans

ARY News · Kumail Shah · 2026-11-09

Google removes Gemini Pro models from budget AI Plus subscription plans
- By Kumail Shah -
- Nov 09, 2026
Google will no longer offer Pro model access with its cheapest paid Gemini plan. In an email to subscribers, shared by 9to5Google in mid-October, Google explained that starting November 13, “the ability to select Pro models in both the Gemini app and AI Mode in Google Search will only be available to Google AI Pro subscribers and above.”
Google AI Plus subscribers will still have access to the faster models. The email says, “You’ll still have access to our latest Flash-Lite model and Flash.” Previous conversations are not deleted: “Your previous chats using the Pro model can be continued with a different model.”
According to Google’s Gemini subscription page, AI Plus now only includes access to the Flash model. We checked the page on November 9. Gemini 4 Argon and the Pro model are available starting with Google AI Pro, which costs $19.99 per month in the US, while AI Plus is $4.99. In Germany, the prices are €4.99 and €21.99. Google has not explained the reason for this change or announced any compensation for current subscribers.
There are other differences between the plans besides model choice. Google’s Gemini help page says AI Plus offers usage limits “2x higher than standard limits” and a context window of 128,000 tokens. AI Pro offers “4x higher than standard limits” and a context window of 1 million tokens. This difference is most important if you upload long PDFs or large file sets.
Flash works well for most short questions, emails, and summaries, and it responds quickly. Pro models provide more detailed answers for longer research, coding questions, or complex math. Think about how often you used Pro recently. If you rarely used it, this change will have little impact on you.
Before you decide to pay $15 more each month, consider comparing other options. Our AI subscription price comparison shows current plans from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot. Some Gemini features, like editing generated PDFs in chat, are also free. According to Google’s email, you can manage or cancel your subscription whenever you want.
