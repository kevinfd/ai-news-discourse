Tradition and Mission
Now in its sixth year, 365 Data Science continues its commitment to accessible, high-quality education through its annual Free Access Initiative, first launched during the global pandemic in 2020. The initiative’s impact has expanded significantly with 2025 marking its most successful edition to date. More than 220,000 unique users from 216 countries participated, engaging in over 7.2 million minutes of learning and earning more than 36,000 certificates.
Krastev remarks, “Artificial intelligence is transforming industries at an unprecedented pace. Understanding how AI systems work and how to build, deploy, and integrate them has become essential for anyone pursuing a data-driven career. At 365 Data Science, we aim to bridge that gap by empowering learners to gain not only data literacy but also practical expertise in AI engineering and intelligent agents: the skills defining the next generation of tech professionals.”
 
365 Data Science helps learners move beyond traditional data analytics into the era of AI engineering and intelligent agents, equipping them with the skills to design, deploy, and collaborate with AI systems that can reason, plan, and act autonomously.

During this special period, learners will enjoy unlimited access to the entire 365 Data Science platform: a comprehensive hub for mastering AI and data. Participants can build hands-on experience through real AI and data projects that simulate practical work scenarios, helping them apply their knowledge in meaningful ways. Newly added interactive exercises and guided challenges further reinforce key concepts and help deepen understanding.
In addition, 365 Data Science provides structured, career-oriented learning paths that guide users step by step: from beginner to job-ready professional, offering a clear roadmap to success in today’s AI-powered world.
Certifications that Open Doors
In today’s rapidly evolving job market, recognized certifications are key to standing out. Through this free access initiative, 365 Data Science provides learners with the opportunity to earn industry-recognized certificates at no cost.

The initiative bridges the gap between learning and professional recognition by offering verifiable, career-boosting certifications that showcase real-world competence.
