Amazon just knocked $100 off the Google Pixel Watch 5, bringing the 41mm WiFi model down to $299.99, its lowest price yet.
If you have been waiting for the right moment to pick up our favorite Android smartwatch without paying full price, this limited-time deal completely wipes out the launch-day price bump.
When our team rounded up the year's best smartwatches, the Pixel Watch 5 took top honors for Android users. Last year's Pixel Watch 4 was already a running favorite for former Mashable reporter Christianna Silva, but the 5 earned its upgrade by adding a faster processor, double the storage, faster charging, and more accurate GPS route tracking for workouts.

It also sports a domed Actua 360 display shielded by scratch-resistant Gorilla Glass, along with up to 40 hours of battery life to comfortably get you through a day and a half between charges.
A lot of that appeal comes down to how smoothly it fits into a typical routine. Google built Gemini directly into the device, acting as a helpful companion on your wrist. It can display flight details on the way to the airport or suggest contact numbers while you text about dinner plans, and certain Gemini features even function offline when your phone lacks service.
Health tracking also sees a notable upgrade. Along with heart rate metrics and nightly sleep-stage summaries, the Pixel Watch 5 monitors long-term trends in blood pressure and insulin resistance. It also checks breathing regularity while you sleep and includes Loss of Pulse Detection, which can prompt an emergency call if it detects you need immediate help.
Grab the Google Pixel Watch 5 while it's down to its lowest-ever price.
