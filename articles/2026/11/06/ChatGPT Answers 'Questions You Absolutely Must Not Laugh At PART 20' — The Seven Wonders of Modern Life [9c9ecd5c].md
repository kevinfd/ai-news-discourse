how many people can you actually call?
Connecting and 
understanding each other might be two different things.
2. Why are we busy every day even though tools to save time have increased?
Emails are instant. 
Shopping takes only a few minutes. 
AI even helps with work.
And yet, 
we fill our spare time with other work.
Perhaps modern people 
are not running out of time, 
but are simply bad at leaving time empty.
3. Why are there more people who cannot make decisions even though there are more choices?

Being able to choose should be a source of happiness.
But when there are too many options, 
the act of choosing itself becomes a job.
Freedom needs a little break, too.
4. Why do you pack your schedule even on holidays when you want to rest?
A holiday morning.
"Since I have the chance, I should do something!"
Shopping, cleaning, studying, exercising.
Before you know it, you're busier than on a weekday.
And on Sunday night, 
you mutter, "I don't feel like I've rested at all."
Working hard even at resting.
This, too, is a strange habit of modern people.
5. Why do you keep looking at social media while claiming you have no interest in others?
"Others are others, I am me."
Even though I think that, 
I end up looking at someone's post.
Travel, meals, career success.
And, for some reason, I feel down.
Seeing someone else's happiness, 
even though there should be no need to diminish my own.

A new smartphone. 
Convenient home appliances. 
High-performance computers.
If I buy them, life will be easier.
But, after a while, 
I want something new again.
Convenience has no end point.
Being able to think "that's enough" at some point

7. You know many ways to become happy, so why can't you feel happy?
A healthy lifestyle. 
An ideal way of working. 
How to build relationships.

Happiness

but perhaps something to feel.
