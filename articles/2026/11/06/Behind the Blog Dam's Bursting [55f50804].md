# Behind the Blog: Dam's Bursting

404 Media · Samantha Cole · 2026-11-06

This is Behind the Blog, where we share our behind-the-scenes thoughts about how a few of our top stories of the week came together. This week, we discuss election outcomes and code as a human endeavor.
JOSEPH: This week I got a real wave of ‘oh man this fucking sucks’ regarding AI. I won’t link to it because it’s just a rando person, but scrolling Reddit I saw someone who said they used Claude to build stuff with Max. Max, or Max/MSP, is a visual programming language that is heavily used in the world of music and visual art. It is the foundation of a lot of electronic music. You can build essentially anything in it, if you put in the work to understand how to put it together. And that can be a lot of work.
I’ve built various little things in Max. I made a tool that takes a drum break and generates new, random drum and bass style breaks from it. I am a big, big fan of generative music (both listening and making) where you create rules and parameters, press play, and let the rules decide what comes out. I made another that has various synthesizers, and from scratch manipulates the audio so they sound like Tibetan singing bowls, with their harmonies and individual melodies morphing over time. I also started working on the visual part of Max, called Jitter, to take audio output from Max and generate visuals.
