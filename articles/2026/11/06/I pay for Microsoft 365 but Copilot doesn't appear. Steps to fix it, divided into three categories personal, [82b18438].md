# I pay for Microsoft 365 but Copilot doesn't appear. Steps to fix it, divided into three categories: personal, company, and settings

note · やさしいAi設定室｜画面どおりの手順書 · 2026-11-06

I pay for Microsoft 365 but Copilot doesn't appear. Steps to fix it, divided into three categories: personal, company, and settings
My colleague's screen shows Copilot, but there is no button in my Word
Even though I have a Microsoft 365 subscription, I cannot find the Copilot button when I open Word or Excel. It appears on my colleague's screen, but not on mine. When I search online, I see unfamiliar terms like "license," "administrator," and "account," and I get lost on where to look. This article will guide you through checking things step-by-step, from your current state to either getting it to "appear" or understanding why it doesn't.
Who this article is not for
- People who only use the Mac or mobile versions of Word and Excel. The names of screens may differ, as this article's steps are primarily intended for the Windows PC version (the Mac version has different screen names, such as settings being located under "Preferences").
- People who already have the button and want to know how to use it.
- People in charge of company IT systems who configure the administrator-side screens. This article is a guide for the "user side."
Overview
Copilot is an AI that helps you create documents and spreadsheets. There are four things to check as reasons why the button might not appear. You won't get lost if you check them in order from the top.
- Which account are you currently signed in with?
- Does that account have a subscription that allows the use of Copilot?
- Are Word and Excel up to date?
- Is Copilot turned off in the app settings?
The required time is about 15 minutes if you don't need to contact an administrator for a company account. If you need to ask an administrator, additional time will be required to wait for a response.
In this article, we will proceed by dividing the reader's situation into three categories: A: Individuals paying for their own subscription. B: People with accounts provided by a company or school. C: People whose contract and account are correct, but the app is outdated or the setting is off. If you decide which one you are first, you can skip the steps you don't need. For those in category B, since you may not be able to fix it yourself, we have prepared examples of how to communicate with your administrator and how to proceed. We will guide you to the point where you know what to tell your administrator, even if the button still doesn't appear.
Note that starting in April 2026, the location of the Copilot button in Word and Excel began to change. Microsoft's official page states that the entry point for Copilot is being consolidated into a button fixed to the bottom right corner of the document (the Copilot dynamic action button), with rollout beginning in April 2026 and expected to be completed by June. If you are only looking within the ribbon (the strip where buttons are lined up) at the top of the screen, you might miss it.
What is included in the paid section ahead
- Three ways to categorize your situation (Personal subscription / Company or school account / App is outdated or setting is off)
- Four confirmation steps, including screen names and button names.
- For those with company/school accounts: How to proceed when asking an administrator (four steps: information to capture, how to send it, and how to categorize after receiving a reply).
- Common pitfalls and how to deal with them.
- Three ready-to-use examples (inquiry to administrator, request for Word, request for Excel)
- How to check if it is finished
