Summary
- Meta Platforms is rated Hold, trading near my $624 fair value, reflecting concerns over rising AI-driven costs and margin compression.
- META's robust ad business is funding a massive AI infrastructure buildout, with 2026 capex expected at $135–145 billion versus $72.2 billion in 2025.
- I forecast 2027 EPS at $31.2, about 12% below consensus, as depreciation and operating expenses from AI investments weigh on profitability.
- Free cash flow is weakening, and the upcoming Class C share distribution introduces dilution risk; a more attractive entry point would be around $562.
