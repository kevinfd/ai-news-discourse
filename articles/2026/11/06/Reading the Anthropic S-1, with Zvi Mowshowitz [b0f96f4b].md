Postscript: Q3 was a supply story, not a demand story
Patrick: After recording the episode, I thought through this a bit more, including with Claude assisting, and have a better hypothesis.

Anthropic seems to have underestimated or —in Zvi's parlance elsewhere—perhaps simply not to have been aggressive enough.
Their Q3 revenue softening isn't a demand story. It's a supply story. They're running all the GPUs they have, and it just isn't enough. Either because you want to make the principled decision that you don't want to climb the cost curve in the spot market and sell at wafer-thin margins or for a loss just to inflate your top-line metrics, or perhaps because you just tapped all the spot that capitalism had available in Q3 2026.
I think analysts who read this as softening demand would need to explain tapping spot for 50% of one's spend. I frankly don't think there is an explanation available there. These analysts are just straightforwardly wrong. Even if one allocates all of the spot compute to training runs for Opus 6.x or whatever, that's still saying they fully saturated the long-term contracted chips, and their customers still had tokens waiting.
