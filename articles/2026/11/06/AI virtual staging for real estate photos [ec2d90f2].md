Frequently asked questions
What is virtual staging?
Virtual staging adds furniture and decor to a photo of an empty room, so buyers and tenants can picture the space. It costs a fraction of physical staging and takes seconds instead of days.
Will the walls, windows or floors change?
They shouldn't, and we check. The AI is told to keep the room exactly as photographed, and every result is compared with your photo. Results that move a window or change the floor are re-rendered or discarded; if nothing passes, your credit is refunded.
How long does it take?
Usually 20–60 seconds for two options. You can leave the page; the result is saved in My stagings.
Which photos work best?
Empty rooms photographed horizontally from a corner, in daylight, as JPG, PNG or WebP up to 20 MB. Rooms with some leftover items work too, but decluttering comes in a later update.
Can I ask for specific furniture?
Yes. Write a short request such as “a red armchair in front of the window” in any language. Requests that would change the room itself (for example repainting walls) are left out.
Do I have to disclose that a photo is virtually staged?
In most markets, yes. Every image carries a small “Virtually Staged” label. Please also follow the rules of your listing portal or MLS.
How do credits work?
One credit stages one photo and gives you two options. With purchased credits the first re-style of the same photo is free; every other re-style uses half a credit. Subscription credits roll over for 1–3 months depending on the plan; pack credits never expire.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the billing page. Credits you already have stay valid until they expire.
Who owns the images?
You do. You must have the right to use the photos you upload; we use them only to provide the service.
