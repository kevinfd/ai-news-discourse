# AI's Fundamentals Have Not Been Derailed, ING's Treon Says

Bloomberg · 2026-11-06

Anneka Treon, global head of private banking, wealth management and investments at ING, says companies across technology and other industries continue to report tangible value from AI and a desire to invest further, indicating strong demand. "Has all of that been derailed because of the news of the last two days? I think that would be a bit overstated," she tells Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
