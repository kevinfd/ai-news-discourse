# Weekly Startup Funding Roundup: Fal Secures USD 1.3 Billion, Nooks Raises USD 500 Million

Analytics Insight · Somatirtha, Manisha Sharma · 2026-11-06

News
Weekly Startup Funding Roundup: Fal Secures USD 1.3 Billion, Nooks Raises USD 500 Million
Fal raised USD 1.3 billion at a USD 16 billion valuation, while Nooks secured USD 500 million. Titan OS, Supa and Archera also closed rounds, showing strong investor focus on AI infrastructure, automation and cost optimization.
The global startup sector saw several significant deals this week, with AI, smart television technology, property management, and cloud cost optimization attracting investor interest.
AI inference startup Fal led the funding activity with a USD 1.3 billion Series F round, taking its valuation to USD 16 billion. Meanwhile, sales automation startup Nooks secured a USD 500 million financing commitment from General Catalyst.
Other notable deals included Titan OS raising USD 46 million, UK-based Supa securing USD 28 million, and cloud cost management company Archera attracting USD 15 million in Series C funding.
AI inference startup Fal raised USD 1.3 billion in its latest Series F funding round, doubling its valuation to USD 16 billion. Bessemer Venture Partners led the round, with participation from Nvidia, Sound Ventures and T. Rowe Price.
The company plans to use the capital to expand its AI inference infrastructure and meet growing demand for hosting video, image, and audio generation models. The investment also strengthens its ability to support open-source AI models as businesses increasingly rely on generative AI applications.
AI sales startup Nooks secured a USD 500 million financing commitment through General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund. The revenue-based financing facility will support sales and marketing expansion, business growth, and the development of new AI-powered products.
Its AI Revenue Agent Platform automates sales activities, covering processes from initial customer engagement to renewals.
Smart TV operating system company Titan OS raised USD 46 million through an equity and debt financing round. Funds and accounts managed by BlackRock participated alongside existing investors Highland Europe and Mangrove Capital Partners.
The company will use the funding to enter new markets, establish partnerships, and improve AI-powered content discovery and advertising recommendations. It also plans to strengthen its operating system and monetization capabilities.
UK-based property management startup Supa secured USD 28 million in seed funding through a combination of equity and debt. The round was co-led by 2150 and OpenOcean, with participation from KOMPAS VC, Actia Capital Partners and HEARTFELT. A major UK lender provided the debt facility.
Supa plans to expand its AI-powered residential property management platform, acquire local property management businesses, and automate administrative operations.
Cloud cost management startup Archera raised USD 15 million in a Series C round led by Pivot Investment Partners, with existing investor HighSage Ventures also participating.
The company will expand its Guaranteed Commitments offering and cloud cost management tools, including FinOps Agent and MCP. The funding will also support international expansion across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Japan and Latin America.
The week's funding activity highlights continued investor interest in AI infrastructure and automation, as well as technology platforms addressing operational costs and established industry challenges.
