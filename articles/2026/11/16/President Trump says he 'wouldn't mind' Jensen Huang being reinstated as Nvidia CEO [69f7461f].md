# President Trump says he 'wouldn't mind' Jensen Huang being reinstated as Nvidia CEO

Euronews · Pascale Davies · 2026-11-16

The comments come as a petition calls on Nvidia to reinstate Huang.
US President Donald Trump said he “wouldn’t mind” seeing Jensen Huang being reinstated as Nvidia CEO following his resignation last week amid reports of extramarital affairs, including with an alleged Chinese spy.
"I would not mind that," he told reporters on Monday.
"I think he learned a big lesson," he added.
The comments come as tech executives, including Elon Musk, have called for Huang's reinstatement in an online petition, which has gained more than 160,000 signatures.
Asked if he trusts US intelligence that Huang was having an affair with a Chinese spy, Trump replied, "Well, I don’t know that she’s a Chinese spy."
"Any woman coming to the country that’s a beautiful woman that comes from China they say she’s a spy," he said. "I don’t know that she was a spy at all," he added.
Huang and Trump had formed a close relationship, with Huang becoming a key voice for the US on artificial intelligence while he also lobbied for allowing chip exports to China.
Trump left on Monday for a trip to Asia that includes a stop in China and the Philippines.
The trip will discuss economics, among other topics and include a meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping.
Huang is not scheduled to join the trip but Tesla and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk will join some of the voyage.
