# 🔮 What Anthropic is really listing

Exponential View · Azeem Azhar · 2026-11-16

Azeem’s sketchbook is an experimental format we’re trialing for paying members, where we share raw research notes, early frameworks, and unfinished ideas. They are the earliest indication of what I’m thinking through.
It’s Anthropic’s big week. It will become the first real AI company in the West to list on a public market.
Anthropic has an extraordinary market opportunity. The battle hangs on the sustainability of its business model – whether falling token costs or competition from open-weight models won’t simply erode its margins. These forces, no doubt, exist. The bear case claims the company is merely a peddler of progressively devalued tokens. This misses the point.
Anthropic is building an ever-improving system that produces machine intelligence. That machine intelligence is valuable because of the nature of modern economies. Most value in our economies now comes from intangibles, scientific knowledge, patents, software, brand, and data. If Anthropic’s machine intelligence can create intangibles like these – breakthrough science, information, or software – then it should be able to create extraordinary value.
Over the past fifty years, value in the S&P 500 has shifted from tangibles, things you can count in warehouses or can stub your toe on. Most value now resides in that ethereal flim-flam of the intangible: brand, customer lists, patents, and software.
Put another way, Anthropic’s machine intelligence lets it compete for 92% of the value of the S&P 500 before it needs to deploy a single robot.
That is the big macro-level survey that paints the opportunity. But I’ve been digging deeper. My conclusion is that AI companies in general, and Anthropic, are in a unique position. Look at the chart below. Each time Anthropic announced a new product feature that encroached on a given sector, the stock prices of firms in that sector responded as you’d expect.
AI companies might be able to extract a permanent tithe on the economy. Anthropic, as the leading player, is best positioned to do that.
The following are my sketches. They’ll give you a sense of the direction that I think merits further exploration.
Capturing the intangible
Anthropic has three approaches to capturing value in an intangible economy:
- It can ask companies to pay to defend their assets. As I wrote in The Internet’s Wuhan moment, AI has laid bare the software’s non-existent security model. Anthropic’s Mythos is the world’s most powerful security-oriented AI.
