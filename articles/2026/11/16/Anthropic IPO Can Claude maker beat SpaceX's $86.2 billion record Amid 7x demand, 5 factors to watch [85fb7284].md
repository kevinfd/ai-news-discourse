# Anthropic IPO: Can Claude maker beat SpaceX's $86.2 billion record? Amid 7x demand, 5 factors to watch

Moneycontrol · Surabhi Pandey · 2026-11-16

15
Trending Topics
Anthropic's market debut could also set the tone for other anticipated mega IPOs, including that of OpenAI.
  November 16, 2026 / 16:13 IST  
Anthropic's much-anticipated initial public offering (IPO) has attracted bids for nearly seven times the shares on offer, setting the stage for what could become the biggest IPO fundraising in history, surpassing Elon Musk's SpaceX offering.
The maker of AI chatbot Claude is seeking to raise up to $64.4 billion through its public offering, alongside a separate $26 billion private placement. Together, the transactions could generate $90.4 billion, exceeding the $86.2 billion raised by SpaceX in June.
Anthropic is expected to price its IPO on November 18 and begin trading on the Nasdaq on November 19 under the ticker ANT. Here are five key things to know about the proposed mega offering.
- $1.4 trillion valuation puts Anthropic among the world's most valuable companies
Anthropic is targeting a fully diluted valuation of $1.3 trillion-$1.4 trillion, with an IPO price range of $70-$78 per share. It plans to offer 825 million Class A shares.
The proposed valuation is below the $2 trillion figure discussed months ago but remains significantly higher than the $1.1 trillion valuation suggested by some Wall Street observers during the recent technology stock selloff.
- $90.4 billion fundraising could eclipse SpaceX
At the upper end of the price range, Anthropic's IPO could raise $64.4 billion. The additional $26 billion private placement would take the combined fundraising to around $90.4 billion.
That would exceed the $86.2 billion raised through SpaceX's June offering. If underwriters exercise the over-allotment option, the total could cross $100 billion.

- Tech giants line up as strategic investors
The private placement has attracted major technology investors, including Alphabet, Nvidia, Microsoft, AMD, Broadcom, Micron and TSMC.
Alphabet and Nvidia are expected to invest around $10 billion each, while AMD has committed about $2.5 billion. Microsoft, Micron and Broadcom are among the investors putting in $1 billion each.
Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are leading the IPO, with 20 other banks participating.
- Revenue surges, but computing costs remain a concern
Its enterprise customer base has also expanded sharply. The number of customers generating more than $100,000 in annual revenue rose to around 11,900 from over 950 a year earlier.
However, the company faces substantial infrastructure commitments. Its signed commitments for computing resources, infrastructure and leases total around $900.9 billion. The scale of these obligations highlights the high costs of developing and operating increasingly powerful AI models.
- Can Anthropic sustain the momentum after its IPO?
Strong subscription demand points to significant investor appetite, but the real test will come once the shares begin trading. Investors will be watching whether the IPO pricing leaves room for gains and whether the company can sustain its rapid growth.
The bigger challenge is converting its expanding revenue base and AI position into durable profitability while managing enormous computing costs.
