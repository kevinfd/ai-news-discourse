# RYZE partners with DIGITX to expand AI tools for young users

The Business Standard · TBS Report · 2026-11-16

RYZE partners with DIGITX to expand AI tools for young users
RYZE, Banglalink's youth-focused digital lifestyle brand, has signed an agreement with AI technology provider DIGITX to expand the range of AI tools available to young users in Bangladesh.
DIGITX will provide technical expertise and development support to build on RYZE's existing AI services. The two organisations will explore new ways to use AI across the platform, with a focus on making its tools easier to use and more relevant to users' interests and everyday needs, according to the press release.
RYZE Head KM Raihan and DIGITX Founder and CEO Amit Das signed the agreement on behalf of their respective organisations on 12 November.
Banglalink Chief Corporate and Regulatory Affairs Officer Taimur Rahman, RYZE Product Senior Manager Md Moshiur Rahman Aunik, Senior Category Manager Sheikh Shawkat Hossain and DIGITX Director of Operations Arnab Biswas attended the signing ceremony.
The partnership will focus on making AI tools more accessible and personalised for young people who use digital platforms to learn, create content and find information.
"Access to AI should not be limited to people who have the technical knowledge to use complex tools. Its real potential lies in helping every individual to learn something new, express their ideas and get things done in ways that were previously difficult. Our goal as an operator is to make AI and AI tools accessible to everyone through our digital lifestyle brand, RYZE," said Taimur Rahman.
Amit Das said, "Our partnership with a leading brand like RYZE will open new possibilities for AI technology in Bangladesh. Through this collaboration, we aim to bring world-class AI experiences to consumers."
The agreement is part of RYZE's broader plan to build an AI-powered digital lifestyle platform and give young people more ways to use the technology in their daily lives.
