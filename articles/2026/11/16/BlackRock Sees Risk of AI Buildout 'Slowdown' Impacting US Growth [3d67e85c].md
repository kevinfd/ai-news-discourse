# BlackRock Sees Risk of AI Buildout 'Slowdown' Impacting US Growth

Bloomberg · 2026-11-16

Jean Boivin, head of BlackRock Investment Institute, says uncertainty about AI adoption and its eventual financial payoff is becoming harder to set aside as the buildout progresses. "Markets and the economy go quite closely together," Boivin tells Bloomberg Television. "If we are to see some slowdown in the buildout, that's gonna have some real economy impact that's going to be material," he adds. (Source: Bloomberg)
