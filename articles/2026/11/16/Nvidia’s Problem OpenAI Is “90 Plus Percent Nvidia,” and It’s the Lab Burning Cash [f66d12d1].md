# Nvidia’s Problem: OpenAI Is “90 Plus Percent Nvidia,” and It’s the Lab Burning Cash

Yahoo! Finance · ThereWas · 2026-11-16

Key Takeaways
- OpenAI's adjusted operating margin is negative 109%; Anthropic's is about 15% positive on an adjusted basis.
- OpenAI runs 90-plus percent on NVIDIA; Anthropic, one third or less, per SemiAnalysis's Dylan Patel.
- NVIDIA has put its balance sheet behind OpenAI, including a lease guarantee capped at $105 billion.
- NVIDIA's lab exposure makes its growth riskier than its estimates suggest, while Broadcom stands to gain more from Anthropic.
OpenAI is reportedly raising $15 billion in a new round. That matters more to NVIDIA (NVDA:NASDAQ) than to most companies, because OpenAI runs almost entirely on its chips.
SemiAnalysis's Dylan Patel made the point on TBPN after Jensen Huang's exit: "OpenAI has their own chip and has been working on it longer, but they're 90 plus percent NVIDIA, whereas Anthropic is like one third or less NVIDIA."
Anthropic buys much of its compute from Alphabet (GOOG) and Amazon (AMZN:NASDAQ), and Broadcom (AVGO:NASDAQ) helps design Alphabet's TPUs. Anthropic is expected to start trading on the Nasdaq on Nov. 19. OpenAI is the one losing far more.
NVIDIA's Biggest Lab Is Losing the Most
OpenAI's third-quarter revenue rose about 50% to $10 billion, while its adjusted operating loss widened to $10.9 billion, according to investor documents reviewed by the Wall Street Journal. That's an adjusted margin of negative 109%, from about negative 150%, CNBC's Kate Rooney said on the Halftime Report.
Anthropic's adjusted margin is about 15% positive, Rooney said, though its adjustments exclude items such as stock-based compensation. On a reported basis, Anthropic's IPO filing shows a $567 million operating loss on $17 billion of third-quarter revenue.
Hyperscalers Still Carry the Numbers
NVIDIA has demand well beyond OpenAI. Management said in February that the top five cloud providers and hyperscalers make up a little over half of its data center revenue, though some of that capacity serves labs like OpenAI:
Analysts expect revenue to climb from $216 billion in the year ended January 2026 to $896 billion in the year ending January 2029. With next year's growth supply-limited, other buyers would likely take chips a strained OpenAI couldn't.
NVIDIA Put Its Balance Sheet Behind OpenAI
The bigger exposure is support NVIDIA chose to give. It has guaranteed parts of the lease and power payments on OpenAI's Ohio campus, capped at $105 billion, phasing in from 2028 and payable only if OpenAI defaults. NVIDIA is now expected to slow its AI investing and credit guarantees, the Information reported.
