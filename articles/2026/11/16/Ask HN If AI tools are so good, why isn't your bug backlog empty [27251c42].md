# Ask HN: If AI tools are so good, why isn't your bug backlog empty?

Hacker News · ex-aws-dude · 2026-11-16

A lot of people outside tech ask me if AI is going to replace programmers and I just think: if that were the case we should be running out of things to do The bug backlog should be empty and we've caught up to every feature request from PMs to the point that we are bottlenecked on the business side But that does not seem to be the case with my own company or most devs I talk to in the industry So what gives?
