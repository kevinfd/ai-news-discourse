# Apple Watch Series 12 hits new record-low price ahead of Black Friday deal — save $70 at Amazon

Mashable · Hannah Hoolihan · 2026-11-16

Apple Watch Series 12 hits new record-low price ahead of Black Friday deal — save $70 at Amazon
SAVE $70: As of Nov. 16, the Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS, 42mm) has hit a new low price of $329 at Amazon ahead of Black Friday. That's $70 off its list price of $399.
Amazon's dropping more early Black Friday deals ahead of its main event this week, including a bunch of Apple products. Among them is the Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS, 42mm), which has hit a new low price of $329.
As of Nov. 16, this deal saves you $70 on the smartwatch. Considering it only just launched in September, this is a very nice price cut. It's a smartwatch we rate highly for most iPhone users.
        
            The best tech deal this week
                            
                    
            
This latest Apple Watch launch features some exciting new features over its predecessor, including a Health Sensing System for more accurate heart rate readings (even measuring HRV every five minutes for stress and recovery data), a Readiness score to see your energy levels for the day, and Audio Intelligence with Siri AI. It can even detect emergencies like a hard fall or a car crash and automatically connect you with emergency services.
It also has an impressive suite of tracking features, including a daily sleep score, blood oxygen readings, and Pacer and Heart Rate Zones for workouts. Plus, you can enjoy up to 24 hours of battery life.
If you've been thinking of jumping into the Apple Watch ecosystem, the Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS, 42mm) is one we'd highly recommend. Don't miss out on this great early Black Friday deal at Amazon.
