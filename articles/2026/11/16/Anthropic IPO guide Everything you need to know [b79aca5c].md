# Anthropic IPO guide: Everything you need to know

CNBC · Caleigh Keating · 2026-11-16

Anthropic is racing towards its Nasdaq debut in what will be a historic initial public offering.
The artificial intelligence company was founded in 2021 when CEO Dario Amodei and a small group of founders split off from now-chief rival OpenAI. Since then, Anthropic has become a darling of the corporate world, generating blockbuster revenue growth by selling its "Claude" suite of AI models and tools, primarily to other businesses.
But its massive buildout isn't without risk, as Wall Street grows increasingly concerned about how companies are funding the AI boom and if the spending justifies the costs.
Anthropic has distinguished itself as a safety and regulation-minded AI lab, with Amodei calling publicly to "pace the frontier" of AI development in September. Anthropic's risk-forward approach has also landed it in hot water with the U.S. government this year.
Institutional demand is expected to be even stronger than for SpaceX, which went public in June in a record $86 billion IPO. Anthropic could very well eclipse the benchmark set by SpaceX on its debut.
IPO fast facts:
- Anthropic, PBC will trade on the tech-heavy Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT
- Date: The IPO is expected to price the evening of Nov. 18, and begin trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday, Nov. 19.
- Anthropic is offering 825 million shares in the IPO, at a price between $70 and $78 per share.
- At the current range, the offering would bring in more than $64 billion, giving the company a valuation of $1.405 trillion.
- Up to 20% of the offering is expected to be targeted towards retail.
