# Jensen Huang allies build a "war room" to get him back at Nvidia

Ars Technica · Michael Acton, Financial Times · 2026-11-16

Jensen Huang is considering a comeback as Nvidia’s chief executive, as allies in Silicon Valley pressure the company’s board to reinstate him days after the tech executive resigned over reports he had affairs with a suspected Chinese spy and a subordinate.
Huang has discussed with confidants the possibility of returning, according to multiple people familiar with the matter—even as an independent probe by Nvidia’s board investigates the conduct that led to a spectacular leadership crisis at the $4 trillion chip company this week.
One Huang ally told the FT they were putting together a “war room” of Silicon Valley executives to lobby for the former Nvidia chief’s return and would try to garner support from the White House.
“Everyone that matters in tech outside the frontier labs loves Jensen,” they said. The efforts had Huang’s support, they added.
A public campaign to reinstate Huang has gathered steam over the weekend. Tesla and SpaceX chief Elon Musk and Epic Games chief executive Tim Sweeney were among executives backing a public letter demanding that its board “Bring Jensen Back.”
Huang continues to command fierce loyalty from some employees, several of whom have gone public in calling for his return in the days since he resigned and have signed the open letter.
