# Overwhelmed by Holiday Travel? Google Maps' AI Can Now Plan Your Itinerary

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-11-16

Google's Gemini-powered Ask Maps feature is getting an upgrade ahead of the holidays, with new capabilities for itinerary planning, appointment discovery, real-time traffic tracking, and more.
The feature, designed to act as an AI chatbot for travel queries, began rolling out earlier this year and can be accessed through a dedicated Ask Maps button below the search bar. While it could already prep itineraries, today’s update makes it easier to visualize that plan.
For example, once you ask the feature to build a road-trip itinerary using voice or text, it will respond with a day-to-day schedule marked with numbered map pins, insights, images, ratings, and directions for each location. There’s an overview map for the whole trip at the bottom, and a new share button at the top that lets you move the whole thing to a Google Doc on desktop or copy it there.
If you accidentally exit the conversation, tap the clock icon next to Ask Maps to revisit the chat history. When you’re ready to set off, tell Ask Maps to pull up the discussed itinerary, and it will show you updated information on live traffic conditions, opening hours, and even the vibe at your next stop, Google says.
While on the trip, if you feel like adding a new location to the itinerary, that’s possible too. A new “Know before you go” card will also provide additional tips on where to park or charge your car, weather, tolls, and delays.
More Holiday-Themed Features for Ask Maps
Two other features coming to Ask Maps today are its ability to find appointments and search through Saved Lists.
The holiday season can be busy for those hosting gatherings or visiting friends and family, leaving them with little time to find and book appointments. For services such as beauty treatments, pet care, and home repairs, Google says Ask Maps can now find nearby businesses and call them if necessary.
For example, you could ask for a 4 p.m. appointment at a nail salon near you, and Ask Maps will return with a list of salons that are open. You could then pick a business or ask Maps to call everyone and check for a 4 p.m. availability. In case you’re wondering how that's possible, Google Search added a capability to make AI-powered calls to businesses last year.
The final Ask Maps update rolling out today is its ability to search through your Saved Lists and respond to related queries. For instance, if you’d like to give your visitors a tour of your city, you could ask the feature to check the saved spots on your list and highlight places that can accommodate a group.
All of these updates are now available on both Android and iOS. If you can’t find them, try updating to the latest version of Google Maps.
