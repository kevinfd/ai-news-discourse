# AMD completes acquisition of AI model firm World Labs

DigitalToday · Dae-geon Seok · 2026-11-16

AMD has secured an AI model research team, broadening its capabilities across the AI technology spectrum.
AMD said on Nov. 16 local time it had completed the acquisition of AI model developer World Labs. The World Labs research team will work with AMD engineers on AMD's next-generation hardware, software and systems aimed at an open AI ecosystem.
World Labs develops AI models that generate and simulate interactive 3D environments. It also works on technology used for robot learning and simulation. The World Labs team will continue research on foundation models that serve as a basis for various uses. Fei-Fei Li (페이페이 리), co-founder and CEO of World Labs, will join AMD as senior vice president and chief scientist and report directly to chair and CEO Lisa Su (리사 수).
The World Labs team will work with AMD engineers and apply insights gained from emerging model capabilities to the development of future AI infrastructure. AMD said the acquisition will allow it to incorporate cutting-edge AI model data into its semiconductor, software and systems roadmap for running AI models.
Lisa Su said building infrastructure to support the next era of "advanced superintelligence" requires optimising the computing platform and models together. She said she was pleased to welcome Li and the World Labs team to AMD. She added their pioneering model research would help build AMD's future hardware, software and systems. She added AMD would work to support researchers and developers across the ecosystem in expanding the boundaries of what can be done with AI.
