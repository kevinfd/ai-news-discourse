# John Oliver has a brutal recap of the Nvidia CEO scandal

Mashable · Sam Haysom · 2026-11-16

John Oliver has a brutal recap of the Nvidia CEO scandal
It's been a big news week for Nvidia, but for all the wrong reasons.
The computer chip company's CEO Jensen Huang resigned after admitting to an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, before news of two more affairs — with an executive who reported to him and another Nvidia employee — came to light.
"I know what you're thinking," John Oliver said during Last Week Tonight on Sunday, bringing up a photo of Huang in a leather jacket. "This guy cheated on his wife? This guy? This walking midlife crisis cheated on his wife? A 63-year-old man who brags about owning 30 to 40 leather jackets, and whose closet must therefore look like the wardrobe department of a middle school production of Grease, cheated on his spouse? Say it ain't so, there are no heroes left!"

Oliver went on to break down the public reaction to Huang's affair, including Donald Trump's defence of the CEO: "I asked him that question: 'Did you have any idea that she was a spy?' He said, 'I had no idea that she was a spy,' and I really believe him," the president said during a White House briefing.
"First, obviously he had no idea she was a spy, that's kind of how spies work," Oliver responded. "But second, of course you believed him, he has more money than you. And Trump always believes anything a richer guy says, whether it's 'I didn't know my girlfriend was a spy', or [bringing up a picture of Trump with Elon Musk] 'I'll be selling self-driving cars by next year, or [bringing up a photo of Trump and Jeffrey Epstein] 'Relax buddy, our friendship is never gonna be a problem for you.'"
