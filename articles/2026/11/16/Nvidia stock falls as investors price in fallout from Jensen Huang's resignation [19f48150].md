# Nvidia stock falls as investors price in fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation

CNBC · Kai Nicol-Schwarz · 2026-11-16

Nvidia's stock continued its slide on Monday, after the chip giant lost hundreds of billions in market cap last week amid the fallout from Jensen Huang resigning as CEO.
The company was 2.7% down in premarket trading on Monday as of 8:07 a.m. ET.
Huang resigned from Nvidia, the company's board announced on Thursday, after mounting scrutiny following reports that he had a relationship with a woman believed to be a Chinese spy. Huang told employees he had an affair in an email on Nov. 8.
It was later reported that Huang had romantic relationships with two Nvidia employees. Nvidia did not immediately respond to CNBC's request for comment on Friday.
An online petition calling for Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO has drawn more than 170,000 signatures, as prominent tech executives, including Elon Musk, rallied behind him.
Meanwhile, U.S. lawmakers are pushing to launch comprehensive investigations into the scandal.
Huang cofounded Nvidia in 1993 and led the company through its meteoric rise to becoming the world's most valuable company, with its AI chips underpinning the technology's boom.
Nvidia appointed longtime finance chief Colette Kress as interim CEO and said its board would consider both internal and external candidates in its search for a permanent successor.
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on Sunday called for a "dramatic shakeup" at Nvidia, adding that the board should look outside the company for its next chief executive.
He also suggested other Nvidia executives should face scrutiny over the chip giant's compliance with U.S. export controls.
