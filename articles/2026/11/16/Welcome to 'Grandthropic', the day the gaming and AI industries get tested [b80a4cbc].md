# Welcome to 'Grandthropic', the day the gaming and AI industries get tested

Business Insider · Dan DeFrancesco, Dan DeFrancesco (ddefrancesco@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-16

Anthropic's IPO and "Grand Theft Auto VI" drop the same day. Rockstar Games; iStock; Tyler Le/BI This post originally appeared in the Business Insider Today newsletter. You can sign up for Business Insider's daily newsletter here . Grand Theft Anthropic. GTA VI(PO). Grandthropic. Call it whatever you want. Just don't call it boring. Grand Theft Auto VI's release and Anthropic's market debut arrive this Thursday. Not since "Barbenheimer" — when "Oppenheimer" and "Barbie" premiered the same weekend — have we had two cultural and business phenomena collide on the same day. I'll start with Anthropic, since that's the easier sell. A valuation as high as $1.4 trillion with as much as $90 billion raised makes it a behemoth (and potentially record-breaking) IPO. It's also the first major test of investor appetite for a company focused solely on AI . Then there's GTA. The last installment — GTA V — surpassed $1 billion in sales within the first three days of its release in 2013. It remains a top-selling game to this day, with almost 235 million units sold overall. GTA VI is already off to a hot start. Presale estimates have reached 12.13 million units, totaling $1.18 billion in revenue, according to SensorTower data . The impact also extends to companies' PTO calendars . (Or your favorite newsletter writer; I'll be out next week.) Workforce management tech company UKG said time-off requests for the week of GTA 6's release are up roughly 20% compared to the same week in previous years. Some companies are even leaning into it . 'Grandthropic' comes with some incredibly high stakes. For Anthropic, the fate of the wider AI trade rests on its shoulders. And that was before one of the industry's mascots — Jensen Huang — blew up spectacularly. The AI giant's public debut represents the cleanest bellwether for AI. This isn't an offshoot of the AI ecosystem, like a chipmaker or data center, or a company leveraging AI atop a mature business. Anthropic is as pure-play of an AI company as you'll ever find. Which raises the question: If investors don't support Anthropic, why have any confidence in the wider AI trade? I argued that point on my Instagram . For GTA 6, it's tough to compare it to the rest of the video game industry because its incredible success makes it an outlier. But with the threats posed by artificial intelligence and high hardware costs , GTA's success could serve as the anchor that keeps gamers engaged, bridging to other games. As if that wasn't enough pressure, waiting 13 years for a new edition also created lofty expectations. (And an incredible meme: We got X before GTA 6. ) Strauss Zelnick , CEO and chairman of Take-Two Interactive, the company behind GTA, is well aware of that. At BI's The Long Play event last month, he spoke to my colleague Sarah E. Needleman about how his company's mission "is to make the best entertainment on Earth." The full interview is worth a watch .
