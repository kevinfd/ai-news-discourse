# AI Day Seoul Brings Together Korea’s Industry Leaders, Startups and Developer Communities

NVIDIA Blog · NVIDIA Writers · 2026-11-16

Over two days at COEX, more than 2,000 attendees converged for AI Day Seoul, bringing together Korea’s industry leaders, startups and developer communities advancing physical AI and AI agents.
At the plenary sessions, Deepu Talla, vice president of robotics and edge AI at NVIDIA, said Korea is “punching above its weight,” with Doosan Robotics, Hyundai Motor Group, LG Electronics and SK Group — as well as startups like Real World and Luda Robotics — using NVIDIA platforms to bring general-purpose robotics into the real world.
Jonathan Cohen, vice president of applied research at NVIDIA, showed how enterprises can build agents they own and control. Breakout sessions featured speakers from Coupang Intelligent Cloud, Doosan Robotics, LG AI Research, LG Electronics, NAVER Cloud, SK Telecom and Upstage.
“It was a genuinely enjoyable time to have candid conversations,” said Hyunho Yang, research engineer for omnimodal foundation models at SK Telecom. “Seeing the passion of fellow AI developers was a real source of inspiration for me.”
The Agentic AI 10x Bootcamp Community Day brought together Pseudo Lab, Python Korea, PyTorchKR, HAERAE, Hugging Face KREW, Graph User Group, Womxn Who Code Korea, Dnol (Datayanolja) and other developer communities. Five finalists from the Hangul Day hackathon, co-hosted with Fastcampus, built agents trained on the Korean language and culture with the OpenShell runtime. The winning team, FlyGate, received an NVIDIA DGX Spark system.
More than 500 developers also took part in full-day NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute workshops and NVIDIA Certification exams in Korean. Workshops covered deploying and optimizing AI inference at scale, building end-to-end robotics workflows with the NVIDIA Isaac platform and developing secure AI agents. Certification included the new AI Model and Application Development exam.
The Physical AI Startup Connect event included pitches from 10 NVIDIA Inception startups: AIM Intelligence, Avikus, GoLe Robotics, MOVIN, Neubility, ROAI, ROBROS, TESOLLO, TwelveLabs and XYZ. The AI for Science Forum brought together the Korea Institute of Science and Technology Information, Korean universities and NVIDIA Research.
AI Day Seoul sessions will soon be available on NVIDIA On-Demand.
