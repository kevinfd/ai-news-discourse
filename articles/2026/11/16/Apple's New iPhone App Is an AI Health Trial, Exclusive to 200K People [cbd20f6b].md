# Apple's New iPhone App Is an AI Health Trial, Exclusive to 200K People

PCMag · James Peckham · 2026-11-16

Apple’s latest iPhone app is a little different: It’s an AI-powered health research tool designed to give the brand a better understanding of how everyday people talk to chatbots when discussing their medical history and health-related questions.
The Apple Health AI Usage Study was developed in collaboration with clinical platform OpenEvidence, and is available via the App Store for those with an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone running iOS 26 or later. To start, Apple is limiting the study to 200,000 people aged 21+.
If you enroll, you can share HealthKit data, such as activity, sleep, or heart rate, to receive more specialized advice. You can also ask the Apple Intelligence-powered chatbot everyday health questions or talk through any medical diagnosis in easy-to-understand language.
However, Apple says this tool won’t replace medical professionals' advice or diagnose a condition. "Participants should consult a doctor or clinician to address health concerns or verify medical information," it says.
Other examples shown by Apple include brainstorming questions to ask a doctor before an appointment. It breaks down previous conversations to highlight outstanding questions, and puts them together in an easy-to-follow list for your visit to the doctor.
“Health is deeply personal, and conducting a dedicated research study is the most thoughtful way to understand how people engage with, comprehend, and evaluate health AI in everyday life,” said Sumbul Desai, Apple’s VP for health and fitness. Whatever Apple learns will “help advance the broader scientific conversation and ensure that as technology evolves, we continue building trusted tools that genuinely support people wherever they are on their health journey.”
