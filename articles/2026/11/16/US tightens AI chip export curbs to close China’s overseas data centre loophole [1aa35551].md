# US tightens AI chip export curbs to close China’s overseas data centre loophole

South China Morning Post · Xiaofei Xu,Ben Jiang · 2026-11-16

The US Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) has announced new restrictions on Chinese companies’ remote access to advanced US artificial intelligence chips through overseas data centres, targeting a major loophole that has long allowed Chinese developers to access computing power otherwise barred by US export controls.
