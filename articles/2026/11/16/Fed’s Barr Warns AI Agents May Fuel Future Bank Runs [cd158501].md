# Fed’s Barr Warns AI Agents May Fuel Future Bank Runs

Bloomberg · 2026-11-16

Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr warned that autonomous artificial intelligence agents could speed destabilizing stampedes out of banks, “potentially accelerating runs that unfold in seconds, overwhelming traditional liquidity management and regulatory intervention timelines.” Barr noted at a conference in Milan on Monday that major banks are already detecting AI agents attempting to take unauthorized actions in retail accounts. (Source: Bloomberg)
