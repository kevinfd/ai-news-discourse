# Help Papa Johns Rate Your Order With AI and You Could Get a Free Pizza

PCMag · Jon Martindale · 2026-11-16

Influencers aren't the only ones being rewarded for snapping photos of their food. Papa Johns is running a promotion that's handing out free pizza in exchange for pics of your most recent order.
The pizza chain wants to examine the images for quality-control purposes. "Every photo and every review helps us better understand what customers love and where we can improve as we continue our journey to deliver a better experience every time they choose Papa Johns," says Chris Lyn-Sue, Global Chief Marketing Officer at Papa Johns.
The company's press release doesn't mention AI, but CEO Todd Penegor told Reuters that uploaded images will be analyzed by AI via a system it created with Google Cloud. Papa Johns has in-store cameras that do something similar, but they're only in a few hundred of its approximately 6,000 stores, Reuters says.
So, it's looking to supplement those pics with customer images. Plus, it'll help Papa Johns keep an eye on potential inconsistencies across franchises, the "Achilles heel" of the fast-food brand, Penegor told Reuters.
How to Get Free Pizza
The Snap a Pizza, Get a Free Pizza promotion runs until Nov. 20. Here's how to get your free large one-topping pizza in exchange for a photo.
- Sign in to or create a Papa Rewards account and opt in to SMS/text messages.
- Order a pizza on the app or website ($15 minimum order required).
- Wait for a text from Papa Johns.
- Take a real, unedited photo of your pizza before digging in.
- Upload the photo and leave feedback on the bake, crust, and overall experience.
- Once your photo is confirmed, you can unlock a free large one-topping pizza in your Papa Rewards offers, valid with a minimum $5 purchase on your next order.
