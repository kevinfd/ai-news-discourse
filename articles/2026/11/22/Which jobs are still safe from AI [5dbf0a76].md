# Which jobs are still safe from AI?

DW · 2026-11-22

AI and AI-powered robots are advancing rapidly. Entry-level jobs are already disappearing in offices. Which jobs are truly secure, and who might be replaced next?
As artificial intelligence transforms the workplace, a growing question emerges: Which jobs are truly safe from AI?
From skilled trades and nursing to office administration, apprentices and experts weigh in on how automation, AI software, and intelligent robots are already changing job prospects.
The report examines where human skills remain difficult to replace, why some entry-level office roles are under pressure, and how AI is reshaping industries ranging from health care to construction.
While new technologies are streamlining repetitive tasks and boosting productivity, experts warn that some occupations may evolve dramatically or disappear altogether.
For young people choosing a career path, the debate is becoming increasingly urgent. Which jobs offer long-term security in the age of AI, and what role will human expertise, adaptability, and personal interaction play in the future labor market?
