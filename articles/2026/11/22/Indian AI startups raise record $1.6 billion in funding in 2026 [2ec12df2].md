# Indian AI startups raise record $1.6 billion in funding in 2026

Telangana Today · Telangana Today, IANS · 2026-11-22

Indian AI startups raise record $1.6 billion in funding in 2026
Indian AI startups raised a record $1.6 billion in the first nine months of 2026, despite fewer funding rounds. Tracxn said large deals drove growth, while early-stage funding dominated and Mumbai, Bengaluru and Chennai accounted for 96 per cent of total capital
                                        
                    Updated On - 23 November 2026, 04:12 PM
                                    
                                                
                
                            
New Delhi: Equity funding raised by Indian artificial intelligence (AI) startups nearly doubled to a record $1.6 billion in the first nine months of 2026, driven by a handful of large deals even as the number of funding rounds declined, according to a report released on Monday.
The report by data intelligence platform Tracxn Technologies said Indian AI companies raised the amount across 87 equity funding rounds between January and September 2026, up 96 per cent from $822 million a year earlier and 162 per cent higher than $616 million in the corresponding period of 2024.
                                            
                
However, the number of rounds fell 42 per cent from 149 in the first nine months of 2025. The 10 largest funding rounds accounted for $1.5 billion, or 90 per cent of the total capital raised.
Series A and later rounds fell 33 per cent to 24 from 36, while first-time funded companies declined 60 per cent to 24 from 60. Globally, AI companies raised $383.5 billion during the period, with India accounting for 0.4 per cent of the total at $1.6 billion and ranking ninth among countries.
Early-stage funding rose 191 per cent year-on-year to $1.3 billion and accounted for 78 per cent of total funding, compared with 53 per cent a year earlier. Late-stage funding rose 4 per cent to $280 million, while seed-stage funding fell 42 per cent to $69.2 million.
On the investor side, 111 investors backed Indian AI companies in the first nine months of 2026, down 34 per cent from 169. First-time investors fell 39 per cent to 43, while international investors declined 38 per cent to 29.
Mumbai’s share of Indian AI funding rose to 48 per cent in the first nine months of 2026 from 2 per cent, with $765 million raised against $15.7 million. Bengaluru raised $711 million, accounting for a 44 per cent share against 54 per cent a year earlier.
Chennai ranked third with $75.5 million, accounting for 5 per cent of total funding. The three cities collectively accounted for 96 per cent of all funding, the report said.
India produced three new AI unicorns between January and September 2026, compared with one a year earlier, while the sector recorded six acquisitions and no initial public offerings, the report said.
