# 0rwx – A simple model of AI usage, inspired by Unix permissions

0rwx.com · misswaterfairy · 2026-11-22

INTRODUCTION
          We need new language to talk about large language models. 0rwx is my attempt.
          
A labelling system is vital for domain experts to talk about AI in a clear way.
          The difference between the 4 levels is not necessarily about what TOOL you use, but your ability to scrutinise the generated work.
          
I have employed this simple framework to discuss my and others' work with less judgement and confusion, 
          and I hope the 0rwx framework makes it just a little easier for you too. They are:
        
ZER0
AI can't read or write to your work HAND MADE
I read the docs
painting w/paint
playing music
"I think"
          READ
AI has read-only permissions AI INSPIRED
reference images
chord inspiration
"I make"
          WRITE
AI has read-write permissions AI COLLABORATION
generated collages
loop generation 
"I fix"
          EXECUTE
YOU can no longer read your work AI MADE
I vibe code
image generation
song generation
"I curate"
