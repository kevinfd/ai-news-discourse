# SEARCH SHAKEUP: Google Tests Replacing Familiar Search Button With AI Tools

The Yeshiva World · 2026-11-22

Google is testing a redesign of its familiar homepage that removes the traditional “Google Search” button and gives artificial intelligence tools a prominent place beneath the search bar.
The experimental layout has appeared for some users, replacing the longstanding button with options powered by Google’s Gemini AI. The change would mark a significant departure for a homepage that has maintained the same basic appearance for years.
The new options include “Create Images,” which generates pictures using the company’s Nano Banana model; “Ask about files,” which allows users to upload documents and ask questions about their contents; and “Brainstorm,” which helps users develop ideas through an AI conversation.
Traditional Google searches remain available through the search bar, which stays in place. The familiar “I’m Feeling Lucky” button also continues to appear in the experimental design.
The updated homepage additionally features a sidebar displaying users’ recent AI searches, allowing them to revisit earlier conversations and continue where they left off.
A CNET reporter who encountered the redesign said it appeared only while he was signed into his Google account. After signing out, the homepage returned to its familiar layout.
For now, the changes appear to be a limited test. Google has not announced when—or whether—the redesigned homepage will become available to all users.
