# What is Meta's AI Agent 'Muse'? Now Available in Japan! The Potential of a New AI That Can Handle Emails, Reservations, and Shopping

note · WORKSON · 2026-11-22

What is Meta's AI Agent 'Muse'? Now Available in Japan! The Potential of a New AI That Can Handle Emails, Reservations, and Shopping
From the era of asking AI questions to the era of delegating work to AI.
A new service symbolizing this change has emerged.
It is 'Muse,' a personal AI agent developed by Meta.
Muse is not just an AI that creates text or answers questions.
It is an AI agent aimed at organizing emails, checking schedules, operating websites, and performing tasks on behalf of the user.
Announced on September 8, 2026, it officially became available in Japan on October 23.
In this article, we will introduce Muse's basic features, functions available in Japan, pricing, new features released in November, and challenges regarding safety.
1. What is Muse?
Muse is a personal AI agent developed by Meta, the company that operates Facebook and Instagram.
Official website: 
https://muse.ai/
An AI agent is an AI that determines the necessary tasks based on given goals and executes multiple steps.
For example, consider the following requests:
'Organize my schedule for next week and check important emails.'
'Plan a travel itinerary and look up reservation candidates.'
'Summarize ongoing projects and organize what needs to be done.'
Muse is designed not only to respond to such requests with text but also to use linked apps and browsers to carry out actual work.
For time-consuming tasks, it can continue processing even after the app is closed and notify the user when confirmation or approval is needed.
However, supported operations vary depending on the usage environment and permissions, and not all tasks will necessarily succeed.
2. Differences from AI chats like ChatGPT
AI chats are widely used for answering questions, creating text, brainstorming ideas, and organizing information.
On the other hand, what Muse emphasizes is connecting conversations with AI to actual actions.
For example, when planning a trip, it can not only think about destinations and schedules, but also research booking services and proceed with filling out necessary forms.
A key feature is its ability to handle multiple tasks at once while integrating with apps authorized by the user.
Of course, services like ChatGPT also have features that allow them to act as agents to carry out tasks.
Therefore, Muse is not the only one capable of achieving this.
The notable point about Muse is that it aims to provide a personal agent experience centered on integration with Meta's services and everyday apps.
3. What you can do with Muse
Email management
It supports tasks such as checking necessary messages and organizing content by integrating with services like Gmail.
It can also be used to prepare reply drafts.
A mechanism is in place to request user approval when actually sending emails.
Schedule management
It supports checking appointments and coordinating schedules by integrating with services like Google Calendar.
It seems useful when you want to organize work and private schedules together.
Shopping and travel
It can also be used for tasks such as comparing products and thinking up travel plans.
Meta introduces features such as browser operation, reservations, and payments.
However, users need to confirm actual purchase conditions and cancellation policies themselves.
Long-term goal management
Muse is intended for managing long-term goals as well as daily small tasks.
For example, work projects, study plans, or life goals.
There is also a mechanism to provide necessary suggestions based on past conversations and information permitted by the user.
4. Available in Japan starting October 23, 2026
What is important for users in Japan is the official launch on October 23, 2026.
According to Meta's official announcement, it can be used in Japan via iOS, Android, and the web version at muse.ai.
Linked services include Gmail, Google Calendar, Instagram, and more.
Furthermore, it was announced that integration with the following services will be promoted within Japan.
- Mercari
- Money Forward ME
- Ticket Pia
These are services that many people in Japan use on a daily basis.
The scope of Muse's utility could expand to include shopping, household budget management, and purchasing event tickets.
However, an announcement that integration is being promoted is different from all features being available immediately.
Please check the actual support status on the app's integration screen.
5. Dedicated cloud computer 'Muse Secure VM'
A distinctive mechanism of Muse is Muse Secure VM.
VM stands for Virtual Machine.
Muse uses a computer environment in the cloud that is isolated for each user.
The agent operates a browser in that environment to carry out various tasks.
According to Meta, credentials for connected services are stored in a protected area, and the system is designed so that Muse cannot directly view passwords or payment information.
Furthermore, another agent called 'Sentinel' manages network access and critical operations.
The design requires user confirmation for actions such as sending emails or making purchases.
A feature to review operation history is also provided.
However, the existence of such protective features is separate from the guarantee that unexpected behavior will never occur.
When using the service, it is necessary to verify access permissions and the content of operations.
6. What is 'Spaces' introduced in November?
On November 6, 2026, Meta announced a new feature Spaces.
Spaces is a system that allows users to create their own workspaces and tools using natural language.
For example,
'I want to create a screen to manage my work projects in a list'
'I want to compile new songs and concert information for my favorite artists'
'I want to check my travel reservation information on a single screen'
You convey requests like these to Muse.
Muse then creates a space tailored to that purpose.
The completed space can be placed in the app's sidebar or main tab.
It is also possible to change the configuration later.
Meta also introduced a feature that automatically updates spaces using information from linked services.
It is an experience that feels like creating and using your own small apps, rather than just chatting.
It seems like it could be used in interesting ways, especially for work management and organizing hobby-related information.
7. Supports Voice Conversations and Avatars
On November 5, 2026, real-time voice conversations and expressive avatars were also announced.
This feature allows you to brainstorm ideas or plan trips while having natural conversations with Muse.
The avatars are designed to change their expressions and movements in response to the conversation.
However, the initial release of the service began in the United States.
For availability in Japan and support for the Japanese language, you should check the latest information on the official app.

9. Caution Regarding Privacy and Safety is Necessary
Muse is an AI agent for individuals.
While convenient, it may have access to important user information such as emails, calendars, and payment services.
Meta explains that it has established permission management and safety features.
However, on November 18, 2026, Reuters published an investigative article regarding unexpected behavior in Muse.
The article reports cases such as access to information that the user did not intend and the display of unrelated messages.
Researchers verified paths through which message information could be accessed, such as Android notification access permissions and macOS accessibility features.
Meta has explained that the reported issues are limited cases and states that it is continuing to improve safety.
Furthermore, it has not been confirmed whether some of the unnatural messages were actual information from other people or content erroneously generated by the AI.
Therefore, it cannot be concluded that information leaks have occurred for all Muse users.
Nevertheless, it serves as a reminder that careful permission management is necessary when using AI agents.
In particular, you should be mindful of the following points:
- Link only the necessary services
- Distinguish between email viewing and sending permissions
- Check notification, screen operation, and accessibility permissions
- Perform final confirmation for purchases and reservations
- Disconnect integrations that are no longer in use
- Exercise caution when granting access to important personal information or sensitive work data
As the number of tasks we can entrust to AI increases, the things users need to verify will likely change as well.
10. Who is Muse suitable for?
Muse is considered suitable for people who use multiple online services on a daily basis.
For example, office workers who want to organize work emails and schedules.
People who want to streamline travel planning and shopping.
Freelancers who manage multiple clients or projects.
Operators of sole proprietorships or small businesses, and so on.
In particular, repetitive information organization and schedule management are areas where the use of AI agents can easily be considered.
However, when using it for actual business operations, it is necessary to check the handling of customer information, access permissions, and service terms of use.
It is a good idea to start by trying it out with low-priority tasks.
11. The Future of AI: From 'Answering' to 'Acting'
Looking at Muse, I feel that the role of AI is changing.
Until now, it was common for humans to ask AI questions and proceed with tasks based on the answers.
However, AI agents do not just make plans; they actually perform operations.
They check necessary information, link applications, and carry out tasks.
And they ask the user for approval at critical moments.
Such mechanisms may become increasingly important in future AI services.
On the other hand, there are also issues regarding how to stop the AI when it makes a wrong decision and how much authority to grant it.
We need to consider both convenience and safety.

Meta's Muse is not just an AI that answers questions, but an AI agent that helps with personal life and work.
A wide range of uses are envisioned, including email and schedule management, shopping, travel, and support for long-term goals.
Furthermore, with Spaces, a feature has been added to create your own dedicated work screens and tools.
It officially became available in Japan on October 23, 2026, and its future development is drawing attention.
However, as it handles personal data and external services, it is necessary to give sufficient consideration to safety and privacy.
It is not about what you can ask AI, but what you can entrust to AI.
