# I made a picture book with ChatGPT and had 'Muse' read it—the results were unexpected...? I tried making an AI picture book read-aloud video

note · Miyo Ai Note · 2026-11-22

I made a picture book with ChatGPT and had 'Muse' read it—the results were unexpected...? I tried making an AI picture book read-aloud video
Wouldn't it be interesting to add a voice to a picture book created with ChatGPT and turn it into a read-aloud video?
This time, I created an autumn picture book for kindergarteners. I used ChatGPT to generate the story and watercolor-style illustrations, then passed them to Muse to create a video about 3 minutes long.
When I listened to the first video, the intonation of the word 'aki' (autumn) at the beginning felt unnatural. After that, there were a few other words where the intonation was bothersome. So, I tried a different reading voice, but this time the result was so energetic that it would wake you up if you listened to it before bed.
I will introduce the process from creating the picture book, how to provide the illustrations and script, to my impressions after comparing two types of read-aloud voices.
☘️ Creating an 8-page picture book with ChatGPT
[1] First, have it think of a story
This is what I first asked ChatGPT to do.
あなたは絵本作家です。
年長さん向けに、クマとウサギが出てくる秋をテーマにした絵本を作成したいなと考えています。
8ページ程度で、ストーリーを考えてください。
What was created was 'Bear-kun and Rabbit-chan's Autumn Treasure.' It is a story about two friends who go to the autumn forest to find treasure and meet a little squirrel who has lost the acorns she collected for winter.
[2] Comparing 3 art styles using only Bear-kun
ChatGPT suggested that a warm watercolor touch would be appropriate. So, instead of drawing everyone right away, I compared watercolor, a style with clear outlines, and a colored pencil style using only Bear-kun. Since I wanted to depict the autumn forest in soft colors, I chose watercolor this time.
Next, I created character sheets for Bear-kun, Rabbit-chan, and the little squirrel. I lined up full-body shots and expressions from various angles to serve as a reference when creating the illustrations for each page. The little squirrel was initially wearing fallen leaf decorations, so I had them changed to a regular bow tie. This is the completed character sheet.
[3] After looking at the 8-page storyboard, finish it page by page
Instead of creating images for each page immediately, I wanted to see the flow of the entire picture book first to check what the final result would look like, so I had the 8-page storyboard combined into one image.
When I checked the storyboard, I didn't see any major issues, so I decided to have the images for each page created while referring to the character sheet. When I actually created the images one page at a time, there were pages where backpacks or pouches were added to Bear-kun and Rabbit-chan, who only had scarves in the character sheet.
Even if you create a character sheet, it won't be exactly the same every time. But with ChatGPT, you can ask for edits like 'remove this accessory,' so you can correct it to a form closer to the character sheet.
☘️ Give Muse 8 images and 1 script file
From here on, I will work with Muse. I attached one text file containing the text for each page in order from the first page, and 8 images to Muse. I asked it to make a video that displays the pictures one by one in order and reads the text for that page.
If you want to try it in the same way, here is an example of the instructions.
添付した8枚の絵と台本を使って、日本語の読み聞かせ動画を作ってください。
1ページ目から順番に、そのページの絵を表示しながら、対応する文章を読み上げてください。
☘️ Compare two ways of reading with the same pictures and script
[1] Muse's standard voice: The intonation was a concern
At first, I asked it to 'please read it aloud.' Then, Muse created a reading voice using its built-in standard speech synthesis. I did not use any external services for this version.
When I listened to it, the intonation of 'Aki' at the beginning of the speech bothered me. After that, there were some words where the intonation felt unnatural.
[2] Irodori-TTS: It sounded more natural, but it was quite energetic
I wanted to hear it with slightly more natural intonation, so I asked Muse if it could use a different speech synthesis (TTS). I found out that Irodori-TTS could be used, so I decided to try it with the same pictures and script.
In my environment, I was asked for permission to access the site while using Irodori-TTS. If the same screen appears, check which site is being accessed and for what purpose, and only allow what is necessary and safe.
This one also has some unnatural intonation remaining here and there. However, overall, I felt it sounded more natural than the first video. What left an even stronger impression was how energetic the reading was. If you are enjoying it with children during the day, I think this kind of expressive reading is nice. If you are listening to it before bed, this might wake you up a bit.
The Irodori-TTS version switches to a much more energetic reading style a little way into the beginning. You can play the video from the X post above, so if you are curious about the differences in how it reads, please try comparing them.
☘️ I want to try other reading voices with Muse
I usually use VOICEVOX for reading aloud. I also used it when I made a 'Yukkuri' commentary video. I feel the intonation is decent, but after trying a few other speech synthesis tools, I found it difficult to find a reading style that fits picture book storytelling.
This time, knowing that I could even handle the reading with Muse, I tried it with some expectations. In the video I made, the standard Muse voice had some Japanese intonation issues that bothered me, but it was a gain to learn that other speech synthesis options can be used. It seems Irodori-TTS has other voices as well, so I would like to try other speech synthesis options and look for one that suits storytelling.
