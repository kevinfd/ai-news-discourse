# Your ML model passed every test. Here’s what those tests might have missed

VentureBeat · 2026-11-22

Most ML models are tested the way students cram for an exam: On clean, familiar material, under comfortable conditions. Then they graduate into production — where the inputs are messy, the labels drift, and nobody is grading on a curve. The result is the most common failure mode in applied ML: A model that looked excellent in the lab and silently degrades in the wild. Read more
