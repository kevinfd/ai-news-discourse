# Scoop: DeSantis' secret book project fuels 2028 speculation

Axios · Alex Isenstadt · 2026-11-22

Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis is quietly working on a book, two sources tell Axios — a project likely to fuel speculation about his making another run for the White House. Why it matters: DeSantis hasn't publicly acknowledged the book project or disclosed what it will be about. But writing a book is often the first step toward a presidential campaign, and it could give the term-limited governor a platform to lay out his vision for the Republican Party's future. DeSantis' state was a rare bright spot for Republicans in this month's midterms, as Democrats made historic gains across the nation. Driving the news: Word of DeSantis' book comes as he's become increasingly outspoken on a range of policy issues, particularly the economic and social impact of artificial intelligence — potentially offering clues about what the book might address. DeSantis has become a vocal critic of the potential dangers of AI, warning that it could eliminate jobs, threaten children's safety and privacy, and drive up electricity bills as data centers expand. DeSantis has pushed for an "AI Bill of Rights" and signed state legislation aimed at preventing tech companies from passing the costs of data centers on to residential consumers. His stance has put him at odds with the Trump administration's efforts to limit states' ability to regulate AI. DeSantis has emphasized affordability, immigration and education — issues likely to remain central to national politics long after he leaves office. Zoom out: DeSantis has made other moves that could keep him in the national spotlight after he leaves office in January. NBC News recently reported that DeSantis is preparing to launch a conservative think tank, which could enable him to retain a political and donor infrastructure. After the midterms, DeSantis embarked on a flurry of media appearances that resembled a victory tour, highlighting how Florida Republicans had largely escaped the Democratic wave. Yes, but: Some DeSantis allies are highly skeptical that he'll mount another presidential campaign after his unsuccessful 2024 bid. DeSantis has sent mixed signals about his political future. He downplayed the prospect of another presidential bid last year and, after the midterms, questioned how much a Republican president could accomplish with Democrats controlling Congress. He also disputed a report that he was building a campaign operation. But he hasn't ruled out a 2028 run, saying he'll assess the political landscape after leaving office. What they're saying: DeSantis' spokespeople did not respond to requests for comment about the book.
