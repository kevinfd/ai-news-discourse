# Adobe slashes Creative Cloud Pro plans by up to 77% off for Black Friday — live through Nov. 27

Mashable · Soumya Kumar · 2026-11-18

Adobe slashes Creative Cloud Pro plans by up to 77% off for Black Friday — live through Nov. 27
TL;DR: Through Nov. 27, eligible first-time subscribers can get Adobe Creative Cloud Pro for Individuals for $29.99 per month for the first year at Adobe (down from $69.99 per month), while eligible students and teachers can get their first year for $15.99 per month (77% off the standard individual rate).
While Adobe runs smaller discounts throughout the year, its biggest price drops usually show up around Black Friday — and this year's deals cut deeper than usual.
Through Nov. 27, Adobe is knocking 57% off Creative Cloud Pro for individuals and 77% off for eligible students and teachers. Here is how both plans break down:
Creative Cloud Pro for Individuals
If you are not enrolled in school, this is the flagship deal to grab. Through Nov. 27, eligible first-time subscribers can lock in Adobe Creative Cloud Pro for Individuals for $29.99 per month for the first year on the annual, billed-monthly plan — down from its usual $69.99 per month rate.
That saves you $40 every month, or $480 over your first 12 months. If you prefer to pay upfront, Adobe is also discounting the annual prepaid plan to $334.18 for the first year (regularly $779.88).
You get unlimited standard image and vector features (like Generative Fill in Photoshop) plus 4,000 monthly generative credits for heavier video and audio tools. That includes features like Harmonize in Photoshop, Turntable in Illustrator, Generative Remove in Lightroom, Firefly Boards for brainstorming, and new beta AI assistants — alongside access to third-party models like Google Gemini (with Nano Banana Pro), Kling AI, and Topaz Astra right inside your apps.

However, remember that this promo is strictly for first-time subscribers who buy directly from Adobe and requires a 12-month commitment (limit one per customer). Once your first year ends, your plan automatically renews at the regular rate of $69.99 per month (or $779.88 per year) unless you cancel beforehand.
Creative Cloud Pro for Students and Teachers
If you are a student or teacher, the discount gets even better. Through Nov. 27, eligible first-time education subscribers can sign up for Adobe Creative Cloud Pro for Students and Teachers for just $15.99 per month for the first year on the annual, billed-monthly plan — saving 77% compared to the standard $69.99 per month individual rate. If you would rather pay for the year upfront, the annual prepaid option drops to $191.83 for the first year (regularly $479.88).
Instead of settling for basic freeware for class assignments, passion projects, or building out a job portfolio, this plan puts the same professional toolkit on your laptop that creative agencies use every day. Alongside the full lineup of 20+ desktop and mobile apps — including Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, and Acrobat Pro — you get 100GB of cloud storage, Behance and Adobe Portfolio access to showcase your work, and discounts on Adobe Certified Professional exams.
It also keeps your workflow fast when deadlines pile up, bundling unlimited standard generative image tools with 4,000 monthly generative credits for video and audio creation. Whether you are using automated features like Color mode in Premiere, Harmonize in Photoshop, Turntable in Illustrator, or Generative Remove in Lightroom, or testing outside AI models like Google's Gemini (with Nano Banana Pro) and Topaz directly inside Firefly, you get full access to the latest tools without paying full price.
Just keep in mind that this offer is for first-time student and teacher subscribers only, and it comes with a 12-month commitment. Once your first year wraps up, it automatically renews at the standard $39.99 per month rate unless you cancel or change your plan first.
Whichever tier you qualify for, both plans unlock the exact same 20+ Creative Cloud apps, 100GB of storage, and 4,000 monthly AI credits — just be sure to grab your discount at Adobe before Nov. 27.
