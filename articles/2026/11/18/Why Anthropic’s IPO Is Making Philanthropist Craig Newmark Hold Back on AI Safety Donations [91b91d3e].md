# Why Anthropic’s IPO Is Making Philanthropist Craig Newmark Hold Back on AI Safety Donations

The Information · Tiffany Li · 2026-11-18

Craigslist founder Craig Newmark says he’s recently become worried about AI’s potential to cause serious harm to critical infrastructure and human society. He’s considered directing some of his philanthropic funding towards organizations trying to make sure powerful AI doesn’t harm humans. But Newmark told me he is holding back for now because he expects a tsunami of donations from newly wealthy Anthropic employees to such organizations, following Anthropic’s blockbuster IPO this week. These potentially outweigh what he could give, and he feels his money could have more utility elsewhere.…
