# How To Stop Copilot Reading Your Tabs In Edge (2026)

The Droid Guy · The Droid Guy · 2026-11-18

How To Stop Copilot Reading Your Tabs In Edge (2026)
Copilot in Microsoft Edge can read the page you have open, your other tabs and your browsing history to answer questions.
That access is controlled by a switch inside Edge settings, and a few related Copilot features have their own switches next to it.
This guide shows where each one is, what Microsoft says it covers, and what stays on after you change it.
Quick answer: the Copilot and AI page
All three live under Settings and more (…) > Settings > Copilot and AI, according to Microsoft’s context clues page.
Stop Copilot reading your pages and tabs
- Select the More menu (…) at the top right of Edge.
- Select Settings.
- Select Copilot and AI.
- Turn off Allow Copilot to read context clues on the web.
Microsoft says that once this is off, Copilot cannot use the current webpage, page title, open tabs or browsing history in its answers.
Microsoft also says Copilot then gives more generic responses.
What context clues can use
Microsoft notes that suggestion tiles use the page title and URL, but context clues is not used to generate them.
Browsing data is not saved to your conversation history, and only your prompt and Copilot’s response are, according to Microsoft.
Remove browsing context already in use
Microsoft says to close the relevant tabs and clear your browsing history to remove information from the browsing context.
It also advises against sharing anything sensitive that you would not want used as browsing context.
Microsoft’s page does not say how long browsing data is kept outside the conversation history.
Turn off Journeys
- In Edge, select Settings and more (…), then Settings.
- Select Copilot and AI.
- Select Journeys and turn it off.
Microsoft’s Copilot Journeys page says turning the feature off stops data collection right away.
It also says this deletes the information already stored for your Journeys.
Hide or clear individual Journeys
Microsoft does not describe a single button that deletes every Journey at once.
The Copilot new tab page
The Copilot and AI page also has a Copilot new tab page toggle, which Microsoft’s getting started page mentions in the same menu path.
Microsoft describes only turning that toggle on, so check its state on your own PC if the new tab page looks different from before.
For the news feed on the regular new tab page, see our Edge new tab feed guide.
Browse with Copilot
Browse with Copilot lets Copilot click, type and move between tabs for you, according to Microsoft.
For consumers, Microsoft says it is rolling out to Microsoft 365 Premium subscribers in the US, with more markets to follow.
The consumer page gives no off switch for it, but Microsoft’s Copilot in Edge at work page points to Edge Settings > Copilot and AI > Allow Copilot to browse with me.
Check that toggle on your own PC, because an organization can set it by policy on a work or school device.
The consumer page also lists controls that limit what Copilot does.
Controls that limit Browse with Copilot
Microsoft says you can interrupt Copilot or take control at any time, and stopping a task returns control to you.
What Browse with Copilot saves
Microsoft says screenshots are taken only while Copilot is working, limited to the tabs it uses, and saved with the conversation.
It says they are not used for training, are deleted with the conversation, and are otherwise kept for up to 30 days.
Copilot cannot access autofill data, saved passwords or wallet information, but it can access cookies, so sites you are signed in to may stay signed in.
Microsoft advises deleting cookies first or using a new browser profile, and avoiding the feature for banking, trading or highly confidential data.
If Edge is signed in with a work account
Microsoft says Copilot in Edge is normally signed in automatically with your work account after you sign in to the Edge work profile.
It says Enterprise Data Protection then applies, so prompts and responses are not used to train the AI model.
Your organization may show a consent dialog that asks whether Copilot can access page content, and Microsoft says you will not see it if your admin has already enabled or disabled page access.
On a managed device, a switch described above may be locked by your administrator.
Chats used to personalize Edge
Microsoft says Copilot chats can be used to personalize Bing, Edge and MSN.
To turn that off in the Copilot app, go to Settings > Personalization and turn off One shared experience.
The same Personalization page has a Saved memories toggle with a Manage option, a Web search toggle and an Allow ads personalization toggle.
Microsoft says its Copilot privacy page applies to the standalone Copilot app and does not cover turning Copilot off inside Edge.
The sidebar button is separate
Microsoft’s getting started page says only that you select the Copilot icon in the upper-right corner to open Copilot, and it gives no way to hide that icon.
Our guide to turning off the Edge sidebar and Copilot button covers that part.
Delete what Copilot already saved
Turning these switches off does not clear your Copilot chats.
To remove saved conversations and memory, follow our guide on deleting Copilot history.
