# China’s ‘token export’ ambitions take shape with new Huawei-powered data centre

South China Morning Post · Ann Cao · 2026-11-18

China has set its sights on becoming an export hub for artificial intelligence computing power, as the country launches its first AI computing cluster powered by Huawei Technologies’ latest Ascend 950DT chips that specifically targets overseas customers.
The facility, which is based in the southern coastal city of Shantou, runs on the home-grown Ascend 950DT chip and is designed for a “token export” business model, according to local media reports. It started operations last week.
