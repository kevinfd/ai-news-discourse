# Two ways big bets on data centers might affect you (Two Indicators)

NPR · Ricky Mulvey · 2026-11-18

Two ways big bets on data centers might affect you (Two Indicators)
Your money might be fueling the AI boom … just as some AI data center bets are starting to look riskier. If you own stocks, that money is likely tied up in giant tech companies like Microsoft and Nvidia. And if your 401(k) has money invested in the US bond fund, you might be lending tech companies the money to build data centers.
Today on the show, two stories from our short daily podcast The Indicator from Planet Money on how risky these data center bets are for investors and the companies making them. And, of course, for all of us.
