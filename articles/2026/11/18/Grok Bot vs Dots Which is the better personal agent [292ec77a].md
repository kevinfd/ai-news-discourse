By The AI-Augmented Engineer There’s a lot of work in my life that doesn’t need my full attention but still manages to consume it. Checking whether someone replied. Finding the document for a meeting. Keeping track of the small commitments scattered across email, calendars, and project boards. Personal agents are interesting because they can take responsibility for that work over time. Grok Bot and ChatGPT Dots both provide persistent agents with connected tools and cloud computers that can keep working after you close your laptop. Their differences become clearer when you decide what you actually want to delegate. If you’re in a hurry, my recommendation is that you choose Dots if you want one personal assistant to connect the pieces of your work.
