# Nvidia board rules out Jensen Huang return after ex-CEO says he’d gladly come back

South China Morning Post · Howard Liu · 2026-11-18

Nvidia’s board has no plans to reinstate co-founder Jensen Huang as chief executive, chairman Harvey Jones said on Tuesday, as interim CEO Colette Kress raised the chipmaker’s growth outlook and sought to reassure investors about its future without its long-time leader.
“The Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO,” Jones said at the start of the company’s first earnings call since Huang’s departure last week.
