# Xi Jinping breaks protocol to greet and hug Trump at China airport

Axios · Herb Scribner · 2026-11-18

Chinese President Xi Jinping greeted President Trump in a surprise appearance on the tarmac in Shenzhen, China, Wednesday. Why it matters: Xi paid Trump a special honor by greeting him on the tarmac, reciprocating a gesture by Trump during Xi's state visit to Washington in September. The two are expected to hold a summit Thursday with Russian President Vladimir Putin, where the three are expected to talk about nuclear arms control, Iran, Ukraine, trade and AI chips. Driving the news: Trump arrived in China for the annual APEC conference just hours after spending time in the Philippines. Xi met Trump at the bottom of Air Force One's stairs. The two leaders shook hands and then embraced in a hug. Dozens of performers, waving pom-poms, stood on a red carpet outside the plane, chanting and cheering for Trump's arrival. Context: Xi's surprise appearance was an unusual diplomatic gesture, per multiple reports . The Chinese president did not personally greet Trump when he visited Beijing in May. Similarly, when Putin visited Shenzhen earlier today, he was greeted by Xi's chief of staff Cai Qi . Xi met former President Obama at the airport in 2009 when Xi was still vice-president. Flashback: In September 2025, Trump welcomed President Xi at Joint Base Andrews for the Chinese leader's state visit to Washington D.C.. U.S. presidents typically receive foreign leaders at the White House. Trump met Xi at the bottom of the stairs after Xi's jet landed at the U.S. military base.
