# Target heads into crucial holiday season with strong third-quarter sales

Business Insider · Dominick Reuter, Dominick Reuter (dreuter@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-18

Target's sales growth last quarter came almost entirely from increased customer visits. Dominick Reuter/Business Insider Target reports its best quarter of sales growth in five years as holiday shopping ramps up, CEO says The gains were driven almost entirely by increased visits, with shoppers seeking strong value. CEO Michael Fiddelke said investments in key focus areas are paying off in this critical season. Target's turnaround strategy may be gaining a foothold as the big-box chain enters the all-important holiday shopping season. The bullseye retailer reported its strongest quarter of sales growth in half a decade, CEO Michael Fiddelke said in a media briefing ahead of Target's third-quarter earnings report. Third-quarter net sales grew 7.1% to $27.1 billion, with comparable sales up 5.6%, ahead of analysts' expectations. Almost all of the comparable sales gains were driven by increased traffic, as shoppers responded positively to Target's improved merchandise, shopping experience, and value proposition, Fiddelke said. The company's performance in the quarter also benefited from hundreds of millions of dollars in investment in training and scheduling workers, which helped improve customer satisfaction scores and product availability, he said. Fiddelke also told Business Insider the company's new Ask Target AI shopping assistant is already having an impact on sales, with shoppers who use the tool completing purchases at more than twice the rate they did before it launched. In terms of merchandise categories, the results so far suggest that the areas where Target has chosen to focus its energy and attention are seeing growth, while others like home goods and apparel have yet to turn the corner.
