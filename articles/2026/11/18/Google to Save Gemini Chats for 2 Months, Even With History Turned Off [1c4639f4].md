# Google to Save Gemini Chats for 2 Months, Even With History Turned Off

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-11-18

Google is making important changes to Gemini's chat history that affect how long it retains the information you share with its AI and what it does with it.
The company is splitting its Keep Activity setting in two, letting you choose whether to retain your chats and whether Google will use that data to train its AI. With this change, however, Google will hold on to your chats for longer.
Previously, if you used Temporary Chats or turned off Gemini History, Google kept chats for 72 hours. With a policy update announced this week, the company will store chats in either case for around two months and use them "to respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public, with help from human reviewers," Google said in an email to users.
Meanwhile, the second setting will let you "control whether your chat content, including text and attachments, is used to train generative AI models," Google says in the email. "With this change, you will be able to keep your history while independently controlling generative AI model training."
You can still have your chat history automatically deleted. It is set to 18 months by default, but can be changed to 3 or 36 months from the My Activity page.
Changes to Temporary Chats, meanwhile, are outlined on Google’s support page. With this option, you can stay signed in to Google while having chats with Gemini that don't factor into your history. (Like Gemini's version of Chrome's Incognito mode.) Google doesn’t use these chats for AI training.
Google says it will "start rolling out these changes in the coming weeks."
Gemini’s two-month chat retention is now the longest among competing AI platforms, TechCrunch notes. OpenAI retains temporary ChatGPT conversations for 30 days, while Anthropic retains them for 30 days on personal accounts and more if required by an organization on an enterprise plan.
