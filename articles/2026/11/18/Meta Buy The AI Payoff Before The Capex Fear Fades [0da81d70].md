# Meta: Buy The AI Payoff Before The Capex Fear Fades

Seeking Alpha · Justin Miller · 2026-11-18

Meta: Buy The AI Payoff Before The Capex Fear Fades
Summary
- Meta Platforms is a Buy: AI-driven ad pricing supports a $712–$782 base case, despite spending and funding risks.
- Q3 revenue grew 26% as ad prices rose 14% and impressions increased 10%, showing advertisers are paying more for performance.
- META’s 37% operating-income decline reflects a $9.9 billion legal charge; excluding it, operating income grew 11%, although costs rose faster than revenue.
- At roughly 18x 2027 earnings, META looks discounted, but negative free cash flow, missing 2027 capex guidance, and equity issuance limit conviction.
