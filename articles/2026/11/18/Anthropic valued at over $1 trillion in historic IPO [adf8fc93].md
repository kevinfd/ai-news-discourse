# Anthropic valued at over $1 trillion in historic IPO

Axios · Madison Mills, Dan Primack · 2026-11-18

Why it matters: Wall Street expects the AI boom to continue, despite some recent tumult. Zoom in: Anthropic priced at $83 per share, well above its planned range of $70 to $78 per share.
