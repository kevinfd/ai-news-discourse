# Who is the book on generative AI for business efficiency, 'LLM: Pitfalls in Large Language Model Development,' intended for?

note · ミナト｜Ai仕事改善ノート · 2026-11-18

Who is the book on generative AI for business efficiency, 'LLM: Pitfalls in Large Language Model Development,' intended for?
Have you ever bought a book on generative AI, only to find that nothing about your work changed the next day after you finished reading it?
It is also common to experience a mismatch where you open a book expecting a 'collection of prompts,' only to find content aimed at developers. The book we are covering this time, 'LLM: Pitfalls in Large Language Model Development,' is exactly the kind of book that stands at that crossroads.
Before choosing a book for business efficiency, let's first confirm which problems this book addresses.
The book's position as read from its title
The word 'pitfalls' in the title best describes the nature of this book. It is not an introductory book that introduces how to use ChatGPT, but a book that deals with the areas where one is likely to stumble when building a system that incorporates large language models.
The price is 4,730 yen, which is set higher than typical business books. This price range is also a sign that it is a specialized book with a targeted readership.
If you buy it with the feeling of casually browsing, you are likely to find a gap between your expectations and the content. Note that the detailed information provided here is mainly the title and price.
Please check details such as the chapter structure and the author's background on the product page before purchasing.
Choose books on business efficiency based on whether you are a 'user' or a 'creator'
Books on business efficiency using generative AI are broadly divided into two types. One is books that reduce work using existing tools, such as for drafting emails or summarizing meeting minutes.
The other is books for building internal chatbots or automation mechanisms yourselves. For the former, books filled with prompt templates and use cases are helpful.
For the latter, walls such as unstable accuracy after running it or costs exceeding expectations become real issues. The title 'LLM: Pitfalls in Large Language Model Development' can be read as being aimed at readers who are conscious of the latter's walls.
For those who just want to know how to use it, it might be a bit of a distant book.
When to pick it up
For example, suppose a project comes up to incorporate an LLM into internal inquiry responses. Although the demo worked, you want to identify 'what is dangerous' before releasing it into production.
At such a stage, a book with a perspective on failure cases becomes a source of judgment. It is also suitable for information systems department personnel or those tasked with promoting AI utilization to organize points of discussion before implementation.
Conversely, if you just want to speed up your writing starting today, reading another introductory book first is a faster path.
Three points to check before buying
The first is whether your work involves 'using' or 'creating.' If you choose without clarifying this, you will likely end up unable to finish a technical book.
The second is prerequisite knowledge. Books that deal with development pitfalls often assume the reader knows the basics of programming and APIs, so it is safer to check the level by looking at the table of contents or a sample.
The third is whether there is a use case that justifies the price. If it directly relates to creating internal proposals or considering implementation, 4,730 yen can be a reasonable investment.
In summary
Generative AI efficiency books have different roles depending on whether they are for 'using' tools or 'creating' the mechanisms. 'LLM: Pitfalls in Large Language Model Development' is worth considering as a book to keep on hand for those on the development side to avoid failure.
If you want to lighten your daily workload, start with an introductory book; if you are facing decisions about implementation or development, start with this one. By deciding your position before choosing, you can avoid the failure of finishing a book and having nothing change.
