# Play GTA 6 in OLED with $130 off this Samsung Odyssey monitor in Amazons early Black Friday sale

Play 'GTA 6' in OLED with $130 off this Samsung Odyssey monitor in Amazon's early Black Friday sale
TL;DR: The Samsung 27-inch Odyssey OLED G5 (G50SF) gaming monitor is on sale for $319.99 at Amazon, down from its $499.99 list price.
With GTA 6 finally launching on Nov. 19, there's hardly been a better time to upgrade your console gaming setup to OLED. Fortunately, Amazon has knocked a hefty amount off an ideal Samsung monitor ahead of Black Friday, making it a lot cheaper to experience Vice City in even better visuals.
As of Nov. 18, the Samsung 27-inch Odyssey OLED G5 (G50SF) has fallen to just $319.99 at Amazon, slashing $180 off its $499.99 list price for a 36% discount. 
        
            The best tech deal this week
                            
                    
            
That's not only the best price seen since late July, but also $29.55 below the $349.54 Labor Day deal we spotted back in September. Plus, Amazon is selling and shipping the monitor itself rather than going through a third-party seller, with free delivery estimated for Nov. 23, or Nov. 19 for Prime members, at the time of writing.
You're getting a 2560 x 1440 QD-OLED display with HDR10, delivering richer blacks and contrast to bring out every detail when exploring Leonida on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S.
What’s more, the 180Hz refresh rate over DisplayPort and 0.03ms response time are also accompanied by NVIDIA G-Sync compatibility, AMD FreeSync, and Glare Free technology for smoother, less distracting gaming overall.
