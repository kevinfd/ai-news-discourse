# 6 amazingly mundane things you can do in GTA 6

Mashable · Alex Perry · 2026-11-18

6 amazingly mundane things you can do in 'GTA 6'
For a lot of folks, Grand Theft Auto VI is an excuse to live out the life of a powerful criminal, or at least try to be the world's best virtual boyfriend. But for those of us who left the "rampaging through the streets in a tank" days behind us, it sounds like the game has a lot more to offer.
GTA 6 seems to be defined as much by its embrace of the mundane as anything else. Early reviews and Rockstar's previews have highlighted some of the seemingly countless, completely boring activities in the game. There are plenty of clips making the rounds, too, and with AI making fake gameplay easier to pass off as the real thing, we can't verify every video out there. Still, I’ve seen enough to know what I’ll be doing while everyone else is on their crime sprees.
Home repair
One feature that some early GTA 6 launch coverage has highlighted is the ability for the protagonist Jason to fix up his house. This doesn't just have cosmetic benefits. Doing repairs can also earn Jason some goodwill with Lucia, his partner in crime and love interest.

That means part of GTA 6's story will involve fixing a leaky roof, repairing a porch railing and stairs, and perhaps most excitingly, painting a bedroom wall. Yes, you can literally watch paint dry in GTA 6. This is what I've always wanted.
Birding
Developer Rockstar Games has become especially good at rendering the natural world in its more recent games, and it sounds like GTA 6 takes this to the next level. One major example of that is that you can devote your time to bird-watching, if you want.
There are apparently 62 different kinds of birds in the game, and you can check them out with a pair of binoculars before recording them in a wildlife logbook. I'm not joking when I say I know at least one person in real life who will absolutely go through the effort of finding all 62 birds. Heck, I might do it, too.
Doomscrolling
One of my favorite real-life pastimes is staring at my phone for way too much of every day. Constantly switching between and refreshing the same three social media apps is sorta like putting my brain in a microwave, and I just can't stop doing it.

That's why I'm excited about the prospect of literally doing the same thing in GTA 6. You can sit in bed and just look at fake social media apps, which is genuinely probably a better and healthier use of time than looking at real ones.
Petting cats
Dogs are wonderful. I love dogs, and I'm glad that petting them has become an expected mechanic in so many video games in recent years. The fact that you can pet dogs in GTA 6 is old news at this point.
But I'm also a lifelong cat guy, and video games don't give cats nearly as much attention. Rockstar must have read my mind because you can totally pet cats in GTA 6.

You can bet your bottom dollar that I'll be doing that instead of robbing convenience stores.
Watching cartoons
Previous GTA games have given players the ability to watch things on TV. I'm old enough to remember watching Katt Williams do virtual stand-up in GTA 4, for example.
But GTA 6 steps that up by introducing an original cartoon series called Macca & Friends that you can apparently watch entirely in-game. That is stupid enough that I might actually do it.
Fishing
Last but certainly not least, there is one optional side activity that every good video game ought to have: fishing. And guess what? You can totally fish in GTA 6.
