# Can Universities Survive? AI vs. Higher Education [audio]

Past Present Future · frereubu · 2026-11-18

Can Universities Survive? AI vs Higher Education
Today’s episode in our series about the challenges facing universities looks at what might be the most direct threat of all: can they still fulfil their function if AI can write better essays than the students? David talks to political philosopher and university teacher Paul Sagar about his experiences of what has changed over the last year as the LLMs have come on in leaps and bounds. How is AI corroding trust between teachers and students? Are universities remotely equipped to know how to handle this challenge? Can they, in fact, survive it?
