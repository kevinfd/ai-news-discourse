# Bret Taylor’s Sierra in Talks to Raise at $30 Billion Valuation

The Information · Stephanie Palazzolo, Valida Pau · 2026-11-18

Sierra, the AI customer support startup founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, has been in talks to raise new funding at around a $30 billion valuation, according to two people with knowledge of the fundraise. That price would nearly double the $15 billion-plus valuation it received in its last round announced in May. 

 The three-year old startup is discussing raising around $1.5 billion in new funding, one of the people said.  Lightspeed Venture Partners, Greenoaks and Benchmark are in talks to invest, the person said. The talks are still early and terms could change.
