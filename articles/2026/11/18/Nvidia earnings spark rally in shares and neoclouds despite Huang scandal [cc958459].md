# Nvidia earnings spark rally in shares and neoclouds despite Huang scandal

CNBC · Kai Nicol-Schwarz · 2026-11-18

Nvidia stock was up nearly 4% in premarket trading on Wednesday after reporting another earnings beat, as the fallout from CEO Jensen Huang's resignation continues to unfold.
The chip giant's stock was 3.69% higher at 5.53 am ET on Wednesday in premarket trading.
Neoclouds were buoyed by the results. CoreWeave was up 5.7%, Nebius 7.3% and Iren 6.2%.
Nvidia's third-quarter revenue came in at $115.84 billion, more than $6 billion above analysts' expectations of $109.10 billion, while fourth-quarter guidance of $130 billion topped the $125.1 billion consensus, according to LSEG.
Shortly before the results, Huang made his first public statement since resigning last week, saying that he was open to returning to the company, after an outpouring of support from prominent tech figures.
But Harvey Jones, the new and first-ever chair of Nvidia's board, said on the earnings call that the board does not intend to reinstate him as CEO.
″[Hyperscalers] are the ones that are right now feverishly looking for more focus in terms of the compute that we have available," interim CEO Colette Kress told Jim Cramer on CNBC's "Mad Money."
"This concept that anything is changing in terms of any of our types of customers — that is just not true. They are all continuing to be with us, and we have a very deep breath of different customers."
The U.S. Commerce Department on Monday announced a legal change to try and stem China's access to the most advanced Nvidia chips — which are already under export controls effectively preventing their import into the country — by restricting Chinese companies' ability to access compute remotely in overseas data centers.
Nvidia told investors in its earnings commentary that it was "not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in our outlook," in keeping with its recent language on the country.
