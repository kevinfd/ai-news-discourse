# Former Elon Musk Adviser: 'We've Given Tech CEOs A Free Pass'

Bloomberg · 2026-11-18

Nvidia's board has shot down Jensen Huang's offer to return to lead the company, as the saga over his affair with an alleged Chinese spy rumbles on. Dex Hunter-Torricke tells Bloomberg Radio "we have been living for the last couple of decades with this whole mythos that a powerful founder CEO is essential to the success of these companies." He's the founder of The Center For Tomorrow and once worked for Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg and has been speaking to Bloomberg's Tiwa Adebayo and Ben Boulos about the state of Silicon Valley, and the oversight of tech companies in the AI era.
