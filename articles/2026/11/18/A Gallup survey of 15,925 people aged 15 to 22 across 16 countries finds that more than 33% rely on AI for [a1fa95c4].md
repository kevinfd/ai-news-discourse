# A Gallup survey of 15,925 people aged 15 to 22 across 16 countries finds that more than 33% rely on AI for companionship, rising to 50% among daily AI users (Jason Parham/Wired)

Techmeme · 2026-11-18

Jason Parham / Wired : 
 A Gallup survey of 15,925 people aged 15 to 22 across 16 countries finds that more than 33% rely on AI for companionship, rising to 50% among daily AI users   —  Young people who have online-only friends or use AI as a social companion face higher odds of loneliness than those who do not, a new Gallup report reveals.
