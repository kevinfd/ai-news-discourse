# Apax Sees Growing Split in Credit Markets From AI

Bloomberg · 2026-11-18

"What is becoming more obvious is that there is a real bifurcation between the credits that are impacted by potential AI disruption, and the ones who's cashflows are going to be resilient irrespective of what's happening in that AI cycle," says Apax Partners Capital Markets Europe Head Roxana Mirica. She speaks to Bloomberg's Anna Edwards and Guy Johnson on "The Opening Trade" as London's annual Global Credit Forum begins. (Source: Bloomberg)
