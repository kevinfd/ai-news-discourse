# CEO Colette Kress was left in the dark about Jensen Huang's return

Yahoo! Finance · Betelbuddy · 2026-11-24

Nvidia's longtime CFO and now former interim CEO Colette Kress was left in the dark about Jensen Huang's return: Report
Colette Kress has been the CFO of Nvidia (NVDA) since 2013, helping to build the company at the center of the AI boom alongside founder and public face Jensen Huang.
She stepped into the line of fire for 12 days as interim CEO while Huang worked behind the scenes to return.
She withstood fresh reports of Huang berating her in meetings, and President Trump saying in an interview that she did a poor job in her post-earnings TV interview last week.
In the end, Nvidia's longtime CFO Colette Kress — a widely respected figure on Wall Street — has been left holding the bag.
And, apparently, left out of the loop on Huang's return.
"The talks that culminated in an Nvidia board meeting late on Sunday were so secretive that even interim chief executive officer Colette Kress was kept out of the discussions," a new report on Tuesday from the Financial Times said, citing people familiar with the matter.
An Nvidia spokesperson didn't respond to Yahoo Finance's request for comment.
Shares of Nvidia fell 5% in early trading on Tuesday after a 31.2% surge on news that founder Jensen Huang had been reinstated as CEO. That came just 12 days after he resigned amid a personal scandal and federal chip-export probes.
Kress is returning to the CFO role, though her LinkedIn profile still says interim CEO.
The dramatic reversal followed intense lobbying from President Trump and Vice President JD Vance, who intervened directly with directors and said Huang had been cleared of national security concerns.
Upon returning to Nvidia's Silicon Valley headquarters, Huang promised employees he would be a better CEO the second time around.
Despite the rally, Nvidia still faces ongoing DOJ and SEC investigations into alleged chip smuggling through blacklisted Singapore-based firm Megaspeed, prompting the board to appoint a new chief compliance officer.
