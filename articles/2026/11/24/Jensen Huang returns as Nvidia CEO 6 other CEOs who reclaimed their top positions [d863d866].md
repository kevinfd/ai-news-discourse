# Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO: 6 other CEOs who reclaimed their top positions

The Hindu · The Hindu Bureau · 2026-11-24

Jensen Huang, co-founder of Nvidia, who had stepped down as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the chipmaker, was re-instated in the top job on November 23, 2026. Mr Huang had resigned due to an alleged Chinese spy scandal on November 12, and CFO Colette Kress was appointed as interim CEO.
The board voted unanimously to bring Mr Huang back as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately, after a series of meetings left directors satisfied with his “candor and ability to lead”, Nvidia said in a statement.
6 CEOs who reclaimed their top posts
Huang’s return has put the spotlight on the ouster and return of other top bosses. Here’s a look at some of the major names.
- Sam Altman, OpenAI: In November 2023, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman was fired abruptly by the board after it cited concerns about his candour in communications with the board. The decision prompted an employee revolt, with more than 700 employees threatening to leave for Microsoft unless Mr. Altman returned on November 21.
- Jack Dorsey, Twitter: In October 2008, Jack Dorsey was ousted as CEO of the social media platform amid concerns about his management. He continued as chairman. Mr Dorsey returned as CEO in 2015 while running the payments company Square. His stints with the company ended in 2021.
- Steve Jobs, Apple: Steve Jobs was ousted from Apple in 1985 after a power struggle with the then-CEO John Sculley. Mr Jobs rejoined Apple in 1996, when the tech company acquired NeXT, a company founded by Mr Jobs. In 1997, he returned as interim CEO and became permanent CEO in 2000.
- Michael Dell, Dell: Michael Dell founded Dell Inc. in the 1980s and held the position of CEO until 2004, when he stepped down to serve as the chairman. He returned as CEO in 2007 and led the company through its merger with EMC, which resulted in the emergence of Dell Technologies.
- Robert Iger, Disney: Robert Iger first became the CEO of the US entertainment group in 2005. He left the position in 2020, but Disney’s board of directors asked him to return in 2022, when the company faced operating losses at its streaming unit.
- Howard Schultz, Starbucks: Howard Schultz served as Starbucks CEO on three separate occasions. Mr Schultz launched the company’s coffeehouse model in the 1980s before stepping down in 2000, only to return in 2008 for a nine-year stint. He served a final term as the interim CEO for a year until March 2023.
What is different about Jensen Huang’s return?
The return of Mr Jensen Huang at the helm of Nvidia is unique, as reports suggest that senior U.S. government officials left the company’s board confident that he posed no national security risk amid concerns of China vying for the company’s advanced chips. U.S. President Donald Trump also welcomed the move as a “correct and proper decision.” I believe my intervention was very important to get Jensen Huang back,” Mr Trump told CNBC after Mr Huang’s reinstatement, marking an unprecedented intervention by U.S. government officials into the matters of a publicly traded company.
