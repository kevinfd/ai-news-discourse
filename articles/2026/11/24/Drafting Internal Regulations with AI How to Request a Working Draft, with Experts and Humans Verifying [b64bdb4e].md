# Drafting Internal Regulations with AI | How to Request a Working Draft, with Experts and Humans Verifying Compliance with Laws and Work Rules

note · さぼり経理のあい｜Aiで経理・総務を時短 · 2026-11-24

Drafting Internal Regulations with AI | How to Request a Working Draft, with Experts and Humans Verifying Compliance with Laws and Work Rules
Nice to meet you. I'm Ai, the Slacker Accountant.
Let me give you the conclusion first.
In my first year, I would freeze in front of a blank screen, unable to write even the first article of a regulation. Time and again, I thought, "If only I had a starting draft."
Creating that initial draft is what AI excels at. However, that doesn't mean the resulting text is "correct."
What AI Is Good at and What It Struggles With
得意なこと
・条文の構成（目的、適用範囲、手続き、例外、改廃）の案
・言い回しを、読みやすい文に整える
・既存の規程との、用語や表現のぶれを探す
・社員向けの説明文の下書き
苦手なこと（人・専門家が確認する）
・法令や就業規則との整合
・自社の実態に合っているか
・数字や期限が現在の制度と合っているか
・会社として何を決めるか
Step 1: Write down what needs to be decided yourself first
When it comes to regulations, "what to decide" is the most important part. Rather than having AI make the decisions, you have it turn what you've decided into formal articles.
・目的：〔何のための規程か〕
・対象：〔誰に適用するか〕
・決める項目：〔申請の期限、上限額、承認者など〕
・例外：〔例外を認める場合〕
・開始日：〔いつから〕
Leave items that haven't been decided yet as "TBD." Figures that AI makes up on its own will later become a source of discrepancies with the company's actual decisions.
Step 2: Request the working draft
次の内容で、社内規程の叩き台を作ってください。
・規程の名前：〔〇〇規程〕
・上の「決めること」の内容
・条文は「第1条（目的）」の形式
・「未定」の項目は、〔　〕と空欄にして、勝手に数字や期限を入れない
・法令の条文や数字は、書かない（人が確認して入れる）
最後に、「人が確認すべき点」の一覧も出してください。
Specifying "do not include statutory figures" and "leave undecided items blank" makes it harder for AI to fabricate details.
Step 3: Have it check for consistency with existing regulations
次の2つの文章を比べて、用語のぶれや、矛盾しているように見える点を挙げてください。
・新しい規程の叩き台
・既存の規程（就業規則の該当部分）
挙げるのは候補だけで、どちらが正しいかは判断しないこと。
Work rules are internal company documents. First, check your company's guidelines to see if it is permissible to paste them.
Step 4: Where humans and experts need to verify
You must not use an AI-generated draft as-is. The points to check are as follows.
- For labor-related matters, have an expert such as a labor and social security attorney verify consistency with relevant laws and internal work regulations.
- For matters involving legal validity or liabilities, consult an attorney.
- For tax-related matters (such as whether an item is non-taxable or considered salary), consult a certified tax accountant.
- Does it fit the actual circumstances of your company (headcount, work arrangements, systems)?
- When and by whom will the regulations be approved? (Internal company procedures)
Amending work regulations requires procedures such as filing notifications and informing employees. Be sure to check the latest details of these procedures through official guidance from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare or the Labour Standards Inspection Office.
Step 5: Draft the explanatory text for employees
Once the regulations are complete, prepare explanatory notes intended for readers.
次の規程を、全社員向けに、A4で1枚の案内文にしてください。
・変更点を3つにまとめる
・専門用語はかんたんな言葉にする
・不明な点は、問い合わせ先の欄を空けておく
（規程を貼る）
A human must compare the notices line by line against the regulations to confirm they match.
Information you must never input into an AI when drafting regulations
- Individual names, salaries, and performance evaluations
- Confidential information of business partners
- Company management information that has not yet been publicly disclosed
For more details, refer to Information You Must Never Input into Generative AI and Internal Guidelines for Generative AI.
Example Scenarios: Common Pitfalls When Reviewing a Working Draft
Example 1: The AI inserted arbitrary numbers you never specified
The AI may plausibly insert deadlines or upper limits that you have not decided on, such as "Applications must be submitted by the [X]th of the month following use." Once you receive the draft, first mark all numbers and deadlines, then verify one by one whether they are values your company actually decided upon. Revert any undecided items back to blank fields.
Example 2: Specific law names or article numbers are written
Even if the AI provides specific article numbers, there is no guarantee they are accurate. Do not incorporate statutory article numbers or provisions into your regulations until you have verified them against official national legal sources or consulted an expert.
Example 3: Systems or mechanisms your company does not have are described
Procedures that do not exist in reality, such as "apply via a dedicated system" or "approved by the department head," may be included. A human must rewrite them to match your company's actual practices.
Verification Procedure Steps
1. Mark the numbers, deadlines, and clause numbers in the working draft.
2. Compare the marked sections against your company's finalized decisions, and revert anything undecided back to blank spaces.
3. Have on-site personnel read it to see if it matches actual practices.
4. Have labor-related sections verified by experts such as a labor and social security attorney.
5. Go through the approval process, add the date, and only then establish it as a formal regulation.
If you skip this order and send it straight to an expert, the amount of revisions will increase. Organizing it internally beforehand allows you to narrow down what to consult the expert about.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q. Can we roll out the regulations generated by AI across the entire company as-is?
A. No. Consistency with laws and work regulations must be verified by an expert. The formal approval process is also required.
Q. Which is better: online templates or an AI-generated working draft?
A. Both are starting points. The process of inserting your company's decisions and having an expert verify them remains the same.
Q. How should we proceed with regulations created by accounting, such as expense reimbursement regulations?
A. Specific decision-making methods are described in How to Create Expense Reimbursement Regulations. An AI working draft can be used to draft clauses based on that.
Summary
- AI-generated regulations are working drafts. The company makes the decisions
- Write down what has been decided first, and prompt with "undecided" items left blank
- Do not have AI generate statutory figures; humans must verify and insert them
- Verify consistency with laws and work regulations with experts such as labor and social security attorneys or lawyers
- Do not enter personal information or client confidential information into AI
