# Former Elon Musk Adviser: 'Nvidia Now Intertwined With Washington'

Bloomberg · 2026-11-24

Nvidia’s board already faced criticism for lacking the gravitas required to oversee the AI chip powerhouse – and that was before President Donald Trump chose to moonlight as a director.

Dex Hunter-Torricke tells Bloomberg Radio "Nvidia has been deemed too big to fail" by the White House. He's the founder of The Center For Tomorrow and once worked for Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg and has been discussing the future of the AI sector Bloomberg's Caroline Hepker and Jack Sidders. (Source: Bloomberg)
