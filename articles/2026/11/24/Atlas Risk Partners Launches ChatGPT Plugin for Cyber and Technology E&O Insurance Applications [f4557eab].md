# Atlas Risk Partners Launches ChatGPT Plugin for Cyber and Technology E&O Insurance Applications

Markets Insider · Markets Businessinsider Com · 2026-11-24

FinanceWire
Nov. 24, 2026, 01:19 PM
			
		
San Francisco, California, November 24th, 2026, FinanceWire
The broking can help technology companies to start or resume insurance applications, managing coverage assessment and policy comparisons with the help of technology.
Atlas Risk Partners, an independent commercial insurance broker focused on technology businesses, announced a conversational interface plugin that lets companies start or resume cyber and technology errors and omissions (E&O) insurance applications.
Businesses can enter company information, answer insurance questions and save their progress in their Atlas accounts through the application. The integration links conversational application intake with Atlas’s existing broking process, where insurance professionals review coverage requirements and compare available options.
How the ChatGPT Plugin Works
Applicants can select coverage, confirm business details, complete forms and schedules and review their requests prior to submission, according to Atlas Risk Partners. If you do not complete your application, you may restart it in the company's account system.
The plugin supports new cyber and technology E&O insurance requests as well as the continuation of existing Atlas applications.
Atlas's privacy policy says that information entered through connected chat services could appear in those conversations. Supporting documents and exact business location information are submitted through the company’s secure website form. Chat users are advised not to share passwords, tax identifiers, payment information or other sensitive information.
The conversational interface adds one more channel of application. Company records and insurance processing stay connected to the existing systems of Atlas.
Connecting Technology Businesses With Insurance Markets
Tech companies often have very pressing insurance needs in the course of negotiating an enterprise contract.
A SaaS provider about to enter into an agreement with a major customer may need cyber liability and technology E&O insurance. These coverages may overlap but are addressing different exposures. Cyber insurance may cover certain security incidents and associated liabilities, whereas technology E&O may cover claims for failures in software products or professional services.
The limits of your coverage are determined by the terms of the policy.
At Atlas, specialists will review the core of your operations and contractual obligations comprehensively before accessing the best in class insurance markets. Each option is evaluated on important factors including coverage limits, hidden exclusions, deductibles, pre-existing conditions, and overall price.
This disciplined approach is a reflection of our fundamental commitment that every coverage option reflects the true operational and contractual risk that tech companies face on a daily basis.
Remember, the plugin just helps to streamline your application intake. But the actual underwriting and binding of the policy are still specialised flows that only brokers and insurers do.” Just submitting your details via the tool does not mean you are covered straight away.
Availability and Application Process
The plugin is for SaaS providers, machine learning and AI businesses, enterprise software developers, developer tools companies and cybersecurity firms.
Businesses can access the Atlas Risk Partners’ ChatGPT plugin through the company’s website or initiate an application directly through Atlas’s online platform. Availability of the ChatGPT plugin may differ based on your account and workspace settings.
Businesses need to have current company information, appropriate insurance policies and client contracts that state the necessary coverage caps or endorsements to apply.
Applicants can create or resume a submission when they are ready, validate their information and submit it to Atlas for a complete broking review.
The addition of the plugin provides a conversational option to the insurance application process of the company but it is preserving its established approach to evaluating coverage and market alternatives.
About Atlas Risk Partners
Atlas Risk Partners is an independent insurance agency and brokerage serving technology and technology-enabled businesses. The company helps organizations assess commercial insurance needs, review contractual requirements, and compare coverage options, policy terms, and pricing.
