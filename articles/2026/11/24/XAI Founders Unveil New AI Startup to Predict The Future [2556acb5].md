# XAI Founders Unveil New AI Startup to Predict The Future

Deccan Chronicle · Bloomberg · 2026-11-24

XAI Founders Unveil New AI Startup to Predict The Future
The “time portal,” as the company calls it, is intended to “simulate the past and teach AI to predict the future”
Three founding members of Elon Musk’s artificial intelligence business, xAI, have teamed up to build a new AI company with the ambitious goal of predicting future events.  
The startup, called Epochal, said it has developed an initial product that lets AI agents browse an archived version of the internet as it existed on specific dates. The “time portal,” as the company calls it, is intended to “simulate the past and teach AI to predict the future,” according to a statement. 
Epochal’s goal is to create a cutting-edge model that will be able to help with decisionmaking. “Superhuman prediction will redefine almost every industry because every decision is a prediction,” the startup said in the statement.
The team behind Epochal includes Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai and Manuel Kroiss, three of the founding members that left xAI after Musk merged the company with SpaceX in February. Other former staffers from xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind have also joined Epochal, the statement said. 
Zhang is personally investing $100 million in the company, according to a person familiar with the matter, who spoke on condition of anonymity as the information is not public. An Epochal spokesperson declined to comment on funding.
Other startups have also sought to build AI systems that are meant to help clients anticipate certain outcomes and behavior. Epochal’s aim is more encompassing. Its first product will allow users to do “point-in-time” search queries with accuracy. 
Epochal is the latest startup to come from the wave of employees who left xAI earlier this year. XAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin previously launched River AI, a startup focused on personalized AI, with a group of former xAI and Tesla Inc. staffers. Former staffers Yuchen He and Eric Zelikman also helped start Humans&, an AI lab.
