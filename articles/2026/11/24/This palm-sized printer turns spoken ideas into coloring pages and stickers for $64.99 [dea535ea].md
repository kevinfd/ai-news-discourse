This palm-sized printer turns spoken ideas into coloring pages and stickers for $64.99
TL;DR: Get the Dodad Dept. Magi Print Pro bundle with five paper rolls and five sticker rolls for $64.99 (reg. $119.99), and print coloring pages, stickers, and stories from a spoken idea.
Ask a kid to describe a cat flying a spaceship, and you’ll get plenty of detail. Dodad Dept. Magi Print Pro turns that description into a picture you can preview on its 2.4-inch screen, then prints it in black and white, ready for markers or colored pencils. That makes it an easy gift for a creative kid, and right now the Magi Print Pro bundle, with five rolls of print paper and five rolls of sticker paper, is $64.99 (reg. $119.99).
What you can make with the Magi Print Pro
Pictures come in six styles, from simple drawings and line art to anime and ink-wash. Print a few in a row, and you have a custom coloring book or a short comic. Magi Print Pro can also generate an illustrated story to print for bedtime, add dotted cutting guides to craft patterns, and turn any picture into a sticker for a notebook or water bottle.
        
            The best tech deal this week
                            
                    
            
Thermal printing means no ink cartridges or toner to buy, and pages still print at 203 DPI. At 0.9 pounds, the printer fits in your palm and recharges over USB-C, so kids can carry it from room to room. For schoolwork, they can ask questions, print knowledge cards, or make practice questions before a test.
Note that you’ll need an internet connection for the AI features, and the printer won’t create licensed cartoon or movie characters, so every picture starts with an original idea. Magi Print Pro comes with a one-year warranty and free shipping to the contiguous U.S.
