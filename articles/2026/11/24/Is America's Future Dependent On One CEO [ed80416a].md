# Is America's Future Dependent On One CEO?

CBS News · 2026-11-24

Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia's CEO just eleven days after being ousted over an affair with an alleged Chinese spy. Powerful voices in tech, an extreme market dip, and the president himself are just some reasons for his return. David Feith, senior fellow at the Hudson Institute, says Huang has been wrong, and even dangerous, when it comes to chip security. He breaks down how much the country's AI future depends on one man.
