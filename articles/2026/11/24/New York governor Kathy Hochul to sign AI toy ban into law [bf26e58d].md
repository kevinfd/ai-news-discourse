# New York governor Kathy Hochul to sign AI toy ban into law

NBC News · Emily Lorsch, Jared Perlo · 2026-11-24

New York Gov. Kathy Hochul is expected to sign a bill Tuesday banning for five years the manufacture and sale of toys powered by AI chatbots, her office told NBC News. The law is slated to take effect in February across New York, the second state to ban the toys after California.
