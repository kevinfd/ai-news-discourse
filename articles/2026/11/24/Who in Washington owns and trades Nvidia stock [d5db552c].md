About the data
- Holdings: Trump's annual report values his Nvidia stock at $12.5M to $58M on 31 December 2025, or 67,000 to 311,000 shares at that day's close.
- If he still held them, he lost $0.5 million to $2.2 million from 11 to 23 November. We can't tell whether he did: his 2026 trades are reported in ranges too wide to net.
- His accounts are run by independent managers, according to the Trump Organization (AP). The reports don't show who decided a trade.
- Trades can be reported up to 45 days late, so November's will appear by early January. We'll update the page then.
- Congress: periodic transaction reports only, including family accounts. Trades in annual or candidate reports, like those NOTUS found for Eric Swalwell and Ritchie Torres, aren't included.
