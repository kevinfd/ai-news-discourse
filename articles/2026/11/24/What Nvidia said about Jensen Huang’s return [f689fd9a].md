# What Nvidia said about Jensen Huang’s return

South China Morning Post · Ben Hu · 2026-11-24

Nvidia’s board of directors unanimously voted to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer, and board director on November 23, 2026. Following the decision, the company announced that Colette Kress would continue as chief financial officer, Harvey C. Jones would remain board chair, an independent investigation into foreign intelligence efforts would proceed, and a search for a new chief compliance officer would commence.
