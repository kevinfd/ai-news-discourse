# What's Next for Huang, Nvidia and the AI Trade?

Bloomberg · 2026-11-24

Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive officer just 11 days after he left the role following links to a former Megaspeed executive and suspected Chinese spy. Huang is set to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on Dec. 3, while concerns also remain around the costs and risks of the AI buildout. Bloomberg’s Neil Campling has more.
