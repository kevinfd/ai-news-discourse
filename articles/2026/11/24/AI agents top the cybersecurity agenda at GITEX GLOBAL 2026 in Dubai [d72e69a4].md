# AI agents top the cybersecurity agenda at GITEX GLOBAL 2026 in Dubai

The Next Web · Cristian Dina · 2026-11-24

AI agents can now reach sensitive data and carry out transactions with little human oversight. Governing them will be a central theme of GITEX Cyber Valley, the organisers say. It is the security showcase at GITEX GLOBAL 2026. Cloudflare, Forcepoint, Keypasco and Rapid7 will show tools to control what agents can access and do.
“The teams that win will stop asking ‘is this user real?’ and start asking ‘is this agent authorised?’” said Cloudflare’s Waseem Hattar.
Cyber Valley runs from 8 to 11 December at Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City Dubai, as part of the expo. The organisers expect more than 6,000 enterprise and startup exhibitors across GITEX GLOBAL and Expand North Star. TNW covered the event’s new summit and venue earlier this month.
The numbers behind the theme
The organisers cite headline exhibitor CrowdStrike. Its 2026 Global Threat Report recorded an 89% rise in attacks by AI-enabled adversaries in 2025. CrowdStrike agreed this week to buy Zscaler, another Cyber Valley exhibitor.
In the World Economic Forum’s Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2026, 87% of respondents named AI flaws as the fastest-growing cyber risk. The UAE Cyber Security Council reports more than 800,000 cyberattack attempts a day, Gulf News reported.
What exhibitors will show
Cloudflare predicts that AI agents will overtake humans as the internet’s main users by 2030. At the event it will show tools to build and secure agents. They include shadow AI visibility, workload protection and one gateway to manage AI models. It is also expanding its data centres in the UAE.
Forcepoint expects that by 2030 a company with 40,000 employees could run millions of AI-powered working entities. It will show its AI Data Security platform and its ARIA AI Assistant. ARIA lets security teams write protection policies in plain language.
“As AI tools begin to act on sensitive data, protecting that data and governing the AI become a single discipline,” said Samer Diya of Forcepoint.
Keypasco will launch a new version of its Trust Identity Platform. It extends Zero Trust controls to agents that use company systems. That fits a wider push to give agents their own identity.
“By 2030, organisations will manage AI Agents with the same rigour they apply to employees today,” said Keypasco CEO Chengi Lin.
Rapid7 will demonstrate its AI-enabled Command Platform at distributor Mindware’s stand. An agent with business access needs the safeguards of a new colleague, said Gopan Sivasankaran, Rapid7’s regional director for the Middle East and Africa. That means an accountable manager, the right permissions and clear duties.
Government cyber chiefs on stage
The Cybersecurity Stage will cover sovereign security, nation-state threats and supply-chain flaws. It will also cover the convergence of IT and operational technology, and post-quantum readiness.
Speakers include Dr Mohamed Al Kuwaiti, head of cybersecurity for the UAE government; Michelle Bordachar Benoit, national director of Chile’s National Cybersecurity Agency; and Ioannis Alexakis, director general of Greece’s National Cybersecurity Authority. Mikko Hyppönen of Sensofusion, Samsung Semiconductor’s Kevin T. Shin, Huawei’s Corey Deng and Cloudflare’s Christian Reilly are also on the list.
GITEX GLOBAL opens on 7 December with the GITEX Summit at Dubai World Trade Centre. This year’s theme is ‘Global Reset 2027’.
