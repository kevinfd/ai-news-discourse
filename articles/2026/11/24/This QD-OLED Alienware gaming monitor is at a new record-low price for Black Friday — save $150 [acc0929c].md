# This QD-OLED Alienware gaming monitor is at a new record-low price for Black Friday — save $150

Mashable · Ben Williams · 2026-11-24

This QD-OLED Alienware gaming monitor is at a new record-low price for Black Friday — save $150
TL;DR: The Alienware 34-inch 280Hz QD-OLED gaming monitor (AW3426DW) is on sale for $649.99 at Amazon, down from its $799.99 list price.
If you've been holding off on replacing your old PC gaming monitor until Black Friday, Amazon has just pulled the trigger on a massive price cut for one of Alienware’s newer ultrawide QD-OLED displays — this 280Hz model is going for under $650.
As of Nov. 24, the Alienware AW3426DW QD-OLED gaming monitor is available for $649.99 at Amazon, shaving $150 off its usual $799.99 price. That's the lowest-ever price for this stunning monitor.
        
            The best tech deal this week
                            
                    
            
This monitor gives sprawling RPGs more room to breathe with its 21:9 aspect ratio and 1,800R curve. The 3440 x 1440 display uses newer QD-OLED Penta Tandem technology, offering up to 1,300-nit peak brightness and improved text clarity.
Whether you want to enhance Red Dead Redemption 2 or get set up for when GTA 6’s PC port is hopefully announced this year, this 34-inch monitor is a fantastic upgrade that’s also fit for work. For more competitive games like Fortnite, the combination of a 280Hz refresh rate, 0.03ms response time, and NVIDIA G-SYNC compatibility should make tracking fast-moving opponents considerably easier.
Plus, there's extra reassurance for anyone concerned about investing in an OLED, with built-in burn-in mitigation technology and a three-year warranty.
