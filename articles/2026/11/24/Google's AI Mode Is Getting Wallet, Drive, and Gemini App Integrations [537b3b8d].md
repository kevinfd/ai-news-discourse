# Google's AI Mode Is Getting Wallet, Drive, and Gemini App Integrations

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-11-24

Google is expanding the apps and services supported by its Personal Intelligence feature in AI Mode.
Launched earlier this year, Personal Intelligence taps connected Google services to provide tailored responses to your queries in AI Mode. Since launch, the feature has been able to access Gmail, Calendar, and Photos with your permission. On Monday, Google added support for Wallet and announced plans to integrate Drive and the Gemini app in the coming weeks.
With the Google Wallet integration, Personal Intelligence in AI mode can provide tips to achieve your financial milestones and reduce everyday spending. It can also review the credit cards linked to your Wallet account and help you maximize benefits.
The access won’t allow AI Mode to go rogue and take actions autonomously, with Google confirming, “AI Mode can only read information from your accounts; it can’t move, manage, or invest your money."
When the same support expands to Drive, AI Mode will be able to reference your Docs, Sheets, and PDFs whenever required. Meanwhile, connecting to the Gemini app will allow AI Mode to carry over context from your previous Gemini conversations.
Soon, AI Mode will also display the context it is using via a Personalization icon. Whenever it pulls outdated information, you can update it in the chat window. For example, if you've switched your diet preferences, you could just type “I’m no longer a vegetarian,” Google says.
Personal Intelligence is disabled by default and is available only to users over 18 who have enabled Personalized Recommendations in the Search Personalization settings. From the same page, you can head to Connected Content Apps and select the Google apps you’d like Personal Intelligence to reference.
