# Tech sell-off resumes as euphoria from Jensen Huang’s Nvidia return subsides

The Guardian · Guardian staff and Agence France-Presse · 2026-11-24

Sell-off signals investors are bracing for further volatility in key AI sector driving economic growth worldwide Technology shares led a market retreat on Tuesday as caution returned after Jensen Huang’s extraordinary return to Nvidia days after he was forced to resign, with investors bracing for further volatility in a key sector driving economic growth worldwide. A day after Huang’s reinstatement as CEO drove Nvidia shares up more than 30%, adding more than $900bn to the firm’s market value, the euphoria dissipated as its stock lost almost 6% by Tuesday’s close.
