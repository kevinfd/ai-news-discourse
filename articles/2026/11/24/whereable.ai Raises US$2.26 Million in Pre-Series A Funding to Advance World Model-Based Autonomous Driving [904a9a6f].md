# whereable.ai Raises US$2.26 Million in Pre-Series A Funding to Advance World Model-Based Autonomous Driving

PR Newswire · Whereable Ai · 2026-11-24

whereable.ai Raises US$2.26 Million in Pre-Series A Funding to Advance World Model-Based Autonomous Driving
- Funding from Quad Ventures, LOTTE Ventures and Korea Credit Guarantee Fund
- Advancing world model-based autonomous driving for industrial and logistics environments
- Shipyard robot Escort Bot to support data collection and world model development
SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- whereable.ai, an autonomous driving startup led by CEO Doosan Baek, announced that it has raised US$2.26 million in pre-Series A funding from Quad Ventures, LOTTE Ventures and Korea Credit Guarantee Fund (KODIT). The round brings its total funding raised from investors to US$3.76 million. Founded in 2024, the company previously secured seed funding from investors including NAVER D2SF and Quad Ventures.
The company focuses on industrial and logistics sites where automation must work with existing equipment and workflows. In these brownfield environments, workers and special-purpose vehicles share the same space, while routes change frequently. These conditions are challenging for rule-based autonomous driving systems.
whereable.ai is developing world model-based end-to-end (E2E) autonomous driving technology tailored to these environments. Its world model is designed to learn site layouts and interactions between vehicles and workers from data, then predict changes in the surrounding environment to guide driving decisions.
In July 2026, whereable.ai was selected to participate in a Korean government-funded R&D project on autonomous driving for shipyards. The project has US$14.3 million in total government funding, and whereable.ai secured an R&D budget of US$2.45 million for its participation. Led by HYUNDAI MOVEX, the consortium includes Hanwha Ocean, Samsung Heavy Industries, HD Hyundai Heavy Industries and HD Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering.
Through the project, the company is developing Escort Bot, a robot designed to guide forklifts, transporters and other special-purpose vehicles in shipyards and automate signaling tasks typically performed by human spotters. It is intended to help prevent collisions between vehicles and workers and support companies in meeting heightened workplace safety responsibilities under South Korea's Serious Accidents Punishment Act. Field trials are planned at shipyards in South Korea and at Hanwha Philly Shipyard in the United States in 2028–2029.
The company plans to deploy Escort Bot at industrial sites first and collect real-world driving and operational data for model training, validation and operation. It aims to create a data flywheel in which field data improves its world model, enabling broader deployment and further data collection. Through this cycle, whereable.ai plans to expand its autonomous driving software to forklifts, transporters, trucks, autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and tractors.
In the defense sector, whereable.ai is developing an autonomous purpose-built vehicle (PBV) for troop and cargo transport through a project supported by the Defense Acquisition Program Administration (DAPA) and the Korea Research Institute for Defense Technology Planning and Advancement (KRIT). Field trials are planned for 2027 at the Republic of Korea Army Logistics Command's Consolidated Maintenance Depot.
At Incheon International Airport's Terminal 2, 11 four-seat linq autonomous indoor shuttles supplied by whereable.ai are in commercial operation. linq was named a CES 2026 Innovation Awards Honoree in both the Smart Communities and Accessibility & Longevity categories.
Building on its airport operating experience and development work in shipbuilding and defense, the company plans to expand its autonomous driving technology to a broader range of special-purpose vehicles at airports and ports.
whereable.ai will use the funding to advance its world model-based autonomous driving software, build machine learning operations (MLOps) pipelines, and develop and field-test Escort Bot and other industrial products. Funding will also go toward validating product safety and reliability, preparing for certification, and expanding its customer and partner network.
About whereable.ai
whereable.ai is an autonomous driving startup developing world model-based technology for complex real-world environments. Its work spans passenger mobility, industrial and logistics applications, and military transport, combining commercial operating experience at airports with development projects in shipbuilding and defense.
