# Save 23% On Lumo, Proton's Privacy-Focused AI With This Outstanding Deal

PCMag · K Thor Jensen · 2026-11-24

Lots of companies are staking their claims in the AI space, with a wide variety of chatbots, agents, and helpers to meet every need. But one concern many users bring to the table is privacy. Large language models need data, and companies like OpenAI use your ChatGPT logs to train them. That can open up avenues for exposure, as we saw in 2023. We're all about security and privacy here at PCMag, so when Proton announced their venture into the AI space with Lumo, our curiosity was piqued. Right now, you can try it yourself for a 23% discount.
Proton announced Lumo in 2025, billing it as an AI assistant that "keeps your conversations confidential and your data fully under your control — never shared, sold, or stolen." The company secures your chats with zero-access encryption and doesn't store anything on its servers, keeping everything you type completely for your eyes only. That means your chats aren't used to train models, they're not shared with other companies to exploit your activity, and even Proton can't see them, no matter what. The same privacy-first philosophy it uses for its VPN service is core to Lumo's design.
There's no compromise in productivity, either. Lumo works exceptionally well as a virtual assistant for tasks that would otherwise take hours of human effort. Use it to summarize long documents into easy-to-follow bullet points, turn meeting notes into action items and assign them to the appropriate staff, and develop strategies for success. If you're working with internal documents, sensitive personal information, or other materials that are your eyes only, it's the only AI solution that's acceptable. For even more security, you can enable Ghost Mode, which deletes all traces of your chats as soon as you close the window.
You also control how open Lumo is to the Internet at large. If you want, you can give it Web access to search and gather information to expand its knowledge, or keep it confined to its existing data. Proton puts the power in your hands to craft an AI experience that works for you.
One extremely cool feature is Lumo Paper Trail, which lets you import your conversations with other chatbots into Lumo to see what information has been stored without your knowledge. PCMag security expert Neil Rubenking took it for a test drive and was astounded by how much of his data ChatGPT had either collected or inferred, across six different categories. It's a killer illustration of how different Lumo is from the other leading LLMs.
Another thing we like about Lumo's setup is its transparency. The system runs on a selection of models, including Mistral Small 3, OLMO 2 32B, and OpenHands 32B, and automatically routes your queries to the most appropriate one. Its client-side interface is completely open-source, with every aspect undergoing independent review. You can use Lumo through the Web client, or with dedicated Android and iOS apps.
