# Tech Life

BBC News · 2026-11-24

Episode details
Available for over a year
The One Hundred Year Study on Artificial Intelligence is a project charting the technology's evolution over a century. Experts write a progress report every five years. In this edition of Tech Life, Chris Vallance interviews one of the panelists, Professor Gina Neff. Also this week: Laura Cress talks to game designers who say their unreleased new game was copied using AI. And Shiona McCallum reports on a new AI police chatbot.
