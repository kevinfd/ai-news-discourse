# Franklin Templeton Urges Investors to Look Beyond AI

Bloomberg · 2026-11-24

Franklin Templeton Chief Market Strategist, Stephen Dover, sees Nvidia’s rebound as a near-term positive, with the “Bank of Nvidia” back in play, but warns that circular AI funding and corporate-governance risks remain. He favors broader equity exposure, emerging markets and diversification beyond the AI trade. He appeared on Bloomberg's Insight with Haslinda Amin. (Source: Bloomberg)
