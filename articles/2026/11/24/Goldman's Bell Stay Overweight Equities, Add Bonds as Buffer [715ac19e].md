# Goldman's Bell: Stay Overweight Equities, Add Bonds as Buffer

Bloomberg · 2026-11-24

Sharon Bell, senior European equity strategist at Goldman Sachs, discusses the outlook for the AI trade after the rally in stocks that followed Jensen Huang’s return to Nvidia. "We may stay overweight equities, but you want to buffer that with bonds," Bell tells Bloomberg Television. "Take less risk maybe, but also diversify and mix your exposures." (Source: Bloomberg)
