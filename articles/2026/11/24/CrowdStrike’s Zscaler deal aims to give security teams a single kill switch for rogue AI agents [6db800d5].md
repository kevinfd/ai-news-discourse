VentureBeat · Louis Columbus, louiswcolumbus@gmail.com (Louis Columbus) · 2026-11-24

CrowdStrike's AI kill switch can cut off access to AI models at the AI gateway today. The company agreed on Monday to acquire Zscaler in an all-stock deal valued at about $43.9 billion, and it plans to take that kill switch onto the network, where AI agents travel between the device and the cloud.
