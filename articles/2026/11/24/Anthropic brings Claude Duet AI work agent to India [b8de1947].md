# Anthropic brings Claude Duet AI work agent to India

The Hindu BusinessLine · BL Bengaluru Bureau · 2026-11-24

Updated - November 25, 2026  at 06:28 PM.
Anthropic has announced that it is expanding the Claude Duet research preview to India. Claude Duet is a new app in which Claude works as a proactive work agent.
Anthropic announced the Claude Duet research preview in a company blog post on Monday.
“Claude Duet shows you what needs your attention, completes tasks you ask of it, keeps working even when you step away, and checks with you before acting on your behalf. It’s built for professionals and small business owners with more on their plates than they can track, and we think it will resonate with India’s founders, operators, consultants and small business owners, who are juggling email, invoices, customers, hiring and meetings all at once,” the post said.
The announcement was made in the US on Monday through a company blog post, with Pro, Max and Ultra users in the country able to join the waitlist from that day. On Wednesday, Claude Duet opened as a research preview in India, with waitlist access for Pro, Max and Ultra users, a public beta for Team plans and a private beta for enterprise customers. The service is available as a standalone app on iOS and Android, as well as on claude.ai.
According to the blog, Claude Duet is built around Claude Opus 5.5 and designed to be resourceful.
Each morning, Claude Duet sends the user a brief on the most important items across connected apps. Throughout the day, it monitors apps and messages the user only when something needs attention.
Claude Duet can handle tasks—from simple administrative ones like scheduling meetings to more complex ones, such as building dashboards with dozens of tools, creating financial or sales forecasts, prototyping, and building web apps.
The blog noted that Claude Duet draws on years of security, safety, and privacy work on agents, both Anthropic’s own and the infrastructure businesses use to run theirs.
“We’re continuing to develop and strengthen these protections, and will share more details about how Claude Duet is designed for safety in the coming days,” it said in the blog.
Claude Duet carries over memory, skills, and connected apps from the user’s Claude account. Users can message it by typing or voice in one ongoing thread.
Claude Duet also has a virtual machine with its own browser, so it doesn’t require a laptop or phone to be on. “Describe a task in a short message, and it works out which apps to use and what steps to take in the background. You’ll receive a notification when the task is complete or needs your review,” the blog said.
Claude Duet reads, drafts, and prepares work on its own. But before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document, or deletes anything, it waits for the user’s approval. “A few things are off-limits even with your approval, like trading securities or moving money between accounts. You’re also able to disconnect any app Claude Duet has access to,” the blog noted.
