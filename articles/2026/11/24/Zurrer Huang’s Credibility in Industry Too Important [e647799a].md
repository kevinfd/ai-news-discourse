# Zurrer: Huang’s Credibility in Industry Too Important

Bloomberg · 2026-11-24

Jensen Huang is back as head of the world’s most valuable chipmaker, in a stunning reversal less than two weeks after he left Nvidia under a cloud of scandal. This has sent the company’s stock on its biggest rally in decades. Ryan Zurrer, Founder and Director of Dialectic had said a week ago Huang will be making a comeback, and he spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa. (Source: Bloomberg)
