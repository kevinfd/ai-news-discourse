# Nvidia reinstates Huang as CEO: Trump, Musk, Altman react while the internet has jokes

Mashable · Amanda Yeo · 2026-11-24

Nvidia reinstates Huang as CEO: Trump, Musk, Altman react while the internet has jokes
Nvidia has reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as CEO less than two weeks after he stepped down, and less than a week after the company's board said it had no intention of bringing him back. Reactions to Huang's return have been a mix of praise, criticism, and jokes, with several other tech executives rallying around him.
Nvidia announced Huang's resignation on Nov. 12, a day after the board accepted it with immediate effect. He stepped down amid reports that he'd had extramarital affairs with both an alleged Chinese spy and a Nvidia employee, with allegations of a third affair with another worker subsequently surfacing. His relationship with Alice Huang (no relation) drew particular scrutiny, as she was suspected of smuggling Nvidia chips. She previously led Megaspeed, a company U.S. authorities have been investigating over concerns it smuggled advanced Nvidia chips to China in circumvention of U.S. restrictions.
While Huang said that he would be "honored" to return as CEO if asked, the board stated said on Nov. 17 that it did not plan to bring him back. Yet after reported pressure from President Donald Trump, the chipmaker has put Huang back in the top job after all. In a press release announcing Huang's reinstatement, Nvidia stated that it had based the decision on "input from senior levels of the United States government."

"The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," said Nvidia board chair Harvey Jones, who last week said that the company "does not intend to reinstate Jensen."

Trump has been characteristically celebratory about Nvidia's decision, making several posts on his social media platform Truth Social on the topic. This included an edited image based on a photo of Trump after a shooter attempted to assassinate him in 2024. Originally posted by X account @grandoldmemes, the picture has been edited to show Huang holding a red bra aloft as a Nvidia banner is displayed in the background.
Calling Huang's reinstatement an "absolutely GREAT decision" (emphasis original), Trump stated that having him installed as Nvidia's CEO is vital to the U.S. economy.
"The Country needs Jensen’s leadership at Nvidia in order to ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy," wrote Trump. "The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to Nvidia poses any risk to National Security…. On a personal note, I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure."
The U.S. president also spoke about Huang's reinstatement in a phone call to CNBC on Monday.
"I think he's gonna start off at an almost higher level," Trump said. "He's apologised for [his] indiscretion, which was unfortunate. Probably that’s what brought it to such a level, because sex sells, fellas. Now [CNBC host Jim] Cramer would not know that because he’s a very, very, very committed person. But sex sells."

Musk, Altman, and other tech execs cheer Huang's return
Trump's on-and-off ally Elon Musk also celebrated Huang's return, reposting Nvidia's announcement on X and writing that "the mistake has been corrected." Last week, Musk stated that he was signing a petition to reinstate Nvidia's then-ousted CEO.
"Everyone I know at Nvidia wanted Jensen back and it shows," the Tesla and SpaceX CEO continued, sharing a post from investor Gavin Baker. In it, Baker shared images of people crowding around Huang and applauding him on his return to Nvidia's offices.
"Grateful to the President, everyone in the administration, everyone who signed the petition, everyone who supported Jensen publicly, everyone who called the board, everyone on the board who fought for him, everyone," Baker wrote. "This is why America is the greatest country on earth."

Various other prominent figures in tech have also voiced support for Huang's return, several of whom have previously faced comparable turmoil at their own companies.
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman appeared to express approval for Huang's reinstatement, publishing a post on X that simply read, "Jensen!" Altman went through a similar incident when OpenAI's board fired him in 2023, accusing him of lying, retaliating against critics, and creating a "toxic atmosphere." Despite these allegations, Altman was ultimately reinstated after days of outcry from supporters.

Meanwhile, Binance co-founder and former CEO Changpeng Zhao posted to X that he was "glad to see @JensenHuang back," resharing his previous reaction that it was "a mistake for Nvidia to let Jensen resign." Zhao resigned from his role as CEO of Binance and pleaded guilty to failing to maintain an effective anti-money laundering program in 2023, serving a four-month prison sentence. However, he remains Binance's majority shareholder and retains significant influence over the global cryptocurrency exchange.

Uber co-founder and former CEO Travis Kalanick weighed in as well, stating that he would "always sign up to serve" Huang. Kalanick had previously posted support for reinstating Huang at Nvidia and asked followers to share "some great ideas for how to 'persuade' the Nvidia board to bring Jensen back."
"Jensen is a great man, flawed like the rest of us, but at the same time the best amongst us," Kalanick posted to X on Monday. "The world and humanity needs him to fight for a shot at liberty, a counterweight to techno-authoritarianism."
Kalanick resigned from his role as Uber CEO in 2017 after demands from multiple major investors. This came after Kalanick took a leave of absence amid allegations of a misogynistic workplace culture. Though one of the investors sued Kalanick for fraud later that year, the ousted CEO remained on Uber's board until resigning in 2019.

Given their own personal experiences, it's no surprise that many former and current tech executives have supported Huang's return as Nvidia's CEO. Other reactions have been a mixed bag — some celebrating the news, some condemning it, and others simply enjoying the jokes and memes.
"Reinstating Huang is a disaster for corporate governance and even the rule of law, but it is objectively very very funny," author and tech reporter David Z. Morris posted to X.
