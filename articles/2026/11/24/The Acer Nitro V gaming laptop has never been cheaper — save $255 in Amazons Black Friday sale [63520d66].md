# The Acer Nitro V gaming laptop has never been cheaper — save $255 in Amazons Black Friday sale

The Acer Nitro V gaming laptop has never been cheaper — save $255 in Amazon's Black Friday sale
TL;DR: The Acer Nitro V gaming laptop is on sale for $1,444.99 at Amazon, down from its $1,699.99 list price.
Among the many gaming laptop discounts cropping up in the run-up to Black Friday, one of Acer's newer RTX 50 Series-equipped machines has just received its biggest Amazon markdown ever— bringing this particular 16-inch Nitro V below $1,450.
As of Nov. 24, the Acer Nitro V gaming laptop is going for $1,444.99 on Amazon, with $255 off that you can put towards games and accessories. Shipped and sold by Amazon directly rather than a third-party seller, you’ll get free delivery. 
        
            The best tech deal this week
                            
                    
            
With this particular configuration — which we’ve confirmed is at its best-ever price on Amazon — the high-spec portable rig you’re getting offers an Intel Core 7 240H processor alongside an Nvidia GeForce RTX 5050 laptop GPU. Coupled with DLSS 4.5 support, you'll have some extra breathing room when pushing the graphical settings in compatible PC games like Cyberpunk 2077, Valorant, and Fortnite.
What’s more, its 16-inch 1920 x 1200 IPS display boasts a 165Hz refresh rate, which is handy for other fast-paced and fluid competitive games like Apex Legends. Meanwhile, the dual-fan cooling system should help keep temperatures in check during longer gaming sessions.
There's also 16GB of DDR5 RAM, a 1TB Gen 4 SSD to accommodate those increasingly hefty game installations, and two months of Xbox Game Pass thrown in for free.
