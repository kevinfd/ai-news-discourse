# I read 'How Generative AI Will Change the World' by Shota Imai, SB Shinsho, January 2024

note · HEISAN · 2026-11-10

SYSTEM NOTICE
I read 'How Generative AI Will Change the World' by Shota Imai, SB Shinsho, January 2024
I often hear about generative AI. The technology seems to be advancing day by day. However, I don't know the basics. Feeling that was a problem, I read this book, which seemed understandable. Thanks to the careful and easy-to-understand explanations starting from the basics, as well as the clear definitions provided, I was able to get a rough understanding. However, even the author, who is an expert (perhaps because he is an expert?), says that predicting the future is difficult. Well, anyway, I will aim to become a person who uses AI and live a human life. I have listed the contents I (think I) understood below. It's like a memo.
Chapter 1: The Historical Turning Point of the Generative AI Revolution
Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that has the ability to create new ideas and content, much like humans do. It learns patterns from vast amounts of data and uses them to create new outputs. Being 'creative' does not mean creating completely new ideas or content from nothing. It is a process of learning patterns from existing data and combining them to produce new outputs. This is similar to human creativity. It is realized through deep learning, which learns from deep neural networks that mimic the human brain. There are discriminative models (facial recognition, news article classification, etc.) and generative models. Both language and video are possible. ChatGPT. General Purpose Technology: Technology that can be applied in various fields, such as electricity and the internet. The generative AI revolution is bringing about social change at the fastest pace in history. Superintelligence that surpasses humans is also realistic. A sense of happiness to have encountered such a marvelous and dramatic era.
Chapter 2: The Technology Behind Generative AI. AI: Artificial Intelligence. 1956, scientist John McCarthy. First boom until 1970. Search and inference, won at chess in 1997. Second boom, around 1980-1990. Giving knowledge to AI, conversational AI. Third boom, around 2012. Until then, humans organized problems and provided knowledge, but machines began to learn automatically (machine learning). Deep learning using neural networks. In the human brain, the smallest components, neurons (nerve cells), are connected via synapses to receive and transmit electrical signals. This process is reproduced on a computer. The strength of the connections between neurons is called parameters. Neurons form input layers, hidden layers, and output layers, which are deep. In other words, there are a large number of parameters. Types of machine learning: 1. Supervised learning: Humans teach. 2. Unsupervised learning: Machines do it automatically. 3. Reinforcement learning: Machines make decisions based on experience data and rewards?? 4. Self-supervised learning: The teacher is also a machine; this is generative AI.
Language model, used for language generative AI. A model that assigns probabilities to generated words and sentences. Example: Sentence 1: This apple is ( ). Sentence 2: I got an apple that is different from the usual one from a farmer friend. This apple is ( ). Options: 1. Einstein, 2. Yellow, 3. Delicious, 4. Go. The options (probabilities) change depending on the sentence. Repeating such fill-in-the-blank problems. Knowledge that apples are usually red is also necessary.
But this alone is not enough. For 'I have a bad relationship with my boss. What should I do?', an answer like 'Just punch him' is not good. Humans create the answers. Humans rank the generated answers (feedback). Doing these three things on a large scale. Transformer: Large-scale neural network. Scaling law: Model performance improves when the number of parameters, data used, and computational amount are increased simultaneously. The answer improves depending on the prompt (instructions entered by humans). If done in stages, a chain of thought occurs. Image generation AI is a diffusion model. It creates images by gradually turning an image into noise (diffusion process) and then gradually removing the noise (reverse diffusion process).
Chapter 3: Jobs That Will Disappear and Jobs That Will Remain. As of 2013, 'the lower the wage and the less special skills required, the higher the likelihood of being affected by automation by computers and AI.' As of 2023, 'the higher the wage and the higher the level of education and skills, the higher the likelihood of being affected by automation by computers and AI.' The premise has changed with generative AI. Polanyi's paradox: 'People know more than they can express in words (tacit knowledge).' Routine work is work where the content is decided in advance and can be described in human language. Simple work. This can be programmed into a computer. Non-routine work is work that requires tacit knowledge. Driving a car, distinguishing between a cat and a dog, anomaly detection. The feeling that 'something is strange.' Generative AI has made it possible to realize part of tacit knowledge, creative intelligence, and social intelligence. Moravec's paradox: 'For AI, high-level tasks that humans think about carefully are easy, but things that humans do easily without thinking are difficult.' Picking up things with chopsticks, moving around in a messy room, skipping, etc. This is still difficult for AI. That is why physical labor is currently less affected. Being affected and being taken away are different. If the technology is labor-complementary, it leads to labor efficiency, wage increases, and the creation of new labor. Electricity and mass production technology during the Industrial Revolution. If it is labor-substituting, jobs are taken away. Spinning machines during the Industrial Revolution, etc. But in the long run, both technologies bring benefits to industrial development. There is debate over which one generative AI is. Perhaps it is slightly more labor-complementary. Even so, safety nets and social and political efforts are necessary. Effects are appearing in customer service and programming. Benefits such as product descriptions and scripts to promote conversation with customers can be expected in marketing and sales. Personal health management is also possible. Education and teachers are also possible. Researchers use it for papers. It is also applied to medicine, but ethical issues remain.
Chapter 4: The Value of 'Creation' Re-examined by AI. Notable elements regarding culture, art, and creative activities since the advent of generative AI: 1. People without special creative abilities can now produce high-quality content. 2. Cultural content that has meaning because it is 'created by AI' or that is only possible because of AI is being born. 3. Those who have creative abilities can create higher-quality content than general users at a faster speed than ever before by combining their abilities with generative AI. 4. With the advent of generative AI, the perspective and values by which humans evaluate creative works are changing, and there is a possibility that not only surface quality but also 'whether it was generated by AI' will affect the evaluation of the work. Classification of creativity: 1. Combinational Creativity: Creating something new by combining existing ideas or knowledge. 2. Exploratory Creativity: Creating something new by exploring existing ideas or knowledge according to some rules or procedures. 3. Transformational Creativity: Creating something completely new by jumping over the framework of existing ideas or knowledge and defining new rules. 1 and 2 are possible with generative AI. 3 is not yet possible. But most of what humans do is 1 or 2. Humans each live completely different lives, and individual emotions and thoughts reflect their lives. I believe that creative acts that reflect these emotions, thoughts, and stories are what deserve to be called 'creation.' AI having a certain kind of creativity and AI being able to create are not the same; mere generation and creation are different.
The development of AI as a creative tool is remarkable. I have high expectations for the future. New technologies encourage the development of new cultures. In the past, there were photography, synthesizers, Vocaloid (Hatsune Miku), etc. Currently, there are many negative opinions about things created by AI in creative works and jobs. This is thought to be due to thinking about the effort, passion, and hardship involved in completing a creative work. Conventional wisdom is wavering. Future developments are unpredictable.
Chapter 5: The Future of Humanity Walking with Generative AI. I will state what could happen from a medium- to long-term perspective and the outlook for the future as the author's opinion. - If you ask AI, all questions will be solved. - If you have an idea, you can realize anything. - Humans will concentrate on things that only humans can do. Routine work will disappear, and work like coming up with ideas or thinking will remain. In life, there will be more time to enjoy. - Is it okay for generative AI to use learning data without permission? AI requires vast amounts of data, but legal regulations regarding this copyright have not caught up. If strictly regulated, the use of all services will be hindered. - It will become impossible to distinguish between real and fake digital content. It is necessary to provide functions for detection along with services. - The connection between the sender and receiver of information will become thin. The connection with people who gave advice or taught you will disappear. You can just ask AI. - The acquisition of language brought dramatic changes to human history. Language expanded information sharing, promoted the understanding of concepts, and strengthened human communities. It enabled accelerated development. Deep learning is the 'birth of the eye.' Generative AI is a cognitive revolution. - In front of superintelligence, will the difference between an ordinary person and Einstein also become meaningless?
Dialogue and Conclusion. Because progress is so fast, it is difficult to predict the future. If I dare to say something in that situation: - Increase metacognition and acquire strategic thinking. The winning pattern is to take extreme actions. You cannot win if you do the same thing as ordinary people. Increase your own metacognition. Observe your own habits of action and thought from a bird's-eye view. Efforts that others cannot make, identifying the crucial moments. - Skills to give instructions to AI well are necessary. - As society as a whole changes significantly, the idea of a 'human-centered society' that finds meaning in what humans do, away from productivity and efficiency, will grow.
That is all.
