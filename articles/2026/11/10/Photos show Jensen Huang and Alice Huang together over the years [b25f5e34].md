# Photos show Jensen Huang and Alice Huang together over the years

Business Insider · Katherine Li,Aditi Bharade, Katherine Li,Aditi Bharade (katherineli@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-10

Nvidia board investigates CEO Jensen Huang's affair with Alice Huang. CTI/Reuters Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has admitted to having an affair. The admission followed reports about his relationship with Alice Huang, who US officials believe was passing information to Beijing, per The New York Times. Here is a compilation of public sightings of the duo together. Headlines and markets have one thing on their minds this week: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang 's affair. The semiconductor tycoon said on November 8 that he had been unfaithful to his wife, days after The New York Times reported on his close relationship with Alice Huang, an executive at the Singaporean data center company Megaspeed who US officials believe was passing information to Beijing. Following the Times' initial story in late October, Nvidia characterized Jensen Huang and Alice Huang's interactions as routine business . Megaspeed, established in 2023, is an Nvidia customer and a spinoff of the Chinese company 7Road Holdings. But the pair have been seen together several times in recent years, at conference after-parties and often surrounded by other tech executives. Little is known about Alice Huang — identified as Huang Le in Singapore corporate filings — but the Times wrote that she previously worked in a Shanghai-based investment fund that backed 7Road.
