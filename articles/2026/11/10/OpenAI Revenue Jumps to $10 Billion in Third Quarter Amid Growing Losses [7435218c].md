# OpenAI Revenue Jumps to $10 Billion in Third Quarter Amid Growing Losses

The Information · Valida Pau, Jemima McEvoy · 2026-11-10

OpenAI has told investors that revenue jumped nearly 50% to $10 billion in the third quarter, rebounding from a period of slower growth as demand for its more recent models and enterprise products picked up, according to a person familiar with the matter. 

 However, the company’s operating losses also grew during the third quarter from $10.1 billion to $10.9 billion, the person said, demonstrating how it is still struggling to make its sprawling business model profitable. There are signs of progress: Its adjusted operating margin improved from -150% to -109%.
