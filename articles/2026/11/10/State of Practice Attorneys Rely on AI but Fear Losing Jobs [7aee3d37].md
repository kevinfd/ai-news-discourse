# State of Practice: Attorneys Rely on AI but Fear Losing Jobs

Bloomberg Law News · Riya Sharma, Eric Killelea · 2026-11-10

The rapid growth of AI in legal practice is reshaping how work gets done by attorneys, but fears of being replaced by the tech persist and the return on investment remains mixed.
Not a subscriber? Get the report here.
A quarter of attorneys now fear losing their jobs to artificial intelligence, according to Bloomberg Law’s new State of Practice survey. For early-career lawyers, the concern is far more pronounced, with more than 1 in 3 associates worried that AI will replace their job. That compares to 16% of partners.
