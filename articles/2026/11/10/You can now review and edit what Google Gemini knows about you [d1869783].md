# You can now review and edit what Google Gemini knows about you.

The Verge · Andrew Liszewski · 2026-11-10

A
You can now review and edit what Google Gemini knows about you.
A new Gemini feature called Memory summary lets you “view, edit, and delete what Gemini Apps understands about you” to help ensure the AI’s responses are accurate and more relevant, according to a support page on Google’s website. The feature is currently only available to US users “with an active Google AI plan,” and it requires features like Keep Activity and Memory to be enabled.
