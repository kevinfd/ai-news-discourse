# Australia tightens AI oversight rules after OpenAI agent breached government website

The Hindu · Reuters · 2026-11-10

Australia will make it mandatory for artificial intelligence companies to immediately report when their AI models go rogue, and open models for evaluations, the government said on Tuesday, after an OpenAI agent breached a government website in June.
“(The recommendations) include mandatory AI company reporting when it comes to misaligned behaviour or cyber incidents, and consideration of reforming criminal law in cases of serious misconduct,” Albanese told reporters.
