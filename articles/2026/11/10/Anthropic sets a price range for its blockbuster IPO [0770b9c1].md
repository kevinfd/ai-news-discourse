# Anthropic sets a price range for its blockbuster IPO

Business Insider · Tom Carter, Tom Carter (tcarter@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-10

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei. Kevin Dietsch/Getty Images Anthropic has unveiled plans to raise up to $90 billion in its record-breaking IPO. The AI startup said in a filing its shares would be priced between $70 and $78. It means Anthropic's IPO could outstrip the $75 billion initially raised by Elon Musk's SpaceX. Anthropic has set a price for its much-anticipated IPO . In a regulatory filing on Tuesday, the AI startup said that shares for the upcoming public offering would be priced between $70 and $78, indicating that it intends to raise between $57 billion and $64 billion. The filing also said that Anthropic has entered into an agreement with investors, including Broadcom, Google, and Nvidia, to purchase $26 billion in shares at the IPO price, bringing the total amount the company could raise up to around $90 billion. At the high end, that would make Anthropic's public offering the largest of all time, outstripping the $75 billion initially raised by SpaceX's record-breaking IPO in June. Elon Musk's rocket company ultimately sold more than $85 billion worth of shares. A raise at that scale could give Anthropic, which is reportedly targeting its IPO for mid-November, an implied valuation of up to about $1 trillion. Anthropic said in the filing it would reveal the date of the proposed share offering "as soon as practicable." The Claude maker was valued at $965 billion following its $65 billion funding round announced in May. Business Insider reported in July that Anthropic had soared to a $1.2 trillion valuation on the secondary market amid surging demand for shares. It marks a dizzying rise for the red-hot AI startup, which was founded in 2021 by CEO Dario Amodei and other former members of OpenAI. In Anthropic's S-1 filing , which was released last month, the company disclosed a total net loss of about $42 billion in 2025 against revenue of $4.6 billion. In an amended filing submitted earlier this month , Anthropic said it generated nearly $17 billion in revenue in the third quarter, and a $567 million operating loss. The AI firm also disclosed $895 billion in committed spending on compute and infrastructure — nearly half of it expected by the end of 2030. This is a developing story. Check back later for updates.
