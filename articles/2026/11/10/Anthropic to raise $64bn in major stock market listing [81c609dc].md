# Anthropic to raise $64bn in major stock market listing

BBC News · Dearbail Jordan · 2026-11-10

Anthropic to raise $64bn in major stock market listing
- Published
Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude chatbot, is planning to raise up to $64bn (£48bn) when it lists its shares for sale on New York's Nasdaq stock exchange.
The loss-making company said it will offer its shares for between $70 and $78 each in its initial public offering (IPO), which is expected to go ahead this month.
This would value Anthropic at between $1.1 trillion and $1.3 trillion which, although large, is below the $2 trillion some analysts had predicted weeks ago.
It is floating at a volatile time for AI firms. After last week's US midterm elections, the Nasdaq fell sharply on concerns a Democrat-led Congress could slow development of AI and the buildout of data centres.
There are also wider fears over the billions of dollars being spent on AI and whether it will pay off.
Fiona Cincotta, a senior market analyst at City Index, said the amount Anthropic wants to raise is "fairly bullish" within "the volatility [and] concerns towards AI that we've seen in sort of particularly last week".
She told Reuters: "It's showing that they are still sort of upbeat about the outlook as far as AI is concerned and that obviously that they think that the demand is going to be there."
