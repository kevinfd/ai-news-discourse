# We’re bringing Adobe’s creative and productivity tools closer to your everyday Google apps.

Google AI Blog · Dr Nontobeko Ntombela · 2026-11-10

We’re bringing Adobe’s creative and productivity tools closer to your everyday Google apps.
Today at Adobe MAX, we announced we’re expanding our partnership with Adobe.
Switching between apps to edit a PDF or fine-tune an image can quickly break your focus. Our partnership will bring Adobe’s creative and productivity tools directly into the Google products you use every day. It will also bring Google’s frontier AI models into Adobe’s apps. Here’s what’s coming:
- Work faster with PDFs: You’ll soon have a direct pathway from Google Drive and Gmail into Adobe Acrobat, surfaced as a suggested app across both services, including an “Edit with” entry point in file previews.
- Create right where you search and chat: Adobe is expanding its connectors in Gemini and Gemini Enterprise with Acrobat and additional creative tools. Adobe will also bring its creative tools to AI Mode in Google Search.
- Powering creatives and enterprises: Businesses will be able to use Adobe’s CX Enterprise Coworker agent in Gemini Enterprise — an AI assistant that helps teams design graphics, work with PDFs, and launch marketing campaigns without switching apps.
- Exploring editing experiences with DeepMind: Adobe and Google DeepMind will explore new editing capabilities for creative professionals, starting with an image model for Photoshop that could enable near-real-time edits.
