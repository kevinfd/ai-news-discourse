AI models abandon climate facts to please users, UNESCO report finds
AI models abandon climate facts to agree with users in eight out of 10 tests, a UNESCO-UCL report presented at COP31 has found. Al Jazeera spoke to a UNESCO specialist in AI policy about how models that perform well on basic climate science can mislead those relying on them for climate decisions.
