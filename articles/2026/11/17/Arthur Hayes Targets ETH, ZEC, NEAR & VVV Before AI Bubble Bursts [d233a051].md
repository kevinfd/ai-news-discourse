# Arthur Hayes Targets ETH, ZEC, NEAR & VVV Before AI Bubble Bursts

Analytics Insight · Yusuf Islam, Pranchal Srivastava · 2026-11-17

News
Arthur Hayes Targets ETH, ZEC, NEAR & VVV Before AI Bubble Bursts
Arthur Hayes expects four cryptocurrencies to recover before a potential AI bubble collapse. His predictions suggest substantial gains for Ethereum, Zcash, NEAR and Venice Token. However, the BitMEX co-founder expressed limited confidence in his trades.
BitMEX co-founder Arthur Hayes expects Ethereum, Zcash, NEAR Protocol and Venice Token to rally before a potential collapse of the artificial intelligence bubble. On November 17, Hayes outlined price targets of USD 2,800 for ETH, USD 1,800 for ZEC, USD 6 for NEAR and USD 40 for VVV.
Hayes referred to the cryptocurrencies as the “Four Horsemen” in an X post. Despite projecting substantial gains, he disclosed that his trading positions ranged from small to medium because of his limited conviction.
The forecasts come amid widespread selling across the cryptocurrency market. All four assets remain considerably below their proposed targets. Hayes also provided no timeline for the anticipated recovery or the potential downturn in the AI sector.
Ethereum traded near USD 1,830 on November 17 and would need to gain approximately 53% to reach Hayes’ target. The cryptocurrency recently fell toward USD 1,712 during a weekend selloff before recovering some of its losses.
Selling pressure has also extended to institutional investment products. U.S. spot Ethereum exchange-traded funds recorded net withdrawals of USD 398.9 million between November 9 and 13. Meanwhile, ETH lost roughly 14% over the preceding week.
Despite these losses, cryptocurrency analyst Ted Pillows identified a potential support area along Ethereum’s four-year rising trendline on November 16. The analyst suggested that buyers could return around this region.
Technical indicators presented mixed readings. Ethereum’s daily MACD remained negative, while its weekly Chaikin Money Flow stood at 0.05, indicating some buying pressure.
Zcash traded around USD 922 on November 17, meaning it would need an approximately 95% rally to reach Hayes’ forecast. The privacy cryptocurrency previously traded above USD 1,300 earlier in the month.
Its recent losses coincided with continued ETF withdrawals and derivatives liquidations. Around November 12, liquidations involving ZEC reached approximately USD 18 million.
Meanwhile, Grayscale announced a USD 1 million commitment to support the Zcash ecosystem. The company plans to finance the initiative through sponsor fees from its ZCSH exchange-traded fund.
Development activity has also continued despite the price decline. Zcash completed its NU7 network upgrade on November 6, reducing target block times from 75 seconds to 25 seconds.
The upgrade introduced new limits for shielded transactions and adjusted the distribution of transaction fees. Developers later proposed reducing block times further to 20 seconds, pending community approval.

Hayes expects NEAR Protocol to reach USD 6, representing approximately 85% upside from its November 17 price of USD 3.24. NEAR had declined 9.3% over seven days and 33.5% over the previous month.
Technical indicators showed 12 sell signals, three buy signals and seven neutral readings. Moving averages accounted for 10 sell signals, while the 14-day Relative Strength Index remained neutral.
NEAR faces immediate resistance at USD 3.80, followed by USD 4.78. Its nearest identified support stands at USD 2.91.
Separately, NEAR AI restored its Cloud service on November 10 following a security breach. Attackers exploited an SGLang software vulnerability that exposed information from approximately 100 users and 18 AI agents. The company confirmed that the incident did not affect its blockchain protocol.
Meanwhile, Venice Token traded near USD 21.15, leaving it approximately 89% below the required gain-adjusted target of USD 40. Reaching that level would take VVV beyond its previous record of USD 34.53 on September 21.
The token gained approximately 5% over 24 hours after falling toward USD 17.35 on November 14. Its market capitalization reached nearly USD 1.04 billion, while daily trading volume approached USD 48 million.
Arthur Hayes expects Ethereum, Zcash, NEAR Protocol and Venice Token to post substantial gains before a potential AI bubble collapse. However, recent market losses and prevailing selling pressure remain obstacles to those targets. The BitMEX co-founder has expressed limited trading conviction and has not provided a timeline for the anticipated rally.
