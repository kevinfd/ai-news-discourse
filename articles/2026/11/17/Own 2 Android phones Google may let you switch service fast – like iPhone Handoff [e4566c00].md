# Own 2 Android phones? Google may let you switch service fast – like iPhone Handoff

ZDNet · Jada Jones · 2026-11-17

ZDNET’s key takeaways
- Code strings point to an incoming Android Fast switch feature.
- This feature allows two phones to share one eSIM.
- Both devices must use the same Google account.
In iOS 27, Apple announced iPhone Handoff, a networking feature that allows users to switch cell service between two eSIM-enabled iPhones. Text strings in Google’s SIM Manager app suggest the company is preparing a similar feature for compatible Android phones, according to Android Authority.
Code strings include “Use a SIM on two phones,” and note that the feature will be called Fast Switch. Little information is currently available about the feature’s limitations, including which phones or mobile carriers may not be compatible.

More from ZDNET
On Apple’s side, any iPhone in the US running iOS 27 with a T-Mobile or Verizon-provided eSIM can use iPhone Handoff. AT&T does not currently support iPhone Handoff.
However, the code strings uncovered by Android Authority show that Android’s Fast Switch will work similarly to iPhone Handoff, in that users must be signed in to both devices under the same Google account for the feature to work.
How will Fast Switch work?
If Fast Switch works anything like iPhone Handoff, which it appears it will, one eSIM can be switched between two phones. So, calls, messages, location services, and other information synced to your Apple ID can remain consistent between two devices.

Some third-party apps, like banking, communications, or social media, may also remain consistent across the two phones, depending on the app’s syncing behavior.
Who would need Fast Switch?
Features like Apple’s iPhone Handoff and Google’s Fast Switch are ideal for people who regularly use two phones. Business owners and digital creators may want one phone strictly for business purposes and the other for personal use.

In that case, one person can keep up with two phones without paying for two phone service lines through their carrier. Still, users will incur a fee, as T-Mobile charges $5/month for iPhone Handoff, while Verizon charges $10/month.
