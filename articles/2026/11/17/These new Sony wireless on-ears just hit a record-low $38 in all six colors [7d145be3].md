These new Sony wireless on-ears just hit a record-low $38 in all six colors
SAVE $31.99: As of Nov. 17, the Sony WH-CH530 wireless on-ear headphones are on sale for $38 at Amazon, down from their $69.99 list price — a 46% discount and their lowest price ever, available across all six colorways.
Amazon's early Black Friday sale just dropped the Sony WH-CH530 wireless on-ear headphones to $38, down from their $69.99 list price. That saves you $31.99 for a brand-new record-low price on a pair Sony only launched in September of this year. Better yet, the $38 price applies across all six colors (black, white, pink, cobalt, coral, and mint), so you don't have to pay extra just to skip basic black.
The standout feature here is the battery life. You get up to 55 hours of playback on a single charge, which easily stretches across a full week of commutes or a long weekend trip without hunting for an outlet. Forgetting to plug them in is not a big deal either, since a quick three-minute charge gives you an extra hour and a half of listening time.

For everyday comfort and commuting, the lightweight build features soft padded earcups and a swivel design that lies flat in a bag. Multipoint Bluetooth lets you stay connected to two devices at once — making it easy to watch a video on your laptop and seamlessly take a call on your phone — while Fast Pair handles quick Android pairing and built-in AI noise reduction keeps your voice clear on hands-free calls.
Using the free Sony Sound Connect app, you can tweak the sound with a 10-band equalizer, enable audio upscaling to sharpen compressed streaming tracks, or cap the maximum volume at 5 levels. Physical buttons on the earcup also handle volume and playback without reaching for your phone.
Just keep in mind that these on-ear headphones rely on cushioned earpads rather than active noise cancellation to block out background noise, and they don't include a wired audio cable — though at $38, those are very easy compromises to live with.
At $38 across all six colorways, these are an easy win for daily commutes or holiday gifting. Grab the Sony WH-CH530 wireless on-ear headphones on Amazon now.
