# Nvidia results beat expectations, as ousted CEO Huang told no way back

South China Morning Post · Agence France-Presse · 2026-11-17

US chip giant Nvidia doubled revenue in the third quarter to more than US$115 billion, the company reported on Tuesday while ruling out any return for ousted CEO Jensen Huang after a Chinese spy scandal.
The results, which beat expectations, came hours after Huang said he wanted his job back in the latest twist for the company at the centre of an AI boom that has propelled markets to record highs and boosted global economic growth.
