# Alice Huang’s Husband Built a Data-Center Empire Backed by the Chinese State

Wall Street Journal · darrenjyc · 2026-11-17

Alice Huang and her husband, Kang Wei, operated on two tracks pointed at the same goal of vaulting China’s artificial-intelligence capabilities ahead of the U.S. Her job: cultivating a relationship with Nvidia’s then-CEO Jensen Huang and securing a pipeline of the company’s advanced-computing chips that Washington has sought to keep from China. His job: building a sprawling network of government-backed data centers across China, a review of Chinese public records shows.
