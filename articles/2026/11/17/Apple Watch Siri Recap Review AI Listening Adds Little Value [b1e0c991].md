# Apple Watch Siri Recap Review: AI Listening Adds Little Value

Bloomberg · Natalie Lung · 2026-11-17

With its new Audio Intelligence smartwatch features, Apple Inc. is taking a small step into a hot new segment: AI wearables that can summarize everyday conversations to help you stay present. But after a week of testing Apple’s tools alongside Amazon Bee, Granola and Otter, I found that rivals delivered more useful notes and that Apple’s results rarely justified the privacy trade-offs.
