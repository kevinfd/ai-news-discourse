# Anthropic IPO Yields 10X Returns for Venture Firms

The Information · Julia Hornstein · 2026-11-17

For the first two years of its existence, Anthropic was shunned by most venture capitalists, drawing instead on a mix of wealthy individuals such as former Google CEO Eric Schmidt and cloud computing companies like Google . 

 But even after VC firms made relatively late entries into Anthropic, a handful of them, including Spark Capital, Factorial Funds, Sound Ventures and Menlo Ventures, will hold stakes worth over 10 times their investment when Anthropic prices its IPO Wednesday at an expected $1.4 trillion valuation, according to people with knowledge of their investments.
