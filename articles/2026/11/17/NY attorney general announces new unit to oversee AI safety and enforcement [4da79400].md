# NY attorney general announces new unit to oversee AI safety and enforcement

The Baltimore Sun · WRGB Staff · 2026-11-17

Getting your Trinity Audio player ready...
The AI Safety, Standards, Enforcement, and Transparency (ASSET) Unit will focus on enforcing AI safety laws, protecting consumer privacy and addressing potential threats involving cybersecurity, digital scams and online safety in New York. The unit will also oversee enforcement of the state’s Responsible AI Safety Education (RAISE) Act that takes effect on Jan. 1.
