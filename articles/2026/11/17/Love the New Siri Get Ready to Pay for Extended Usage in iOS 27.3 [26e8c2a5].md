# Love the New Siri? Get Ready to Pay for Extended Usage in iOS 27.3

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-11-17

Apple overhauled Siri with new AI capabilities at WWDC in June, and then-CEO Tim Cook signaled the following month that users would eventually be able to “buy up the stack on iCloud+” for expanded access. With the iOS 27.3 beta 2 released on Monday, Apple finally shared a glimpse of how its planned AI paywalls would work.
As reported by MacRumors, Siri AI and Apple Intelligence features come with daily usage limits in the latest iOS 27.3 beta. The daily limit applies to features processed on Apple’s servers, including Image Playground and Clean Up in Photos, automatically resetting at midnight with no carryover.
How Apple calculates AI usage is unclear, but MacRumors reports that the company plans to notify users when they are nearing their free daily allowance. The limits are calculated per device, not per account, and will vary depending on the complexity of the request, the outlet adds.
Higher usage can be unlocked with iCloud+ plans, starting with the 200GB option at $2.99 per month. If you’re on the base 50GB plan, you’ll get the same free daily allowance as non-subscribers.
The 200GB plan doubles the free limit. The 2TB plan ($9.99 per month) unlocks up to 5x the limit, the 6TB plan ($29.99 per month) unlocks 10x the limit, and the 12TB plan ($59.99 per month) unlocks 20x the limit. Those on a family plan will get extended usage from a shared pool, divided on a first-come, first-served basis.
Once you reach the daily limit for these tiers, Apple will restrict certain Siri and Apple Intelligence features. For instance, Siri AI may not process multi-step personal context requests or cloud-based workflows and automations in Shortcuts. Apple Intelligence’s image generation and editing tools may also be restricted, alongside third-party AI features that rely on Apple’s models.
Similar limits have been introduced in iPadOS 27.3 and macOS 27.3 betas as well. The updates arrive a week after Apple introduced charges for high AI use in Logic Pro.
Based on what we’ve tracked so far, iOS 27.3 will be a major update for iPhone users. It is expected to roll out a new face-unlocking feature on the iPhone Duo, scanning of public iCloud links for harmful content, and many other minor improvements.
