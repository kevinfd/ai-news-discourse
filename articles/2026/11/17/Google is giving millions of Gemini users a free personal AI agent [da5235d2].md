# Google is giving millions of Gemini users a free personal AI agent

Android Police · Rajesh Pandey · 2026-11-17

Google has so far kept Spark, which was recently renamed to tasks, limited to its paid Pro and Ultra subscribers. But in a major move, the company is making Gemini tasks free for millions of users. Plus, the feature will now use Google's latest Gemini 4 Hydrogen model for even better understanding, context, and speed.
With its latest round of improvements, Google is opening Gemini tasks to hundreds of millions of users in six countries: the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. This comes as Gemini Tasks now uses Google's latest Hydrogen model, which is faster, more efficient, and smarter.
That's not it. Gemini tasks are also getting useful improvements, including the ability to call local businesses via Google Duplex, pay and check out via Google Pay, and the ability to log in to sites without sharing passwords using Google Password Manager. For now, these improvements are only rolling out in the US.
Thanks to these upgrades, you can use Gemini tasks to make hotel or restaurant reservations, book service appointments, and more. Google's AI will automatically call the business, find a suitable time after checking your Google Calendar, and then add the appointment to your calendar.
Even better, Gemini task can make purchases on your behalf, with Google working with Mastercard and Visa to keep the entire process secure. Plus, you can set a hard spending cap, so Gemini will stay within that budget. The assistant will ask for confirmation or require prior approval before it makes any purchases, though.
Let Gemini handle the tedious and repetitive tasks
Another nice quality-of-life improvement is the ability to forward emails to Gemini (gemini@google.com) with the relevant instructions, and Gemini will take care of the rest. Alternatively, you can reach Gemini via an RCS text. For now, these features are limited to the US.
In a post on X, Josh Woodward, VP of Product for AI at Google, says the company has also rolled out its "biggest update yet" for the Gemini x Google Workspace integration. This should ensure that tool calls trigger more reliably, and you can take more actions within Gemini than ever before.
Gemini Task is available both on the web and mobile. Open the Gemini app, switch the toggle on the home screen from Chat to Task, and then describe the action you want Gemini to do.
Gemini's agentic capabilities are nowhere near as good as Meta's AI Muse, OpenClaw Hermes, or other such AI agents. Still, it's great at handling tedious and repetitive work. And now, with Google making Gemini tasks free, it's almost a no-brainer over other such AI agents. Hopefully, the company's servers will be able to keep up with the additional load this move will bring.
