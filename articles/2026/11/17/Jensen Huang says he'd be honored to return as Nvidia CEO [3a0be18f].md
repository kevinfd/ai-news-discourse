# Jensen Huang says he'd be "honored" to return as Nvidia CEO

Axios · Herb Scribner · 2026-11-17

Jensen Huang acknowledged in a statement that he'd be willing to return to Nvidia as CEO. Why it matters: A push to reinstate Huang began almost as soon as he exited the company, but Nvidia's board isn't currently considering reinstating the former CEO, according to multiple reports. What they're saying: "Since my departure, I've seen an outpouring of calls from Nvidia's employees, investors, business partners, and customers for me to return as CEO," Huang's statement said. "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO." The Wall Street Journal first reported Huang's statement. State of play: Multiple analyses and reports have mentioned a slew of replacement candidates for Huang's successor at Nvidia. President Trump suggested former Apple CEO Tim Cook for the role. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has urged Nvidia to look for outside candidates. Nvidia CFO Colette Kress is currently serving as the company's interim CEO. Catch up quick: Nvidia announced last week that Huang resigned as CEO after he allegedly had affairs with two Nvidia employees and former Megaspeed CEO Alice Huang, who is suspected of being a Chinese spy. Jensen Huang immediately faced Congressional backlash over whether Nvidia's AI chips were compromised by Chinese intelligence and whether his company did enough to protect American tech. He has agreed to testify before Congress next month, lawmakers said Tuesday. Since he stepped down , Nvidia's stock has tumbled, and tech CEOs have questioned whether Jensen Huang should have been ousted in the first place. His backers have included Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan , Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney , Replit CEO Amjad Masad and Shopify CEO Tobias Lütke. Elon Musk has also backed the campaign to reinstate Jensen Huang, which has collected more than 180,000 signatures Many supporters say that a lack of proof of the allegations about his relationship with Alice Huang is reason enough to reinstate him. The other side: Nvidia's investigation into Jensen Huang's conduct is ongoing. Editor's note: This story has been updated with additional context throughout.
