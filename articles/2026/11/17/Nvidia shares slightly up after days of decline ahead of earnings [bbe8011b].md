# Nvidia shares slightly up after days of decline ahead of earnings

CNBC · Kai Nicol-Schwarz · 2026-11-17

Nvidia shares were slightly up on Tuesday, ahead of earnings and following days of decline amid the fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation as CEO.
The chip giant fell before moving higher in choppy premarket trading. It was up 1.2% as of 6:55 a.m. ET on Tuesday.
Nvidia's stock slid 4% on Monday, bringing shares to their lowest close since July 1, 2025.
Huang resigned from Nvidia, the company's board announced on Thursday, following reports that he had a relationship with a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly believes to be a Chinese spy. Huang told employees he had an affair in an email on Nov. 8.
An online petition calling for Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO drew more than 190,000 signatures, including Elon Musk.
But Nvidia's board of directors is not considering bringing back Jensen Huang as CEO, a person familiar with the matter told CNBC on Monday.
The chip giant is also facing increasing scrutiny from U.S. lawmakers over Chinese companies' access to its advanced chips, which U.S. export controls restrict from going to China.
The U.S. Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security, or BIS, announced on Monday an interim final rule designed to prevent Chinese companies' remote access to AI chips.
Nvidia announced on Tuesday morning that its board had established a committee responsible for oversight of the company's national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management.
The National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee will be chaired by director Suzanne Nora Johnson and will include directors Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri as members, the company said in a statement.
