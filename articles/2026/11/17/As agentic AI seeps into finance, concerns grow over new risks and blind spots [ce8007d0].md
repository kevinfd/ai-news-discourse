# As agentic AI seeps into finance, concerns grow over new risks and blind spots

South China Morning Post · scmp_news · 2026-11-17

As a software glitch disrupted services at several major US banks on Monday and a US Federal Reserve governor warned about the risks posed by increasingly autonomous artificial intelligence systems, regulators and industry leaders gathered in Hong Kong to debate how best to oversee the technology’s rapid spread through global finance. Top regulators from around the world, including Julia Leung Fung-yee, CEO of Hong Kong’s Securities and Futures Commission, discussed challenges ranging from market volatility to cybersecurity risks at the Bloomberg Global Regulatory Forum on Monday.
