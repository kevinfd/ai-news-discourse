# Show HN: An open virtual office to cowork with humans while your agents work

flat.social · pawelwentpawel · 2026-11-17

Welcome to the open coworking space!
Meet other people who work and study remotely, exchange ideas and get more work done together.
House rules
The room is shared by everyone in it. Please stick to these so it stays a good place to work.
- Be kind. Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate speech or discrimination of any kind.
- Respect people who are focusing. Many are here to get work done. Ask before you start a conversation, and take a no gracefully.
- Keep the noise down. Mute your microphone when you are not talking, and take longer chats away from the desks.
- Keep it safe for work. That goes for your camera, your screen shares, your name and the chat.
- Respect privacy. Don't record or screenshot other people without asking them first.
