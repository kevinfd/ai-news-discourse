# Bengaluru Leads India's AI Startup Funding & Unicorns

Rediff Money · Rediff Money Desk · 2026-11-17

Bengaluru Leads India's AI Startup Funding & Unicorns
x
Bengaluru accounts for 36.3% of India's native AI startup funding and 71% of AI/deep tech unicorns, solidifying its position as a global innovation hub.
Key Points
- Bengaluru accounts for 36.3% of India's native AI startup funding and hosts 71% of AI/deep tech unicorns founded since 2023.
- The city has attracted USD 85 billion in venture funding since 2010, representing 47% of all Indian startup funding.
- Bengaluru is home to 57 unicorns, 44% of India's total, making it the world's fifth-largest unicorn hub.
- Startups in Bengaluru achieve unicorn status in approximately six years, faster than other Indian cities.
- Government initiatives are actively promoting the expansion of Karnataka's tech ecosystem beyond Bengaluru.
Karnataka Minister for Electronics, IT and BT Priyank Kharge released the report at the 29th edition of Bengaluru Tech Summit 2026, being held at the Bangalore International Exhibition Centre here.
Prepared jointly by the Karnataka Digital Economy Mission (KDEM) and 3one4 Capital using data from Tracxn, the report analyses Bengaluru's talent pool, investments, unicorns, policies, startup ecosystems beyond the city and six emerging technology sectors.
The data was updated up to September 23, 2026, officials said.
Of the 1,556 deep tech startups founded in Bengaluru since 2010, 1,319 are active and funded, and they have raised USD 3.77 billion, the report said. Of 799 native AI startups, 764 are active and funded.
Bengaluru-based startups have raised about USD 85 billion since 2010, accounting for 47 per cent of all venture funding raised by Indian startups, with USD 76.5 billion of it in the past 10 years, it said.
The city has 3,810 active, funded startups founded since 2010, nearly twice Mumbai's 1,982.
According to Priyank, the report shows how far Karnataka's founders have come and how much more they can build from here.
"Our next task is to make sure a world-class AI, quantum or semiconductor company can grow just as well in Kalaburagi, Hubballi or Mangaluru. Deep tech rewards patience, and Karnataka will stay the course," he said.
The report said close to 30 per cent of India's active, funded women-led startups in deep tech and native AI were founded in Bengaluru, and that together they have raised USD 323.65 million.
Karnataka State Electronics Development Corporation Limited (KEONICS) Chairman Sharath Kumar Bache Gowda said the findings reaffirmed the state's leadership in AI and deep tech while highlighting the opportunity to extend this momentum across Karnataka.
"If we consider startups that originated in Karnataka, listings worth approximately USD 42 billion have taken place since 2024. Another important aspect is the time taken by startups to achieve an IPO exit. Globally, the average time taken is approximately 11 years, while startups in Karnataka have achieved IPO exits in around eight years," he said.
"This is a notable achievement. Whether it is Silicon Valley, China or any other country, we have not seen such examples of startups achieving IPO exits so quickly. This is something we can particularly highlight about Karnataka," he added.
The report said Bengaluru's 57 unicorns are valued at USD 211 billion, accounting for 44 per cent of India's 131 unicorns and 55.3 per cent of their combined value. This makes the city the world's fifth-largest unicorn hub and the largest outside the US, Europe and China, it said.
Bengaluru produced 46 of India's 100 unicorns since 2020, and its startups take about six years to reach unicorn status, against seven to 11 years in other Indian hubs, it said.
The city is home to 212 soonicorns, 36 per cent of India's 589 and more than Delhi NCR (134) or Mumbai (121), the report noted.
Its startups reach soonicorn status in about five years, faster than in any other Indian city, and about 38 per cent of all funds raised by India's soonicorns have gone to Bengaluru companies.
About 300 venture capital firms operate in Karnataka, and Bengaluru holds 75 Category I Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), 21 per cent of India's and second only to Mumbai, it said.
The state's ELEVATE grant-in-aid programme has backed 1,479 startups with Rs 393.58 crore since 2017, with 38.6 per cent of them beyond Bengaluru, the report said.
It added that the Rs 75 crore Beyond Bengaluru Cluster Seed Fund has been set up as a SEBI-registered Category I AIF, Global Technology Centres are coming up in Mysuru, Hubballi and Belagavi, and the Rs 1,000 crore Local Economy Accelerator Programme (LEAP) targets five lakh jobs over five years.
A unicorn is a privately held startup valued at USD 1 billion or more, while a soonicorn is one that is nearing that mark.
