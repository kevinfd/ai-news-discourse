# 'Hero of mine': Sam Altman praises Nvidia's Jensen Huang

Euronews · Ilaria Federico · 2026-11-17

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has praised Nvidia chief Jensen Huang for his role in driving the artificial intelligence revolution, saying it would be difficult to imagine the industry without him.
Speaking at the 'Silicon Valley and the World' conference in Menlo Park, California, on 16 November, Altman highlighted his admiration for the Nvidia chief and the company's contribution to AI development.
"Jensen has long been a hero of mine. Heroes have flaws. I am grateful for the company he's built." Altman said.
He also stressed how closely Nvidia and its chief executive are associated, pointing to Huang's ambition and the scale he has brought to the industry.
Asked whether uncertainty surrounding Huang could have a ripple effect across the AI sector, Altman said he believed the industry would remain strong.
