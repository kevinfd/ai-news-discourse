# I make up to $800 a month completing AI surveys in my free time. My side hustle has helped pay for vacations and Lego sets.

Business Insider · Perri Ormont Blumberg, Perri Ormont Blumberg (insider@insider.com) · 2026-11-17

Ethan Quinn. Courtesy of Ethan Quinn Project manager Ethan Quinn uses Prolific AI studies to turn his spare hours into travel money. He reviews AI podcasts, songs, and images, using his professional audio and lighting skills. Over five years, he's earned $14,000 on Prolific, funding Lego sets and trips to Sedona and Tokyo. This as-told-to essay is based on a conversation with Ethan Quinn, a 36-year-old project manager based in Deerfield, Illinois, who has a side hustle completing AI surveys on Prolific to fund his vacations. It has been edited for length and clarity. I'm a project manager who's very passionate about live events. I love utilizing my technical skills and leadership abilities to help turn my clients' visions into reality. I typically work on shows for corporations and live concerts throughout Chicago. While working full-time, I went back to school in 2018 to earn my Bachelor's degree in business management. In October 2024, I transitioned to a new company and role as a project manager for live events, focusing on audio, video, and lighting. I also have a lucrative side hustle on Prolific that brings in extra money I mostly use for travel. When I was looking for extra income at the end of 2020, I found Prolific Prolific is a data collection platform that connects researchers, AI developers, and product teams with real people who take part in studies online. To get onboarded, I verified my identity through the platform and connected my PayPal account. After successfully completing my studies, I became eligible for more specialized opportunities, including AI-related work. I thought this could be a fun way to help with research and earn money from the comfort of my home. The goal at first was to build some rainy-day money and see what the earning potential was. The first surveys I completed were mostly from educational institutions seeking academic research In the last few years, there have been surveys to help train and review AI. With my unique background in audio and lighting, I've been able to help assess AI from a more technical background. These AI studies have allowed me to generate a side income. I've completed more than 6,700 surveys. The best part is this was all done in my spare time . Each survey takes about 10 to 15 minutes. The AI tasks run the gamut from analyzing images and AI-generated podcasts to fact-checking AI-generated answers Some of the AI training involves a user asking a question, for instance, the score of a basketball game last night, and I have to verify that the answer given by the AI is factual. I've reviewed AI-generated songs and been asked to determine which sounded more realistic or human-made. I've also listened to AI-generated podcasts and have an easy time hearing audio errors or glitches in speech that might not be as noticeable to someone without my professional experience. I've caught myself getting so caught up in podcasts and songs because they sound so realistic that I momentarily forget they're AI-generated. This is leaps and bounds ahead of the AI studies I did a year or two ago, when they were clearly artificial-sounding. Additionally, my background in lighting helps me judge pictures more critically and notice when sunlight is not accurately reflected off a person's face. I enjoy helping shape the future of this field by drawing on my specialized background. I've made about $14,000 on Prolific over the last 5 years Initially, I'd only do a few surveys a month . As I've learned more about the platform and process, most of my income has come in the last few years. In my experience, AI surveys pay more than traditional academic-research surveys, but they take more time and concentration, requiring about 20 to 40 minutes per survey. The amount I do each month ebbs and flows. During a good month when I can do a lot of surveys, I make between $300 and $800. If I had a month where my job didn't demand so much attention and focus, I believe I could make over $1,000. I recently had the goal of buying an expensive Lord of the Rings Lego set. Rather than tapping into my savings or my job income, I set aside earnings from Prolific and was able to pay for the set. I was able to save for a fall 2025 vacation to Sedona with my wife, paid for by my Prolific earnings. We also recently went to Tokyo, and we were able to pay for a lot of our meals, a couple of nights at one of the hotels, and had extra spending money from my Prolific earnings. I see this as a long-term opportunity for me These studies are unique, and it's fun to see how AI improves over time. This work isn't for everyone, and I know many people in demanding jobs would rather watch a movie or play a video game to decompress at night. Honestly, some days that's what I need for my mental health. The most important part is treating AI reviews and surveys with the same level of effort and detail that you would put into your career. Low-quality effort is noticeable and does a disservice to the companies paying you to help. Careful, high-quality work matters.
