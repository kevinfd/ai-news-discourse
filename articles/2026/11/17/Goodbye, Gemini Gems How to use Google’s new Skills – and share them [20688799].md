# Goodbye, Gemini Gems: How to use Google’s new Skills – and share them

ZDNet · Artie Beaty · 2026-11-17

ZDNET’s key takeaways
- Google is retiring Gemini Gems starting today.
- Existing Gems will automatically transition to Skills.
- You can share Skills and add files from Google products.
Google is replacing Gemini Gems with Skills starting Nov. 17, 2026, and it’s carrying over features that let you share Skills and add files from Google products.
Google has announced that it’s turning Gems, a Gemini feature that lets you create customizable AI chatbots, into Skills, which let you create custom instructions for Gemini. Any Gems you have created will automatically convert, but you can transfer them manually if you want to start using them now.
What’s happening to Gemini Gems, and how do Skills work?
While the Gemini Gems feature is technically going away, any Gems you’ve created aren’t.
If you access the Gems portion of Gemini (since the feature is on the way out, you now have to access this through Settings), you’ll see a message that says “Starting November 17, 2026, we’ll automatically begin migrating all of your Gems to Skills.” (The cutoff date for accessing your Gems will be Dec. 16, 2026. Workspace business, enterprise, and non-profit accounts will get cut off in March 2027, and Workspace education accounts will be cut off in June 2027.)
A Gemini Skill functions much the same as a Gem. You describe specific situations when Gemini should use the Skill automatically and tell Gemini what you want the Skill to do (it’s best to give specific examples of output). Google gives the example of a marketing team sharing a brand-voice skill that references their brand guidelines doc in Drive, or a team lead sharing an onboarding skill based on the team handbook.
The biggest difference is that Gems were standalone chats, while Skills are custom instructions that work in any conversation.
You can create as many Skills as you want, but only 100 can be active at a time.
How to share Gemini Skills and add files from Google products
To get users familiar with Skills, Google is bringing over two features from Gems.
First, you can now share any Skill with other users. When you share a Skill, you can choose whether the other person has permission to edit or view only. To share a Skill, go to the three-bar menu in the top left > Customize > Skills. You can view or change permissions from that screen.

When a Skill is added, Gemini can use it automatically, or you can @mention it in chat to trigger it. One small caveat, though: you can only share Skills through the Gemini web app at the moment, not the mobile app.
Additionally, when you’re creating a Gemini Skill, you can choose to include files of up to 100MB from other Google products like Drive, Photos, and Notebook, along with files from your local device. Gemini will reference these files every time the Skill runs and update in real time, Google says. These features existed with Gems; Google is just moving them to Skills to make the new feature more comparable.
