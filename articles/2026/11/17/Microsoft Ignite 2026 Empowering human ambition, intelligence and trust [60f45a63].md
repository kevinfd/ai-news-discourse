# Microsoft Ignite 2026: Empowering human ambition, intelligence and trust

Microsoft Source · Erin Medley · 2026-11-17

One year ago at Microsoft Ignite, we introduced Frontier Transformation. The premise was simple: technology exists to serve humanity and deliver real business outcomes. Over the course of this past year, we have been leading our customers to focus on the three durable traits that are required for successful transformation: human ambition, intelligence and trust.
Ignite 2026 is the next milestone in this journey as we introduce new innovations that deliver on the promise of all three of these pillars. Our goal is to build a frontier ecosystem, one that lets every organization benefit from progress at the frontier while preserving what makes it unique.  
“We need to parlay any technological advance into real outcomes and new points of leverage for people, organizations and society at large,” said Satya Nadella, Microsoft Chairman and CEO, in his opening keynote at Ignite. “For us, that comes down to three things: keeping human ambition at the center, ensuring you have ownership over your organization’s intelligence and learning, and trust.”
Human ambition: AI should serve humanity, not the other way around
It starts with the work people are trying to do, which is why we think of Microsoft Copilot as the new operating system for work.
New Copilot Workspaces gives people and agents a shared space to work together as a team. Autopilot, a long-running agent, can manage recurring tasks and move projects forward without being prompted at every step, and Copilot Code lets anyone build dashboards, trackers and apps in natural language with enterprise security and governance built in. Together with Office in Copilot, and features like Today, they make up the new Copilot, which is built to translate raw intelligence into real value.
Copilot is already driving transformation at scale among our customers. Volkswagen Group is equipping more than 330,000 employees with Copilot and The Estée Lauder Companies has expanded Copilot to more than 14,000 people across its global workforce.
Copilot is also connecting to the systems teams rely on most, including Dynamics 365, as well as announcing Salesforce in Copilot, a plugin available in preview with more than 38 pre-built sales skills.
Intelligence: Compound your intelligence without giving away your IP
Agents are only as effective as the context they work with. Microsoft IQ is the enterprise context management platform that helps organizations build their own IQ by capturing, governing and reusing the business knowledge apps and agents need. New business plugins let teams package that knowledge into reusable, trusted context, skills and tools that can be shared across the business.
Customers are already turning that intelligence into business outcomes. Publicis Groupe is empowering employees to build custom AI skills that combine Microsoft IQ with Publicis’ industry expertise and proprietary data, then test them against real-world client scenarios. The result is faster innovation, quicker decision-making and greater value for clients.
In Microsoft Foundry, new continuous improvement tools help customers develop more powerful agents and even train models automatically based on real production signals: optimizing for quality, latency and cost-effectiveness.
New models for coding, cyber image and audio are also being introduced, as well as the general availability of Microsoft-Decision-1.1, a fast, low-cost decision model for tasks like routing, classification, prioritization, verification and workflow control.
Trust: Observability at every layer of the stack and for every person
As agents move from answering questions to taking action, trust becomes the deciding factor. There should never be a choice between innovation and safety. Microsoft Perception, now generally available, brings together specialized agents to find, defend and remediate vulnerabilities — powered by models including our MAI-Cyber line — to help defenders stop threats at machine speed.
Agent 365 Business Analytics gives leaders a clear view of what their AI is costing and what it is delivering, from task success to hours assisted. And Windows is expanding its operating-system-level protection for agents, with more granular containment with Microsoft Execution Containers, built-in agent identity with Entra support and Agent 365 policies enforced on every device, giving IT more control over agent activity in their organizations.
Building on its use of Microsoft Defender, Entra and Purview, BNY is exploring capabilities in Project Perception to further strengthen security and governance. These efforts support BNY’s broader AI ambitions, including its collaboration with Microsoft to complete the first agentic negotiation of a U.S. Treasury trade.
Infrastructure: A cloud built for AI at global scale
Microsoft builds Azure as a single system, co-designing silicon, servers, networking and racks around what we learn running workloads at scale. As agents increasingly work outside the model, from running tools to compiling code, CPUs matter even more. Cobalt 200 virtual machines, generally available today, deliver up to 50% better performance than the previous generation. Cobalt will reach 23 regions by year end and becomes a significant share of new Azure compute capacity in 2027.
Azure Catalyst brings Azure and GitHub agentic tools together in one connected experience for modernizing the applications, databases and infrastructure that AI depends on, and hybrid intelligence on Windows lets AI run wherever it makes the most sense, on the device or in the cloud, starting with GitHub Copilot for coding and Copilot for everyday work. Taken together, today’s announcements demonstrate that the organizations that pull ahead in this next phase are the ones that connect intelligence to their people, their data and their processes, with trust built in from the start. That is the frontier ecosystem we are building with our customers and partners. To see it in action, watch the Microsoft Ignite keynote and explore the full set of announcements in the Book of News.
