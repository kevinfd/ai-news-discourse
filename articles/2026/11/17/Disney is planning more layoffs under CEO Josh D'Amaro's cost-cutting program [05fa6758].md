# Disney is planning more layoffs under CEO Josh D'Amaro's cost-cutting program

Business Insider · James Faris, James Faris (jfaris@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-17

Disney is preparing to lay off staffers once again under CEO Josh D'Amaro. Kevin Mazur/Getty Images for Disney; Rie Kikuchi / AFP via Getty Images Disney is planning more layoffs, its finance chief told CNBC. These cuts will be part of Disney's goal to save $3 billion a year. The Mouse House topped earnings estimates in its October quarter. Disney says it's planning to let go of more employees after several major rounds of cuts this year. "We do expect more layoffs," finance chief Hugh Johnston told CNBC as the company announced its earnings results , which exceeded analysts' expectations. Johnston didn't share the specific timing or scope of the coming cuts, and Disney didn't share details in its shareholder letter. Disney said it expected to deliver $3 billion in annual savings by fiscal year 2029 from a cost-cutting program. The company said those savings would be "used primarily to fund future growth." Inside Disney, employees have been on edge after several substantial rounds of layoffs this year — and press reports that more cuts were coming. Streaming staffers pushed their bosses for answers about the timing and size of coming staff reductions in a town hall, Business Insider reported this month. Here's a timeline of Disney's layoffs in 2026: April : A major round of layoffs after unifying marketing and brand teams July : A more modest round that especially affected ESPN staffers after acquiring the NFL's media assets September : A smaller round of cuts affecting several teams, including tech and HR October : A WARN notice filed in California, expressing the company's intention to let go of 159 employees in its parks division Disney isn't just downsizing. The Mouse House went on a tech-focused hiring spree and plans to pay about $60 million yearly for 66 employees from Character.AI, a chatbot startup that filed for bankruptcy in October.
