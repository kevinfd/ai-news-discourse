# Ask HN: Are the more software malfunctions now than before?

Hacker News · sirnicolaz · 2026-11-17

I have the feeling (reading HN every day for the past 15 years) that the amount of major software malfunctions has increased drastically over the last year, maybe two. I remember that back in 2016-17 big failure events (cloud outages, critical infrastructure issues etc) were special discussion topics. Today they feel more like a routine event. If you also believe it's the case, what could be the reason? Cultural shift towards software? Global load increase? AI slop?
