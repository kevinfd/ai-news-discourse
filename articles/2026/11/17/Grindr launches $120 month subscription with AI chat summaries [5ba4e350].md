# Grindr launches $120/month subscription with AI chat summaries

Mashable · Anna Iovine · 2026-11-17

Grindr launches $120/month subscription with AI chat summaries
After testing a rumored $499/month subscription tier, gay hookup app Grindr is officially launching Edge, an AI-focused plan running for $120 a month in the U.S. or $70 per week.
Edge has three main features: Insights, which shows users compatibility and likelihood to reply; Recommendations, which provides five curated profiles a day; and Reconnect (called A-List in beta), which highlights past connections and generates summaries of your conversations.
Edge includes everything that's in Grindr's Unlimited subscription, as well, which includes features like being able to scroll through unlimited profiles, incognito mode, unsending messages, and expiring photos.

Edge is powered by Grindr's proprietary AI stack called "gAI." Users can control which sensitive data to provide gAI and whether the app can use this to develop gAI over time. Such data can include preferred position, ethnicity, and user chats.
Grindr states that certain health data won't be used in gAI, like HIV status, last tested date, testing reminders, the "Poz" tribe and tag (which stands for the HIV-positive community), and vaccination status. Grindr recently settled a lawsuit for £26 million after allegedly sharing users' HIV statuses with third parties. (Grindr denied the allegations.) Shortly after, the app acquired a provider of the HIV-prevention medication PrEP, and plans to provide PrEP on the app.
Mashable received an early demo of Edge last week, during which Grindr's chief product officer, AJ Balance, said that Edge is the app's most personalized experience yet.
Balance said that Grindr users are at the forefront of culture and tech, and the Grindr team believes it's their job to leverage new technology. As AI was unfolding and it was clear it was the next paradigm shift, Grindr saw an opportunity to utilize it to add value to its users' lives.
Enthusiasm for Edge was apparently high during beta testing, Balance told Mashable, and beta testers ultimately want more connection with less effort and reduced friction in finding a match. The blog post Balance penned for the release is even called, "More Action, Less Effort."
How much is Grindr Edge?
What about the alleged $499/month price tag for the subscription?
Balance said that the app tested a range of pricing. And while $120 is much less than $499, Grindr CEO George Arison defended the price to BBC News today, saying that, "I think we're a capitalist society, and everything hinges on whether you can afford it or not." Balance told Mashable that 91 percent of its users use the app for free, while the remaining 9 percent are power users who want more convenience and ease to save time.
Edge is now available globally to all Grindr users on iOS and Android.
