# How Google’s New AI Chip Software Helps It Battle Nvidia

The Information · Ryan Barwick · 2026-11-17

A software launch last month that got little attention in the wider world could have big implications for Google’s burgeoning AI chip business.  Google late last month announced an open-source version of TorchTPU, a software layer for its tensor processing units that represents the biggest leap on the software side of the Google chip business in years.
