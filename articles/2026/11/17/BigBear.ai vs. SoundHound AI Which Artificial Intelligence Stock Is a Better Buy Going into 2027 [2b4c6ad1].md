# BigBear.ai vs. SoundHound AI: Which Artificial Intelligence Stock Is a Better Buy Going into 2027?

The Motley Fool · Robert Izquierdo · 2026-11-17

As artificial intelligence moves from theoretical potential to practical applications, many investors are choosing between specialized players like BigBear.ai (BBAI -1.10%) and SoundHound AI (SOUN -5.26%) to capture long-term growth.
BigBear.ai provides decision intelligence tools for complex logistics and national security, while SoundHound AI focuses on conversational voice experiences for automotive, restaurant, and other commercial brands. Both companies are positioning themselves as essential software providers in the broader artificial intelligence movement. This comparison looks at which business model offers more durability for the coming years.
The case for BigBear.ai
BigBear.ai sells AI-powered decision intelligence solutions to global supply chains, governments, and border access points such as airports. It serves the U.S. Intelligence Community and the Department of Defense, focusing on data-driven insights for national security and manufacturing operations. This position makes it a notable name among tech stocks that cater to government agencies. Customer concentration like this adds a layer of risk to the business, as one customer contributed roughly 51% of revenue in 2025.
In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached $142.1 million. This figure represents a slight revenue growth decline of 1.5% compared to the prior twelve-month period. The company also recorded a net loss of $180.2 million during this specific time frame, reflecting the ongoing costs of scaling its platform.
As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was zero. This metric compares a company's total debt to its shareholder equity, and a zero value means the company has no debt. The current ratio, which compares short-term assets to short-term debts to see if a company can cover its immediate bills, was 1.8x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was negative $46.3 million.
The case for SoundHound AI
SoundHound AI provides voice and agentic AI solutions that enable natural conversational experiences across smart devices, kiosks, and in-vehicle systems. It serves restaurants, hospitality, and automotive industries, providing tools like Smart Ordering and Smart Answering for brands like Stellantis and Chipotle. Following its recent acquisition of LivePerson, the company has expanded its reach into digital engagement. Unlike its competitor, no single customer accounted for more than 10% of total revenue as of late 2025.
In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached $223.6 million. This reflects a significant revenue growth rate of 50.7% over the previous year as demand for voice interfaces increased. Despite the high growth, the company recorded a net loss of $74.2 million for the period.
As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the current ratio was 4.6x, indicating a strong position for covering short-term liabilities. The debt-to-equity ratio was zero, meaning the company does not carry debt compared to the equity held by shareholders. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was negative $103.1 million.

Risk profile comparison
BigBear.ai faces significant risks from its high revenue concentration among a limited number of government customers. The company must manage long sales cycles and fixed-price contracts, which can lead to unexpected costs. Recent challenges include material class action litigation related to accounting errors and recurring non-cash goodwill impairment charges that may impact financial stability.
SoundHound AI has experienced substantial net losses and negative operating cash flows since its inception. It competes in a crowded market where large technology companies like Microsoft (MSFT -2.27%) and Amazon (AMZN +0.05%) may offer competing voice services. The company also faces risks related to integrating its recent acquisition of LivePerson and a dual-class share structure that gives founders significant voting control.
Valuation comparison
BigBear.ai offers a slightly lower P/S ratio, which measures market value against sales over the past twelve months. However, SoundHound AI is growing its top line at a much faster pace.

Which stock would I buy for 2027?
Between artificial intelligence software players BigBear.ai and SoundHound AI, I would invest in the latter for 2027. BigBear.ai is highly reliant on the U.S. government for revenue, and last year, after federal cutbacks, the company ended 2025 with $127.7 million, a substantial drop from 2024's $158.2 million.
It is doing better in 2026, experiencing organic growth across all three quarters this year. Management projects to end the year with sales between $150 million to $165 million, signaling a gradual return to revenue levels not seen since 2024. Even so, I would want to see additional quarters of growth to ensure the current trend can continue before considering an investment.
SoundHound AI has consistently expanded sales. For instance, its Q3 revenue of $62.4 million represented growth of 48.5% year over year. A big part of its rising revenue has been through acquisitions, which have increased costs and eroded margins.
However, the company announced it would hit adjusted EBITDA profitability in the second half of 2027. With its relentless sales expansion and potential shift toward a profitable business, SoundHound looks like the more solid company to invest in while BigBear.ai still has to prove it can thrive amid the artificial intelligence boom.
