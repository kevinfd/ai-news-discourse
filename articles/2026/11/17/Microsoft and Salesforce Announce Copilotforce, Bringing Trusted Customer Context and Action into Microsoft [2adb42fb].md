# Microsoft and Salesforce Announce Copilotforce, Bringing Trusted Customer Context and Action into Microsoft AI Experiences

Microsoft Source · Dharmik Desai · 2026-11-17

- New integrations connect Salesforce with Microsoft Copilot, Teams and Fabric, helping enterprises turn Salesforce customer insights into action while maintaining existing security and permissions.
- New two-way Zero Copy integrations between Salesforce Data 360 and Microsoft Fabric enable organizations to securely query and analyze data across both platforms without maintaining duplicate datasets.
- Accenture is serving as design and implementation partner and pilot customer, deploying the integrations in its own global sales organization.
REDMOND, Wash., and SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 17, 2026 — Microsoft Corp. (NASDAQ: MSFT) and Salesforce Inc. (NYSE: CRM) today announced Copilotforce, a new partnership that helps employees get more done in the tools they already use by bringing Salesforce customer context, business processes and actions into Microsoft Copilot and other AI-powered productivity experiences.
Unveiled at Microsoft Ignite, the partnership introduces new integrations across Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Fabric, including Salesforce in Copilot, a plugin available in public preview with 38+ prebuilt sales skills. Together, these capabilities help businesses move faster, reduce manual work and get more value from their existing technology investments — while maintaining the security, governance and permissions they already rely on.
Bringing Salesforce workflows together with conversations, meetings and documents in Microsoft 365 means less time spent moving between systems and more time on the work that matters. Salesforce in Copilot connects customer context with the work happening across the business so employees can make more informed decisions and take action without constantly switching applications.
Microsoft Work IQ, the intelligence layer powering Copilot, provides relevant context from Teams meetings, Outlook emails and documents across SharePoint and OneDrive. Work IQ and Salesforce help sellers understand not only what’s recorded in CRM, but what’s happening across customer conversations and collaboration — and what needs to happen next.
Through authenticated Salesforce MCP access, sellers can securely retrieve customer information and take supported business actions within their existing Salesforce permissions. Salesforce continues to govern access and business actions, while Microsoft applies its existing enterprise compliance controls to communications and documents. Data, context and the apps where sellers work come to life in practical workflows across the sales cycle, including:
- Grow Revenue. Use the Pipeline Review and Close Planning skills to decide which opportunities deserve attention and build a concrete path to signature. Customer priorities and deal context inform the proposals and business cases sellers develop in Word and PowerPoint, with clear owners, milestones and next steps.
- Manage Risk. Connect Salesforce opportunity data with customer concerns raised in Outlook, escalations discussed in Teams and unresolved commitments. The Deal Review, Customer Health and Renewal Radar skills see beyond the recorded stage or renewal date to the customer concern in an Outlook thread, the escalation discussed in Teams or the unresolved commitment behind a stalled deal, helping sellers identify where to intervene.
- Move faster. Turn customer conversations into follow-up drafts, internal recaps and proposed CRM activities using the Call Follow-Up skill. Sellers can review and confirm next steps instead of manually reconstructing conversations and updating multiple systems.
With Copilot as the front door to business process, Salesforce in Copilot allows organizations to bring together Salesforce data, workflows, agents and prebuilt skills from the systems they already rely on through Copilot’s open and interoperable platform.
“AI is only as powerful as the data and context that grounds it,” said Judson Althoff, CEO, Microsoft Commercial Business. “Bringing Salesforce customer data and sales skills together with the work context delivered by Work IQ in Microsoft Copilot allows our joint customers to unlock actionable insights across their core business applications. Copilotforce will give sales teams the intelligence they need to make faster, higher-confidence decisions while maintaining the governance and trust enterprises demand.”
“Through Copilotforce, we’re bringing Salesforce to the largest surface in enterprise software today, embedding trusted Salesforce context and intelligent sales workflows directly where sellers work,” said Miguel Milano, President & Chief Operating Officer, Salesforce. “By bringing together the #1 CRM with the #1 productivity surface, we’re transforming AI from a standalone tool into a powerful driver of productivity, smarter decisions and customer success.”
Expanding connection in the places people work
For many organizations, the work first starts in Microsoft Teams, where people share files, make decisions and hand work to each other. Sales Cloud in Teams, now available in pilot, brings Salesforce customer context directly into those conversations, so sellers can see pipeline and quota progress, get deal notifications and find briefing materials without leaving the place where decisions are already being made.
Across these Microsoft and Salesforce integrations, customer data remains protected at every boundary: Salesforce controls data access and governs supported business actions, while Microsoft ensures communications and documents adhere to existing enterprise compliance policies. Supported writeback actions to the CRM require explicit user confirmation and respect existing validation rules, so teams can act with confidence while customer records stay governed and auditable.
AI is only as useful as the context it can reach, and for many enterprises customer data lives in Salesforce while operational and financial data lives in Microsoft Fabric. Microsoft and Salesforce are bringing a two-way Zero Copy connection between Salesforce Data 360 and Microsoft Fabric, so teams can work with fuller context across both platforms without building pipelines or maintaining duplicate datasets.
With Data Federation, now generally available, joint customers can now directly query and analyze supported Microsoft Fabric OneLake data within Salesforce Data 360. A service agent can see a customer’s recent purchases on their account, a marketer can build a segment from spending behavior and an AI agent can answer from current data, all without moving the source data out of Fabric. Salesforce Data 360 shortcut, now in public preview, allows organizations to securely share curated Data 360 customer intelligence into OneLake so data teams can analyze it alongside enterprise datasets for analytics, reporting and AI workloads without duplicating data.
From design partner to pilot customer
Accenture is working with Microsoft and Salesforce as the design and implementation partner for the new integrations, bringing deep expertise across 13 industries globally in delivering business outcomes with both partners. In addition, having scaled Copilot to more than 740,000 people and as one of the largest Salesforce users globally with 55,000 users, Accenture is also a pilot customer, deploying and validating the integrations within its own global sales organization and sharing learnings to help clients turn investments they have already made in Salesforce and Microsoft into a better way to sell, driving faster growth and lowering risk.
“As a Global Partner of the Year for both Salesforce and Microsoft and one of their top customers, we are uniquely positioned to bring our expertise to help design and implement Copilotforce so that our clients can quickly get more value from their technology investments,” said Julie Sweet, Chair and CEO, Accenture. “The combined strengths of our three companies will reinvent sales with AI, giving sellers more time with customers while trusted intelligence and agents work alongside them in the tools they already use. We are also excited to serve as customer zero for Copilotforce.”
Availability*
Salesforce in Copilot is available in public preview (beta) beginning today. Data federation from Microsoft OneLake into Salesforce Data 360 is generally available, and data sharing from Data 360 into OneLake is in public preview. Sales Cloud in Teams is in pilot with enterprise customers, with open beta coming later this year.
To learn more about what’s new in Copilot at Microsoft Ignite, visit https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/.
