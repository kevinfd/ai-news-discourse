# [Gemini for Beginners ] Google's AI is evolving from 'just searching' to 'doing the work for you'! New features are rolling out for free in Japan too

note · AIトレンドラボ, 初心者向けAi活用術 · 2026-11-21

[Gemini for Beginners🔰] Google's AI is evolving from 'just searching' to 'doing the work for you'! New features are rolling out for free in Japan too📱✨
“Isn't AI just for answering questions?”
For those who have that impression, some interesting news has arrived😊
On November 17, 2026, Google announced new task features for its AI assistant, Gemini.
It is evolving beyond just answering questions to helping with actual tasks, such as organizing schedules and preparing for shopping.
Moreover, this announcement includes Japan as a region where these features will be rolled out for free✨
Here is an introduction for beginners on what you can do!
🌟 What is Gemini's 'Task Mode'?
Gemini now features two ways to use it: 'Chat' for talking with the AI, and 'Task' for asking it to perform work.
For example, until now,
“Make a shopping list”
was basically answered by the AI returning a list in text format.
With the new task feature, by linking with compatible services, it will be able to proceed to the next steps, such as managing shopping lists or preparing orders. In other words, it is a shift where the AI doesn't just think of the answer, but takes on part of the work.
However, the tasks you can perform vary depending on your region and the linked services.
📱 STEP 1 | Let's open Gemini
First, open Gemini on your smartphone.
The official site is here:
Please log in with your Google account.
According to the new operation method introduced by Google, you can switch from 'Chat' to 'Task' on the home screen.
If 'Task' is displayed, you can enter the work you want it to do from there
However, since this feature is being rolled out gradually, it may not be visible to everyone yet
If it is not displayed, let's have it help you prepare for tasks via regular chat😊
✏️ STEP 2 | Start with simple requests
If you are using it for the first time, it is recommended to try tasks that do not involve any costs
For example, organizing your schedule
💬 Question ① you can copy and paste to try
I want to organize what I need to do this week
Please arrange shopping, cleaning, and organizing documents in a reasonable order
Please create a schedule that can be completed in about 30 minutes a day
First, create a plan for confirmation, and do not register for or make changes to external services
In this way, it is safe to convey both what you want it to do and what you do not want it to do🛒 STEP 3 | Let it help you prepare for shopping
In Google's announcement, examples were introduced where Gemini researches products needed for shopping or helps with ordering from supported services
However, some advanced features related to ordering and payment may be provided in the US first
In Japan, let's start by trying out shopping preparation
💬 Question ② you can copy and paste to try
Please help me with my weekend shopping
The items I need are milk, eggs, bread, and vegetables
Please divide them into food and daily necessities and make an easy-to-read shopping list
Please do not make any purchases or orders, just create the list
This makes it easy to try out even with the standard version of Gemini✨
🗓️ STEP 4 | You can also use it for schedule management!
The examples Google introduced this time include contacting reservation services and integrating with Google Calendar
For example, you can use it to find a car inspection appointment, check available times, and add them to your schedule
However, it has not been confirmed that all AI-driven phone calls and reservation executions will be available in Japan
Therefore, we recommend starting with these types of questions
💬 Question ③ you can copy and paste to try out
I want to organize my schedule for next week
I have shopping on Monday, a doctor's appointment on Wednesday, and house cleaning on Friday
Please create an easy-to-read weekly schedule for me
Please do not change or book any appointments
🔐 Is it safe to let AI handle tasks?
The more convenient it becomes, the more we worry about safety, right?
Google explains that it uses a dedicated, isolated environment for task execution and is designed to ask for confirmation before important operations like purchases
However, the possibility of the AI making a mistake is not zero
In particular, keep the following in mind
- Check purchase amounts and reservation dates yourself
- Do not grant unnecessary app integrations or permissions
- Handle personal information and payment details with caution
- Always verify the AI's work results
It is safe to start with tasks like 'organizing' or 'finding options' first😊
🇯🇵 How much can we use in Japan?
In Google's official announcement on November 17, it was stated that new Gemini task features will be rolled out for free in countries including the US, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India.
On the other hand, some advanced features such as phone calls, email forwarding, message integration, and payments are being provided starting from the US.
Not all features will be available in Japan all at once.
Please try them out little by little while checking the features displayed on your screen.
🌷 Summary | AI is evolving from a 'consultant' to a 'task assistant'!
This Gemini announcement is news that makes us feel how the way we use AI is changing.
From now on, it seems we will increasingly have opportunities to not only ask questions but also delegate some tedious tasks.
Let's start by practicing asking AI to help with familiar things like organizing schedules or making shopping lists.
Please find a way to use it that suits you while being mindful of both convenience and safety😊
