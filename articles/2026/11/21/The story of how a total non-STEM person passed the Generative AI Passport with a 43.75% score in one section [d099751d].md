# The story of how a total non-STEM person passed the Generative AI Passport with a 43.75% score in one section

note · のっぴ · 2026-11-21

🎓 The story of how a total non-STEM person passed the Generative AI Passport with a 43.75% score in one section
Forgive me for posting every day.
I mentioned this briefly the other day, but I somehow managed to pass the "Generative AI Passport" exam hosted by GUGA.
I mean, first of all, I am a total non-STEM person from a commercial high school. 
I'm actually pretty good at memorizing historical dates and laws, but when it comes to math and science mechanisms or algorithms with foreign names, I'm just completely hopeless. 
So, right after the exam finished, to be honest, I had absolutely no confidence.
Then, the results notification arrived and it said "Pass". Instead of feeling happy, my first thought was, "Wait, why?"
I passed, but I somehow got a failing grade (in my personal opinion)
So I took a peek at X (formerly Twitter) and saw that the overall pass rate for this exam and the section-by-section scores everyone was posting were trending.
Apparently, the pass rate for this October 2026 exam was 81.74%, and the timeline was filled with glorious reports like 
"I got over 80% correct!" or "I almost got a perfect score!"
I remembered I could check my own score on my personal page, so I nervously opened the results screen to take a look.
…Huh? Chapter 2, 43.75%?
Wait, isn't that a failing grade? 🤣
The overall average score was 75%, so I was 31.25 points below that. 
It's true that thanks to using generative AI for music and video creation, I was above average in Chapter 3 (+11.67 points) and Chapter 5 (+13.50 points).
But Chapter 2 was just full of hardcore STEM questions about deep learning structures and algorithms, and for someone from a commercial high school like me, it looked like nothing but code.
On top of that, even in Chapter 4, which covers law and literacy where I tried to show some liberal arts pride, I was still below average (-6.08 points). 
Still, even though I made up for it in other sections, is it really okay to pass with such a low score in one section? 
The pass rate this time (81.74%) is about the same as the provisional driver's license exam (about 80%), but don't you need to get over 90% correct to pass the written part of the provisional license? 
Well, a driver's license involves human lives, so I shouldn't compare them too easily, but I just compared them using familiar numbers.
My position in practical work also feels like "a provisional license level where I've learned the minimum traffic rules to use AI safely", but I feel like I'm the only one who passed with a score in the 40s (according to Noppi's research).
43.75% is a "certificate of honesty"
Well, this exam is taken online from your home computer or smartphone, and the pass rate is 81.74%. 
" What's the point of a qualification that you can pass in that environment, even with a failing grade in one section?"
Even I, the person who passed, have to tilt my head at that.
But precisely because it's an exam where you could cheat if you wanted to, this dismal number of "43.75%" is proof that I did not cheat at all, and I can say that with my head held high.
Thinking about it that way, maybe this failing score isn't so bad after all. 
I've decided to proudly display it as a "certificate of passing by pure self-effort."
Oh, and by the way, just because I passed, I have absolutely no intention of writing about "what study methods I used" as an information product or for engagement bait, so please continue to feel safe coming to read my posts.
This was Noppi, the tweeter!
