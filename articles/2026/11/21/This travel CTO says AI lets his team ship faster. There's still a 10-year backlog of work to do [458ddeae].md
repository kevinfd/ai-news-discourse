# This travel CTO says AI lets his team ship faster. There's still a 10-year backlog of work to do.

Business Insider · Kelsey Vlamis, Kelsey Vlamis (kvlamis@businessinsider.com) · 2026-11-21

Sy Esfahani, CTO of Travel + Leisure Co., said AI has been a "gamechanger" for the travel industry. Photo courtesy of Travel + Leisure Co. Travel + Leisure Co.'s CTO says AI is making his team faster but that there's still plenty of work. He said AI has been a "game changer" and has shifted development timelines from years to months. Travel + Leisure Co. recently launched Voya, an AI concierge that can research and book trips. AI is helping one travel company's software engineers work faster. Its CTO says there's still plenty of work to go around. Sy Esfahani, chief technology officer at Travel + Leisure Co., says that while the technology is allowing his team to get more done in shorter periods, he still sees enough unfinished work to keep them busy for years. "People worry about the staffing," he said. "There's enough work for the next 10 years for us to do the backlog stuff. But you have to be thoughtful about when and how to use it and allow the innovation to happen in a thoughtful manner rather than just spending the money." Esfahani, who previously served as chief information officer at MGM Resorts International and Qatar Airways, said his team can start AI workflows, go home while they run overnight, and return to the office to find the generated code ready to be refined. He said AI has turned work that would take "years into weeks and months." Travel + Leisure Co. , headquartered in Orlando and operating Club Wyndham, is one of the largest vacation ownership companies, alongside Marriott Vacations Worldwide and Hilton Grand Vacations. Like the broader travel industry , Esfahani said the sector is undergoing a technological transformation driven by AI, which he described as a "game changer." Part of that shift is Travel + Leisure Co.'s recent AI launch, Voya, an AI travel concierge that helps Club Wyndham members plan and book eligible reservations across its resort network. Booking through an AI concierge is especially useful for the vacation ownership model, Esfahani said, where balancing location availability and points can make the booking experience challenging. It's also a good time to make the booking process more seamless, as the industry is growing, including with younger generations. Nearly 40% of Travel + Leisure Co.'s new owners are millennials or Gen Z, the company says. About 58% of all US timeshare owners are millennials or Gen Z, according to ARDA, an industry trade association. Baby boomers are Voya's largest user group. The company says owners from millennials to baby boomers are using the concierge at roughly the same rate. Esfahani said that while he thinks there is still plenty of work for the humans on his team to do, the idea that we've created a "monster" with AI that's going to take all our jobs may still be a possibility, but at least not yet.
