# Google launches Gemini 4 Neon taking on Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.2 Sol

The Hindu · The Hindu Bureau · 2026-11-21

Google has launched a new AI model, Gemini 4 Neon, that it said was primed for complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows.
According to the company, Neon ranked ahead of comparable models, including Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5.5 and OpenAI’s GPT-6.2 Sol, on several benchmarks.
Gemini 4 Neon is priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens with a million-token context window.
Google noted that the new model was designed to support long-running agentic workflows and ensure that tasks are completed, while balancing out costs.
Meanwhile users of the Gemini app could expect better writing and knowledge work, reliable research, and faster responses when faced with challenging tasks, per the company.
“Get more done while stretching usage limits further when handing off tedious to-dos, like analysing billing statements to spot why a utility charge spiked or cross-checking multiple receipts and documents to negotiate a refund,” suggested Google in a blog post.
The search giant affirmed that its new model maintained Gemini 4 Carbon-level safeguards against severe misuse and prompt injection, and also advanced model alignment.
Google Gemini 4 Neon can be accessed via Gemini API in Google AI Studio, and Gemini for business via API and model dropdown. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can find it on Google Antigravity, and Pro and Ultra subscribers can access on the Gemini app.
“Neon is built for complex coding, analytical reasoning, and production-grade enterprise workflows. While Carbon tackles your hardest, long-horizon projects, and Hydrogen handles fast everyday execution, Neon balances depth and efficiency. Neon delivers frontier-level reasoning for complex, high-frequency workflows faster and more cost efficiently,” stated Koray Kavukcuoglu, SVP, Google DeepMind and Chief AI Architect at Google.
