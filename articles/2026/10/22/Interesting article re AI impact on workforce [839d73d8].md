# Interesting article re AI impact on workforce

The New York Times · Dianeinchicoca · 2026-10-22

We do better or worse depending on the economy at large, I.e., when lots of jobs are available then workers displaced by AI can adapt. So when economy is slow then tech change should be slower for the sake of workers that lose their jobs.
