# DeepSeek Raises $15 Billion To Scale and Challenge OpenAI and Anthropic Dominance

Ventureburn · Omorogie Alele · 2026-10-22

In the last few weeks, we’ve seen massive developments from the frontier models. DeepSeek, which rattled Wall Street in 2025 with a cheap, surprisingly capable AI model, has completed a major funding round to challenge this growing dominance.
DeepSeek raises $15 billion in its latest funding round to scale and heat up the AI race even further. The funding, worth about 100 billion yuan, came from battery giant CATL and Tencent, the company behind WeChat, and other top tech firms.
DeepSeek Doubles Funding Round
DeepSeek didn’t set out to raise $15 billion (about 100 billion yuan). The Hangzhou-based company started with a 50 billion yuan target. Demand pushed that to 80 billion. Then it climbed again, to roughly 100 billion.
New names include carmaker Geely, Legend Capital, Stony Creek Capital, China Fortune-Tech Capital and CPE Asset Management. Old backers came back, too. IDG Capital, Lux Capital and Silicon 100 joined CATL and Tencent. CATL alone is reportedly adding about $1.38 billion. That’s roughly double its first-round cheque.
Dealroom puts DeepSeek’s pre-money valuation at around $68.8 billion. The money is reportedly split almost evenly between new and existing shareholders. That balance matters. It shows early backers still believe, even at a much higher price.

DeepSeek Closes Over $20 Billion in Just Six Months
DeepSeek closed a first round of more than $6.88 billion in June. It opened this second round in July. Add them up, and you get roughly $20.6 billion in six months. Dealroom calls it the largest primary market financing in China this year.
So where does the money go? Mostly into model research and computing power. There are also reports of heavier spending on data centres and open-source models. A corporate restructuring is coming. And an IPO on Shanghai’s Star Market is reportedly in the works.
Can $15 Billion Close the Gap With OpenAI and Anthropic?
Let’s be real. Fifteen billion dollars is huge. But it doesn’t level the field.
OpenAI and Anthropic have each raised more than $100 billion. DeepSeek still has far less firepower. Yet it has never needed to match US labs dollar for dollar. Its whole reputation rests on efficiency. It proved strong models don’t need bottomless budgets.
Now picture that same discipline with much deeper pockets. That’s what should keep rivals up at night.
And DeepSeek isn’t alone. Moonshot AI is reportedly nearing a $50 billion valuation, with Hong Kong IPO plans of its own. Chinese AI is raising money at a pace we’ve rarely seen.

Why This Round Matters to You
Back in 2025, one DeepSeek model launch sparked a sharp sell-off in US tech stocks. Now imagine what a far richer DeepSeek could ship next. If you invest, build products, or just use AI tools daily, pay attention.
More capital usually means faster releases. DeepSeek’s open-source models could get stronger and cheaper. That puts pricing pressure on everyone, US providers included.
The investor list matters, too. CATL makes batteries. Geely makes cars. Their money hints at AI moving deeper into hardware and industry, not just chatbots. One caution, though, the report has not been officially confirmed by DeepSeek.
Either way, the message is loud and clear. DeepSeek is no longer the scrappy underdog. It’s a heavily funded contender. And the AI race just got a lot more crowded.
