# SB Energy Considers Waiting Until December or Later for an IPO

The Information · Phoebe Liu, Valida Pau · 2026-10-22

SB Energy, the SoftBank Group–controlled developer of a massive planned Ohio data center project for OpenAI, is considering going public in December or possibly waiting until next year, according to people familiar with the discussions. 

 That is a later timeline than previously expected. The data center developer was aiming to go public as soon as September, The Information reported in August . But since then, the public market has soured on initial public offerings from data center and AI cloud companies.
