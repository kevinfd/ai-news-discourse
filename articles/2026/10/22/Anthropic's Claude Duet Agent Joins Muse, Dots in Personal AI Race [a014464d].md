# Anthropic's Claude Duet Agent Joins Muse, Dots in Personal AI Race

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-10-22

Anthropic on Wednesday announced a new personal AI agent that can accomplish tasks on your behalf, called Claude Duet. The agent, built around Claude Opus 5.5 for professionals and small business owners, is currently available in preview via claude.ai on the web and a dedicated app on iOS and Android.
The personal agent connects to permitted apps and sends a briefing each morning with tasks lined up for the day. Throughout the day, it tracks activities on those apps in the background and sends alerts if something requires your attention. With your approval, it can also execute a wide range of tasks, from something as simple as scheduling a meeting to something as complex as building web apps.
With plug-ins and connectors, Anthropic says Claude Duet can accomplish much more, including finding Gmail and Slack messages you missed and preparing draft replies, drafting and posting job listings on LinkedIn, preparing and running payroll in Gusto, setting up your Shopify store, and purchasing items for your business using Stripe’s Link.
On sensitive information, Anthropic says it built a new classifier and a system prompt to check messages and prevent leaks. Together, they “reject requests to share the strictest tier of information, including credit card numbers and government identification. Other sensitive information, such as health or personal finances, are let through only when you’ve explicitly instructed Claude Duet to send it,” the company says in an announcement post.
Personal agents have become a new battleground for AI companies. Anthropic’s Claude Duet arrives shortly after Meta’s Muse and OpenAI’s Dots. It’s the proactive approach that sets them apart from regular chatbots and other AI tools. Instead of waiting for your prompts, these agents come to you first with tasks they can handle for you.
In preview, Claude Duet is available only to users on Claude’s Pro and Max plans, which cost $20 and $100 per month, respectively. Interested users must join a waitlist to gain access.
