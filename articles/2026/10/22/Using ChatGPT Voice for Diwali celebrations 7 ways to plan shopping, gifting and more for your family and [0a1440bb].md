# Using ChatGPT Voice for Diwali celebrations: 7 ways to plan shopping, gifting and more for your family and friends

Moneycontrol · MC Tech Desk · 2026-10-22

15
ChatGPT Voice can help users organise Diwali budgets, plan festive menus, manage shopping lists and coordinate family responsibilities through voice conversations.
  October 22, 2026 / 16:50 IST  
Diwali preparations often involve several tasks, from buying gifts and decorating the house to preparing festive meals and arranging family gatherings. Keeping track of everything can become difficult, particularly when multiple family members are involved. ChatGPT Voice can help users organise these activities through spoken conversations, allowing them to discuss their requirements and get suggestions without having to type every detail.
With Neer and Leher, the two Indian voices available in GPT-Live-1.1, users can have voice conversations with ChatGPT to plan different aspects of their celebrations. Here are seven ways to use ChatGPT Voice for Diwali preparations.
1. Plan your Diwali budget
Gifts, sweets, decorations and new clothes can add to festive expenses. Users can tell ChatGPT their overall budget, existing expenses and priorities to create a spending plan. They can also allocate specific amounts for different categories and keep some money aside for unexpected purchases.
2. Find gift ideas for family and friends
Users can describe the interests of the people they want to buy gifts for, along with their spending limits. ChatGPT can suggest suitable options, including personalised gifts, kitchen accessories, books and home decor items.
3. Organise festive shopping
Instead of maintaining multiple shopping lists, users can dictate the items they need to buy. ChatGPT can group the requirements into categories such as groceries, decorations, clothing and gifts, making it easier to track pending purchases.
4. Plan a Diwali dinner menu
Hosting family and friends involves accounting for different food preferences. Users can explain the number of guests, dietary restrictions, available ingredients and preparation time. ChatGPT can suggest a menu and identify dishes that can be prepared in advance.
5. Record traditional family recipes
Family recipes are often passed down through verbal instructions. Users can ask ChatGPT Voice to help document a recipe by recording the ingredients and preparation steps as they explain them. They can clarify measurements and review the resulting instructions before saving them.
6. Divide household responsibilities
Preparing the house for Diwali can involve cleaning, decorating, cooking and arranging gifts. Users can explain who is available to help and ask ChatGPT to divide tasks based on everyone's availability and preferences.
7. Manage last-minute changes
Unexpected guests or missing ingredients can disrupt festive plans. Users can explain the situation, list the ingredients available at home and ask ChatGPT for quick meal adjustments or additional dishes that fit the existing menu.
ChatGPT Voice can help bring structure to Diwali preparations by turning spoken instructions into practical suggestions. Users should review the recommendations, verify prices and quantities, and make adjustments based on their family's needs before putting their plans into action.
