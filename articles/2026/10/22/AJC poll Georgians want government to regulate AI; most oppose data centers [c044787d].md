# AJC poll: Georgians want government to regulate AI; most oppose data centers.

The Atlanta Journal-Constitution · Mirtha Donastorg · 2026-10-22

AJC poll: Georgians want government to regulate AI; most oppose data centers.
In this heated political climate, it can feel like there are few things Republicans and Democrats can agree on. But there is at least one area where they find common ground: government regulation of artificial intelligence.
It comes as both adoption and wariness of AI are growing.
In a new Atlanta Journal-Constitution poll, nearly seven in 10 voters polled said they believe the U.S. government should be doing more to regulate the use of AI rather than the companies regulating themselves. That includes 61% of Republicans and 82% of Democrats.
Three of five also oppose the construction of more data centers in Georgia to serve AI, though that opposition is strongest among Democrats and independent voters.
