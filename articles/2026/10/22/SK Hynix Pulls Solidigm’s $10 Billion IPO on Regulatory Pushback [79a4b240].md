# SK Hynix Pulls Solidigm’s $10 Billion IPO on Regulatory Pushback

Bloomberg · Yoolim Lee and Youkyung Lee · 2026-10-22

SK Hynix Inc. scrapped plans for a potential $10 billion US listing of its Solidigm unit after facing strong opposition from regulators and shareholders, becoming the latest firm to pull an IPO while growing investor unease over the artificial intelligence boom rocks markets. SK Hynix shares rallied.
