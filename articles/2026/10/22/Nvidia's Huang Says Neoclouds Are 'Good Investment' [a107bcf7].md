# Nvidia's Huang Says Neoclouds Are 'Good Investment'

Bloomberg · 2026-10-22

Nvidia Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang discusses neoclouds and why they are a good investment. "The demand of their compute is incredible," Huang tells Bloomberg's Tom Mackenzie. He also addresses the need for neoclouds to acquire more working capital as demand for computers and AI is high. "Everything else will get solved when demand is high," Huang says, adding he is "quite confident" neoclouds will find solutions. (Source: Bloomberg)
