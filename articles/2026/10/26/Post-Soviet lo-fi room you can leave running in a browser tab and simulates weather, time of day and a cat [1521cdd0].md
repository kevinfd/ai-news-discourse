# Post-Soviet lo-fi room you can leave running in a browser tab and simulates weather, time of day and a cat.

Pages · FlaccidWizard69 · 2026-10-26

Kvartira 47: a small bedroom in an Eastern European panel block, early 2000s. The day goes by, the weather moves in fronts (rain, snow, storms), traffic and people run in the street below, a cat does what she wants and there's an old beige box PC with a CRT and a radio with real internet stations. I made this With Claude and GPT Plain JavaScript + WebGL2, no framework. The code with Claude Opus 5.5 (medium), the graphics with GPT 5.6 Sol (medium); the pictures are cut up, masked and relit in code, and the street is a small traffic simulation. I'd love to hear if this runs ok on your device…
