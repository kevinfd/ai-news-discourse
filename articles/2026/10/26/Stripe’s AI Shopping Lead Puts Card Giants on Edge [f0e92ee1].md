# Stripe’s AI Shopping Lead Puts Card Giants on Edge

The Information · Yueqi Yang · 2026-10-26

As AI assistants like Instinct and Meta Platforms’ Muse start to gain traction in shopping, Stripe has taken a leading position in how the agents pay. That’s putting the credit card giants on edge. 

 The reason for their apprehension: Stripe in many cases is enabling AI agent purchases through virtual cards for one-time use, which can leave card networks with less control over the shopping and payment process, people who work closely with the card firms say.
