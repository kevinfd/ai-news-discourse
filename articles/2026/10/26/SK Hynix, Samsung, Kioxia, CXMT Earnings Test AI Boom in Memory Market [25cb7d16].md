# SK Hynix, Samsung, Kioxia, CXMT Earnings Test AI Boom in Memory Market

Bloomberg · Yoolim Lee and Winnie Hsu · 2026-10-26

From South Korea to Japan and China, the world’s biggest memory chipmakers will this week seek to quell fears that AI spending is outpacing future demand. How they handle back-to-back earnings disclosures and investors’ questions will help determine the trajectory for markets and shed light on the broader outlook for the artificial intelligence industry.
