# AI Bubble Burst: Confirmed By Alphabet, Caterpillar, ServiceNow, And Amazon

Seeking Alpha · Damir Tokic · 2026-10-30

AI Bubble Burst: Confirmed By Alphabet, Caterpillar, ServiceNow, And Amazon
Summary
- Alphabet and Microsoft both missed AI capex expectations for Q3 - this bursts the AI infrastructure bubble.
- Caterpillar reported a 22% increase in inventory; this signals overproduction and confirms the AI capex slowing thesis.
- ServiceNow reports strong AI ACV but provides weak revenue guidance, indicating that AI monetization is lagging AI adoption.
- These three stocks crashed after the report by 10-27%; that's what the bubble burst looks like.
