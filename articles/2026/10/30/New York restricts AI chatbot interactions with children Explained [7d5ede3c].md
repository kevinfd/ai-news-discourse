# New York restricts AI chatbot interactions with children | Explained

The Hindu · Mantasha Ahmed · 2026-10-30

The story so far: “As a mother and grandmother, I believe kids deserve to be kids, without dangerous AI chatbots putting their safety and mental health at risk,...Big Tech, you’re on notice,” New York Governor Kathy Hochul wrote on social media after signing two bills on Wednesday (October 28, 2026) aimed at protecting children from the risks associated with artificial intelligence (AI) chatbots and internet-connected baby monitors.
The legislation prohibits AI chatbots from engaging in potentially harmful interactions with minors, including simulating romantic relationships, encouraging emotional dependency, and promoting self-harm. It also requires manufacturers of internet-connected baby monitors to introduce safeguards against unauthorised access to audio and video feeds.
