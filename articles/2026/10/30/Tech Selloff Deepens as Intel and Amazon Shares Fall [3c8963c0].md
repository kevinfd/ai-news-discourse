# Tech Selloff Deepens as Intel and Amazon Shares Fall

Bloomberg · 2026-10-30

Technology shares sold off for a second consecutive day as another round of disappointing earnings accelerated a rotation out of the artificial intelligence trade. Amazon slumped 7.3% as the firm reported greater capital spending than anticipated, while Intel plunged 23% after its revenue forecast missed analysts’ estimates. Bloomberg's Ruhell Amin reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
