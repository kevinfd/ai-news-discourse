# Anthropic’s Rapid But Slowing Growth Could Weigh on IPO Valuation

The Information · Cory Weinberg · 2026-10-15

Anthropic reported rapid-but-slowing revenue growth in a meeting with potential investors in its planned initial public offering, leaving some attendees expecting the company will need to keep its valuation target in check. 

 At a private investor meeting Wednesday in San Francisco, the company said revenue hit $16.8 billion in the third quarter, according to people briefed on the numbers. That was a jump of about 45% from the second quarter. The figure was more than 3.6 times the company’s revenue for all of 2025.
