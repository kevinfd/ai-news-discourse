South China Morning Post · Enoch Yiu · 2026-10-15

HSBC, Hong Kong’s biggest lender, said on Thursday it would double its artificial intelligence specialist team in the city to 300, a figure much larger than the workforce planned for its recently announced AI centre in Singapore.
The announcement comes after Hong Kong’s regulators were reported to have raised concerns about the Singapore hub.
