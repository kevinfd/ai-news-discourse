# Why AI-Written Software Needs a Flight Simulator

The Information · Laura Bratton · 2026-10-15

Luis Chávez, CTO of coding assistant provider Replit, says his company uses an AI-assisted software known as a deterministic simulator, a kind of flight simulator for software.
