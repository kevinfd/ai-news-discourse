# I run an e-shop. AI didn't make my customers smarter, just more confident

Reddit · fomodesk · 2026-10-15

A year ago customer emails were "hi, when does my package arrive?". Now I get half a page of perfectly formatted text with bold headings, bullet points and "I look forward to your prompt response". I don't mind that people use AI to write. What gets me is that they believe it blindly. Customers quote return rules and consumer laws that simply don't exist, with total confidence. When I correct them, they don't trust me, they trust the chatbot. "I double checked." They asked ChatGPT again. Nobody writes "I think" or "maybe I'm wrong" anymore. Everything is 100%. Some days it's honestly funny. Anyone else dealing with customers this way?
