# GPT-6.1-Sol feels infinite!

Reddit · meeninta · 2026-10-15

Been using 6.1 sol medium reasoning fast mode since last week with Hermes for 8+ hours per day and I still have 94% usage left on the $20/month plan. I love this! Edit: For those asking what I use it for: I use it for my full time tech job, my homelab, side business, and for any random questions I have throughout the day. I don’t know if we’ve gotten resets, I don’t check often enough to notice. If we did, then that’s definitely the reason it feels infinite lol for those saying it’s slow, use low or medium reasoning and turn on fast mode. Speed is decent like that. the model isn’t perfect. My main issue with it is that it keeps stopping. I’ll tell it to do something and if it runs into some sort of blocker, it’ll just give up instead of figuring out a solution. I worked around this by forcing it to continue until the job is done by using /goal in Hermes with nearly every prompt. Works great now and it’ll keep going until the job is completely done.
