# The Anxiety Machine

Cepa · CEPAORG · 2026-10-15

It's predictable that an anxious culture will predict doom. James Lewis argues AI existential risk is the fifth media-driven moral panic since 2000, and that the case against it rests on two tests. Probability, where he notes no extreme prediction of the last seventy years has come true, from peak oil to global famine by 2000. And process, where he argues a hypothetical risk only becomes real once you can name the steps, and that nobody has connected superintelligence to mass casualties without routing through a weapon that already exists.
