# Anthropic co-founder backs 'common sense standards' for AI regulation

Nikkei Asia · APORNRATH PHOONPHONGPHIPHAT · 2026-10-15

Artificial intelligence
Anthropic co-founder backs 'common sense standards' for AI regulation
Jack Clark calls for confidence-building in the booming technology at IMF-World Bank meetings
Anthropic co-founder and Head of Policy Jack Clark speaks during a panel discussion, "AI and the Global Economy: From Risk to Opportunity", at the IMF and World Bank annual meetings in Bangkok on Oct. 16. (Photo by Ken Kobayashi)

October 16, 2026 20:50 JST
BANGKOK -- Countries producing artificial intelligence need "common sense standards" in regulating the technology, Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark said on Friday, adding that such efforts would instill confidence in AI and help advance the global economy.
