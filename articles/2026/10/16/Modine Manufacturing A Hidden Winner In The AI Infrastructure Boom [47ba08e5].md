# Modine Manufacturing: A Hidden Winner In The AI Infrastructure Boom

Seeking Alpha · Kurt Christensen · 2026-10-16

Modine Manufacturing Company (MOD) recently came onto my watchlist. The company is a pick-and-shovel play on the AI boom as it provides cooling for data centers. The company has seen a strong uptick in business
Modine Manufacturing: A Hidden Winner In The AI Infrastructure Boom
Summary
- Modine Manufacturing is a key beneficiary of the AI-driven data center buildout, with data center cooling now its largest and fastest-growing segment.
- Despite a sharp stock pullback, MOD maintains robust long-term growth prospects, underpinned by record order intake, long-term hyperscaler contracts, and sold-out capacity.
- The recent spin-off of Performance Technologies reduced MOD's scale and near-term valuation, but sharpened its focus on higher-growth, higher-margin core businesses.
- Supply chain constraints and margin compression impacted Q1, but management expects these to be resolved, with operating leverage and EBITDA ramping through FY2027–2028.
