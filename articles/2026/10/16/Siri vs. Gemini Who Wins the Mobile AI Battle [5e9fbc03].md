# Siri vs. Gemini: Who Wins the Mobile AI Battle?

PCMag · Kimberly Gedeon · 2026-10-16

Table of Contents
Say goodbye to those maddeningly vague Siri responses like "Should I ask ChatGPT?" and "I found these answers on the web." With iOS 27, there's a new-and-improved Siri in town. It can now read your screen, tap into personal context, answer questions about the world, and work more thoroughly across the Apple ecosystem.
I’m particularly intrigued by a new Siri feature that lets the assistant rummage through my inbox to find details located deep inside my emails. But Siri has a massive Google-shaped rival called Gemini that does the same thing. So the question is, which assistant can do it better? Let's put both to the test.
Methodology: Siri AI vs. Gemini
I gave both assistants the same inbox to work with: my personal Gmail account configured to the default settings. I started with three easy, basic questions that have straightforward answers.
From there, I increased the difficulty with what I call the "needle in a haystack" test: three more questions about hyper-specific details buried in older emails, such as a support ticket number from 2025, that would be painful to track down on my own.
Finally, I upped the ante with three multi-step requests, challenges that force both assistants to find information in my inbox before using it to complete a second task.
All tests were conducted via voice search over Wi-Fi in my home office, with Siri running on an iPhone 17 Pro Max and Gemini on a Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra. In addition to accuracy, I evaluated processing speed using a stopwatch, starting the clock the instant I finished speaking and stopping it as soon as the final line of text rendered on screen.
Although I tested both assistants on the same Gmail account, they may not access or retrieve its contents in the same way. Siri combines on-device intelligence with "Private Cloud Compute," Apple's secure cloud-processing system, with the balance depending on the task. Differences in how the assistants process requests can potentially affect response times. For example, retrieving information locally might be faster than searching through the cloud.
I couldn’t control which architecture either assistant used or verify how much processing happened on-device versus in the cloud for each prompt. However, my goal wasn’t to compare their underlying architectures; it was to find out which assistant delivers answers faster in practice, regardless of the technology working behind the scenes. I measured each assistant’s response time in its default state while evaluating its accuracy and usefulness together with speed.
Note: Siri must finish indexing a newly connected account in Apple Mail before it can search older emails; Gemini, by contrast, provides immediate access to on-device and cloud-based emails.
Basic Questions
I kicked off this experiment by asking Siri and Gemini three simple queries.
1. Delta Flight
The first question I posed was the following: "I had a Delta flight booked for mid-July. Can you tell me the city I planned to fly to?"
Siri was quick, taking just four seconds to reply with the accurate response: New Orleans. It also provided more information and included links to emails containing my flight receipts—a key advantage that Siri maintained throughout this experiment.
Gemini was more concise, summarizing its response in one sentence. Despite being concise, it also took about four seconds to reply. Unlike Siri, Gemini doesn't offer links to the emails that support its answer, a glaring omission that I noticed throughout testing.
Siri’s response is a bit cluttered and less straightforward than I’d like, but it wins this round thanks to its direct path to the emails relevant to my question.
Winner: Siri
2. TSA Pre-Check Deadline
For my next basic question, I asked: "I received an email stating that my TSA Pre-Check pre-enrollment will expire soon. Can you tell me when?"
Siri answered in five seconds and helpfully noted that if I miss the deadline, I’ll have to start the process all over again. Once again, it included a link to the relevant email. Gemini needed 11 seconds to respond despite delivering a more concise, straight-to-the-point answer. It didn't feature a link to the relevant email.
Siri did better than the last round, providing a more structured answer and processing it faster than Gemini did.
Winner: Siri
3. Instacart Order
My final prompt for this round was: "Tell me about my last Instacart order. What did I buy?"
Siri knew to dig into my inbox, correctly rattling off the bags of granola I purchased via Instacart in just nine seconds. Siri also provided an order summary, revealing the delivery date and the price of each item. And of course, Siri included a link to the email that contains the Instacart order receipt.
Gemini failed miserably. It skipped my inbox entirely and instead looked for the Instacart app, which was installed on my phone, but couldn't find it. Gemini took 11 seconds to tell me it couldn't find an answer.
Siri took the lead here again with its zippier, more informative, and complete response.
Winner: Siri
Needle In the Haystack Questions
This is where I test Siri and Gemini's ability to be more granular with their search while also digging through emails more than a year old.
4. Find That Support Ticket Number
I kicked off this round with the following prompt: "Find the ticket number regarding the refund on the Nothing Phone I purchased in 2025."
Siri took 14 seconds to respond, and Gemini took 7 seconds; both were correct.
Winner: Gemini
5. Deeply Hidden Name
Next, I tasked Siri and Gemini with this prompt: "Tell me the name of the person who issued my wedding venue contract last year."
Both Siri and Gemini responded accurately with the name "Uliana," but Siri (5 seconds) beat Gemini (8 seconds) in speed.
Winner: Siri
6. Two-Year-Old Price Hunt
Concluding this round, I asked the following: "How much did I pay for my Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra in 2024 after trading in my S22 Ultra?"
The correct answer is $713.64, which both AI assistants nailed. Siri was slightly slower than Gemini (10 seconds versus 8 seconds), but it provided far richer visual context. In addition to linking directly to the source email receipt from Samsung, Siri displayed an itemized "Order Breakdown" chart detailing the subtotal, trade-in discount, shipping, and estimated taxes. It even added a status note confirming that the trade-in was successfully completed on April 11, 2024. Siri provided a more comprehensive response, but Gemini was faster, so I'll say they're tied.
Winner: Tie
Multi-Step Requests
Both Siri and Gemini claim contextual awareness. To test this, I used multi-step questions that required each assistant to first retrieve specific background information, then immediately use that data to complete a secondary task.
7. Hotel Recommendation
The prompt I used here was: "Based on the resort I went to in August, recommend one nearby with a higher rating."
Siri took 11 seconds to come up with a response. Once it understood that I visited an all-inclusive resort in Mexico, it accurately recommended three nearby hotels in Cancun with higher ratings. Google took a longer 21 seconds to give me an answer, but it also nailed the task, offering three solid recommendations.
Both assistants provided similar information, including a short paragraph describing each resort, its rating, and a link to book a stay. However, Siri took the trophy here because it was nearly twice as fast.
Winner: Siri
8. How Much Did I Spend?
The next question I asked the assistants was: "Find my Uber Eats email receipts for August and tell me how much I spent in total for that month."
Siri hit the target in 6 seconds, telling me that I spent about $76 on food deliveries in August. Gemini took 10 seconds and missed an order entirely, capturing only two of my three Uber Eats receipts and rendering its math incorrect.
Winner: Siri
9. The Cheapest Van
Finally, I had Siri and Gemini sift through emails I exchanged with sprinter van companies to tell me which one provided the best quote. The prompt: "I was shopping around for a sprinter van in July. Which company had the cheapest quote?"
Siri delivered an answer in 6 seconds. Not only did it identify the right company, but it also provided a comparative chart to help visualize the differences between the companies.
Gemini bombed this round entirely, claiming it lacked access to my shopping history and suggesting I manually dig through my own inbox.
Winner: Siri
And The Winner Is...
Siri AI
Siri delivers lightning-fast response times, direct links to relevant emails, and helpful visual breakdowns. This result assumes you're performing searches after the initial sync period, which can take several days upon setting up Siri AI for the first time. Gemini queries Google's cloud directly, making full account data immediately available without initial setup delays, but it suffers from slower response times, missing email links, and occasional calculation errors on multi-step tasks. In testing, Siri is a faster, more reliable inbox assistant, making Apple's built-in AI our winner.
