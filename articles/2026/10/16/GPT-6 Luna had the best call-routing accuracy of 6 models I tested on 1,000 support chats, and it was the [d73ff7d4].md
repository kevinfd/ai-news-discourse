# GPT-6 Luna had the best call-routing accuracy of 6 models I tested on 1,000 support chats, and it was the cheapest LLM

Youtu · misterespresso · 2026-10-16

Benchmark: 6 models labeling the same 1,000 customer-service chats (ABCD dataset), 7 questions each, scored against system logs. Default settings, same prompt, strict structured output. GPT-6 Luna: Best zero-shot model on "why did the customer reach out" (86.6%) and on the 55-option intent question (78.8%). Its +5.4 point lead over the decision model Jev on call reason was the strongest result in the test, holding after correction for 42 comparisons. Cheapest LLM: $0.11 per 1,000 chats (87% of input was a cache hit on the shared question block). Trade-offs: slowest median (1.95 s per chat)…
