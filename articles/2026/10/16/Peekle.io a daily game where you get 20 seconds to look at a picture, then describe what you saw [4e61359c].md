# Peekle.io: a daily game where you get 20 seconds to look at a picture, then describe what you saw

Peekle · johnumbaugh · 2026-10-16

Peekle.io is a daily game, build with Claude code, that is meant to help people improve their powers of observation and recall. Every day there's a new pic, the same for everyone. You get only 20 seconds to look at the picture then it disappears and you write down everything you remember. An AI grader checks your results and gives you 0–5 stars. Or, If writing a description sounds like too much (or if you are on your phone and don't care to type a bunch of stuff), you can do a multiple-choice quiz mode.
