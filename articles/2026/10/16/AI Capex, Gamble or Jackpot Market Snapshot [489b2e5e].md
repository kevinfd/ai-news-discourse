# AI Capex, Gamble or Jackpot?: Market Snapshot

Bloomberg · 2026-10-16

This week started with a striking warning from the Broadcom CEO, who likened the artificial intelligence market to a "casino." The statement was followed by strong earnings from ASML and a better-than-expected outlook from TSMC, though neither was enough to stem a global tech stock rout. Bloomberg's "The Opening Trade" spoke to analysts and experts about what this tells us about the state of expenditure within the booming trade and whether its becoming an increasingly riskier bet.
