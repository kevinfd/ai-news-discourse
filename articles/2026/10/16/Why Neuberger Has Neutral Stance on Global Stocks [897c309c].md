# Why Neuberger Has Neutral Stance on Global Stocks

Bloomberg · 2026-10-16

Neuberger Multi-Asset Strategies Co-CIO Maya Bhandari says the firm recently cut back its stance on global equities to “at target” for the first time in four years, with Europe still underweight due to its cyclical bias, heavy concentration in autos and banks, and limited AI exposure. She tells Bloomberg this is in contrast to Japan and China, with their stronger earnings and valuations. (Source: SBS Studio)
