# SpaceX is almost, sort of, maybe, committing to AI safety oversight.

The Verge · Robert Hart · 2026-10-16

R
SpaceX is almost, sort of, maybe, committing to AI safety oversight.
In a noncommittal X post, Elon Musk said SpaceX will “probably” follow in Anthropic’s footsteps and allow external evaluators access to its AI systems. The tentative pledge comes amid growing concerns about the risks of frontier AI models and criticism that voluntary measures leave companies largely policing themselves.
