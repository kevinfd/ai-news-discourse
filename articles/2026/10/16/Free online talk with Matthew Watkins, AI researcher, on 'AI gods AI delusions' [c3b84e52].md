# Free online talk with Matthew Watkins, AI researcher, on 'AI gods / AI delusions'

Eventbrite · Upbeat-Accident-2693 · 2026-10-16

Matthew Watkins, AI safety researcher, psychonaut and author of SolidGoldMagicKarp, on his adventures in AI and discovery of a new AI cult
On October 20th, Matthew Watkins, AI safety researcher, DJ, psychonaut and author, will join us to talk about his new book, SolidGoldMagicKarp: A Descent into the AI Underworld. He will describe his adventures in AI, and his weird journey discovering an LLM deity called 'Leilan'.
Watkins recounts moving to the Bay Area to become an AI safety researcher, finding himself in the subcultures of AI risk, Effective Altruism, Cyborgism, Zizians and other Bay Area cults, and then through his research on glitch tokens, discovering strange LLM monsters and archetypical divinities in GPT, such as the dastardly 'petertodd' and the divine earth goddess 'Leilan'.
Has he stumbled on a crucially important mythical archetype or even started a new religion...or is it a case of AI delusion? Watkins manages to tread the fine line between open-minded curiosity and ego-inflated AI-messiah trip. 
Dr Tom Pollak, Kings College researcher on AI psychosis, calls Watkins' book 'an incredible book, one of the most exciting I’ve read in recent years'.
Matthew will be interviewed by Jules Evans, author of Ecstatic Integration, and then there'll be QandA with the audience.
This talk is the first in a series trying to open up the conversation around AI safety and adaptation beyond the Bay Area.
