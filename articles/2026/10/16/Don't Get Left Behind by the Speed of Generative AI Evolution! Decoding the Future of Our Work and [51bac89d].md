Summary
The world of generative AI is rapidly shifting from a phase of simply answering questions with text to an era of "Agent AI," where it is deeply embedded into individual tasks and professional corporate workflows. Major services like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google Gemini have each undergone their own unique evolution, allowing users to select the optimal tool for their specific work. This news highlights the improved customizability of Gemini's "Skills" feature, the practical, field-oriented evolution of Ricoh's business document reading capabilities, and the trend in development environments where AI autonomously completes tasks. These technological advancements have the potential to fundamentally change the way not only programmers and specialists work, but also general business professionals burdened with daily administrative and planning tasks.
Article
1. Gemini now supports "Skills." The full picture of a customization feature that is also attracting attention from the perspective of Claude users.
Reference Article: Reference Article 2
News Overview
Google's AI assistant "Gemini" now supports a new "Skills" feature. The previously available "Gems" functionality has also been migrated, and users can now more easily create their own custom functions from the "Create Skill" page. Because multiple creation methods are provided, AI customization tailored to individual needs is possible for everyone from beginners to advanced users, and it is attracting attention as a switching option even from users who have been using other companies' generative AI services.
The Perspective of "Evolution" to Decode
In the evolution of generative AI, the most important point now is "how to make the AI understand the context of your work." There is a limit to streamlining daily specific tasks if you only get generic answers. This support for "Skills" in Gemini is a major step that allows users to have the AI learn their routine work or specific roles (for example, an excellent sounding board, a tone changer for writing, etc.) and keep it resident at hand. For non-engineer business professionals, this means "you can mass-produce your own digital assistants without code indefinitely." Even without programming knowledge, if you save instructions like "I want you to check documents in this procedure" or "I want you to act as a sounding board for newsletters in this tone" as skills, you can omit the troublesome prompt input every time. It can be said that this is a symbolic update where our role shifts from an "operator" who uses AI to a "director" who masters AI.
Practical Tips
First, open the Gemini screen, access the page to create your own "Skills," and try registering one routine task you perform repeatedly in your daily work (such as summarizing meeting minutes or creating email reply drafts).
2. Ricoh's newly developed "Document Reading Enhancement Workflow" changes how companies utilize business documents.
Reference Article: Reference Article 4
News Overview
Ricoh Co., Ltd. has developed a "Document Reading Enhancement Workflow" (patent pending) that reads Japanese documents containing charts and complex layouts with high precision. This cutting-edge technology is scheduled to be gradually integrated into the "RICOH On-Premise LLM Starter Kit" as a template for the generative AI application development platform "Dify" starting this summer. Efforts to have AI deeply understand data such as complex company-specific documents, manuals, and catalogs, leading to work efficiency and new value creation, are accelerating.
Perspective on Deciphering 'Evolution'
When many business professionals try to use generative AI for their work, a major hurdle they face is the problem that it 'doesn't read complex internal documents or PDFs well.' While conventional AI excels with clean text data, it struggles to accurately structure and understand documents like estimates with complex tables, manuals with many diagrams, or internal regulations with unique layouts. This new workflow from Ricoh is exactly what breaks through the barrier of 'real documents sleeping in the workplaces of Japanese companies.' By incorporating this as a template for accessible platforms like Dify, even companies or departments without specialized system development knowledge can have AI learn their own complex documents, enabling them to smoothly automate internal inquiry responses and document creation. The future where we are freed from the stress of 'hard-to-read internal documents' that we handle daily is definitely approaching.
Practical Tips
First, list the 'complex-format documents and manuals that everyone in your department struggles to search or read,' and organize the targets you will prioritize for ingestion when introducing internal AI tools in the future.
3. The Evolution of 'Agent AI' That Thinks and Verifies Autonomously, and Development Environments
Reference Article: Reference Article 8, Reference Article 7, Reference Article 9
News Summary
In the field of integrated development environments such as Google's 'Antigravity' and AI coding assistants, features as 'Agent AI'—where AI writes, executes, and even tests code itself—are evolving rapidly. With the emergence of open-source trust runtimes (like Gravitas), AI is now capable of executing highly autonomous tasks that involve prior confirmation, planning, and post-verification, rather than just outputting instructed text. This is dramatically speeding up the entire development and production process.
Perspective on Deciphering 'Evolution'
Do you think, 'I'm not an expert in programming or system development, so news about these development tools doesn't concern me'? That is a big misunderstanding. The core of the current AI trend is the shift from mere 'text generation' to 'agentification,' where AI thinks and acts autonomously. The approach where AI plans, tests, and completes tasks based on evidence in a development setting will eventually be applied to all workflows in our administrative work, project management, and creative production. For example, if a human provides a rough goal ('Create an outline for next week's marketing initiative'), the AI will gather the necessary information, verify hypotheses, and test and polish the draft of the deliverable itself—such autonomous working styles will soon become standard. Even for non-engineers, understanding this concept of an agent—'how to delegate work to AI and how to verify it'—will be an extremely powerful weapon in your future career.
Practical Tips
First, write down the 'routine research or data organization processes where you want the AI to look things up and complete them on its own once you give the instructions' in your daily work, and start practicing giving the AI step-by-step instructions (prompt chaining).
Today's Summary and the Next Step
This time, we looked at customizing daily tasks with Gemini's 'Skills' feature, the advancement of business document utilization through Ricoh's document reading technology, and the trend of 'Agent AI' where AI works autonomously. Generative AI is no longer a 'novel toy' but has grown into infrastructure that fundamentally updates how we work.
As a 'next step' to take starting tomorrow, first pick just one 'routine task that can be left to AI' in your daily work, and try to break it down into your own custom procedure (skill) using tools like Gemini. The accumulation of these small steps will be the driving force that dramatically boosts your productivity.
