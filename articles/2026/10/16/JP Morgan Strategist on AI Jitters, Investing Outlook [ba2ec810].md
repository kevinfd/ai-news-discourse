# JP Morgan Strategist on AI Jitters, Investing Outlook

Bloomberg · 2026-10-16

Jordan Jackson, JPMorgan Asset Management Global Market Strategist, discusses whether earnings growth may be nearing a peak even if results are not worsening. Jackson agrees that third-quarter results are expected to be strong, with forecasts calling for about 27% to 28% year-over-year earnings growth. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Kristine Aquino on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
