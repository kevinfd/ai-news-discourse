# Corning: Not Worth The AI Risk (Rating Downgrade)

Seeking Alpha · Ricardo Fernandez · 2026-10-16

Corning: Not Worth The AI Risk (Rating Downgrade)
Summary
- Corning is downgraded to Sell from Hold due to high valuation, aggressive growth assumptions, and a relatively weaker technological edge in the AI photonics trade.
- GLW's revenue and earnings growth are highly dependent on sustained AI data center buildout, with fiber optics projected to reach 65% of revenue by 2030.
- Consensus targets imply 21% upside to YE27, but risk-reward is less attractive versus peers like NVDA, MRVL, and MU, which offer better growth and valuation.
- Key risks include potential delays or cancellations in data center builds and rising input costs, particularly helium and energy.
