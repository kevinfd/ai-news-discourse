# Nscale's Flagship Data Center Hit by Delays Ahead of IPO

Bloomberg · 2026-10-16

Artificial intelligence infrastructure firm Nscale is seeking to raise up to $3 billion in an initial public offering as soon as this month, just as investors are growing increasingly wary of the risks of the AI buildout. The UK firm has also been hit by regulatory hurdles, a fire and other delays during construction of a flagship data center in Norway. Bloomberg's Paula Doenecke reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
