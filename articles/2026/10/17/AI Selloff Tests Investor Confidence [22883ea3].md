# AI Selloff Tests Investor Confidence

Bloomberg · 2026-10-17

Ritholtz Wealth Management Chairman and Chief Investment Officer Barry Ritholtz tells Bloomberg This Weekend that AI remains a transformational technology, but investors face a difficult task determining which companies will ultimately emerge as winners and losers. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Ritholtz discusses CoreWeave’s selloff, concerns about AI financing and valuations, and the challenges facing Tesla as competition in electric vehicles intensifies.
