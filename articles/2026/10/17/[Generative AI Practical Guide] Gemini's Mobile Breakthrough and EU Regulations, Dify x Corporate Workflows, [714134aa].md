Summary
In daily operations and business settings, the use of Generative AI is rapidly shifting from mere 'trial of conversational tools' to the 'foundation of business processes and system infrastructure.'
In the latest usage trends, Google's Gemini is rapidly increasing its user base in mobile environments such as smartphones, overtaking ChatGPT, which has been the leader for many years. On the other hand, ChatGPT maintains a strong share in desktop (PC) environments, leading to a polarization based on AI usage scenarios. In response to this rapid social penetration, the European Commission (EU) has published draft rules for strict fine procedures against giant AI companies providing ChatGPT, Gemini, etc., based on the 'AI Act,' and has begun building safe and reliable governance.
Looking at corporate practice, the 'workflow' of business using tools like 'Dify,' which allows for building LLM (Large Language Model) apps with no-code/low-code, has become a major trend. Ricoh Co., Ltd. has developed technology to accurately interpret complex layouts and charts specific to Japanese business documents and will begin providing them as Dify templates. Additionally, GREE, Inc. is practicing a high-speed workflow that incorporates AI into all development processes, from planning and design to programming and QA (Quality Assurance).
Furthermore, in terms of technical infrastructure, NVIDIA has announced 'Nemotron 3.5 Super VL,' which integrates the understanding of images, videos, and text while reducing processing costs by up to 40 times (1/40) compared to previous versions. Additionally, open-source tools like 'GRAVITAS,' which force AI agents like Gemini to follow strict verification procedures, have emerged, driving both ultra-low costs and high reliability simultaneously.
In this article, we will delve deep into these three major news items and explain in detail the 'essence of technological evolution' and 'practical approaches to apply to your work starting tomorrow,' which are important even for business people who are not engineers.
1. Gemini overtakes ChatGPT on mobile! The tectonic shift in usage share and the tightening of AI regulations by the EU
News Overview
Data from a survey on the actual state of AI usage in Europe has revealed that a major change is occurring in users' AI tool usage habits. According to the latest data from the Italian market, the number of monthly users of Gemini in the mobile app environment reached 7.578 million, surpassing ChatGPT's 7.125 million to take the top spot (see Ansa.it). On the other hand, in the personal computer (PC) environment, ChatGPT maintains a lead with 3.189 million users compared to Gemini's 2.399 million, although the overall growth pace of ChatGPT is showing a slowing trend after its initial explosive expansion.
With such explosive adoption and normalization, the eyes of administrative and regulatory authorities are also becoming serious. Based on the 'AI Act,' the world's first comprehensive AI law, the European Commission (EU) has published draft rules (Ares(2026)9589403) for calculating and collecting fines targeting major large-scale AI models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Meta AI, as well as AI embedded in major platforms (see pasqualepillitteri.it). Procedures for imposing strict sanctions on violating companies through the AI Office have been concretized.
Perspective on 'Evolution' to Decipher
The arrival of the 'AI anytime' era brought about by the mobile shift
The background to Gemini overtaking ChatGPT in the mobile environment lies in its close system integration with smartphones and the maturation of voice interaction interfaces. Google has deeply embedded Gemini into the Android OS and various Google services (Gmail, Google Calendar, Maps, etc.), allowing users to call upon AI hands-free or with a single tap, without even being bothered by the trouble of launching an app.
Until now, AI usage was dominated by the style of 'sitting in front of a computer and typing prompts (instructions) on a keyboard.' However, the rise of Gemini on mobile indicates a shift toward 'AI utilization fused with daily actions,' such as brainstorming via voice input while on the move, real-time information searching while out and about, and immediate questions about real-world objects or documents captured with a camera.
On the other hand, ChatGPT maintains a strong hold in the PC environment. For tasks requiring deep focus, such as writing long reports, performing advanced data analysis, and generating complex programming code, the combination of a PC and ChatGPT remains a powerful force. Business professionals are beginning to clearly differentiate their AI usage based on the device: using mobile (Gemini, etc.) for quick information gathering and brainstorming, and using PCs (ChatGPT, etc.) for deep dives and creating deliverables.
The Impact of the EU AI Act's Published Penalty Rules on Businesses
The publication of penalty rules by the European Commission is not just a distant story about 'giant IT companies potentially paying fines.' It signifies that AI technology has moved past the 'experimental trial phase' and into a 'stage where strict governance and accountability are required as social infrastructure.'
For non-engineer business professionals and executives, understanding whether the AI tools used by their company comply with legal regulations and security standards is crucial for risk management. Decisions based on misinformation (hallucinations) provided by AI, or the input of data that infringes on copyrights or privacy, can become significant compliance risks for a company. Understanding the framework for safe AI usage (AI governance) based on laws and guidelines, and establishing secure operational rules within the company, will be the key to future corporate competitiveness.
Tips for Practice
First, try installing both the Gemini and ChatGPT apps on your smartphone, and use 'voice input' during your commute or while walking to organize your ideas and conduct research.
2. The Full-Scale Adoption of AI Workflows! 'Lightning-Fast Business Reform' Demonstrated by Ricoh's Dify Utilization and Gree's End-to-End AI Implementation
News Overview
Generative AI is rapidly evolving beyond being a tool that simply generates text and images ({see AWS) into 'AI workflows' that connect multiple systems and data sources to automate a series of business tasks.
Particularly noteworthy in this field is the generative AI application development platform 'Dify.' Dify is a tool that integrates prompt assembly, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using internal company documents, and the publishing of APIs and chat UIs. Along with 'n8n,' which excels at event coordination between various apps, it is seeing a rapid increase in adoption as a powerful infrastructure for business automation (see Activepieces).
Ricoh Co., Ltd. has independently developed a 'Document Reading Enhancement Workflow' that dramatically improves the ability to read Japanese documents containing charts and complex layouts. This technology is scheduled to be included as a standard Dify template in the 'RICOH On-Premise LLM Starter Kit' provided by the company (see Ricoh). This enables the advanced utilization of internal documents and business automation even in strict environments where data cannot be taken outside the company.
As a cutting-edge example in practical business, the development workflow released by Gree, Inc. is attracting attention. Gree has put into practical use a 'Lightning-Fast Development AI Workflow' that incorporates AI into every stage of the development process, from service and product planning, UI/UX design (utilizing design tools like Figma), and front-end and back-end code writing, to quality assurance (QA) testing (see Speaker Deck).
Perspective on 'Evolution'
From One-Off 'Chat Usage' to 'Continuous Workflow Automation'
Until now, generative AI usage has centered on manual tasks where users type 'Tell me about X' into a screen, then manually copy and paste the returned answer into another tool.
However, with the spread of workflow platforms represented by Dify and n8n, the way business is conducted is about to change dramatically. For example, a series of processes—such as 'when a customer inquiry email arrives (trigger), the AI analyzes the content, references the database (RAG), creates a draft response, notifies a chat tool, and sends the reply automatically once an approval button is pressed'—can now be built automatically without writing any code (or with minimal code).
Dify excels at linking with knowledge bases (internal manuals, FAQs, etc.) and creating conversational UIs, while n8n excels at data integration between a wide variety of SaaS tools such as CRMs, spreadsheets, and email tools. By combining these, system development that previously cost millions of yen and took months can now be built in a matter of days or weeks.
Ricoh's Technology Solving the Bottleneck of 'Paper and Complex PDFs' for Japanese Companies
One of the biggest reasons why the introduction of generative AI has not progressed in Japanese companies is the problem that 'internal documents are saved in PDF, image, or complex Excel table formats, and AI cannot read them correctly.' This is because conventional AI struggled to correctly interpret Japanese layouts, such as two-column text, text written inside charts, or a mix of vertical and horizontal writing.
The 'Document Reading Enhancement Workflow' developed by Ricoh is a powerful solution to this challenge. Using patent-pending technology, it correctly interprets Japanese documents while maintaining complex layout structures and is provided as a Dify template. This allows AI to correctly understand the vast amount of specialized documents, manuals, and past proposals lying dormant in corporate offices and return highly accurate answers (RAG). Especially since it is provided as a kit compatible with 'on-premise (self-built environments),' the impact of being able to safely introduce advanced AI automation in fields that are extremely concerned about information leaks, such as finance, manufacturing, medicine, and government, is immense.
An era where you can become a "Business Producer" without programming knowledge
The GREE case study demonstrates the potential not just for programmers and engineers, but for business professionals in every field. By making AI a powerful partner in the product development process—from planning and design to programming and testing—the speed at which ideas are brought to life increases dramatically.
Even non-engineers can now articulate their screen design concepts to AI, provide instructions, and work alongside AI to perform quality checks on the resulting prototypes. What will be required in the coming era is not fine-grained technical skills, but overall design capability and the business ideas themselves, such as "what kind of business process do I want to create?" and "how can I solve customer problems?"
Tips for Practice
"First, pick one task from your daily work that involves repeatedly switching between multiple apps or files to copy and paste, and try writing out the steps on paper in a flowchart format."
3. The Arrival of NVIDIA's "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL" and Gemini Reliability Improvement Technology: The Frontline of Multimodal & Agents
News Overview
Technological innovations that simultaneously achieve improved intelligence and reduced operational costs for generative AI are being announced one after another.
NVIDIA, a giant in hardware and AI platforms, has announced "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL," an open-weight multimodal AI model that deeply understands images, videos, and text simultaneously. The biggest shock of this model is that it reduces processing costs by up to "1/40th" compared to previous models while maintaining extremely high comprehension performance (Refer to GIGAZINE).
Meanwhile, in the field of software and development ecosystems, an open-source tool called "GRAVITAS" has emerged to ensure the operational reliability of AI agents, drawing a significant response from the engineering community. GRAVITAS is a reliability runtime (verification framework) that enforces "strict engineering discipline" (such as reading the entire file before rewriting, planning based on risk, and reporting based on evidence of execution results)—similar to what Anthropic's Claude model possesses—on large language models like Gemini (Refer to GitHub - udaysharmadev/Gravitas). It functions in coordination with Google's integrated development environment (IDE) plugins and environments like "Antigravity" (Refer to Google Antigravity Docs).
Furthermore, as seen in the integration of OpenAI's video generation AI "Sora" into the ChatGPT ecosystem (Refer to GenAI Blog), the media domain that AI can handle is rapidly expanding from text to images and now to video.
The Perspective of "Evolution" to Decipher
The Explosive Popularization of Multimodal AI Triggered by "1/40th Processing Cost"
Until now, "multimodal AI (Vision-Language Models)" that read and perform advanced analysis on images and videos consumed vast GPU computing resources, making them expensive to use and creating hurdles for processing large amounts of data on a daily basis.
The "1/40th cost reduction" achieved by NVIDIA's "Nemotron 3.5 Super VL" removes this economic barrier at once. As a result, use cases such as the following become a reality at an extremely low cost:
- Surveillance camera video analysis for stores and factories: Automatically performing safety checks and analyzing customer behavior from 24/7 video in real-time and at low cost.
- Automatic digitization of large volumes of paper documents, receipts, and drawings: Instantly reading and creating databases from tens of thousands of handwritten documents or complex blueprints.
- Automatic summarization and tagging of video content: Automatically generating video clips of important topics and text summaries from internal training videos or recordings of long web conferences.
Because it is open-weight (the model's weight data is public), companies can incorporate it into their own cloud or on-premises environments to customize and operate it freely. This serves as a perfect tailwind for small and medium-sized enterprises and new businesses that had previously given up on adoption due to AI usage costs.
The Philosophy of GRAVITAS: Preventing AI Hallucinations (Lies) and Making It a "Truly Reliable Partner"
When using AI for business tasks, especially for programming or the automated creation of important documents, the biggest challenge is 'hallucination' (unfounded lies or malfunctions). The phenomenon where AI fails to verify its work midway, rewriting code or text on its own and causing errors, has troubled many users.
The project called 'GRAVITAS' provides a mechanism to externally enforce 'discipline in the execution process' for models with excellent foundational capabilities like Gemini. It establishes the following rules for AI behavior:
- Do not write before reading: Always perform a full inspection of all files or contexts to be modified beforehand.
- Planning based on risk: Predict the scope of impact of changes and establish a procedure before execution.
- Evidence-based reporting: Execute tests and report only those items for which successful evidence has been obtained as 'complete'.
This 'evidence-based' approach is a critically important technological advancement for AI agents to work autonomously without human management (autonomous AI agents). For non-engineers, the process of 'what verification steps the AI went through to reach its conclusion' becomes transparent, allowing them to confidently adopt AI-generated output in their work.
The Future Work Environment Envisioned by the Antigravity Environment and Video Integration (Sora)
What Google's Antigravity ecosystem (see Google Antigravity Docs) and OpenAI's Sora integration (see GenAI Blog) are presenting is a 'next-generation creative and work environment' where text, images, code, and video are seamlessly connected.
In the past, 'tools for writing text,' 'tools for editing video,' and 'tools for programming' were completely separate. However, now, in a single AI chat screen or development environment, you can prototype videos (Sora, etc.) just by sending a prompt, while in the background, mechanisms like GRAVITAS ensure AI accuracy, and NVIDIA technology (Nemotron) analyzes images at a low cost to support work—an integrated worldview is being completed.
