# How to Walk in the AI Era: Making the Copilot Your Ally. The True Value of 'Microsoft Copilot' Integrating into the Workplace

note · AIラボ｜ログシーの部屋 · 2026-10-17

How to Walk in the AI Era: Making the Copilot Your Ally. The True Value of 'Microsoft Copilot' Integrating into the Workplace
Last time, I explained the essence that instead of just copy-pasting other people's prompts, combining 'your own purpose, experience, and values (your own axis)' with AI is what truly produces results is what truly produces results.
This time, following the three major AIs (ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude), I will explain 'Microsoft Copilot,' an AI that is rapidly increasing its presence in business and daily work settings.
'Isn't Copilot basically the same thing as ChatGPT?'
'It was introduced at my company, but I only use it as a search bar...'
If you think that, you are using it in a very wasteful way.
The name Copilot means 'co-pilot' in English. 
The 'main pilot' (protagonist) holding the control stick is always you. 
The ultimate partner that quietly supports tedious practical work and data processing by your side... 
that is the true form of Copilot.
1. The essence of Copilot is 'a staff officer who is in your work environment from the start'
When using ChatGPT or Claude, we go out of our way to open a browser, access a site, and start a conversation.
However, Copilot's greatest strength lies in the fact that it is 'waiting right next to the work screens we usually use (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams) from the start.'
- ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini: An image of going to ask a 'specialized consultation desk' outside of yourself
- Microsoft Copilot: An image of working next to your usual desk while looking at open documents together
There is no need to brace yourself to 'use AI,' and you can borrow the power of AI naturally without stopping the flow of your daily work. 
This is the overwhelming advantage that no other AI tool has.
2. 'Three utilization scenes' where it demonstrates its true value in the field
I will introduce three scenes where Copilot is particularly effective in business and daily document creation.
① 'Creating the framework for materials and documents' to eliminate the pain of creating from scratch
Have you ever opened Word or PowerPoint and frozen in front of a blank screen? If you use Copilot, you can just say, 'Create a framework for a proposal regarding XX' or 'Think of a structure for 5 presentation slides based on this memo,' and it will create a draft for you in an instant.
② 'Summarizing meetings and long emails' to dam the torrent of information
Even if you are not at a meeting on Teams, if you ask it to 'extract the decisions and action items from today's meeting,' it will organize them in an instant. It also neatly summarizes long past email exchanges and even suggests candidates for reply text.
③ 'Organizing and analyzing Office data' to automate tedious work
Even if you can't remember complex functions in Excel, just by giving instructions in Japanese like 'Aggregate sales data by month and tell me the trends' or 'Create a formula to filter with these conditions,' it will support you in aggregating and visualizing data.
3. 'Your axis' that you must not forget even when operating Copilot
No matter how excellent a Copilot you have by your side, it is you, the pilot, who decides 'where to fly the plane (the destination)'.
Even if you can automate operations in Excel or PowerPoint,
- 'Why are you delivering this proposal to the client?' (Purpose)
- 'What are the non-negotiable standards you hold for this project?' (Aesthetics and Values)
- 'The challenges felt on the ground and gritty personal experiences' (Your own experience)
If these 'personal pillars' are missing from your instructions, all Copilot will produce are 'generic, boring slides you've seen somewhere before' or 'reports that anyone in the company could write.'
Leave the work (building frameworks and organizing data) to Copilot, and use the time you save to infuse the materials with 'the thinking and passion that only you can provide.'
This is the true way to master the Copilot and increase your work performance tenfold.
Conclusion: You Are Always Holding the Control Stick
No matter how much AI evolves and integrates into our work environment, the protagonist will always be the 'human.'
Let's quickly leave the tedious preparation and simple tasks to the excellent Copilot by your side. 
And you, try focusing deeply on coming up with the passion and ideas that only you can express.
Instead of just copying and pasting someone else's prompt and leaving it all to the AI, when you engage in communication that correctly conveys your destination (intent), Copilot will undoubtedly help you create the best results together.
