# AskAnyModel Gives You Access to 50+ AI Models for Life for Just $40

PCMag · StackCommerce Team · 2026-10-17

TL;DR: Get lifetime access to AskAnyModel’s 50+ AI models, side-by-side comparisons, and unlimited supported AI chats and image generation for $39.99.
Different AI models can give noticeably different answers to the same prompt. AskAnyModel AI Pro Plan lifetime access is on sale for $39.99 (MSRP $499), putting more than 50 models into one browser-based workspace so you can use—and compare—their responses without jumping between platforms.
The standout feature is side-by-side comparison. Select two to six models, enter your prompt once, and AskAnyModel sends it to all of them simultaneously. You can compare responses, response times, and token counts to decide which output works best.
The lifetime plan includes unlimited chats with 30 supported standard models, including options from DeepSeek, Mistral, Llama, Gemma, and Qwen. You also get access to a Pro model with up to 300 chats per week.
For flagship models, the plan provides 500 premium credits each month. Those credits can be used across 19 premium models, including GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Mistral Large, and Perplexity Search.
AskAnyModel also includes unlimited AI image generation across supported models without using premium credits. You can switch models during conversations, and the model library is continuously updated.
