# US Warns Election Officials of AI-Generated Cyber Threats Ahead of Midterms

The Epoch Times · Aldgra Fredly · 2026-10-17

The FBI and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) warned election officials and the public on Oct. 16 that cyber actors could use artificial intelligence to steal sensitive data ahead of the midterm elections.
“Election officials, candidates, and their political organizations are attractive targets to malicious cyber criminals due to their access to voter registration data, current government officials, or sensitive campaign information,” it stated.
According to the statement, AI technology allows cybercriminals to craft text messages or emails that closely resemble legitimate communications, making it difficult for recipients to identify fraudulent messages.
It also warned that cyber actors could use AI-generated deepfake videos and audio recordings to create new identities, impersonate real people, and bolster phishing schemes.
“During the last two election cycles, election officials experienced a surge of phishing attempts, and within the last year, the FBI saw an increase in smishing attempts through encrypted chat applications,” it stated.
“Malicious cyber actors will continue to develop innovative technologies to breach digital defenses.”
Election officials and the public are advised to carefully verify the sources of emails and text messages they receive, according to the statement.
The agencies also recommend using phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication for all services “to the extent possible,” particularly for email accounts, virtual private networks, and accounts with access to critical systems.
Organizations are advised to enhance their security infrastructure and educate employees on how to identify phishing and smishing attempts. It stated that employees should verify requests for sensitive information through secondary channels whenever possible.
The agencies said organizations should use unique passphrases for password logins and avoid reusing passphrases across multiple accounts.
