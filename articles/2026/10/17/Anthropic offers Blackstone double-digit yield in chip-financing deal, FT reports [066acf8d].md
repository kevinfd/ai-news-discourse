# Anthropic offers Blackstone double-digit yield in chip-financing deal, FT reports

Channel NewsAsia · CNA · 2026-10-17

Anthropic offers Blackstone double-digit yield in chip-financing deal, FT reports
Oct 16 : Anthropic is offering Blackstone a yield of more than 10 per cent to finance its latest chip deal, the Financial Times reported on Friday.
Blackstone funds would invest $9 billion in the debt backed by Anthropic's five-year lease payments on the chips, the report said, citing people familiar with the matter.
Reuters could not immediately verify the report.
