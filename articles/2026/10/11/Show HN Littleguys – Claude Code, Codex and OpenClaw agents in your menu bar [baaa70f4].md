# Show HN: Littleguys – Claude Code, Codex and OpenClaw agents in your menu bar

Littleguys · herval · 2026-10-11

Talk to your Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, OpenClaw and Hermes agents the way you talk to friends on WhatsApp. Each one is a contact with its own chats: reply, approve and schedule them from one window, while the guy in your menu bar shows who's working and who needs you.
The guy in your menu bar acts out what your agents are doing. No dashboards, no tab-hunting.
Chat, and approve in one click.
Every agent is a contact with its own chats: streaming replies, tool calls, attachments. Commands that need you show up as cards: allow once, always allow or deny. No more “[y/N]” buried in terminal tab #9.
Everyone, at a glance.
Click the guy for every agent on every machine, with a live status ring, the last message and a quick reply.
Routines that just run.
Morning digests, nightly dependency bumps, weekly numbers. Friendly schedules, run-now, and results that land in a thread.
From any machine.
Guys on your Mac mini, your homelab and your laptop, all in one place. Finds gateways on your tailnet and LAN by itself.
littleguys speaks the Agent Client Protocol, so new agents are one click away from the ACP registry. It's a friendly face for the agents you already run, not a new place to run them.
Free. Open source. Yours.
littleguys holds your gateway tokens and approves real commands on your machines. You should be able to read every line of it, so you can.
It installs anything missing (the Xcode Command Line Tools, Rust, Node, pnpm) into ~/.littleguys, without sudo or touching your shell profile. The first build takes a few minutes; run it again to update. You can read install.sh before you run it.
