AI chatbots can be useful for research, writing, and everyday problem-solving, but a confident answer isn’t necessarily a correct or complete one. Manually checking every claim takes time, especially when you use AI across multiple platforms.
Caveat AI Pro adds a second-opinion layer to answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. A lifetime subscription is currently $89.99, down from the $449 MSRP.
