# The new Apple Mac Studio M5 Max is already on sale at Amazon — save $200

Mashable · Lauren Allain · 2026-10-19

The new Apple Mac Studio M5 Max is already on sale at Amazon — save $200
SAVE $200: The Apple Mac Studio M5 Max is on sale at Amazon for $2,299, down from the list price of $2,499. That's a savings of $200 from the 8% discount.
We haven't even hit Halloween yet, but it's feeling like Black Friday with all the deals rolling in. As tech prices continue to rise (along with everything else), we're not mad at all that sales are already underway. If you've been checking out Apple's newest tech, there's a nice deal in store today.
As of Oct. 19, the Apple Mac Studio M5 Max is on sale at Amazon for $2,299, down from the list price of $2,499. That's a saving of $200 from the 8% discount. Today's sale price is the lowest we've ever seen at Amazon.
Apple didn't hold back on upgrades with the new Mac Studio M5 Max. It comes with Thunderbolt 5 ports, the ability to connect with up to eight monitors, and of course, there's the M5 Max chip.
Apple considers this model to be exceptional for those who have high-demand tasks to complete but don't want to place anything bulky on their desks. The Mac Studio is a 7.7-inch square that'll easily tuck under most displays.
Adding to the improvements, Apple mentions the Studio M5 Max has up to 3.9x faster AI performance compared to previous models. Plus, it has onboard USB-A and USB-C ports, as well as a headphone jack.
Before we see this sale price vanish, hop on the $200 savings for the Apple Mac Studio M5 Max.
