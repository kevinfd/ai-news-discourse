# Musk Pledges SpaceX Shares to ‘Every Kid in America’

Bloomberg · 2026-10-19

Elon Musk says he will gift a share of SpaceX to each American child, in a post on X, the social media platform he owns. The move comes just hours after artificial intelligence competitor Anthropic PBC said it would set aside a portion of its shares for the public benefit.
