# Get my game for free this halloween

itch.io · pedrohead_uk · 2026-10-28

I like the idea and that it has a story; I'm organizing an Arcade Jam with a "Shmups" theme. If you have any games, "demos," or projects in development to show, they are welcome.
Phase Break
A downloadable game for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android
Phase Break is a 2.5D arcade shoot-em-up with a fun but touching story about a fighter pilot returning home. You fight for a colony that's already gone - a mystery that slowly reveals itself - and the only weapon that can save everyone might just be the reason you don't come back. Six handcrafted missions, 3 bosses, 7 completely original music tracks, live global leaderboards, one price, no ads, no nonsense, all action!
One epic journey from the home planet, out across space to a mysterious final challenge!
- Multiple locations: Veth's rings, an ore plant, a space station, and a dying sun.
- The Super Weapon: lock on to what matters, then tear it out of space.
- Keep every weapon you find and switch between them on the fly.
- The Warden, the Leviathan and the Architect, each fought in stages.
- A story told over the places it happens, with a choice that stays with your save.
- Keyboard, controller or touch, on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android.
Try it free: the demo is the first two missions, from Veth's ring down to the Warden.
My journey
Phase Break is the end of a 20-year journey. The music and the story have been building for two decades, waiting, and last year the tools finally caught up (Godot + Claude). AI wrote code and some of the art alongside me; the music, the story, the level design, and the feel of every mechanic are mine. Lovingly crafted, start to finish. The credits music is a dedication to my friend Jenny who passed away in 2026 and who provided her incredible vocals to the song.
The missions
- Patrol Sweep. Drop out of the carrier over Veth, skim its ring through the rocks, thread the old ore workings and climb the beacon tower.
- Iron Requiem. Come down onto the great asteroid in Veth's ring and fly the length of the ore plant to the Warden.
- The Harvest. Leave the ring behind for open space, a nebula and a sun on the horizon, until the Syndicate's station comes into view.
- The Collector. Fly through the station, then run the Leviathan down as it tries to escape.
- Dead Frequency. Head for the sun as it grows larger and dimmer, past ships the Architect has taken for its own.
- The Fall. Through the Architect's base to the sun itself, and a last fight flown in orbit as you tear its grip off the star.
Flying and fighting
- Keep every weapon you pick up and switch between them. Pick up the same one again to raise it a level.
- The Phase Break is now aimed: lock on to what matters, then strike.
- Kills made while your guns run hot are worth double.
- Enemy fire is always red, so you can tell at a glance what can hit you.
- The Warden, the Leviathan and the Architect are new fights, each in stages, and they show their damage.
- A proper launch from the carrier, new explosions, and new instruments on screen that stay up for the whole mission.
- Touch controls rebuilt, with a button to switch weapons.
The update
Phase Break 2.0.0 is out, and it's a free update if you already own the game.
The missions and the scoring have changed too much for old scores to compare fairly, so every board starts empty for 2.0.0. Only runs flown in 2.0.0 count. Your own best scores are still kept in the game.
The demo
The free demo is updated too: the first two missions, from Veth's ring down to the Warden.
Updating
The itch app updates the game for you. Otherwise, download it again from this page. On Android, install the new APK over the old one. Your progress, upgrades, shards and best scores carry over.
I'm committed to provide updates for the game for as long as people want to play it for... I also intend to add a level design toolkit so the community can build their own levels!
