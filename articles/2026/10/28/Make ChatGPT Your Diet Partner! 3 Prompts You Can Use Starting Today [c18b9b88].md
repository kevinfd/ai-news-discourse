# Make ChatGPT Your Diet Partner! 3 Prompts You Can Use Starting Today

note · ノエル · 2026-10-28

Make ChatGPT Your Diet Partner! 3 Prompts You Can Use Starting Today 😊
What I would try now, having lost 21.4kg
In yesterday's note, I wrote about how it might be interesting to combine dieting with AI.
Actually, I have the experience of losing 21.4kg in about two years starting at age 58.
The tool that helped me back then was the meal tracking app 'Asuken'.
I recorded what I ate and adjusted my meals on my own while checking calories and nutritional balance.
I still use Asuken today 😊
However, I wasn't using ChatGPT at the time.
So this time, it's not about 'how I lost weight with AI,' but rather
is what I'd like to talk about.
I've come up with three prompts that even beginners could easily try.
1. Gently reviewing your meal records
Even if you record your meals, it's hard to look back at them carefully every day.
In that case, how about having ChatGPT be your conversation partner?
📋 Prompt for copy-pasting ①
You can just enter the meal details to the best of your knowledge.
The idea is to leave the accurate nutritional calculations to a tracking app like Asuken and have ChatGPT help you with the reflection process.
2. Resetting your mindset on days you overeat
When you're on a diet, there are days when you end up eating more than you planned.
At times like that,
"Oh, I've blown it!💦"
Instead of feeling down, you want to shift your mindset, right?
📋 Prompt for Copy-Pasting ②
Just because you overate, you don't have to force yourself to make up for it the next day.
Having someone to help you organize your feelings at times like this might be helpful, right?😊
③ Thinking of small habits you can keep up
When it comes to dieting, it's important to know what to start, but the hard part is whether you can keep it up.
If you aim for perfection right away, it can sometimes become exhausting.
In times like that, try this.
📋 Prompt for Copy-Pasting ③
The point is not to try to do too much from the start.
……or so says me, who tends to cram everything into my work!🤣
Is AI better as a sounding board than a teacher?
None of these three prompts are about having ChatGPT tell you the 'correct' way to diet.
Reflecting on your meals.
Organizing your downcast feelings.
Think of habits that are easy to maintain.
Here is how to use it as a partner for times like that.
Of course, ChatGPT's answers are not always correct, and it is also important to consult with a doctor or registered dietitian regarding your health condition and dietary restrictions.
The three prompts I introduced this time were thought up based on my own diet experience.
Actually, I haven't even tried them myself yet 🤣💦
So, today I introduced them as a suggestion of "maybe you can use it this way!"
At a later date, I plan to try them out with ChatGPT myself and write honestly about what was easy to use and what could be improved 😊
Even so, when I started my diet at 58, I never imagined that at 62 I would be writing about dieting with AI 🤣
If you are interested, please feel free to try one of the prompts that caught your eye.
