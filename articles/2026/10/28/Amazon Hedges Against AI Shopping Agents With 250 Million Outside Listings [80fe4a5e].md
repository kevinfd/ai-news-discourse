# Amazon Hedges Against AI Shopping Agents With 250 Million Outside Listings

The Information · Catherine Perloff · 2026-10-28

For more than a year, Amazon has blocked outside shopping agents from its site while finding ways for its own customers to shop elsewhere. Now a new wave of AI assistants like Meta’s Muse is raising the stakes. 

 In recent months Amazon has stepped up its Shop Direct initiative, which promotes products from brands that don’t sell on Amazon’s site, potentially giving shoppers less reason to turn to outside AI tools that can buy across retailers.
