# How to get rid of unwanted AI on your phone

Fox News · Kurt Knutsson, CyberGuy Report · 2026-10-28

You pull out your phone to look something up on Google. Maybe you're researching a purchase or trying to find a restaurant. You expect a list of websites. Instead, Google serves up a lengthy artificial intelligence-generated answer before you even get to the search results. Sound familiar? I like AI when it helps me get something done. But sometimes I just want to search the internet without an AI summary appearing every time.
Fortunately, there are ways around it. One involves adding a few characters to your search. Another uses a built-in Google filter. And if you've had enough of Siri suggestions on your iPhone or AI features popping up on your Samsung Galaxy or Google Pixel, there are settings worth changing there, too.

HOW TO SEARCH ANYTHING ON IPHONE, GALAXY AND PIXEL
How to get rid of Google AI search results on your phone
Google calls those automatically generated summaries AI Overviews. They appear above traditional search results when Google's systems decide an AI-generated answer might be helpful. Sometimes they save time. However, they can also get in the way when you'd rather read information directly from a website. Unfortunately, Google confirms that AI Overviews cannot be permanently turned off through a standard Search setting. However, you have a few ways to avoid them.
Try adding -AI to your Google search
This trick takes only seconds and is worth trying on an iPhone, iPad, Samsung Galaxy or Google Pixel.
Here's how.
- Open Safari, Chrome or your preferred browser.
- Visit Google.com.
- Enter your search terms as usual.
- Tap Search.
- If an AI Overview appears, return to the search field.
- Add -AI at the end of your search.
- Tap Search again.
For example, instead of searching for: best walking shoes
Try: best walking shoes -AI
Adding -AI can keep an AI Overview from appearing. However, this is an unofficial workaround, and Google doesn't guarantee that it will work every time. There's another catch. Google uses the minus sign to exclude terms from search results. Adding -AI could also remove some relevant pages containing that term. If the trick doesn't work, Google's next option is more dependable.
Use Google's Web filter to skip AI Overviews
Google has a built-in filter that shows traditional web links without AI Overviews.
Here's how to find it.
- Open Safari, Chrome or your preferred browser.
- Go to Google.com.
- Type your search.
- Tap Search.
- Look for the filters near the top of the results.
- Swipe across the options or tap More, if necessary.
- Select Web.
Google will display text-based links without its AI Overview. The downside is that you generally need to select the Web filter again when starting another search. Still, it's a useful way to get straight to websites without an AI-generated answer taking up the top of the screen.
One important note: Turning off an AI Overviews experiment in Google Search Labs won't necessarily remove AI Overviews from regular Google searches.
Want an AI-free search engine? Try DuckDuckGo
If you're really tired of AI showing up in searches, there's another option worth knowing about. DuckDuckGo offers a dedicated search experience called DuckDuckGo No-AI. You can access it at noai.duckduckgo.com. According to DuckDuckGo, this version removes its AI-assisted answers and AI feature suggestions. It also filters AI-generated images from image searches. The company acknowledges that AI-image filtering may not catch everything. You can use the website directly without changing your phone's default search engine.
Change your default search engine on iPhone or iPad
If you want to use a different search engine more regularly, Apple makes switching easy.
These instructions apply to iOS 27 and iPadOS 27.
- Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
- Tap Search.
- Select Search Engine.
- Choose your preferred search engine.
- Tap Back to save your selection.
Keep in mind that choosing regular DuckDuckGo as your default doesn't automatically activate its separate No-AI experience. You can visit the No-AI website whenever you want to use it.
MALICIOUS BROWSER EXTENSIONS CAN HIJACK AI ASSISTANTS
How to remove Siri suggestions from the top of iPhone Search
Now let's talk about another feature that can get annoying. Ever swipe down on your iPhone's Home Screen to search for something and immediately see a collection of suggested apps? Those suggestions come from Siri, which can recommend apps based on your activity. Maybe you appreciate the shortcuts. Or maybe you would rather see a clean search screen until you start typing. Apple lets you turn those suggestions off.
Hide Siri's suggested apps
- Open Settings on your iPhone.
- Tap Siri.
- Select Suggestions.
- Turn off Suggest Apps Before Searching.
Your iPhone should stop showing those suggested apps before you begin searching. While you're there, you can also disable Show in App Library if you don't want suggestions appearing there. You can turn off Show When Sharing if you'd rather not see suggested contacts or other Siri recommendations when sharing content. These controls change where Siri suggestions appear. They don't disable Siri itself.
Turn off Siri AI or switch back to Siri Classic
Apple's more conversational Siri AI is available in beta on supported iPhones running iOS 27.
If you enabled it and would rather return to the previous Siri experience, Apple provides that option.
- Open Settings.
- Tap Siri.
- Scroll down and select Turn Off Siri.
- Tap Turn Off Siri again to confirm.
- To restore the previous assistant, tap Turn On Siri.
- Select Siri Classic.
If you don't want to use Siri at all, leave it turned off.
Important: Siri AI requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhone, including the iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and iPhone 16 models or later. Availability also depends on language and region. Older iPhones won't have the same Siri AI options.
Stop Apple Intelligence from summarizing your notifications
There's another AI feature that may bother some iPhone owners. Apple Intelligence can summarize notifications, messages and emails. While that may sound convenient, AI summaries can leave out details or misunderstand what someone wrote. If you'd rather read the original text, you can disable those features.
Turn off AI notification summaries
- Open Settings.
- Tap Notifications.
- Select Summarize Notifications.
- Turn off Summarize Notifications.
Disable AI summaries in Messages
- Open Settings.
- Tap Apps.
- Select Messages.
- Turn off Summarize Messages.
You can also open Settings, tap Apps, select Mail and turn off Summarize Message Previews.
These options are available on compatible devices running iOS 27. Disabling them won't affect your ability to receive regular messages or notifications.
How to turn off unwanted Galaxy AI features on Samsung phones
Samsung has been adding AI tools to its Galaxy phones, including features that help with calls or photos. Some can be useful. However, you may have no interest in having AI analyze information or suggest actions throughout your day. Fortunately, Samsung provides individual controls for many Galaxy AI features.
Disable Galaxy AI features you don't use
- Open Settings on your Samsung Galaxy.
- Tap Galaxy AI.
- On some devices, just scroll down, while on others tap Advanced features, then Galaxy AI or Awesome Intelligence.
- Select a feature you don't want, such as Note assist.
- Turn off the available switches for that feature.
- Repeat for any other features you want to disable.
Samsung notes that some AI features are built into the operating system and cannot be disabled. Also, available controls vary depending on your Galaxy model and software version. Samsung recommends restarting your device after disabling Galaxy AI features to ensure the changes take effect.
Remove Now brief from your Galaxy Lock screen
Samsung's Now brief can show personalized information about your day, including upcoming events and other recommendations. If you don't want those briefings appearing on your Lock screen, try this.
- Open Settings.
- Tap Lock screen and AOD or Lock screen.
- Select Now bar.
- Find Now brief among the available options.
- Turn Now brief off.
You can also open Settings, tap Galaxy AI and select Now brief to adjust the information included in your briefings. On supported devices, you can disable Show Now brief while phone is locked. Keep in mind that removing Now brief from the Lock screen doesn't necessarily remove the feature from your Galaxy phone.
Keep Galaxy AI processing on your device
If you're concerned about information being sent to the cloud for AI processing, Samsung offers another option.
- Open Settings.
- Tap Galaxy AI.
- Find Process data only on device.
- Turn the switch On.
This tells supported Samsung Galaxy AI features to process data locally instead of using cloud processing. However, some AI features require an internet connection and may become unavailable. Also, this control applies to Samsung's supported Galaxy AI features. It doesn't prevent every app on your phone from connecting to its own cloud services.
Disable Circle to Search on Samsung Galaxy
Circle to Search lets you search for something displayed on your screen. However, you might accidentally activate it while navigating your phone.
You can switch it off.
- Open Settings.
- Tap Display.
- Select Navigation bar.
- Turn off Circle to Search.
How to turn off unwanted AI features on Google Pixel
Google Pixel phones have their own AI features designed to anticipate what you might need. Depending on your device, these can include Magic Cue or Google's newer Proactive Assistance. Both can provide suggestions using information from supported apps, depending on your settings and permissions. However, you may prefer to keep those suggestions out of your conversations.
Turn off Magic Cue suggestions on Pixel 10
On supported Pixel 10 models, Magic Cue can display helpful information while you're using your phone. For example, it might surface flight details during a conversation.
Here's how to adjust it.
- Open Settings.
- Tap Magic Cue.
- Review the available suggestion types.
- Turn off any suggestions you don't want.
- Tap Base suggestions on apps.
- Turn off Use data from recent screen activity if you don't want Magic Cue using that information.
- Turn off Use foundational data if you don't want it using supported Google Account information.
Magic Cue is available only on supported Pixel models and in supported regions.
Disable Proactive Assistance on Pixel 11
Google introduced a newer feature called Proactive Assistance on Pixel 11 devices in supported markets, including the United States. When enabled, it can use screen information and notifications to provide suggestions. It may also use information from connected apps and location data.
If you'd rather skip those suggestions, Google gives you a direct switch.
- Open Settings.
- Tap Proactive Assistance.
- Turn off Use Proactive Assistance.
You can also tap Connected apps to decide which apps the feature can access. Proactive Assistance is opt-in, so you won't necessarily see suggestions unless you've enabled it.
Disable Circle to Search on Google Pixel
Circle to Search is also available on supported Pixel phones. If you find yourself activating it accidentally, you can turn it off.
- Open Settings.
- Search for Circle to Search.
- Tap Circle to Search.
- Turn it Off.
Google also lists the feature under System on supported Pixel devices.
Stop Pixel Screenshots from using AI
On supported Pixel phones, the Pixel Screenshots app can generate summaries and help you search through saved screenshots. If you don't want those AI features processing screenshot content, Google provides an option to disable them.
- Open the Pixel Screenshots app.
- Tap Settings in the upper-right corner.
- Turn off Search your screenshots with AI.
If you also want to delete previously generated AI information, select Delete all AI summaries and metadata. That last option removes the generated summaries and metadata associated with the feature. Only select it if you're comfortable deleting that information.
ANTI-SCAM SETTINGS ON IPHONE, PIXEL AND GALAXY YOU SHOULD CHECK
How to stop Gemini from popping up on Android phones
Finally, there's Gemini, Google's AI assistant. On many Android phones, Gemini can launch when you say "Hey Google" or press and hold the power button. If you're tired of activating it accidentally, there are several ways to stop that behavior.
Turn off Hey Google in Gemini
- Open the Gemini app.
- Tap Menu, then your profile picture or initial.
- Select Settings.
- Tap Talk to Gemini hands-free.
- Turn off Hey Google.
This prevents Gemini from responding to the wake phrase. You can still open the app manually if you want to use it.
Stop Gemini from opening with the power button
On Google Pixel and other supported Android phones:
- Open Settings.
- Tap System.
- Select Gestures.
- Tap Press & hold power button.
- Choose a different action instead of launching the digital assistant.
On Samsung Galaxy phones, search Settings for Side button to find the equivalent shortcut control.
Remove Gemini as your default digital assistant
If you'd rather stop using Gemini as your phone's main assistant, Google provides another option.
- Open Settings.
- Tap Apps.
- Select Default apps.
- Tap Digital assistant app.
- Select Default digital assistant app.
- Choose None if you don't want a default assistant.
You can also switch back to Google Assistant through the Gemini app on supported devices. Removing Gemini as the default assistant won't necessarily uninstall the Gemini app or erase any previous Gemini activity.
Can you completely remove AI from your phone?
There isn't one universal switch that removes every AI feature from an iPhone or Android device. Some functions are built directly into the software. Others come from individual apps or online services.
However, you can make a noticeable difference by disabling the features you never use. And remember, turning off an AI assistant won't automatically remove AI Overviews from Google Search. Those require separate controls. If you're concerned about how AI services collect information, we've also covered how to opt out of AI data collection in popular apps.
