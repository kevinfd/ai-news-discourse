# Meta: Strong Quarter, Bright Outlook, But What About The Dividend?

Seeking Alpha · Dividend Collection Agency · 2026-10-28

Meta: Strong Quarter, Bright Outlook, But What About The Dividend?
Summary
- Meta Platforms, Inc. remains a long-term Buy, but near-term dividend growth will be minimal due to elevated capital expenditures and AI investments.
- Q3 earnings exceeded expectations, with EPS at $7.40 (ex-litigation), revenue at $64.6B, and user engagement at all-time highs.
- META's CapEx guidance for 2026 was raised to $135–$145B, over 100% higher than last year, pressuring free cash flow and limiting shareholder returns.
- While free cash flow is under pressure now, I expect AI-driven growth to enable meaningful dividend increases in three to five years.
