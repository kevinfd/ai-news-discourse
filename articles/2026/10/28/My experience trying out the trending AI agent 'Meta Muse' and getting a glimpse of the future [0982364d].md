# My experience trying out the trending AI agent 'Meta Muse' and getting a glimpse of the future

note · Sfz · 2026-10-28

My experience trying out the trending AI agent 'Meta Muse' and getting a glimpse of the future
 In the articles so far, we have tried out basic ways to use ChatGPT one by one, such as creating text, summarizing, and breaking down tasks. 
Now that I've gotten used to the basics, I had the opportunity to try out a new AI that everyone around me has been talking about as 'something amazing.' 
That is the personal AI agent announced by Meta, Muse. 
The name has an artistic ring to it, but when I actually tried it, it felt like a step beyond the 'AI that just answers questions' we've known until now—it truly felt like a partner and secretary that handles tasks for you, and it was so interesting that I wanted to share that experience! 
What makes 'Muse' different from the AI we've used before?
Conventional AI (like ChatGPT) has primarily been based on a 'Q&A' or 'chat format' where we send a question and get an answer back. 
Of course, that alone is quite useful, but this 'Muse' is built not just to answer questions, but as a personal AI agent that thinks for itself and operates emails and apps to carry out tasks. 
The biggest difference from previous chat AIs is whether it works in the background to support you without you having to ask. 
Conventional AI (Chat type): Only works when you input a prompt and say 'tell me' (a partner for searching or consulting) 
Future AI (Agent type/Muse): Checks emails and schedules on behalf of the user, and acts on its own to make suggestions and preparations (an active partner) 
This difference between 'passive' and 'proactive' was much larger than I imagined when I actually used it. 
When I actually tried out Muse, the part that gave me goosebumps and made me think, 'Oh, this is the future!', was how smart it was at processing emails and making suggestions. 
In our daily lives, we receive a lot of emails, from work correspondence to notifications and newsletters. 
Just reading through them all takes a lot of time, and some of them end up piling up as things that 'I need to reply to, but tend to put off...' 
So, when I connected my mailbox to Muse... a surprising experience awaited me. 
・It automatically organizes incoming emails and summarizes the key points. 
・It summarizes them into an easy-to-understand list, saying, 'Here are the 3 emails that arrived today that require your attention.' 
・It even suggests 'how to reply.' 
・It doesn't just summarize; it proactively prepares a draft reply, asking, 'Would you like to reply to this email like this? I've created a draft.' 
・It handles important scheduling on your behalf. 
・It links with your schedule and suggests, 'You are free at this time next week, shall I book an appointment?' 
When I experienced this sequence of events, I was moved, thinking, 'Wait, it's like having an excellent personal secretary right by my side!' 
3 points that make this different from other daily AIs!
I will summarize the 3 'decisive differences in daily life' that I found after comparing useful chat AIs like ChatGPT with agent-type AIs like Muse. 
① Not 'waiting for instructions' but 'proactively making suggestions'
Chat AI only answers what you ask, but Muse looks at your daily data (emails, calendar, etc.) and proactively acts, saying, 'Did you forget this?' or 'I've taken care of this for you.' 
② It works across the walls of apps and tasks
It doesn't just help you think of text; it handles 'practical steps' like checking emails, adjusting schedules, and organizing information. All we do is the final confirmation (approval) of 'Is this okay?' 
③ It remembers your preferences and context
You don't have to explain the situation from scratch every time, as the interaction proceeds based on past exchanges and your lifestyle, making the communication incredibly smooth. 
Summary
In this article, I summarized my impressions of actually trying out the trending AI agent 'Meta Muse.' 
・Muse is not just an AI that answers questions, but a 'personal AI agent' that organizes emails and adjusts schedules on its own and makes suggestions. 
・Seeing it summarize incoming emails and even prepare replies is just like having an 'excellent personal secretary.' 
・From 'chat AI that waits for instructions' to 'agent AI that acts proactively,' the user experience of AI has entered a new stage. 
The speed of technological evolution is truly surprising, but if we think of it as gaining a 'reliable partner that supports our tedious routines and tasks,' our daily lives seem like they will become much more enjoyable. 
If you have the chance to try it, please try talking to it and saying, 'Organize my emails and tell me what I need to do next!'
