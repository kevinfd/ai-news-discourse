# You're saving one minute and now I can't know if I'm talking a human

Hacker News · scoofy · 2026-10-08

I write a golf blog. Recently, it's been getting a nontrivial amount of attention from a very niche section of the nerd-golf community. I've been getting a small number of emails and message from people because of this. This is how it has been going: Me: Oh, this is a nice email. Wait, something seems off. Let me check this in Pangram... Oh, 100% AI predicted. I respond: Hey, thanks for the note. Just FYI, Pangram doesn't really like your writing style. Them: Ahh, you got me. I use AI to help me write. Blah, blah... --- I really don't know what to do here. I still don't know if I'm talking to a human. If you are messaging me because you like my writing and you're using AI to write these short messages for you, I don't know what to tell you. Please stop. It takes two minutes to write a quick message... I really don't care if there are typos or it's a bit awkward. But if you're using AI to facilitate this I'm not going to know if I'm ever talking to a person! And, there is a nontrivial chance I'm going to report your account for spam, which I really don't want to do if you're a human and a fan of my writing. Using AI for these kinds of short messages is poisoning the well in ways that many people don't seem to understand. It's the strangest feeling... getting a flattering note, and ending up not even knowing if anyone actually read my blog in the first place.
