# The RTX 5090 Isn't Gone, It's In Chinese Labs, Where Technicians Rip Off Its Die And 32GB VRAM For AI Servers

Wccftech · chemist_slime · 2026-10-24

If you thought the RTX 5090 was really discontinued, then you need to see what's going on in these Chinese labs.
Huge Inventory of RTX 5090 GPUs Appear in a Chinese Factory, Where Technicians Remove Their GPU Chip and VRAM to Deploy on Custom Boards
This isn't new. We have already seen a huge inventory of the flagship Blackwell RTX 50 series GPUs being turned into AI GPUs in China last year as well. NVIDIA markets the RTX 5090 a lot for its AI capabilities, and it remains a popular AI card in China. We have heard reports that NVIDIA turns a blind eye to a huge stock of RTX 5090 entering the Chinese market, and to this day, the banned GPU keeps entering the region.
What's interesting is that the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 has supposedly been discontinued. Even though NVIDIA recently refuted these rumors, it appears that the RTX 5090 is now solely produced to fulfill the demands of AI servers. The GPU comes with a good 32 GB of fast GDDR7 memory and excellent AI capabilities, which is why it keeps getting turned into dedicated AI GPUs.
In a video shared by HKEPC, we see hundreds of consumer GeForce RTX 5090 cards sitting on shelves in a Chinese lab/factory. These are from various vendors, and we even see the premium ROG Astral RTX 5090, which currently sells for $10,000. The report also suggests that the Founders Edition cards are available there, which are being turned into AI GPUs after the technicians rip off the GPU die and VRAM chips from the board.
The GPU die and VRAM chips are then deployed on a custom PCB, which is coupled with a dual-slot blower-style cooler. This is to accommodate the cards easily inside AI server machines where there are space constraints. So, that's where your GPU is ending up these days, and NVIDIA is likely aware of such factories. However, it appears to be more profitable for the company than selling the card to gamers, and it won't likely stop anytime soon.
