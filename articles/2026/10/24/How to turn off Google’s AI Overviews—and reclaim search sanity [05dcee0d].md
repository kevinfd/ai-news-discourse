# How to turn off Google’s AI Overviews—and reclaim search sanity

Fast Company · JR Raphael · 2026-10-24

We’ve got two competing realities when it comes to tracking down info online these days:
- Getting answers has never been easier, between all the artificial intelligence chatbots in front of us and the unavoidable smearing of “AI Overview” answers at the top of regular Google search results.
- Getting accurate information has never been more challenging—thanks to the fact that all that AI-generated gobbledygook is flat-out wrong a shockingly high amount of the time (anywhere from 10% on the lower end to 50% and up, when it comes to more niche and specialized info).
To use some highly technical terminology, that ain’t great.
BUT: There’s an easy, powerful workaround to skip all those accuracy-challenged internet search “shortcuts” and go back to the basic, traditional web search of yesteryear—without any AI and without any costs or compromises.
Today’s Cool Tool is one you’ll absolutely want to hang onto.

Your AI-free Google search workaround
Few folks are aware—or remember—but back when Google first started gunking up its search results with all sorts of AI drivel, it built in a detour to avoid all of that and get pure, simple, clean web results without any of the added noise.
➜ That workaround is still available, if you know how to activate it. And it does more than just strip out the AI Overview additions. It also removes shopping boxes, info boxes, related news suggestions, and pretty much all the other distractions that litter search results these days.
