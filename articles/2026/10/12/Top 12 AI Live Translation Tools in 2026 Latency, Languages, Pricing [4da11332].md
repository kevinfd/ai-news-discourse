# Top 12 AI Live Translation Tools in 2026: Latency, Languages, Pricing

MarkTechPost · Michal Sutter · 2026-10-12

AI live translation tools turn speech into translated captions or speech while people are still talking. The market now spans consumer apps, meeting add-ons, event platforms and developer APIs. We checked 12 tools against official product, pricing and documentation pages. 

 What is an AI live translation tool? 

 It listens, transcribes, translates and outputs captions or synthetic speech in near real time. Most products chain speech recognition, machine translation and text-to-speech. Captions suit webinars. Speech output suits two-way calls. 

 The 12 tools, grouped by use case 

 Real-time conversations and developer APIs 

 1. Palabra.ai : Palabra.ai does two-way speech-to-speech translation with automatic voice cloning and captions. It claims under 1 second latency and offers a WebRTC and WebSocket API. API speech-to-speech costs $0.04 per minute ; Meetings plans start at $60 per month. 

 2. Gemini Live Translate : Google’s Gemini 3.5 Live Translate covers 70+ languages and preserves intonation and pacing. Google says output runs a few seconds behind the speaker. It works in the Translate app with any headphones, and in the Gemini Live API preview. 

 3. Apple Live Translation : Apple’s Live Translation plays translated speech through AirPods and runs entirely on device . It supports 10 language variants. It needs recent AirPods and an Apple Intelligence iPhone on iOS 26. 

 Inside meeting platforms 

 4. DeepL Voice : DeepL Voice for Meetings translates 40+ languages into voice and subtitles in Teams, Zoom and Google Meet. Meeting data is deleted when the call ends. DeepL Pro plans include 30 Voice minutes per user monthly. 

 5. Microsoft Teams Interpreter agent : Interpreter is a Copilot agent for speech-to-speech interpretation in Teams, with optional voice simulation. Admin docs list 9 languages; the support page lists 10. A Copilot license includes 20 hours per user monthly. 

 6. JotMe : JotMe captures Zoom, Teams, Meet and Webex audio without a bot. Its 200+ languages are translation targets; fast mode offers 13. Pro costs $10 per month billed annually . 

 7. Talo : Talo joins Zoom, Google Meet or Teams with translated voice and captions in 60+ languages. Pro costs $80 per month billed annually for 400 minutes. 

 Events and webinars 

 8. Wordly : Wordly sells conference AI interpretation with captions and AI audio across 3,000+ language pairs. Per its FAQ , audio plays after each sentence ends. Packages start at about $1,500 for 10 hours . 

 9. Interprefy : Interprefy combines human interpreters with Interprefy AI captions and speech. Its knowledge base lists 82 fully supported languages. Interprefy Now turns attendee phones into receivers. Pricing is quote-based. 

 10. KUDO : KUDO pairs an AI speech translator with 12,000+ human interpreters. The AI side lists 77 languages , up to 32 per meeting. Pricing is quote-based. 

 11. Boostlingo : Boostlingo targets healthcare, legal and events. Its AI products cover 130+ languages , backed by human interpreters. It states 90 to 98% AI accuracy and HIPAA compliance. 

 12. Maestra Live : Maestra Live adds live captions, translation and dubbing to Zoom, Meet, Teams, OBS and vMix in 125+ languages. Premium costs $79 per month ; dubbing starts at $159.

 Which AI live translation tool should you pick? 

 Two-way calls or product integration: Palabra.ai or the Gemini Live API. 

 Travel and face-to-face: Google Translate or Apple Live Translation. 

 Daily meetings on one platform: DeepL Voice, Teams Interpreter, JotMe or Talo. 

 Conferences and webinars: Wordly, Interprefy or KUDO. 

 Regulated, high-stakes sessions: Boostlingo or KUDO, with human interpreters on standby. 

 Key Takeaways 

 Gemini 3.5 Live Translate brings speech-to-speech in 70+ languages to any headphones. 

 Teams Interpreter covers only 9 to 10 languages but ships with Copilot. 

 Event platforms like Wordly, Interprefy and KUDO stay mostly quote-based. 

 Language counts are not apples to apples, so pilot on your own pairs.
