# Claude Projects Slaps

Reddit · macaronianddeeez · 2026-10-12

See title. I have a lot of AI subscriptions and have a pretty well integrated system that includes a home built control later app that lets me direct and leverage agent teams from mobile, using different harnesses. I like to use different models for different things, and previously I had most of my life pumping through OpenAI. But Claude projects has really taken the majority of my professional energy and removed it from my other systems. It just works so well, and for an adhd brain like mine the organization layer it provides is fucking excellent. I still use and maintain a Hermes and I’m continuing to build and improve it, and I still use Astra chat for strategy brainstorming, but man, all of my delivery work I am starting to pump through Claude projects and it just works super well. Kudos to Anthropic for the way they built it
