# KAM Theory and the post-modern market

Reddit · Real_Middle_4128 · 2026-10-12

If the possible emergence of AGI is the incoming pendular flip of our level of consciousness, then I pray that the dissonance of the hand of the market will steer humanity into an island of stability and order, inside that new level of chaos, even though a flip would have then happened in our current systemic locality. What do you think of this gibberishly-sound physical hope? Perhaps this bubble has to go on until the singularity, if it is to happen, to maximize dissonance in the system and protect the system itself.
