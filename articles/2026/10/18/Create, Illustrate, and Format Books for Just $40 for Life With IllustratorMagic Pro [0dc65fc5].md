TL;DR: Create up to 1,000 AI-illustrated books with consistent characters, print-ready exports, and commercial rights for just $39.99 (MSRP $499).
Generating an AI image is easy enough. Keeping the same character recognizable across an entire illustrated book is a huge challenge. IllustratorMagic Pro Plan lifetime access is on sale for $39.99 (MSRP $499) and is specifically designed to handle that process from beginning to end.
You start by describing your story and characters, then choose from watercolor, 3D cartoon, clay animation, or sketch styles. IllustratorMagic then generates the story, illustrations, and page layout together while keeping characters consistent from page to page.
