# Former Huawei engineer’s robot AI startup Gewu Tongzhi raises nearly RMB 100 million

TechNode · TechNode Feed · 2026-10-18

Hangzhou-based robot AI startup Gewu Tongzhi has completed an angel funding round of nearly RMB 100 million, according to reports published on October 18. Investors include Hongfu Asset Management, Peak Investment, Hangzhou Capital and Hangzhou Talent Group.
Founded in 2026, the company develops AI for dexterous robotic manipulation, combining world models with learning from physical interactions. Founder and CEO Cai Jia previously worked at Huawei’s research laboratories and automotive business, participating in the development of its ADS driving systems.
The funding will support model training, hardware development, recruitment and real-world data collection. Gewu Tongzhi is working with customers in textiles, optical modules and automotive components on proof-of-concept projects, with its first delivery expected by the end of 2026.
