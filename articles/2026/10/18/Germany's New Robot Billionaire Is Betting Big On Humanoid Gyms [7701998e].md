# Germany's New Robot Billionaire Is Betting Big On Humanoid Gyms

Forbes · Nina Bambysheva, Forbes Staff · 2026-10-18

David Reger’s $7 billion Neura Robotics wants to be the Apple of physical AI, building not only an army of industrial and humanoid robots, but also their operating software system and the “gyms” where they will train. Nvidia, Amazon and Tether are onboard to the tune of $1.4 billion.
