# How CoreWeave’s Debt Binge Brought a Moment of Reckoning

The Information · Dakin Campbell · 2026-10-20

In early 2023, Blackstone CEO Steve Schwarzman gathered with Jas Khaira, the company’s lead AI investor, and other top executives at the finance giant’s Park Avenue headquarters in Manhattan for a debate: Should they give billions of dollars to a little-known data center developer—CoreWeave—that they had turned down just a few months earlier? CoreWeave had gotten Blackstone to return to the table after landing a multiyear, multibillion-dollar contract to sell cloud computing services to Microsoft. It needed $2.3 billion to buy Nvidia GPUs and secure a data center to house them.
