# OpenAI acquires Orchestra Bio to connect research AI with drug-development operations

RuntimeWire · ryanmerket · 2026-10-20

OpenAI acquires Orchestra Bio to connect research AI with drug-development operations
Founder Ashoka Rajendra drew on biology research and life-sciences software work to build the platform; Orchestra had reportedly raised $20 million before the deal.
By Ryan Merket · Published · Updated
Primary source: X
Why it matters
OpenAI is extending Rosalind from scientific research tools into drug-program operations, betting that agents coordinating studies, vendors and timelines can make its life-sciences offering useful beyond the lab.
OpenAI has acquired Orchestra Bio, bringing founder Ashoka Rajendra (@ashokaraj_) and his drug-development operations platform into Rosalind, OpenAI's life-sciences AI business. The acquisition, announced on October 20th in OpenAI's post on X, puts workflow software alongside the models and research tools OpenAI has been building for scientists.…
