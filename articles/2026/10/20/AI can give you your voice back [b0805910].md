# AI can give you your voice back

Science News · Laura Sanders · 2026-10-20

AI can give you your voice back
When people face losing the ability to talk, they need alternative ways to communicate. One way people preserve their voice is by banking it. The process has improved in recent years, but it still produces a voice that can sound unnatural. Jules Rodriguez did this as amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, or ALS, left his own voice diminished. But he wasn’t happy with the sound.
New technology that uses artificial intelligence allowed Jules to create a voice clone, one that sounds like his old voice. With breaths, intonations and even stutters, these voice clones capture the quirks of a human voice. Jules’ voice clone was so realistic that his wife cried the first time she heard it. 
In this episode, hear how AI-powered voice clones are changing the conversation around how people connect. That includes a musician named Patrick Darling who uses his voice clone to sing and perform songs.
