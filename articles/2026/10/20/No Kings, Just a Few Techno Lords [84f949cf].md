# No Kings, Just a Few Techno Lords

Decodingvibes · bakraofwallstreet · 2026-10-20

If people keep relying on AI models served from cloud infrastructure for critical work, platforms will eventually control the means of production. Given the widespread use of AI in every domain (or the attempt to use it), it is not hard to imagine a future where Time magazine would cheesily select Claude as its person of the year, or whatever. It would indicate that just as social media used to be the channel through which we connected, AI platforms are becoming the channel through which we work and generate economic value.
