# Introducing the TechPulse app: All the internet and tech news you need, without the cost

Mashable · Alesha Williams Boyd · 2026-10-20

Introducing the TechPulse app: All the internet and tech news you need, without the cost
Tech and the internet today – from AI and our devices to TikTok trends and the creator economy – are evolving at a pace faster than most people can keep up with.
Every day, our technology and the digital culture that surrounds it are shifting and changing, shaping how we work, how we play, how we live and how we connect with one another.
It can be overwhelming for anyone to digest. To help navigate it all, we need accurate and objective reporting, from expert journalists we can rely upon and trust.
That’s where Mashable, our sister sites in CNET Group, and our new TechPulse app come in.
With the launch of TechPulse, fans of Mashable, CNET, PCMag, Lifehacker, ZDNET, and ExtremeTech will be able to curate and control how they consume their tech and digital culture news, reviews, videos, tips and perspectives, from some of the best brands in the business.
Download the app to:
- Stay ahead of what's new and next with tech, digital culture, space and entertainment news, reviews, original reporting and features from Mashable.
- Make tech buying easy with lab-tested and hands-on reviews from experts across our publications.
- Get the latest breaking tech news from reporters with objective takes on what you need to know.
- Streamline your daily routine and unlock practical hacks and science-based productivity tips from Lifehacker.
- Track disruptive professional tech breakthroughs and emerging industry trends from ZDNET.
- And get the most from your tech and gadgets with product updates and guides from our teams.
More good news: It’s free and doesn’t require a subscription.
It’s written and curated by human experts and journalists. You can hand-pick which publications appear in your unified feed for seamless navigation across your favorite sites. And it makes tech buying easy and safe.
In an algorithm-driven era – one in which it’s getting increasingly difficult to tell fact from fiction and to find the news you need – you can’t afford to go without TechPulse.
Take charge of your tech news experience and explore what matters to you: Get the app today from the Apple app store and on Google Play.
