# Nvidia Moves to Back GPU Cloud Provider Firmus After Investors Balk at Its IPO

The Information · Phoebe Liu · 2026-10-20

Nvidia is in talks to invest hundreds of millions of dollars in Australian AI cloud provider Firmus, which abruptly pulled its initial public offering early this month, according to people familiar with the talks. 

 The discussion indicates that Nvidia is likely to continue backing a group of unproven firms known as neoclouds that is facing financial pressure after they stacked up on debt to purchase Nvidia graphics processing units for data centers.
