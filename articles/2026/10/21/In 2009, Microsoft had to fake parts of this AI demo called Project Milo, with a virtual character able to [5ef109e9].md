# In 2009, Microsoft had to fake parts of this AI demo called Project Milo, with a virtual character able to converse and interact with you and even comment on your drawing. Today this is completely mundane capability.

Youtu · Anen-o-me · 2026-10-21

Many people at this time thought it was a legitimate breakthrough in AI capability. However it was never released, was used to hype the Microsoft gaming console of that day & Kinect the 3D video system it used, and was soon exposed as mostly faked for the demo. But today, we can do all of this easily enough. Even video chatting with the AI, even drawing pictures together. Would be ironic if Microsoft resurrected Milo for the first AI-driven videogames that are surely on the horizon.
