# Huang Says All Industries Across Europe Need to Adopt AI

Bloomberg · 2026-10-21

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang pushed Europe to adopt artificial intelligence more quickly. “For Europe to benefit from AI, and to have really created an AI ecosystem and AI economy, you need all of the end markets, the end industries to adopt AI,” Huang said Wednesday at his company’s GTC conference in Berlin.
