# Life360s powerful pet GPS tracker now comes with a free membership — how to claim

Mashable · Soumya Kumar · 2026-10-21

Life360's powerful pet GPS tracker now comes with a free membership — how to claim
An advertiser paid for editorial consideration of this deal. Our editorial experts vetted the deal based on our independent expertise. Because we determined that the deal will save money for our readers, we wrote the content.
TL;DR: Life360's $99 Pet GPS bundle includes a free year of Silver membership for new and free-tier users. The new $99 setup slashes first-year tracking costs while giving pet owners access to real-time location data, a 100M+ member search network, and AI photo-matching with Petco Love Lost.
You notice the back gate is propped open, and your dog is nowhere to be found. That sudden panic is something every pet owner has experienced or dreads experiencing. But keeping tabs on your four-legged escape artist does not have to be complicated or expensive.
Life360 expanded its family safety ecosystem with the Life360 Pet GPS, a dedicated collar attachment that lets you monitor your pet along with your family members on the same in-app map. Starting today, Life360 is offering a $99 bundle that includes the GPS device and a full $0 introductory year of its Silver membership for new and free-tier users.
The hardware uses GPS and LTE connectivity to stream real-time location updates directly to your phone. You can draw custom virtual boundaries around your house or yard. If your pet crosses that line, an instant escape alert is sent to your entire Life360 Circle, so everyone is notified immediately.
Battery life lasts up to 14 days on a single charge. If the power dips below 6%, the device enters Reserve Mode, switching off high-power GPS and relying on Bluetooth updates through the Tile network. This low-power fallback keeps the tracker findable for up to six months, so a low battery does not cut off your search.
Community search tools and AI matching
Beyond live location updates, the device links into multiple recovery networks if your pet strays too far. Triggering a Lost Pet Alert broadcasts a notification with your pet's photo, details, and verified phone number to nearby Life360 members aged 18 and older, keeping the alert pinned to the local map for 72 hours.
Through a partnership with the national nonprofit Petco Love Lost, the system also uses AI photo-matching technology to cross-reference lost-pet reports with records from local shelters and community networks. Each setup also includes a scannable collar tag, allowing anyone who finds your pet to scan the tag with any smartphone — no app download required — to get your contact details immediately.
How to claim the deal
- Purchase the tracker — Head over to Life360's site or Amazon to buy the Life360 Pet GPS.
- Set up a Pet Profile — Download the Life360 app and build your pet's profile using a clear, well-lit photo containing only your pet, along with their breed, weight, and your verified phone number.
- Activate Silver — New and free-tier users will be prompted inside the app during device setup to claim their $0 introductory year of Silver.
Your first 12 months of active tracking are fully covered by the initial $99 purchase. After year one, the Silver plan automatically renews at $99.99/year (billed annually), though you can cancel at any time in your account settings before the renewal date.
Grab the Life360 Pet GPS bundle on Life360's site or Amazon, and never worry about an open gate again.
