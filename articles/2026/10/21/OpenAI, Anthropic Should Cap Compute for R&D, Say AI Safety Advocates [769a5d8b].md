# OpenAI, Anthropic Should Cap Compute for R&D, Say AI Safety Advocates

The Information · Tiffany Li · 2026-10-21

Ideas are flowing fast and furious on how AI labs can “pace” AI development so safeguards keep up with the technology’s smarts. Last week, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman laid out three scenarios he said have been considered in the industry, including merging all of the AI labs into a single entity to develop new AI safely or creating a global regulatory body like the International Atomic Energy Agency .
