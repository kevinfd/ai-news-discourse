# This simple tool fixes Google Calendar’s most frustrating flaw

Fast Company · JR Raphael · 2026-10-21

The problem with most of the whiz-bang AI jibber-jabber we hear about these days, practically speaking, is that it generally tends to be a solution in search of a problem. It’s technology for the sake of technology—something that exists for the very sake of existing and gets thrown at every virtual wall to see what might stick.
The type of tech tool that really makes a difference is the kind that’s carefully crafted from the get-go to solve a real-world pain point and make our lives easier in a specific, tangible way.
The tool I have for you today does exactly that—and it isn’t the first time it’s wowed me and made a very real impact on my life.

A smarter way to share schedules
Now, fair warning: If you’ve been following my ramblings for long, the tool we’re talking about today may well sound familiar. That’s because, as I alluded to a second ago, it’s been impressing me and playing a genuine role in my life for many a month now.
➜ But the capability I want to show you is a brand-new feature that just arrived in the app a matter of days ago.
📅 The app is called Agenda Hero. When we’ve chatted about it before, we’ve focused on its ability to take anything you feed into it—screenshots, photos, PDFs, even entire paragraphs of pasted-over text—then extract all the relevant info and turn it into a collection of perfectly formatted events for your preferred calendar.
