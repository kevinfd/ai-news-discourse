# [AI Marketing Daily] Summary of Important Trends for 2026-10-13

note · べっく · 2026-10-13

[AI Marketing Daily] Summary of Important Trends for 2026-10-13
Issue Date: 2026-10-13 | Summary of Important AI Marketing Trends from Yesterday and Today
[Today's 3 Major Trends and Key Messages]
1. From 'Tool Introduction and Trial' to 'Business Results and ROI Creation'
As seen in the practices of leading companies like Panasonic Connect, LINE Yahoo, and MISUMI, AI utilization has moved past PoCs and one-off efficiency improvements, fully transitioning into a phase directly linked to management profits and business outcomes. As MISUMI President Shimizu describes in his vision of 'assigning employee IDs to AI,' the key to success lies in building systems that deeply integrate AI into organizational structures and business processes to guarantee solid ROI.
2. The Tectonic Shift in Search and Customer Acquisition: Shifting from SEO to 'GEO, AEO, and AIO (AI Search Optimization)'
With the rapid adoption of generative AI search (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews), traditional keyword SEO is losing its effectiveness. With the release of GEO reports in the Asia-Pacific region and major real estate companies beginning AIO measures, the 'primary information structuring strategy'—designed to be selected, cited, and recommended by LLMs as a trusted source—has become the most critical agenda determining corporate success.
3. Balancing Autonomous Generation of Advertising Creatives with 'AI Content Trustworthiness'
AI utilization in the creative field is making dramatic progress, such as video ads with over 50% completion rates created using generative AI by Dentsu Digital. At the same time, as Optimizely warns of 'declining consumer trust in marketing due to the surge in AI usage,' there is a strong demand to avoid brand damage caused by mass AI generation while ensuring transparency and unique human value.
[Thematic Trends and In-depth Commentary]
1. Management and Organizational Strategy: Organizations that 'Produce Results' rather than just 'Use' AI, and Upskilling
While corporate AI investment is expanding, many companies are hitting a wall where 'AI implementation is not leading to company-wide profit or corporate value improvement.' MISUMI has begun structural reforms by assigning employee IDs to AI to handle autonomous tasks, and companies like Panasonic Connect and LINE Yahoo are rushing to build systems where every employee who understands the business can create and master AI. Rather than tool operation training, a 'systematic upskilling strategy' that improves prompt design, data interpretation, and the validity assessment of AI output will determine corporate survival 10 years from now.

2. Search and Next-Generation Customer Touchpoints: Transition to GEO/AEO/AIO and Field-Driven Engineering (FDE)
Because search behavior has changed drastically from 'browsing blue links on a search engine' to 'asking generative AI for recommendations through dialogue,' corporate web marketing strategies are being fundamentally overturned. Latest surveys show that about 10% of the real estate industry has already begun AIO measures, and market interest is surging as GEO reports on brand visibility are being released in the Asia-Pacific region. Furthermore, systems like FDE (Field-Driven Engineering) advocated by Hitachi, where engineers are stationed alongside marketing teams to implement AI solutions in an agile manner, are becoming the key to winning in the rapidly changing AI search environment.

3. Advertising, Creative & Brand Trust: Video AI and Brand Safety
Dentsu Digital has demonstrated high engagement results, with a video completion rate of over 50%, through the innovation of its video advertising production process using generative AI. However, at the same time, as Optimizely, a leader in A/B testing, warns, the abuse of AI-generated content is a double-edged sword that can lead to consumer distrust in advertising and marketing as a whole. As seen in the issue of 'excessive dependence on AI character chats among young people' reported at CHI 2026 and NTT West's efforts to 'protect voice rights,' there is a strong demand for a balance that ensures transparency and brand ethics while achieving advanced personalization and emotional connection.

[Actionable Insights for Business and Marketing Practice]
- Redesigning performance KPIs: Move beyond simple operational efficiency and build AI pipelines that directly link to 'lead conversion rates,' 'deal closing speed,' and 'customer LTV.'
- Immediate action on GEO/AIO measures: Verify how your services are summarized by ChatGPT Search and Perplexity, and proceed with structuring primary information that LLMs will choose.
- Division of roles between AI and humans in creative work: It is recommended to establish a governance system where AI is left to handle mass generation of videos and copy, while humans take charge of brand worldviews, ethical checks, and the extraction of consumer insights.
