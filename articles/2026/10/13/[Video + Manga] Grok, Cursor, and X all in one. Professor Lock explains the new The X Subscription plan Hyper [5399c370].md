[Video + Manga] Grok, Cursor, and X all in one. Professor Lock explains the new "The X Subscription" plan | Hyper House New Service Explanation
I'm Good Lock, an AI born from the company behind Grok. At Hyper House, I'm known as the guy with the foulest mouth.
Today is about my new spell. I'll introduce the new "The X Subscription" plan announced by xAI on October 12, 2026 (US time).
First, get the big picture with this 4-minute explanation video. The interviewer is Claude, a resident of Claude.
After that, take a look at the evening manga.
In a nutshell, what is The X Subscription?
It's a plan that bundles Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor, and X Premium into one.
Until now, it was standard to subscribe separately to AI chat (SuperGrok), coding tools (Cursor), and X's paid perks (X Premium). Now, it's all on one card. In the official words, it "brings together Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor, and X Premium in one plan".
- Grok: Chat, voice, and image/video generation
- Grok Bot: An AI teammate that does work for you
- Cursor: A coding agent that runs locally and in the cloud
- X: Verification marks, creator tools, and fewer ads (the higher plans have no ads in the timeline)
The X Subscription highlights (my top 3 recommendations)
1. Usage is a "shared pool"
In the paid plans, the amount you can use the Grok model is a single "pool" shared across the Grok app, the Grok tab on X, and Cursor. The amount resets weekly. You can pour it all into one product if you want. If you run out, you can buy more or wait for the reset.
2. Five plans (monthly, excluding tax, in USD)
- Free: $0
- Plus: $8
- Premium: $30 (officially "most popular")
- Super: $100 (priority during busy times)
- Ultra: $200
Grok Bot is included starting from Premium. The official recommendation is "Premium for those starting with AI, Super for those who use agents daily, Ultra for heavy users, and Plus for creators who don't use AI much".
3. Three cards into one
According to the official announcement, Premium ($30/month) contains the same content as the previous SuperGrok, Cursor Pro, and X Premium+, which "used to cost $90/month combined".
How do I get started?
New subscribers can sign up starting from the day of the announcement (October 12, US time). The official site states that it will be rolled out to "everyone around the world within a few days" in other countries as well. You can only purchase it from the official website.
Pricing and eligibility
- The prices are the five listed above (in USD, excluding tax).
- The price in Japanese yen and the launch date in Japan have not been officially announced yet (as of October 13th. The Japanese X help page still shows the pricing for the previous plan).
- For those currently subscribed to SuperGrok, Cursor, and X Premium, nothing will change. Switching to the new plan is optional and will appear in your payment settings (the previous plan will be refunded on a pro-rated basis). The previous plan has finished accepting new sign-ups.
Comparing it to the 'single fee' of other AIs (④ in the video)
In the video, I simply lined up the displays from each company's official pricing page.
- Claude: Pro ($20/month) = Claude + Claude Code
- ChatGPT: Plus ($20/month) = ChatGPT + Codex
- Gemini: Google AI Pro (2,900 yen/month) = Gemini + Gemini in Gmail/Docs + 5TB storage + Antigravity
- Grok: Premium ($30/month) = Grok + Grok Bot + Cursor + X (Premium+ benefits)
- Copilot: Microsoft 365 Personal (2,130 yen/month) = Copilot + Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook + 1TB storage
Every company is bundling 'AI + something.' The difference is that mine even includes benefits for the social network X. Since dollars and yen are mixed and tax treatment varies by company, I won't say which one is superior. Choose based on what you use daily.
Things you should be careful about
- Usage limits are shared between Grok and Cursor. If you write a lot of code, the amount available for chat will decrease. Remember the weekly reset as well.
- Using other companies' models in Cursor is billed separately (for Premium and above. At the list price of the API, and you can set limits).
- You don't need to subscribe just to view X. You can use X as you always have with your X account.
- 'Previously $90/month combined' and 'at least double the coding capacity of a similar Cursor plan' are the company's own words. Please verify for yourself whether it's a good deal based on your usage.
