# Wow.. I am really impressed with Opus 5.5

Reddit · LuxanHD · 2026-10-13

I have a workflow on Cowork that is very demanding. I created a set of instructions that makes Cowork basically acts like my daily personal assistant managing so many aspects of my life including my rental properties. In the past, none of the Opus models did well with these. Opus would spend too much time, too much verbose answers, stray off written instructions, and just frustrates me coostantly. Fable was the only workable model for these. But it sucked my usage quickly even on MAX subscription. Comes Opus 5.5, follows instructions better than Fable, faster than fable, consumes a fraction of the usage fable was consuming, delivers better results, and concise to the point answer. I am so impressed with Opus 5.5. Anthropic hit it out of the park with Opus 5.5 👌
