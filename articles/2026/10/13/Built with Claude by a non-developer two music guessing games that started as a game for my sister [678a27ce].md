# Built with Claude by a non-developer: two music guessing games that started as a game for my sister

Reddit · roy979 · 2026-10-13

Less than a year ago, my sister Shani was diagnosed with cancer. She spent almost three months in the hospital, and to give us something fun to do together during those days, I started building a music game for the two of us. She passed away on August 29th, before I finished it. I kept working on it, partly to keep my mind busy, and now it's live as Shabang, with two games: 🥁 TrackStack: every song is split into its instruments. You start with only the drums, and each wrong guess adds a layer: bass, guitar, piano… vocals come last. ⏱️ Songuess: you hear a fifth of a second of the song, and each wrong guess plays more: half a second, 2 seconds, 4… In both, the less you need to hear, the more points you get, and each one has a daily challenge: one song a day, the same for everyone. I'm a mechanical engineer, not a developer. I built the whole thing with Claude: the Flask backend, both games, and the pipeline that splits each song into its instruments. Free, no signup, works on your phone: https://shabangmusic.com/?utm\_source=reddit&utm\_content=claudeai I'd love honest feedback, and to see your daily results in the comments.
