# SambaNova CEO: AI’s Next Phase Is About Efficiency

Bloomberg · 2026-10-13

SambaNova CEO Rodrigo Liang says the AI infrastructure boom is entering a new phase focused increasingly on inference, efficiency and return on investment. As the company closes its Series F with new strategic investors, Liang discusses why enterprises including JPMorgan are running AI infrastructure behind their own firewalls and why frontier models and private AI systems can coexist. He joins Bloomberg's Dani Burger from the CAIS Summit in Beverly Hills, live on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
