# Didn’t expect to be this pleased about a usage limit reset

Reddit · nooruponnoor · 2026-10-13

I know this may already be common knowledge, but I just wanted to acknowledge the quality of life improvements Anthropic has made around Claude usage limits recently. Today I finally used my free limit reset and the confirmation told me my usual weekly reset day (Thursday) stays the same. So I got a full allowance back today AND my normal reset still comes in a few days! It was a pleasant surprise. If you haven't used yours yet it came with the Opus 5.5 launch and is under Settings > Usage (browser or the desktop app only). It expires on 22 October. The other change that's made a noticeable difference for me is in Claude Code, especially when running dynamic workflows. When you hit your 5-hour limit mid-task, the session now gets a small wrap-up allowance to reach a sensible stopping point instead of cutting off halfway through an edit. Essentially it lets Claude Code run a little past the 5-hour limit. Just be aware though that it isn't really 'free' usage. It just runs slightly beyond your 5-hour limit and instead counts towards your weekly limit. There are also differences between subscriptions - Pro gets it once a week, while Max and Team Premium get it every time they hit the 5-hour limit (I've tried it with having multiple sessions running in parallel and it worked across all sessions on my Max plan). I guess none of these are really headline features, but there have been quite a few of these small, welcome upgrades since Opus 5.5 came out, and it's really refreshing to see!
