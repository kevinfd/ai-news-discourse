# ChatGPT is an amazing private tutor!

Reddit · RiceCookerPEPSI · 2026-10-13

A lot of people say that AI makes people dumb or whatever, and while it might be true, AI is a tool. You have to use it properly. In my case, I currently use ChatGPT to make lecture notes, plan my study routine, help me make flashcards, generate lesson summaries for me to print before I start class, and I'm currently in the top class when I had previously struggled with motivation. Yes, obviously if you make him write EVERYTHING for you, of course you won't learn. However, if you use him as a private tutor, write your own stuff, and ask him to correct it and give feedback, you will definitely improve without spending hundreds a month for private lessons.
