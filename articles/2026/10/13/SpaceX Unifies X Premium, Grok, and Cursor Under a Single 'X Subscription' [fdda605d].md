# SpaceX Unifies X Premium, Grok, and Cursor Under a Single 'X Subscription'

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-10-13

SpaceX is bundling access to X Premium, Grok, and Cursor under a new subscription program called The X Subscription.
For individuals, The X Subscription offers four tiers. The free tier unlocks limited use of Grok AI and Cursor, as well as the ability to post, reply, and follow accounts on X.
The $8-per-month Plus plan unlocks Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine, and Build, as well as premium features on X, including the checkmark, reduced ads, enhanced creator tools, and advanced analytics. The plan also includes access to the Grok API and the Cursor coding tool.
The $30-per-month Premium plan includes everything in Plus, with 4x usage limits, and opens the door to Grok Bot, the latest Grok models, faster Chat responses, and image and video generation. On X, the Premium plan makes scrolling ad-free across most pages, lets you pick a preferred @handle, and boosts your replies. On Cursor, which SpaceX acquired in June for $60 billion, it unlocks cloud hosting, cloud agents, and automation.
A $100-per-month Super plan has the same features as the Premium plan but offers 15x the usage limits of the Plus plan.
There are dedicated subscription plans for teams and enterprises as well. You will have to contact X for enterprise pricing, but teams can pick from Premium, Super, and a $200-per-month Ultra plan.
If you already have an X Premium subscription, you’ll be able to keep your subscription, SpaceX confirmed on X. However, switching to the unified X Subscription will provide “usage-per-dollar of our own models than the comparable plan it replaces,” the company adds.
The new subscription model is rolling out gradually, and SpaceX says you’ll need to check your billing settings to see if it's available. From comments under SpaceX’s announcement thread, it appears some users are unable to switch to the new X Subscription system right away.
The subscription overhaul arrives just days after SpaceX CEO Elon Musk confirmed he will rename the company to SpaceXSI, aligning with President Trump’s executive order to rename artificial intelligence as superintelligence. SpaceX’s X handle has already adopted the new name: SpaceX Super Intelligence. (XAI changed its name to SpaceXAI in May.)
