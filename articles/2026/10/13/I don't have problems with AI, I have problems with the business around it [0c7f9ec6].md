# I don't have problems with AI, I have problems with the business around it.

Reddit · firmagorilla · 2026-10-13

Today I stumbled upon another goofy vid of Zuck making an ass of himself drinking a Guinness. He is sat next to another mulleted goofball that cannot stop nodding. They're in a podcast promoting muse. Mullet goofball is Alexandr (not a typo) Wang. Today I learned that Wang is 29. In 2016, when he was 19(!) he started SacelAI, a data labeling company for AI. "Scale AI has received defense contracts from the United States Armed Forces , and has been tapped by the Pentagon 's Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office to test and evaluate the safety and reliability of large language models for military planning and decision-making."[wiki] Wang is head of meta's ai lab, his role is operational, to introduce a war-time mindset in the lab to move things to production at break-neck speed. He aligns with Zuck's "move fast and break things" ethos, but he learned it as being part of the way business is conducted in the military industrial complex. " Alexandr Wang plays a pivotal role in Meta's aggressive shift toward defense, national security, and military applications. Meta explicitly brought Wang on as its Chief AI Officer to act as a "wartime CEO", utilizing his deep defense network to bridge the gap between Big Tech and the Pentagon. "[ https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/55225/alexandr-wang-wartime-ceo-recruited-by-mark-zuckerberg\ ] Long story short, muse and all the other cutesy agents, the shitty glasses, all point towards meta making a play to sit beside Palantir and Anduril. Same with SpaceXai, sorry SpacexSi. (I think all the companies that actively rebranded to Super Intelligence are signalling they want a pentagon keycard) This is what makes AI dangerous, unlike the way original military tech is separated, there is no real difference between foreign and domestic use. The hunger these companies display for these contracts projects into the world as a push for a deterioration of accountability. This is what the tech titans actually mean with legislation, this is what they want solidified. In the end the most serious victim is democracy, simply put, we are privatizing decision making. Honestly, I never thought the military industrial complex poster boy would have a mullet with a cheeky fringe and his boss would choke on a Guinness like a lightweight.
