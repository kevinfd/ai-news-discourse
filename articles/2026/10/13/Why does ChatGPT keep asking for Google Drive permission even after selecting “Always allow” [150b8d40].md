# Why does ChatGPT keep asking for Google Drive permission even after selecting “Always allow”?

Reddit · Far-Management-507 · 2026-10-13

I have Google Drive connected to ChatGPT with full access enabled, but every time ChatGPT tries to update a Google Sheets spreadsheet, I keep getting the same permission prompt. The prompt says: “Allow ChatGPT to use Google Drive? ChatGPT will call Google Drive’s Batch Update Spreadsheet tool.” I’ve selected “Always allow” multiple times, yet ChatGPT continues asking for permission to use the exact same tool. What’s the point of having an “Always allow” option if it doesn’t seem to remember my selection? Is anyone else experiencing this? Is this a known bug, or is there another setting I’m missing that would allow ChatGPT to execute these actions without repeatedly requesting approval? I’m using the ChatGPT iOS app with Google Drive connected.
