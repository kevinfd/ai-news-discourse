# Lightning Machine: GLM-5.3's architecture in 3D

Reddit · gamedevsam · 2026-10-13

Ever wonder how an LLM guesses what to say when you ask it a question? If you have interest and 10 minutes, dive into GLM-5.3's architecture with this engaging 3D visualization into how a large LLM works. I followed a conversation through Z.ais GLM-5.3, an open-weight AI model with 743 billion parameters, and built its architecture as a lightning machine. Each of its 78 layers is a tower, and every time the model picks something from the conversation to read, a spark strikes it. This is the first one with a narrator, so you can sit back and watch. Enjoy!
