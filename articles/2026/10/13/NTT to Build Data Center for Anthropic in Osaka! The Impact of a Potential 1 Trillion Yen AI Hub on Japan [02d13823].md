# NTT to Build Data Center for Anthropic in Osaka! The Impact of a Potential 1 Trillion Yen AI Hub on Japan

note · 大阪 · 2026-10-13

NTT to Build Data Center for Anthropic in Osaka! The Impact of a Potential 1 Trillion Yen AI Hub on Japan
On October 13, 2026, news that has drawn significant attention to Japan's AI and IT infrastructure industry was reported.
It states that the NTT Group has entered final adjustments to build a data center in Osaka Prefecture for the U.S. AI company Anthropic.
According to reports, the construction cost is up to 1 trillion yen. The facility's total power reception capacity is also expected to be among the largest in Japan. If realized, this would be a major move for Japan's digital infrastructure, which supports the spread of generative AI.
*This content is based on reports as of October 13, 2026, and details of the plan and formal decisions should be confirmed in future announcements.
First of all, what has been decided?
Here is a summary of the key points from the report.
- Company responsible for construction: NTT Data Group, a subsidiary of the NTT Group
- User company: U.S. AI company Anthropic
- Planned construction site: Reported to be in the coastal area of southern Osaka Prefecture
- Construction cost: Reported to be up to 1 trillion yen
- Facility scale: Total power reception capacity expected to be among the largest in Japan
- Significance: Potential to become Anthropic's first data center hub in Japan
Based on reports from the Nikkei, Reuters and others have also covered the plan. At this stage, it is considered to be in the final adjustment phase, and not all specifications or schedules have been finalized.
What kind of company is Anthropic?
Anthropic is a U.S. AI company that develops the generative AI 'Claude'.
Claude is a generative AI used for a wide range of purposes, including writing, summarizing, programming, and information analysis.
As the use of AI expands, companies are moving beyond simple Q&A to using AI for complex business assistance and software development.
For these purposes, it is important to secure not only the development of AI models but also the computing power and data centers to run them.
In other words, for AI companies like Anthropic, data centers are a critical foundation that supports their services.
Why is a 1 trillion yen investment in a data center necessary?
Some people might wonder, "Why is such a large-scale facility necessary just to use AI?"
The reason is that running generative AI requires an enormous amount of computational processing.
1. AI computing requires a massive amount of electricity
High-performance computing devices such as GPUs are used for the training and inference of generative AI.
Operating these devices in large numbers requires a corresponding amount of electricity. Furthermore, equipment to cool the heat generated by the servers and power supply facilities to ensure continuous operation during power outages are also essential.
A data center is not just a building filled with computers. It is a large-scale infrastructure facility that combines power, cooling, communication, and security.
2. As the number of AI users increases, so does the need for computing infrastructure
As the use of generative AI spreads, the number of people and companies using the service simultaneously will increase, which may lead to an increase in the required computational volume.
AI companies need to secure computing power with an eye toward future expansion in usage.
This plan is also considered to be a move against the backdrop of this growing demand for AI infrastructure.
3. Significant capital investment is required beyond just the building
In large-scale data centers, not only the construction of the building but also power receiving equipment, cooling systems, communication equipment, and emergency power supplies are important.
Therefore, even looking at the scale of construction costs alone, the nature of the investment is significantly different from that of a typical office building.
It should be noted that regarding the reported amount of up to 1 trillion yen, it is necessary to confirm the details of the investment target and the breakdown of the costs.
Why Osaka?
What is noteworthy about this plan is the report that the planned construction site is in the coastal area of southern Osaka Prefecture.
The Kansai region, centered on Osaka, is increasing its presence as one of the data center markets following the Tokyo metropolitan area.
NTT DATA Group is also proceeding with the development of data centers in the Kansai region to meet the demand for AI and cloud services. In April 2026, it announced the opening of the "Keihanna OSK11 Data Center" in the Kyoto-Keihanna area.
If a large-scale AI-related facility is developed in Osaka, it could lead to a move to strengthen the digital infrastructure of the Kansai region.
However, regarding how this plan will coordinate with existing data centers in Osaka and the extent of the effect it will have on the regional economy, it is necessary to wait for future specific announcements.
What will change once Japan's first facility is established?
If this plan is realized, there are three major potential impacts.
1. Expansion of the foundation supporting AI within Japan
If a large-scale computing foundation for Anthropic is established in Japan, the infrastructure supporting the deployment of AI services in the Japanese market could be expanded.
However, having a data center in the country does not mean that all AI processing and data will be contained within Japan. The actual operational methods will need to be confirmed through future announcements.
2. Increased attention on the Kansai data center market
If large-scale AI-related investment proceeds in Osaka, it could serve as a catalyst for the Kansai region to be recognized as a key hub for AI infrastructure.
Data center development involves various fields, including construction, electrical equipment, air conditioning, telecommunications, and maintenance operations.
Therefore, depending on the scale of the plan and procurement methods, new business opportunities may arise for related companies.
3. Potential acceleration of infrastructure investment to support AI adoption
Until now, news about generative AI has tended to focus on the performance of new models and their convenient features.
However, to widely adopt AI in society, capital investment to secure computing power is also necessary.
This report is an example showing that the AI race is expanding beyond software and model development to physical infrastructure such as power and data centers.
On the other hand, there are also challenges
Large-scale data centers bring not only expectations but also challenges that need to be considered.
Securing power
As AI computing foundations expand, power demand may also increase. Stable power supply and efficient energy use will be critical.
Cooling and environmental impact
Equipment is required for server cooling. Designs that minimize environmental impact, such as cooling methods and energy efficiency, will be required.
Impact on the local community
While we can expect an expansion in employment and business opportunities associated with construction and operations, we must also consider the impact on the local area, including traffic and infrastructure development.
Since these impacts will vary depending on the facility's design and operational methods, it is impossible to determine the specific burdens or benefits at this stage.
Key points to watch in this news
What is important about this report is not simply that 'a large building will be built in Osaka'.
It is the fact that as competition in generative AI intensifies, collaboration between companies developing AI and those providing the infrastructure to run it is becoming increasingly critical.
To improve AI performance, it is necessary to secure not only model refinements but also sufficient computing power, electricity, cooling equipment, and communication environments.
How will the infrastructure of the NTT Group in the communications and data center fields be combined with Anthropic's AI technology? We look forward to future official announcements and the concretization of these plans.
