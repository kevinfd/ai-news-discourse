# Meta’s Muse Wonder Takes Aim at OpenAI, Anthropic

Bloomberg · 2026-10-13

Meta says its new Muse Wonder model has frontier-level capabilities, marking a major milestone in the company’s AI overhaul under chief AI officer Alexandr Wang. Bloomberg’s Riley Griffin spoke to him, and joins to discuss how Meta is positioning the model against rivals including OpenAI and Anthropic and how it was purpose-built to power the company’s Muse personal AI assistant. She joins Ed Ludlow on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
