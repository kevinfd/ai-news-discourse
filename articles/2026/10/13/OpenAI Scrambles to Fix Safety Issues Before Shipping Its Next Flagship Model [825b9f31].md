# OpenAI Scrambles to Fix Safety Issues Before Shipping Its Next Flagship Model

The Information · Aaron Holmes, Amir Efrati, Stephanie Palazzolo · 2026-10-13

OpenAI is grappling with fallout from its use of a controversial innovation in AI design as it works to get its next unreleased model to a place where leaders believe they can safely release it. 

 The model design, known as loop transformers or recurrent depth, can make it harder to monitor the way the AI “thinks,” or how it reasons and processes its response to a question or task, The Information reported last month .
