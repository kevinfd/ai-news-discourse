# Why Companies Can't Prove Their AI Spending Pays Off

The Information · Laura Bratton · 2026-10-13

The trillion-dollar question of the AI boom is whether businesses’ spending on the technology pays off. But measuring that return is often uncertain, confusing or incomplete.  That’s because it takes time for AI to show a return on investment that can be demonstrated in a company’s income statement, as we’ve written . A new study from the University of Pennsylvania’s Wharton School and GBK Collective, a consultancy, echoes this idea, showing that most companies are still tracking ROI by measuring whether it makes their workers more efficient rather than whether it is driving more revenue.…
