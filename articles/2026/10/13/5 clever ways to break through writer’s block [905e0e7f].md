# 5 clever ways to break through writer’s block

Fast Company · Jeremy Caplan · 2026-10-13

This article is republished with permission from Wonder Tools, a newsletter that helps you discover the most useful sites and apps.
Once you start scribbling a few sentences, you’re on your way.
Having gotten stuck and unstuck a lot over the past 325 weeks of writing this newsletter, I’ve found a few tricks that help. Pick one to try, and leave a comment to share what you do to get unstuck.
As a little experiment, I’ve made an Unstuck Studio page to accompany this post and to help you get unstuck playfully.
As Daniel Pink explains, starting comes first: “Action precedes motivation,” he says, “not the other way around.”
Talk first, type later
When I’m stuck, I dictate rather than battle my inner critic.
The critic assumes there’s a better word for whatever I’m typing. It urges my fingers to stop and wait. When I’m rambling with my voice, the critic is quiet.
