# why do i still have to explain the same stuff to ai every single time

Reddit · IndependentCoast3974 · 2026-10-13

ok so ai is supposed to get smarter. but a lot of the time you're typing out the same context again. same preferences, same setup, same here's how i like it. and it's not just chat. think about any small thing you do the same way every day. a person would pick up on it after like a week. software still just waits for you to do it again. honestly not sure if the problem is memory, trust, privacy, or just nobody building it well. probably some of each. so what do you actually want ai to learn about you without being told? and what would feel too creepy?
