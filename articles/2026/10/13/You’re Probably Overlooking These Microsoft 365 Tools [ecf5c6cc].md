# You’re Probably Overlooking These Microsoft 365 Tools

Duke Today · 2026-10-13

You’re Probably Overlooking These Microsoft 365 Tools
From scheduling focus time to transcribing interviews, these features can make everyday work easier for Duke staff and faculty
“Copilot, which has a sense of humor, has hilariously assisted me and kept me from utter frustration on this and other projects in the works,” Berry said.
It’s not uncommon for staff and faculty to only use a fraction of the tools available through Microsoft 365, according to Becca McDaniel, an IT Solutions Architect for Information Services in the Duke Cancer Institute. But it only takes a bit of experimentation to find ways the digital tools might streamline your office work.
Below are some suggestions for dipping your toes into the world of Microsoft 365, courtesy of McDaniel – with the caveat that things evolve quickly in technology so some steps might change.
First, Locate the Applications
To find the applications, open a browser window, go to microsoft365.com or mail.duke.edu, sign in with your NetID and click the grid of small squares (the app launcher) in the top-left corner.
Collaborate with Shared Documents
Create a shared file in Word, Excel or PowerPoint, allowing browser-based editing from virtually any computer without requiring desktop applications. If you’re tired of hunting for the "final" version buried in your inbox, share a link to a file in Microsoft 365 instead of emailing copies. Everyone works from a single source of truth, version juggling disappears and you can see at a glance who has access to a document. When you need it again, Search in Teams or OneDrive will help you find it quickly.
Quick How-To
- Open or create a new Word, PowerPoint or Excel document directly from Teams (teams.microsoft.com) or OneDrive (onedrive.live.com).
- Select Share → Share to invite additional collaborators or Share → Copy link to send to individuals who already have access.
- Edit simultaneously with colleagues in real time, see live edits and who's editing.
- Use @mentions via Comments to notify people when changes are made.
- Select Share → Manage Access at any time to see who you've shared a document with and adjust settings.
Get Design Help for PowerPoint
If you’ve never quite mastered PowerPoint graphic design and want some help with making your slide deck look pretty, PowerPoint Designer is an easy assistant. It turns a simple outline into a polished presentation in a matter of moments with no design experience required. Because it offers professional-looking layouts with a click, it also helps bring a consistent level of quality to presentations across your team, so every deck you share looks similar.
Quick How-To
- Create a slide deck from Teams or OneDrive with content like a simple bulleted list.
- Select Design → Designer / Design Ideas.
- Choose a suggested layout.
Drop a Poll into Outlook
If you have a simple question for a group you’re emailing, there’s no need to create a poll through Microsoft Forms. You can make one right in the web-based version of Outlook. Polls are a quick way to gather feedback or make a group decision without calling a meeting – whether you're prioritizing options, collecting preferences or checking for consensus. Responses are automatically tallied and easy to review, and everything stays within Outlook and Microsoft 365 so there's no extra tool to manage. And polls are still stored in Microsoft Forms, where you can go back later and edit, review and export data. Side note: There is also a specific Poll option created for Scheduling Polls that is helpful for selecting meeting times.
Quick How-To
- Create a new email.
- Select Insert → Poll. (You might need to click "Apps" before "Poll.")
- Enter your question and choices.
- Send the email.
- Watch responses update automatically (include yourself on email for easy viewing).
Automatically Schedule Focus Time
Create time in your calendar to focus on tasks without being interrupted. Viva Insights automatically blocks off time for you so you can ease meeting overload. Crucially, you remain in charge of your calendar: if your plans change, you can adjust or remove a focus block from your whenever you need to.
Quick How-To
- Open Viva Insights in Teams or Outlook. You might need to click "More Apps" and search for Viva Insights.
- Configure a Focus Plan.
- Select how much focus time you want each week.
- Allow Viva Insights to automatically reserve time on your calendar.
Transcribe Audio Files
An invaluable feature for anyone who has recorded a meeting or interview and wants a speedy transcript of what was said. It’s a must-have tool for communicators and writers.
Quick How-To
- Open or create a new Word document directly from Teams (teams.microsoft.com) or OneDrive (onedrive.live.com).
- Click on the down arrow next to the microphone symbol in the upper right and click on “Transcribe.”
- In the pop-sidebar, select “Upload audio” and upload your mp3 or mp4 file.
- Select how to add the transcribed text to your Word document as: Just text; With speakers; With timestamps; or With speakers and timestamps.
