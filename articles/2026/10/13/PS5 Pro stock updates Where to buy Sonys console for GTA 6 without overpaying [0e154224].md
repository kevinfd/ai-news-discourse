# PS5 Pro stock updates: Where to buy Sonys console for GTA 6 without overpaying

Mashable · Tabitha Britt · 2026-10-13

PS5 Pro stock updates: Where to buy Sony's console for 'GTA 6' without overpaying
You'll be hard-pressed to find a PlayStation 5 Pro in stock right now. Major retailers, including PlayStation Direct, Best Buy, Amazon (including bundle offers), Target, and Walmart, are sold out. With the upcoming release of Grand Theft Auto VI, the reseller market has gotten a little ridiculous (as has everything else).
Why is the PS5 Pro out of stock?
Beyond the obvious hype for Rockstar's next release, Sony is dealing with broader supply chain bottlenecks. The global AI boom is squeezing the semiconductor market, which means Sony can't build consoles fast enough. According to distributor reports, these supply constraints could keep stock tight well into 2027.
Should you buy a PS5 Pro from a third-party seller?
Naturally, scalpers are having a field day. We've seen third-party listings on reseller sites (and even GameStop's official pre-owned page) gouging buyers for $1,300 to $1,500+ for a console that retails for $899. Sony is taking the issue so seriously that in Japan, they're forcing buyers to enter a lottery — and prove they've clocked at least 60 hours of PS4 or PS5 playtime over the last two years — just for a chance to buy a PS5 Pro.
If you absolutely have to buy one today and have money to burn, here's what the third-party market currently looks like:
- GameStop: A pre-owned unit will currently run you $1,329.99.
- Swappa: This is a legitimate, verified marketplace for used tech. However, verified PS5 Pro listings are currently sitting at $1,338.
- StockX: The popular resale bidding site currently has the console hovering around $1,290.
- Macy's and Walmart: Don't be fooled by third-party sellers on major retail sites. A low-rated seller on Walmart is currently asking $1,644.99 (open-box units are already sold out), while a third-party seller on Macy's is asking a staggering $1,749.99.
- Mercari: We found a listing here for $800. Since this is $100 below the official MSRP for a highly coveted, sold-out item, it's almost certainly a scam. Avoid it.
How to find a PS5 Pro in stock before launch
If you're set on securing a Pro before launch day, here's how to play the restock game without burning a hole in your wallet:
- Sign up for stock alerts. Restocks happen in small, sudden waves. Set up notifications through stock-tracking accounts on X (like @PS5StockUpdater) or apps like HotStock to get pinged the second inventory hits.
- Bookmark and refresh product pages. Keep direct product pages saved for PlayStation Direct, Best Buy, Amazon, Target, and Walmart. PlayStation Direct in particular occasionally drops invite-only or queue-based stock.
- Check local inventory. Sometimes brick-and-mortar stores (like local GameStops or Target stores) receive small physical allocations that don't always show up online right away. It never hurts to ask an associate in person.
- Preorder the game now. Don't hold your breath for a last-minute GTA 6 PS5 Pro console bundle. Secure your physical or digital preorder now so you're ready to go, no matter which console sits under your TV on day one.
