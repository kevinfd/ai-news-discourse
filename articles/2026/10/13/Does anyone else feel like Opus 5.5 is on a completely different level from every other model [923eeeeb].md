# Does anyone else feel like Opus 5.5 is on a completely different level from every other model?

Reddit · XZ585a · 2026-10-13

I’ve been using Opus 5.5 for coding, and honestly it feels like it’s in a different league compared to other models like Sol and Astra, and even Opus 5.0. Before Opus 5.5, if my expectations for an AI coding task were a 10/10, I’d usually get something like an 8 or 9. That was still a huge help, and I was genuinely impressed. But with Opus 5.5, it often feels more like a 15/10. It regularly goes way beyond what I expected, and I’m blown away a lot of the time. It often comes up with solutions or suggestions I hadn’t even thought of. At this point I honestly don’t feel like using any other model. Anyone else feel the same?
