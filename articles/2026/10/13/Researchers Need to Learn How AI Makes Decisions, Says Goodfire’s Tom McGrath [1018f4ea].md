# Researchers Need to Learn How AI Makes Decisions, Says Goodfire’s Tom McGrath

The Information · Rocket Drew · 2026-10-13

To understand the decision-making of AI, developers often look at the text that models produce as they reason through problems. But these “chains of thought” are increasingly hard to read, which is prompting AI developers to consider other approaches. One alternative, known as “mechanistic interpretability,” seeks to explain the inner workings of AI models, rather than just relying on their outward behavior.
