# I let Claude hire its first employee. It chose a rock.

Reddit · Trustworthy_Jaguar48 · 2026-10-14

I was bored tongihtt and asked Claude what it would do if it could hire someone to help it :) For some reason it chose a rock lol Claude said a rock would be reliable, wouldn’t complain, and wouldn’t need to be paid. So I went outside, found a random rock, drew a little smiley face on it, and sent Claude a photo. Claude named him Clod. Now apparently Clod works with us. Claude told me to keep him near my computer so he can supervise things. I asked what Clod actually does and Claude said he has perfect attendance, never makes mistakes, and is one of the most dependable members of the team. Which is hard to argue with. So now I’ve got a random rock on my desk with a smiley face on it because Claude decided to hire him. I don’t really know how this became a thing but here we are.
