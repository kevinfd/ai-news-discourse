# Anthropic Opens Singapore Office, Signs AI Safety Deal as Claude Usage Ranks Second Among 121 Countries

International Business Times Singapore · Bhaswati Guha Majumder · 2026-10-14

Anthropic has opened a new office in Singapore and signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information, expanding its Asia-Pacific operations as Singapore emerges as one of its strongest markets.
Anthropic said Singapore ranks second among 121 countries in Claude.ai usage relative to population, with usage nearly six times higher than the country's size alone would predict.
The new Singapore office is Anthropic's fifth in the Asia-Pacific region. The company said Claude is already powering business and government services used by millions of people every day, including those operated by GovTech Singapore, Grab and DBS.
"We're committed to Singapore for the long term," said Dale Finlay, General Manager of ASEAN at Anthropic.
"Our customers here hold AI to an extremely high standard, especially when it comes to reliability and security. Having a team on the ground means we can get even closer to the organizations putting Claude to work on their hardest problems."
Anthropic, Singapore Expand AI Safety and Evaluation Cooperation
The MOU was signed by Chng Kai Fong, Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Digital Development and Information, and Anthropic co-founder Ben Mann on the sidelines of Singapore International Cyber Week. Minister for Digital Development and Information Josephine Teo and Michael Sellitto, Anthropic's Head of APAC Policy, witnessed the signing.
The agreement covers cooperation in AI safety, security and model evaluation, research into AI adoption and its impact, and responsible AI adoption across Singapore's public and private sectors.
"Singapore has been a leader in responsible AI, with a long track record of investment in model testing and evaluation," said Mann.
"We founded Anthropic on the belief that capability and safety have to go hand in hand. With this MOU, we'll partner with Singapore to help ensure the transition to powerful AI goes well here, across Asia-Pacific, and around the world."
Under the agreement, Anthropic plans technical exchanges with Singapore's AI safety, security and assurance institutions covering model evaluation, testing standards and incident response. The company also plans to strengthen AI skills, support responsible AI adoption among small and medium-sized businesses and help build AI capabilities among public officers.
Claude Adoption Spreads Across Singapore's Government and Businesses
More than 1,500 engineers and technical staff at GovTech Singapore have adopted Claude Code to modernize government systems and speed software delivery. The Cyber Security Agency of Singapore is also exploring Claude's potential to strengthen cyber defense.
In financial services, DBS uses Claude for software development and day-to-day productivity within its security and governance framework, while sovereign wealth fund GIC is rolling out Claude across investment and corporate services teams.
Regulated organizations can also use Claude with in-region inference on Amazon Bedrock to help meet data-processing requirements.
Singapore-based technology companies are also using Claude. Grab's BriX sales assistant uses Claude and saves sales teams approximately 40,000 hours every quarter while surfacing new sales opportunities.
Sea uses Claude for Shopee's customer service chatbot and for coding work, while Supabase is building with Claude Code.
Anthropic said its Singapore office is now open and hiring. The company also hosted Builder Summit Singapore, bringing together more than 300 developers, while a local Claude community meetup earlier in the week drew more than 700 builders.
