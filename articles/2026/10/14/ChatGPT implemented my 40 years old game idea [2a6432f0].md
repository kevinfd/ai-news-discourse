# ChatGPT implemented my 40 years old game idea

Reddit · gewappnet · 2026-10-14

In 1987 with 16 years old, I created a strategic board game just for myself. I wrote up the rules and put together a simple version of the game, which I called TOKE. Now, almost 40 years later, I gave ChatGPT the rules and asked it to implement it as a web page. It should be possible to play it against the computer. This is the result: https://tobias-steinke.de/TOKE.html I am pretty amazed by it. It looks great, and the computer opponent plays really well. And all of that just in an 80 KB HTML page.
