# ChatGPT deleting its previous response when I ask a follow-up question

Reddit · facesnorth · 2026-10-14

I've seen this mentioned in the past as happening from time to time, but lately for me it's been happening in just about every chat. Often on my 2nd query in any chat, the first response will completely disappear. So my 1st 2 queries will appear back to back, with no response in between. Then I'll yell at it for doing so and it will inform me that it doesn't have the power to delete a chat and it's either the UI or a browser extension. Then I yell again that I don't care how it views itself, I'm obviously speaking to it as the entire chatgpt service, not just the LLM. And then it will redeliver it's original response back. It's not just the first response that gets deleted, although that seems most common, but I've had many chats where after a while of talking, any response could be deleted as it's about to deliver it's next one. It's happening now in just about every chat I have and it's getting extremely tiresome.
