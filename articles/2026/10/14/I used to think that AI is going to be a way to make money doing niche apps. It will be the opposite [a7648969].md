# I used to think that AI is going to be a way to make money doing niche apps. It will be the opposite.

Reddit · Desert-Noir · 2026-10-14

AI is going to kill commercial software or software as a service. As we’ve seen with reverse engineering, I just don’t think apps and software are going to make money anymore. Why would anyone buy or subscribe to ab app when AI is making it possible for it to be open source. I’ve made so many apps and services that would typically require a subscription or purchase and I don’t know a lick of code. But obviously I’m beyond the average user, but when free, open source versions exist of apps that are as good or better then what is the point of buying a proprietary app? I started making an incredibly niche app as a commercial venture but ultimately bailed out 2/3rds the way through because what is the point when my prospective clients will either have it added as a feature to software they likely already use or have someone on their team create a custom app catered to their individual needs. In the not so distant future he only ones making money will be the AI companies.
