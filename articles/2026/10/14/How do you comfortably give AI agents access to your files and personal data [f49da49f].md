# How do you comfortably give AI agents access to your files and personal data?

Reddit · BananaEducational617 · 2026-10-14

I’m new to AI, and it blows my mind how easily people give agents full access to their machines, emails, and data just to automate a few tasks. To me, handing over that level of access feels like a massive privacy and security risk—it’s the main reason I’m hesitant to connect Claude or other tools to my daily life. Am I missing something fundamental here about how these integrations work? For those doing this regularly: how are you actually keeping your machine and personal data safe?
