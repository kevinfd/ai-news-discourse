For major tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot, their respective areas of expertise (idea generation, coding, researching the latest information, office work coordination, etc.) have become clear, making it essential to use them selectively according to the purpose. 1. 2026 Edition: Optimal Solutions by Purpose for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot, and Trends in Gemini Free Plan Revisions
News Overview
According to the latest research and comparisons, the leading generative AIs—ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Copilot—each have different strengths. It is necessary to use the right tool for the right job: ChatGPT for idea generation, Gemini for the latest web information and processing large volumes of literature, Claude for coding and essay writing, and Copilot for coordination with Office products Reference Article 1, Reference Article 2. Also, according to Google's announcement, the environment for use is being updated, such as changes to the models available in the Gemini app without a plan Reference Article 2.
Perspective on "Evolution"
The dawn of generative AI was a phase of "how to master one versatile AI," but we have now shifted to an advanced utilization phase of "how to combine the expertise of each AI." For example, using Gemini, which excels in real-time performance, for morning market research; Claude for complex logical thinking and text composition during the day; and relying on ChatGPT for creative idea generation in the evening—a style of delegating tasks to AI, similar to human multitasking, is becoming standardized. This evolution cleverly covers the "limitations of AI" that we feel in our daily work and enables us to produce higher-precision deliverables in a shorter time. Even for non-engineers, by understanding these characteristics, it is possible to dramatically increase the speed and quality of all intellectual production, from daily email drafting to creating outlines for project proposals.
Practical Tips
First, try consciously switching the AI you use according to the content of the work you will be doing starting tomorrow. Start by fixing browser tabs for specific purposes, such as opening Gemini for morning news and research, Claude for proofreading and structuring text, and ChatGPT for idea generation.
2. The Rise of "Agent Workflow Tools" for Automating Tasks Without Code and Countermeasures for Connection Errors
News Overview
In selecting tools to automate daily routine work and data organization, methods that can be implemented without writing complex commands or programs are attracting attention Reference Article 3. On the other hand, when linking LLMs like Ollama with various automation tools (such as n8n or Dify) using local environments or Docker, technical practical knowledge is also being shared, such as troubleshooting connection errors (ECONNREFUSED) Reference Article 4.
Perspective on "Evolution"
"Automation" was once perceived as a privilege for engineers, requiring programming knowledge. However, in the current generative AI frontier, an environment is being prepared where anyone can build their own assistant simply by combining pre-prepared, abundant "skills" and workflow components Reference Article 3. For non-engineer business professionals, this means a major opportunity to be freed from daily routine tasks (for example, the initial steps of daily report aggregation and data organization). Although there are technical connection barriers Reference Article 4, as the interfaces and ecosystems of the tools themselves become more refined, the way of working where "you don't write code yourself, but have AI build the work mechanism" is finally becoming a reality.
Practical Tips
First, write down just one task from your daily routine that you repeat using the same steps every day, and try consulting an AI about whether it can be automated.
3. Prototyping Custom Agents and New Forms of Development and Thinking as Seen in Google Antigravity
News Summary
New mechanisms like Google's "Antigravity" are making it possible to prototype custom agents with minimal code Reference Article 8. Additionally, an ecosystem for managing and utilizing AI agents in daily life is expanding, with the emergence of unique peripheral tools (such as coucou) that allow users to monitor and approve the actions of various AI coding agents using features like the Mac notch or iPhone Reference Article 7.
Perspective on the "Evolution" to Decipher
As AI evolves from an entity that simply returns requested text into an "agent" that autonomously executes tasks, the relationship between humans and AI is also changing significantly. With the advent of tools like Antigravity, the hurdle for quickly shaping and verifying ideas, rather than developing complex systems from scratch, has been dramatically lowered Reference Article 8. This makes the approach of "trying to build a working prototype yourself first" accessible not only to developers but also to general business professionals who have ideas for new services or operational improvements. In a future where AI agents think and act on our behalf, humans will be able to focus on direction and decision-making—deciding "what should be done"—and it is expected that society will shift toward one where individual creativity is maximized.
Practical Tips
First, write down the role of a "virtual assistant" to solve the business ideas or challenges you are facing, and simulate the steps by which work would proceed if that AI were actually running.
