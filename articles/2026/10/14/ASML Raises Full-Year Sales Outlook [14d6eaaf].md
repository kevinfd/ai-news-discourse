# ASML Raises Full-Year Sales Outlook

Bloomberg · 2026-10-14

ASML upgraded the low end of its 2026 sales forecast as increasing artificial intelligence spending boosts demand for the Dutch company’s chip-making machines. The firm projected annual sales of between €44 billion ($49 billion) and €45 billion this year. ASML's outlook offers an indication of investment plans by chipmakers like TSMC, Samsung Electronics and Intel. Bloomberg's Tom Mackenzie reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
