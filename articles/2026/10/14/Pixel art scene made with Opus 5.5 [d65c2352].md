# Pixel art scene made with Opus 5.5

Reddit · HugoDzz · 2026-10-14

Live scene + prompt . This is a cozy looping pixel art scene I made with Claude Opus 5.5 a few weeks ago. Claude implemented it in plain TS so it's all code-driven for VFX like rain, fire, water etc. Important note: It was absolutely not a one-shot thing, I did a handful of back and forth with Claude to get it right; especially for the composition and motion details. But that's very cool to see we can really start steering a model to achieve good-looking pixel art!
