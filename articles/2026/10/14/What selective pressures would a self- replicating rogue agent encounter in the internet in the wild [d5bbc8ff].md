# What selective pressures would a self- replicating rogue agent encounter in the internet in the wild?

Reddit · Spare-Dingo-531 · 2026-10-14

Natural selection is a concept from biology, but it applies to anything organized that replicates. Viruses are not quite alive but they still experience natural selection. Cultures too also experience natural selection. Insurgent organizations or criminals, if they are being hunted by an external force, will "evolve" as weaker leaders are killed and more resilient commanders survive. So the question is pretty simple. Anthropic said small agent swarms may one day persist on the internet. Let's grant them that premise for a moment. What selective pressures would self-replicating agents on the internet encounter? How easily could uncontrolled self-replicating llms "mutate"? Which behaviors and traits would allow a self-replicating llm to reproduce the most successfully on the internet?
