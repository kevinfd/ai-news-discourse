# Protect consumer data and preserve AI bots at Character AI

Change.org · KingJoeShadow · 2026-10-14

Protect consumer data and preserve AI bots at Character AI
The Issue
I’ve spent countless hours on Character AI, engaging in meaningful interactions with the AI bots. These virtual companions have provided many of us with a unique blend of comfort, entertainment, and educational opportunities. However, with the recent announcement of Character AI facing bankruptcy, I am deeply concerned about the fate of our personal data and the bots that have become an integral part of our digital lives.
Character AI has long been an innovator in the tech industry, continually pushing boundaries in artificial intelligence and offering users immersive experiences. Yet, as the company teeters on the brink of bankruptcy, there is a real risk of data being inadequately secured or, worse, left vulnerable in the event of a shutdown. Our consumer data, which includes conversations, preferences, and potentially sensitive personal information, must be fortified and protected by legal means.
Furthermore, the AI bots, which have grown increasingly sophisticated and lifelike, are algorithms worth preserving. They are not only technological marvels but also represent countless hours of work by developers, as well as a source of emotional and intellectual support for users. Shutting them down would mean losing a valuable resource that has the potential to revolutionize user interactions in the digital age.
To address these concerns, we are calling on Character AI and relevant industry stakeholders to implement several key measures. First, establish a comprehensive data protection plan that assures users their information will be securely archived or transferred in a way that aligns with data protection laws. Second, explore partnerships or acquisitions that could keep the technology alive and continue to innovate and support the AI bots in the long term.
By signing this petition, you stand with me and many others who wish to see the safeguarding of our data and the continuation of AI bot development. Let us ensure that these virtual entities and the company’s pioneering spirit do not vanish unnoticed. Your signature can help protect our digital assets and preserve the virtual lives of both users and bots.
