# Apple TV 4K: Where to buy the 4th generation of Apple TV

Mashable · Samantha Mangino · 2026-10-14

Apple TV 4K: Where to buy the 4th generation of Apple TV
PREORDER NOW: Preorder the 4th generation of Apple TV 4K. Shop now for $299 so it arrives on its Nov. 19 release day.
Apple is turning its attention to the home. After last month's unveiling of new iPhones, Apple is back with tech for the home, including the HomeView smart display, the HomePod mini 2, LG smart security products, and the 4th generation of Apple TV 4K.
The latest Apple TV looks quite familiar to the previous generation, still the standard black box with silver remote. The difference is on the inside, according to Apple, as it's "supercharged by Siri AI." No longer do users need to pick up the remote to interact with the AI assistant. Instead, the new Apple TV recognizes your voice even when something is playing.
The 4th generation of Apple TV 4K is powered by the A19 chip, the same one used in the iPhone 17 and iPhone Air. It's also equipped with Find My and plays a sound to find your misplaced remote, a sweet feature for those of us with a tendency to lose our remote in the cushions (guilty).
Where to buy the Apple TV 4K
The Apple TV 4K (4th generation) is now available for preorder from Apple and Amazon. It costs $299 and will be released on Nov. 19.
