# DeepL CEO on Voice AI Growth and Competition

Bloomberg · 2026-10-14

DeepL CEO Jarek Kutylowski discusses surging demand for voice-to-voice translation and how AI is transforming global business. He also addresses competition with OpenAI and Anthropic, DeepL’s AWS strategy, European data sovereignty, recent workforce cuts and why the company is comfortable remaining private. He spoke with Bloomberg’s Tom Mackenzie, at the International AI Summit in Dublin, Ireland. (Source: Bloomberg)
