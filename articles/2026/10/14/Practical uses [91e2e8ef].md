# Practical uses?

Reddit · Fluffy-Revolution- · 2026-10-14

AI is being used for so many things, but the one thing we are confronting globally right now alongside climate change is that we are running out of fossil fuels and without those we can't really make plastic or a whole host of chemicals so we really need to stop spunking them on producing power and concentrate on recycling what we have already used. I'm old and all my life fringe researchers have been ruminating on cold fusion. Is anyone currently using AI to conclusively prove whether its ever going to be possible/practical? What about designing ways to effectively use wind power on a small scale? Water havesting from humid air? Rehabilitating overused soil on a massive scale? Sorting and recycling rubbish from historic dumps? Cleaning and reusing microplastics and cheamicals from our waterways and oceans? More efficient household cooling systems? Claude is so good at software design now. Are there any tools that are near getting good at iterative real world physical designs for practical engineering?
