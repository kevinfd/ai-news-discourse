# I wanted to keep up with AI without reading a whole newspaper, so I built Nyoose

Reddit · MaximumSafety8706 · 2026-10-14

I want to keep up with AI, but I don’t want catching up to become another daily task. I kept seeing the same announcement across tweets, shorts, and blogs, each with someone’s own explanation. Finding the source still took work. And everyone seemed to present whichever story they were covering as the most important thing happening. I wanted one short overview: what happened, what deserves attention first, and where to read the original. So I built Nyoose: https://nyoose.xyz Each daily edition has at most 10 stories, ranked by significance, with short factual summaries and links to sources. I group duplicate coverage, and quieter days get fewer stories. AI assists with selection and ranking, with the criteria explained on the site. The summaries stick to source facts rather than adding my own take. The idea is to spend about five minutes catching up, then read deeper only where you’re interested. It’s free to read without an account. I’d love some early readers - after looking at an edition, what would you leave out, and what did I miss? You can also subscribe for free to get each day’s briefing by email.
