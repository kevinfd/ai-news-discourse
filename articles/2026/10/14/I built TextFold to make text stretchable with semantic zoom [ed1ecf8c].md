# I built TextFold to make text stretchable with "semantic zoom"

Reddit · Lopsided_Scarcity979 · 2026-10-14

I think we've been reading too many feeds from AI text lately! I actually want to reduce my cognitive burden... So I built TextFold, it turns any text into 5 zoomable layers—from a one-screen section map down to verbatim text. You pinch (or press 1–5) to zoom between them without losing your reading position, your agentic output, any papers, or documents. - Web Demo: https://chenxiachan.github.io/textfold/gallery/ - GitHub (Apache 2.0): https://github.com/chenxiachan/textfold https://preview.redd.it/mbyue0akthvh1.png?width=1080&format=png&auto=webp&s=1cb40536d6b397adae2ad1c5e6bd51d87517260d
