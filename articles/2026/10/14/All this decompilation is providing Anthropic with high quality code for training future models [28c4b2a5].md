# All this decompilation is providing Anthropic with high quality code for training future models

Reddit · red_star_rising · 2026-10-14

Yes, I know Anthropic already got their hands on every last line of code that's available anywhere, but people decompiling obscure games and applications from 00's or even earlier is definitely providing them fresh, untapped data. Decompilation is a lengthy process even for agents, so there's no way they ever went through the trouble of doing all this hardwork themselves. We're doing it for them. I'm not complaining at all. I want these models to keep getting better and better. I'm just expressing my realisation that as they provide us with more capable models, we will utilise them for more and more novel tasks, which in turn will give them data to produce even more capable models. We're part of the loop. Always have been.
