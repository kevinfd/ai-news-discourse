# My son got hooked on Subway Surfers, so I built him my own with Claude Code (free, runs in the browser)

Reddit · MrFelliks · 2026-10-14

My son (7) got hooked on Subway Surfers. As a vibe-coding dad I couldn't just walk past his new obsession, so I built my own Subway Surfers with blackjack and AI slop :) The main difference is that the music builds the levels, every obstacle lands on the beat. Kicks turn into jumps, snares into slides, a held note becomes a long ceiling you have to slide under and hold. Right before a drop the villain (an AI cursor called Struna) deletes the floor and you fall straight onto the drop. The chorus gives the same obstacles every time it comes back, and the same song always makes the same level. Made almost entirely with Claude Code. I mostly playtested and complained, it wrote the audio analysis, the level generator and the stick figure animation from free mocap. My favorite part: it plays every song itself with a bot in a headless browser to check that a perfect run is possible, and fixes the generator when it isn't. My son reports bugs right from the game and Claude fixes them, so he's officially the most productive QA I've ever worked with. What would you improve? Free, no sign up, phone or PC: https://struna.ai
