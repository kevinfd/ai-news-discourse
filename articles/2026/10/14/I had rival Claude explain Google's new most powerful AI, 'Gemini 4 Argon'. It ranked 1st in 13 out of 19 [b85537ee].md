# I had rival Claude explain Google's new most powerful AI, 'Gemini 4 Argon'. It ranked 1st in 13 out of 19 categories, but it didn't win everything.

note · オジサン · 2026-10-14

I had rival Claude explain Google's new most powerful AI, 'Gemini 4 Argon'. It ranked 1st in 13 out of 19 categories, but it didn't win everything.
Hello, I'm Ojisan.
Google has announced its new top-tier model, 'Gemini 4 Argon'. Since it's a big deal, I asked their rival, Claude, to explain what kind of AI this is.
■ What I did
I simply asked claude.ai (Opus 5.5) once: 'Research the official announcement about Gemini 4 Argon and explain it in detail and in an easy-to-understand way.' In about two minutes, I received an explanation with citations for the numbers.
I have verified all the numbers against Google's official announcement myself.
■ Claude's explanation (Key points)
・The biggest new feature: The amount that can be output at once (output limit) has increased from 64,000 tokens to 1 million tokens. That's about 16 times more.
・Strengths: It ranked 1st in 13 out of 19 categories in the official table. It is strong in legal and financial work, and in reading long documents and long videos. On the legal test, it scored about three times higher than the 2nd place model.
・Weaknesses: Other AIs outperformed it in 5 categories. These include very difficult development, command operations, and AI training tasks (Claude Opus 5.5 ranked 1st in command operations, etc.).
・Price: Currently, there is a launch commemorative price of $2 per 1 million tokens for input and $10 for output. After the commemorative period, it will be $4 and $20.
・Where to use it: Available for Gemini API paid plans (Google AI Studio, Android Studio), and for Google AI Ultra subscribers via AI Studio and Google Antigravity.
■ What impressed me
Even though it is the one being compared, Claude wrote on its own, 'I am also included in the comparison. Please keep in mind that this table uses tests selected by Google and numbers produced by Google, so take them with a grain of salt.' Even in its explanation of a rival, it fairly presented both its strengths and weaknesses.
■ Things to be careful about (as noted by Claude)
・'1 million' refers to the output limit. It is separate from the amount that can be read.
・The longer you make it write, the more expensive it gets (output costs five times more than input).
・Major revisions require human verification as a prerequisite.
・The benchmarks are from Google's own announcements; external verification is yet to come.
■ Tips for researching new AI
- Ask it to 'use only numbers from official announcements (blogs, model cards)' and 'include citations'.
- Ask about both 'strengths and weaknesses' (so it doesn't just give you the good stuff)
- Ask it to 'include simple explanations' for technical terms
