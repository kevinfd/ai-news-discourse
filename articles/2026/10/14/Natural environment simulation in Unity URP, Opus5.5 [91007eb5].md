# Natural environment simulation in Unity URP, Opus5.5.

Reddit · imlo2 · 2026-10-14

About 7 days into building a North Atlantic island in Unity URP (WIP). I wanted to see how much I could enhance my tech art and graphics programming abilities with AI, using mosty Opus 5.5 and Astra for a few critiques. The goal: one island you can walk across from the surf to a snowy summit, that looks good wherever you stop and look around. And also dive a bit near the shores. The terrain is real elevation data from Lofoten in Norway, reshaped and modified into an island about 4 × 5 km with a 1,000 m peak. Overcast is normal, fog is common and sunshine is rare. Not a single thing was placed manually, there's procedural systems to place anything from fallen leaves to the meso-/macro-scale terrain changes, undergrowth and rock placement etc. So far: the terrain and its rock, moss and heath; birch and pine forests from my own tree and forest generator, 25,000+ trees; and hundreds of thousands of ferns and ground plants; an ocean with waves and surf, underwater visuals, lakes, ponds, a stream and a waterfall; clouds that form and dissolve; rain, fog, starry sky and a full day cycle. The video comes straight from the engine, kilometres of draw distance, custom volumetrics, atmospheric system, lighting/shadow-casting and geometry generation in compute shaders. Footage straight from the engine, running well above 60 fps at 1440p. Work in progress. Wildlife and sound not yet implemented. Terrain based on Nasjonal høydemodell DTM 1 m, © Kartverket (CC BY 4.0), modified.
