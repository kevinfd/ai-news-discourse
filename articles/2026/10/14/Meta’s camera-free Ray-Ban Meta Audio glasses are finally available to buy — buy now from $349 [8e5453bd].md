# Meta’s camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio glasses are finally available to buy — buy now from $349

Mashable · Soumya Kumar · 2026-10-14

Meta’s camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio glasses are finally available to buy — buy now from $349
AVAILABLE NOW: The brand-new camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio Glasses in the Clubmaster and Burbank styles are officially available to buy at Amazon.
Remember when Meta first teased its camera-free audio frames back at Connect? The idea was simple: drop the camera lens that makes people uncomfortable in public and build a sleek pair of glasses focused entirely on music, calls, and voice AI. Following that initial debut, the Ray-Ban Meta Audio Glasses have officially launched and are available to buy on Amazon starting at $349.
Removing the camera hardware allowed Meta to shave the frames down to its slimmest, lightest AI eyewear to date — while packing in double the battery endurance of previous generations. The collection brings that tech into two distinct, classic Ray-Ban shapes that look and feel just like standard analog frames.
Wearing them means keeping your phone tucked away in your pocket. Directional open-ear speakers with Spatial Audio beam music and podcasts straight to your ears without closing you off from ambient street noise, while an upgraded 6-microphone array cancels out over 90% of background noise so calls sound crisp on WhatsApp or Messenger.
You can use the touchpad on the temple or press the assignable action button to trigger Meta AI — to ask for flight reminders, check your Gmail or Outlook calendar, get turn-by-turn walking directions, or tap into real-time translation across 20 languages.
If you prefer a clean, rectangular silhouette, the Burbank is the lightest frame in the entire lineup, starting at just 43 grams. It sits naturally on your face with full-frame coverage, offered in timeless finishes like Matte Black with clear lenses, Shiny Black, Havana, and a seasonal Transparent Sky Blue.
To make sure they stay comfortable through long days, the Burbank includes three swappable nose pads right in the box, along with adjustable temple tips that an optician can easily custom-fit to your ears.
For a bolder retro look, the Clubmaster brings that same hands-free audio experience to a classic browline design. You can grab it in Shiny Black on Gold with green lenses, Transparent Iron Grey, or Transparent Orchid, complete with adjustable gooseneck metal nose pad arms and over-extension hinges that relieve pressure against the sides of your head.
Whichever style you pick, both frames pack Meta's longest battery life yet. You get up to 12 hours of mixed-use power on a single charge — or 6 hours of non-stop audio playback — plus an additional 48 hours stored in the included slim charging case. A quick 22-minute rest inside the case gets you back up to 50% battery. Both frames support a full range of clear, sun, polarized, and Transitions lenses, as well as prescription compatibility spanning −12.00 to +6.00.
Head over to Amazon to pick up your pair in Burbank or Clubmaster, starting at $349.
