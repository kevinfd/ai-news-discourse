# Opinion: STAT+: Is AI more a partner, competitor, or crutch for biomedical scientists?

STAT News · Paul Knoepfler · 2026-10-29

Worried about artificial intelligence destroying all of humanity soon, or, alternatively, turning your toaster or other home appliances against you?
For the moment, I’m most concerned about what it might be doing to science, specifically the biomedical sciences and especially us biologists. I’m excited about how useful AI can be. However, I’m also deeply concerned about its potential impacts, especially for new and future biologists who “grow up” doing science with AI.
There’s reason to think that overdependence on AI could make us less capable as researchers. Then there are potential job losses in science due to AI.
