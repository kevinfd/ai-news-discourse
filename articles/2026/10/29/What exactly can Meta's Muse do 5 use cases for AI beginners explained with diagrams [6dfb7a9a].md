# What exactly can Meta's Muse do? 5 use cases for AI beginners explained with diagrams

note · ともぉぉ · 2026-10-29

What exactly can Meta's Muse do? 5 use cases for AI beginners explained with diagrams
Recently, you may have started seeing the name "Muse" in discussions about AI.
It is an AI agent provided by Meta, but how is it different from regular AI?
“I’ve heard the name, but I don’t know what it can do.”
“How is it different from AI like ChatGPT?”
“Is it useful for my daily life?”
For those who have such questions, I will introduce the features and use cases of Muse in an easy-to-understand way.
I have written this article to be readable while imagining specific situations, avoiding difficult technical terms as much as possible.
1. What is Muse in the first place?
Muse is a personal AI agent provided by Meta.
What is important here is the term "AI agent."
With general AI chat, you ask a question and get an answer back.
For example, if you ask, "Tell me recommendations for a trip to Kyoto," it will provide information about tourist spots and dining.
On the other hand, an AI agent aims to carry out multiple tasks toward a requested goal.
In the travel example, you could use it to organize information based on your desired criteria and build a travel itinerary.
Of course, the features actually available will vary depending on linked services and settings.
In other words, what is important for understanding Muse is not to view it simply as an AI that answers questions, but to think of it as an AI that aims to carry out tasks on your behalf.
2. 5 things you can do with Muse
So, in what specific situations is Muse useful?
Here, I will introduce 5 ways to utilize it.
1. Information gathering
When you want to know something, opening multiple pages on the internet, comparing information, and summarizing it takes a surprising amount of time.
For example, when you want to decide on a travel destination.
There are many things you want to look up, such as tourist spots, means of transportation, costs, and business hours.
AI agents like Muse can assist with the task of researching and organizing information in response to your requests.
However, you should never blindly trust the information summarized by AI.
For information where errors could be problematic, such as prices and business hours, be sure to check the official website.
2. Schedule management
As your schedule fills up, it becomes difficult to keep track of what needs to be done and by when.
For example, when school assignments, lessons, and plans with friends overlap.
Just organizing your schedule and setting priorities makes it easier to understand what you need to do next.
If it can integrate with compatible services like calendars, it may be able to further assist you in managing your schedule.
However, the services that can be integrated and the operations that can be performed depend on your usage environment.
3. Checking and organizing emails
When you check your email every day, important messages can sometimes get buried under other emails.
For example, if you want to sort through many emails to find those that require a reply or have a deadline.
If it can integrate with a supported email service, you may be able to get AI to help you with email-related tasks.
However, you need to be careful with operations that affect others, such as sending emails.
Always check the content of any text created by AI yourself before sending it.
4. Travel planning
When planning a trip, it doesn't end with just deciding on a destination.
There are many things to consider, such as transportation, accommodation, budget, and the order of sightseeing.
For example, organizing conditions like the following will make it easier to create a plan.
- Destination
- Number of travel days
- Budget
- Tourist attractions of interest
- Preferred mode of transportation
If you tell the AI your conditions, it can help you think through a travel plan based on them.
When using Muse, this kind of information organization and planning can also be considered one of the use cases.
However, being able to create a plan is different from being able to execute reservations or purchases.
Check reservation status and prices on the official website, and proceed with operations like purchasing carefully.
5. Creating articles for platforms like note
When writing text, there are various tasks involved beyond just writing the main body.
For example, tasks such as brainstorming themes, gathering information, creating an outline, writing the text, and reviewing the content.
By using AI, you can get support at each of these stages.
When utilizing an AI agent like Muse, you can also consider ways to streamline article production by combining information gathering and text creation.
However, publishing text created by AI exactly as it is is not recommended.
It is necessary to verify whether the information is correct, whether it aligns with what you want to convey, and whether it might mislead readers.
Especially in articles about your own experiences, it is important not to write as if you have experienced something you have not actually experienced.
3. What is the difference between a regular AI and Muse?
Reading the examples so far, you might think, "Can't ChatGPT do that too?"
That question is valid.
In reality, general AI chats can also assist with information gathering, organizing schedules, and writing text.
When considering the differences, what is important is not just what it can do, but how it proceeds with tasks.
With general AI chats, you can receive answers and suggestions in response to questions or requests, and then use those to proceed with the next steps yourself.
On the other hand, AI agents aim to carry out multiple processes while using available tools and integrated services.
For example, let's consider the case of writing an article.
If you use a general AI chat, you might request information gathering, check the results, and then have it create an outline.
With an AI agent, it may be possible to connect and execute multiple tasks within the scope of its supported functions and permissions.
However, this is an example to explain the general characteristics of AI agents. It does not mean that Muse can automatically execute all processes.
You need to check the official feature descriptions and usage environment to see what Muse can actually do.
4. Things to keep in mind when using Muse
Although it is a convenient AI, it does not mean you should leave everything to it.
In particular, you need to be careful about the following three points.
1. Information is not always correct
AI can sometimes explain incorrect information in a plausible way.
For fees, dates, laws, and important procedures, be sure to check against official information.
2. Check the linked services and permissions
What an AI agent can do changes depending on the services it is connected to and the permissions it is granted.
It is important to check which services it can access and what operations are permitted before using it.
3. Perform important operations with caution
There are some operations, such as sending emails or making purchases, that are difficult to undo once executed.
Do not just approve AI suggestions as they are; check the content before proceeding.
Leaving things to AI is different from letting AI decide everything.
While it is important to utilize the convenient parts, it is essential to perform the final check yourself.
5. Ultimately, who is Muse suitable for?
Summarizing what has been introduced so far, AI agents like Muse are worth considering for those who want to organize multiple tasks or streamline their workflow.
For example, people who spend too much time gathering information.
People who want to organize their schedules and emails.
People who want to streamline tasks with multiple steps, such as article creation.
For such people, AI agents can be a new option.
However, whether Muse is right for you depends on the features actually available and the problems you want to solve.
It is recommended to think about what you want to make easier before using it, rather than using it just because it is a hot topic.
Summary: Is Muse an 'AI that does everything automatically'?
Muse is an AI agent provided by Meta.
It aims not only to answer questions but also to carry out multiple tasks while using linked services and available features.
As introduced this time, information gathering, schedule management, email, travel planning, and article creation are examples of situations where AI can be utilized.
However, not all of these can be completed solely by Muse in every usage environment.
The important thing is to understand what AI can do and what you must check yourself.
If you use AI well, you may be able to reduce the time spent on tedious tasks and spend more time on what you really want to focus on.
Muse is one of the AI agents to pay attention to when considering that possibility.
