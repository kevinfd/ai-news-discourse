# Nvidia Moves to Backstop a Cloud Provider’s $45 Billion Anthropic Project

The Information · Phoebe Liu · 2026-10-29

Nvidia is in talks to provide Nscale, a young AI cloud provider, with billions of dollars in credit guarantees for debt to develop a 460-megawatt data center that Anthropic plans to use, according to people with knowledge of the deal discussions. 

 The deal is not yet signed, and terms could change. But it is another example of how Nvidia has continued to signal its financial support of so-called neoclouds like Nscale, CoreWeave and Firmus, which have faced pressure from investors because of the debt those companies have raised will still need to raise to develop specialized data centers…
