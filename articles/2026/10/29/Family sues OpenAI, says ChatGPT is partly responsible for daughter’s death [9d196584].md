# Family sues OpenAI, says ChatGPT is partly responsible for daughter’s death

The Baltimore Sun · KOMO Staff · 2026-10-29

Getting your Trinity Audio player ready...
The lawsuit alleges ChatGPT “designed the day-by-day schedule that took [Bowen Wan] off the anti-seizure medication keeping her alive and directed her to hide the taper from the physicians who could have stopped it.” Attorneys for the Wan family also allege the chatbot told her, “You are no longer someone who needs medication to hold them up.”
