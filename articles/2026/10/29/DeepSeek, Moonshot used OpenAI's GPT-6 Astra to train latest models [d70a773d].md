# DeepSeek, Moonshot used OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to train latest models

ET CIO · Www Etcio Com · 2026-10-29

- Artificial Intelligence
- 1 min read
DeepSeek, Moonshot used OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to train latest models
                    Chinese AI innovators, DeepSeek and Moonshot AI, are harnessing OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra model to enhance their AI frameworks through a controversial approach known as distillation. While major AI entities argue that this technique allows for faster and cost-effective replication of sophisticated functionalities, OpenAI has expressed apprehensions about its implications, particularly concerning Moonshot AI. To mitigate issues related to data exploitation from distillation methods, Anthropic has introduced new safeguards.
                                    
Chinese AI developers DeepSeek and Moonshot AI relied heavily on outputs from OpenAI's powerful GPT-6 Astra model to train their latest models, The Information reported on Wednesday, citing people with direct knowledge of the matter.
The practice, known as distillation, has become a flashpoint for leading AI companies as US-China technology competition intensifies, with firms arguing it allows rivals to reproduce advanced capabilities more quickly and cheaply
Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro model, which was released last week, and DeepSeek's upgraded V4.1-Flash 1017 model, which was launched on October 17, benefited from distilling Astra, The Information said
In a September 30 blog post, OpenAI said it had linked a core part of a distillation campaign that began in July to individuals associated with Moonshot AI
Firms including Anthropic, OpenAI and Google have argued the practice enables rivals to reproduce advanced capabilities more quickly and at lower cost
Earlier this month, Anthropic said new measures designed to prevent rivals from copying the capabilities of its Claude AI models have reduced data harvested in so-called distillation attacks by about 90% to 99%.
