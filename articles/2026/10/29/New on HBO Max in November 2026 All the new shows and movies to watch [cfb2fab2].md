# New on HBO Max in November 2026: All the new shows and movies to watch

Tom's Guide · Dave LeClair · 2026-10-29

New on HBO Max in November 2026: All the new shows and movies to watch
Tons of new movies and shows are hitting HBO Max this month
November is officially here, so it’s time for cool fall nights spent watching shows and movies on the best streaming services, and avoiding frigid outdoor temperatures if you live in a cold climate. HBO Max has a stacked lineup to make the most of that couch time.
Everything gets going on November 1 with "The Gilded Age" season 4. On the same day, "Youth" season 1 wraps up with its finale. A slew of popular films also kick off the month, with some fantastic Christmas movies like "A Christmas Carol" and "A Christmas Story" leading the charge. Next, HBO's original series "City Of God: The Fight Rages On" season 2 hits the service on November 8. Midmonth, Dawn Porter’s documentary "When a Witness Recants" drops on November 17, which could be worth a look.
Here's a guide to everything new on HBO Max in November 2026.
New on HBO Max in Novemmber 2026: Top picks
'The Gilded Age' season 4
"The Gilded Age" starting its fourth season is without a doubt the main event of HBO Max's November offerings. I hate to admit it, but I haven't seen any of "The Gilded Age" myself. Based on the trailer and my research for this article, I must check it out. I love a period drama set in late-19th century New York City. The lead cast includes Carrie Coon, Morgan Spector, Cynthia Nixon, and Christine Baranski, and reviews say they all do a tremendous job of making the show feel period-accurate.
Stream "The Gilded Age" season 4 on HBO Max starting November 1
'City Of God: The Fight Rages On' season 2
"City of God: The Fight Rages On" follows up on the 2002 film "City of God." It's just as intense as the full-length feature. Now back for its second season, director Aly Muritiba dives deeper into the intense conflicts between the police, drug dealers and militias in the favela and how it affects the community. It's a heavy watch, but the writing and translations are solid, and it is entertaining despite feeling very real and authentic.
Stream "City of God: The Fight Rages On" Season 2 on HBO Max starting November 8

'When a Witness Recants'
Nothing beats sinking your teeth into a good documentary, even when the subject matter isn't always easy to handle emotionally. This is one that looks to be emotionally charged, covering the story of Andrew Stewart, Ransom Watkins, and Alfred Chestnut, who were wrongly convicted of the 1983 murder of 14-year-old DeWitt Duckett. The trio spent 36 years in prison for a crime they did not commit, and the documentary dives into all the painful details. It won't be an easy watch, but it'll suck you in through to the end, especially given the praise it's getting in screenings and at Sundance.
Stream "When a Witness Recants" on HBO Max starting November 17
