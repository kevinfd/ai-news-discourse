[Moving Beyond Typing] How a 'Feature Phone' Typist Stopped Typing a Single Character Using 'Voice Input x Generative AI'
I realized that a whole month has passed since my last note post...! Long time no see!
This time, I want to talk about the world of 'Voice Input x Generative AI,' which I am currently incredibly passionate about and truly moved by, to the point where I feel I can never go back to the world of keyboard typing.
I have been involved in the IT, DX, and AI industries for over 20 years. From security software to SFA, CRM, groupware, core systems, and now generative AI, I have done plenty of proposals to clients and internal implementations.
When I look back on my career and think, 'What was a transformative experience that truly moved me to the level of changing my life?', this is undoubtedly the one that reigns supreme right now.
I want this to be read by those who think, 'Wait, voice input? That's been around forever,' 'In a world where voice meeting minutes are standard, why now?', or 'Isn't this an article about Muse or Grok?' Seriously, it will change your world!
1. The 'Crazy Transformation' That Came After the Chat Revolution
Let's look back at history for a moment.
The arrival of the internet and smartphones were high-level transformations that fundamentally changed the world. So, what is something one step below that which 'dramatically changed our daily work and lives'?
I believe it is undoubtedly the 'arrival of chat'.
For personal use, there's LINE. In the past, we would call or go to a friend's house to ring the doorbell to hang out, but now, nobody uses email to make plans. In business, Slack and Teams have become the norm.
I still remember the excitement I felt when I first encountered 'Chatter' back when I was at Salesforce. It was the moment when the 'speed of work called communication' increased dramatically. (By the way, as I wrote in my previous article below, I now finish most of my work by interacting with AI agents on Slack!)
So, here is the main topic for today.
With the same level of impact as that 'chat revolution,' the thing that has me so moved that I can no longer live without it is 'Voice Input x Generative AI'.
2. Why doesn't everyone do it? The leapfrog experience of a 'feature phone' typist
But when I look around, I'm surprised that almost no one is using it.
Even walking down the street, almost no one is talking to their smartphone, and when I go to the office, everyone is quietly tapping away on their keyboards. They are in a state of 'I know it looks convenient, but I'm not using it.' I stand out completely as I talk to my smartphone by myself while on the move (lol).
Why can't everyone take that step forward? I've been thinking about the reason, and I realized one thing.
It's an unbelievable story for someone promoting AI Native, but I'll swallow my pride and confess... I can't use smartphone flick input!! I am still a 'feature phone' typist who taps the keys repeatedly to get to the right character.
But, actually, this was the perfect answer.
The same phenomenon (so-called 'leapfrogging') that happened to people in Southeast Asia, who skipped landlines and PCs to jump straight into a smartphone society, happened to me.
Precisely because I was bad at flick input, the gap and the thrill I felt when I encountered 'Voice Input x Generative AI' were so immense that I adopted it immediately.
【My current rule】 Whether on mobile or PC, I don't type a single character if it's more than one line—or even more than five characters. I enter everything by 'voice'!
People who are good at flick input or typing might find it harder to switch because they are so used to their current methods. But please, just humor me and try letting go of it once!
3. My partner tool 'Typeless' is god-tier
'Voice input has a lot of typos, and it includes things like 'um' and 'uh,' so it's a pain to fix, right?'
For those who thought that, modern AI voice input is on a different level. The tool I pay for and love using is called 'Typeless'.
It cost a little under $150 (a bit over 20,000 yen) annually, but I feel like I get my money's worth at a level where I get an ROI (return on investment) in one week.
※ I'm a super heavy user so I have a paid subscription, but the free plan is also amazing and usable, so I'm assuming you'll try the free version first.
(I'll put aside the 'I wish Apple would just make this standard and free already' thought for now lol)
What makes it so amazing is:
- Smartphone (iPhone/Android): It launches instantly when you try to input text, and when you finish speaking, the AI automatically summarizes and formats it 'nicely' (it becomes clean text that's halfway between a transcription and meeting minutes).
- PC (Mac/Win): It launches with a single press of a function key (Fn), allowing you to input text instantly in any app.
- Context understanding is insane: It goes without saying that it automatically removes hesitations like 'um.' Even if you say, 'Change the meeting tomorrow to, oh, actually the day after,' the AI understands your intent and neatly corrects it to 'Change to the day after!'
While typing speed is on average 40-90 characters/minute, human speaking speed is said to be 150-180 characters/minute, so by simple calculation, you can create text 3 to 6 times faster.
Those who know me personally are aware—I usually speak at about 1.5x speed lol, but when I use Typeless, I speed it up even more to 2x, so I can say it results in about 10x or more productivity.
In short, this is a perfect match for 'prompt creation'!
4. Use it starting today! The super-fast 'Voice Input x Generative AI' utilization method
From here on, I'll describe how I use it in my daily life. It's super obvious, but these are concrete techniques—in reality, I basically run various generative AIs in the background of my Slack front-end, but I've rewritten this into a version anyone can do.
① All daily Slack & Salesforce tasks are done by 'voice'
I just open Slack while on the move and talk to the AI agent.
- When you want to see management metrics: Just say, "Show me the dashboard by business unit for this term, with conditions like XXX and YYY, and in a format like ZZZ." In the background, the AI automatically creates a prompt and pulls the numbers for you.
- Sales field reports: Just say, "The meeting with Company XX today went really well. We've moved into competitor comparison, so please update the phase and amount to XX," and your Salesforce opportunity data will be updated automatically.
The stress of having to open a screen and type in numbers becomes zero!
② Try it without spending a single yen! The "Ultimate Free Stack" for document creation
This is a completely free combination that I want people who think "writing prompts is a hassle" to use.
[What you need (all free!)]
- Typeless (Free version)
- Gemini Deep Research
- ChatGPT (Free version)
When you want to quickly create an outline for a proposal or a single image, I use these two patterns.
[Pattern A] When your ideas are already solid in your head
Flow: Typeless ➔ ChatGPT
If you have everything organized in your head down to the punchline, just talk your thoughts out into Typeless. Then, feed that directly into ChatGPT.
💡 A quick tip: Prompts and voice input are a perfect match. While you speak, add a few tips to match the AI's "quirks."
- Example: Always specify the size, like "Create an image in 16:9 size that fits perfectly on a PowerPoint slide."
- Example: Use slightly exaggerated language to match the proposal outline, like "with a worldview that evokes a grand theme."
[Pattern B] When your thoughts are still fuzzy (when you don't want to think too deeply)
Flow: Typeless ➔ Gemini Deep Research ➔ ChatGPT
When you're thinking, "I want to do something like this, but how should I summarize it...", first use Gemini Deep Research as a sounding board.
- Launch Typeless and just mutter whatever is on your mind.
- Finally, add, "Research and organize this content, and then format it into a prompt to pass to an image generation AI!" and speak it.
- Deep Research organizes it incredibly well, so I copy that and pass it to ChatGPT (for image generation).
My impression is that ChatGPT currently has the highest quality for single-image generation, but Claude, or even notebookLM for slides, or anything else is fine. Just by following this workflow, you get high-quality output that will surprise even yourself in an instant!

I am sure that in another six months to a year, this level of voice transcription and formatting will be a standard, free feature on iPhones and Androids.
But waiting for that future is too much of a waste! Try installing a tool like Typeless right now. You will get completely used to it in a week.
If you talk to yourself on your smartphone while walking, you might still stand out a bit, with people wondering, "Is that person okay?" (lol). But personally, I have almost stopped typing on a keyboard at home, at the office, and while commuting.
If the day comes when everyone in the city and office inputs by voice, I will be incredibly happy because I won't look like a weirdo anymore!
Because it is such a "commonplace technology," its destructive power when combined with generative AI is tremendous—that is "voice input." I hope you all experience it for yourselves!
