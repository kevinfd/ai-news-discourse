# Oura, the AI-driven health monitoring ring, just pulled their $2.2B IPO citing "uncertainty in the IPO market".

YouTube · kernelangus420 · 2026-10-29

Gabriel Jarrosson (from Lobster Capital) went through the IPO filings line by line. That’s a cover story, and the real reason is sitting in one number. Some of what he covers: Of the 50 million shares on offer, 36.5 million were existing shareholders cashing out Which means only about $567 million was going to Oura, and roughly $1.5 billion to the people selling About 93% of what Oura would have raised was earmarked for employee tax withholding, so almost nothing left to grow the business
