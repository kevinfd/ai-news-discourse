# Sebastian Thrun Raises $130 Million for AI Engineering Startup Dulo

The Information · Rocket Drew · 2026-10-29

Sebastian Thrun, the computer scientist who pioneered several AI projects at Google, has co-founded a new startup focused on creating AI models for engineering, according to a person with knowledge of the project. 

 Thrun and co-founder Adrian Aoun started the company, Dulo, earlier this year in San Francisco, and in recent days closed a seed funding round of $130 million, the person said. Investors in the round include former Google Chief Executive Eric Schmidt, Kleiner Perkins Chairman John Doerr, as well as venture firms DST Global, Construct Capital and Lowercarbon Capital.
