# Why Publishers Are Adding Invisible Ads for AI Agents

The Information · Alix Coutures · 2026-10-29

For as long as there have been ads on the internet, marketing has targeted human eyeballs. But as AI agents crowd onto the web, advertisers and publishers are beginning to deliver ads only AI can see, sparking consternation among some of the agents’ developers, who fear marketers could try to sway agents to take actions without their users’ knowledge.
