# Nvidia CEO Says Ties to Company in Spy Probe Were ‘Normal’

Bloomberg · Ian King and Tom Giles · 2026-10-29

Nvidia Corp. Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang said the chipmaker’s dealings with Megaspeed International were “normal and not unusual” following a New York Times report that one of the Singapore firm’s executives is under investigation for spying on behalf of China.
