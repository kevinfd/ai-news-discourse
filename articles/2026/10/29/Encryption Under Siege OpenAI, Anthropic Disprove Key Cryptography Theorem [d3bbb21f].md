# Encryption Under Siege? OpenAI, Anthropic Disprove Key Cryptography Theorem

The Information · Jemima McEvoy, Tiffany Li · 2026-10-29

Cryptographers who develop encryption technologies may not be sleeping very well these days. OpenAI and Anthropic both said Monday that their internal AI models had disproven the Exponential Time Hypothesis , a longstanding computer science theorem that predicts the minimum difficulty of solving a certain category of problems. Cryptographers had long assumed the hypothesis was true, and built other findings on the basis of that assumption.
