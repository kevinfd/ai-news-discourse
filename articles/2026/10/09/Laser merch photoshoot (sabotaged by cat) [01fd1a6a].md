# Laser merch photoshoot (sabotaged by cat)

AI Weirdness · Janelle Shane · 2026-10-09

Laser merch photoshoot (sabotaged by cat)
For years I've been jealous of astronomers and biologists with their fun space-themed and animal-themed clothes (some of which I've worn!). Recently I finally had a chance to something I've always wanted to do, and I designed some laser-themed clothes. Hoodies that say LASERS on them! Button-down shirts with laser beam patterns! Scarves with fun little lenses on them! Who's fabulous now, astronomers?
Not to keep the joy of laser-themed clothes entirely to myself, I made an online store to sell the laser gear to anyone else who needs a little more pew-pew in their life. Which means I needed photos.
I borrowed a big roll of paper from a friend and set it up on my kitchen table, draped over an ancient projector screen that I found in my basement. My cats were instantly interested.
They say that a roll of paper makes a perfect neutral background for any sort of product photography and they're right, it looks great. It is also, as it turns out, a cat magnet.
That's nothing compared to a pile of neatly-folded soft tank tops.
Within seconds of me placing the tank tops on my nicely prepared paper backdrop, Neon had sat on them, and was not going anywhere.
Not even as I started setting more t-shirts around him.
In fact, by the time I added a hoodie and a button-down to the stack, Neon had settled in for a full-on snooze.
Krypton cruised in for a cameo but the prime nap spot was taken and so she soon found other cat things to do.
Eventually Neon moved on and I was able to get a few cat-free shots for the site.
But I had to use the healing brush to erase SO MUCH cat hair.
So much for photoshoot one!
Would you like laser merch of your own? Would your cats like nice soft laser-themed clothes to sit on? Maybe dogs like these too, worth a shot!
Also please tell me if there is a laser-related thing you wish you had on a shirt or other item, or a design of mine that you wish was on a different thing!
