# AI Ushers in New Era of Cybersecurity Needs

Bloomberg · 2026-10-23

The US government is working to address and stop any potential cybersecurity issues stemming from flaws in PHP, a widely used open-source software, that may impact federal agencies and American critical infrastructure, according to a White House official.  The programming language, called PHP, contains two vulnerabilities which could potentially expose organizations using it in websites and software platforms to cyberattacks. Jay Chaudhry, CEO & Founder, Zscaler says we need new more proactive measures beyond firewalls. (Source: Bloomberg)
