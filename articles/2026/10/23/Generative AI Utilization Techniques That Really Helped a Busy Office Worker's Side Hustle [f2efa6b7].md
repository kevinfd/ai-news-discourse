Generative AI Utilization Techniques That Really Helped a Busy Office Worker's Side Hustle
Hello, this is Yamada!
This article is for those who feel that "my main job is too busy to find time for a side hustle" or "tasks take too long, so I can't keep it up."
(I also run a completely free practical media site for office workers to gain the power to earn independently called "Yamadalab", so please take a look!)
Overtime on weekdays, exhausted when I get home. The time I can use for a side hustle is at most one hour a day.
I'll be honest. The reason I've been able to continue my side hustle isn't because I worked hard to make time, but because I started letting generative AI handle half of the work.
Research tasks that used to take three hours per item now take about an hour and a half by using AI. In this article, I will share the ways I actually use it and tips for prompts (instruction text).
Three situations where using it had a big impact
1. Preliminary research
Before searching the internet right away, I have the AI summarize the big picture. Then, I just verify the information that comes out myself.
2. Drafting text
Writing from scratch takes time. If you have the AI create a draft and then rewrite it in your own words, your work time will be significantly reduced.
3. Creating proposals and messages
Even for crowdsourcing application letters, if you provide the project details and have it create a draft, it can be finished in a few minutes.
Three "prompt tips" that change the results
Even with the same AI, the quality of the output changes significantly depending on how you give instructions.
Tip 1: Assign a role Tell it the role first, such as "You are a web writer."
Tip 2: Write conditions specifically Specify the character count, target audience, and writing style. Vague instructions lead to vague answers.
Tip 3: Decide the output format If you specify the format, such as "three headings, each 100 characters," you can use it as is.
Here is an example of a prompt I actually use.
副業ライターとして記事を執筆するのを手伝ってください。
読者は「副業を始めたい20代〜40代の会社員」です。
テーマ「スキマ時間の作り方」で、
見出し3つ・各100文字程度・ですます調で、記事の下書きを作ってください。
Caution: Do not use AI output as is
AI is convenient, but it can present incorrect information in a plausible way. Always verify numbers and proper nouns yourself.
Also, do not input confidential company information or personal information of clients. Depending on the project, the use of AI may be prohibited, so check the contract terms first.

Summary: Instead of working harder, delegate
The reason side hustles don't last is almost always a "lack of time," not a lack of motivation. By simply delegating the parts you can to AI, it becomes easier to balance with your main job.
Start today by asking AI to do the preliminary research for your next task.
