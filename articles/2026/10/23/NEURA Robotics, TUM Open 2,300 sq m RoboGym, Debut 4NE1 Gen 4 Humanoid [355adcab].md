# NEURA Robotics, TUM Open 2,300 sq m RoboGym, Debut 4NE1 Gen 4 Humanoid

Vector Wire · VectorWireAI · 2026-10-23

NEURA Robotics and the Technical University of Munich on 22 October 2026 opened the TUM RoboGym powered by NEURA, a 2,300-square-meter research and robot-training center at LabCampus near Munich Airport, built with TUM's Munich Institute of Robotics and Machine Intelligence1. Alongside the opening, NEURA debuted 4NE1 Gen 4, a humanoid robot designed for serial production that is 20 percent lighter than its predecessor, features a black textile outer skin, and runs on Neuron AI, the company's foundation model for Physical AI. Munich is the first site in a planned international network of NEURA Gyms.
