# AlixPartners Co-CEO Rob Hornby on AI Bull Case

Bloomberg · 2026-10-23

Rob Hornby says AI costs are falling faster than those of comparable technologies historically, but remain substantial. He describes companies as being in a transition period: investment is underway while returns are uneven, and the shift from flat fees to per-token charges has exposed costs. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Jess Menton on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
