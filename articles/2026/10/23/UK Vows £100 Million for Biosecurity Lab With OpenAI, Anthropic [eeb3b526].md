# UK Vows £100 Million for Biosecurity Lab With OpenAI, Anthropic

Bloomberg · Jacob Reid · 2026-10-23

The UK announced £100 million ($134 million) for a AI Biosecurity Lab which will be supported by OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind, part of a raft of measures designed to improve the government’s ability to understand and tackle the risks posed by the technology.
