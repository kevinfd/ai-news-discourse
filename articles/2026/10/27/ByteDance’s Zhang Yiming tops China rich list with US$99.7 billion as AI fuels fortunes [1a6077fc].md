# ByteDance’s Zhang Yiming tops China rich list with US$99.7 billion as AI fuels fortunes

South China Morning Post · Mandy Zuo · 2026-10-27

ByteDance founder Zhang Yiming has reclaimed the title of China’s richest person with a nearly US$100 billion fortune, as artificial intelligence powers a surge in wealth among China’s top entrepreneurs, according to the Hurun Research Institute.
