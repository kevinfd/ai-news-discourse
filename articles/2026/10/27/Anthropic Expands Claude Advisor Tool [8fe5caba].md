# Anthropic Expands Claude Advisor Tool

Wealth Management · Wealth Management Staff · 2026-10-27

Anthropic Expands Claude Advisor Tool
The flurry of integrations, including CAIS, Conquest, FINNY, Invesco and MSCI, comes a day after Anthropic filed for its highly anticipated IPO.
October 27, 2026
In this article
Anthropic has rolled out a bevy of integrations with its Claude for Financial Advisors tool on the heels of the firm's IPO filing.
The integrations cover CAIS, Conquest, FINNY, Invesco, MSCI and Jump.
CAIS
Alternative investment platform CAIS is expanding how its AI-powered intelligence agent, CAISey, can be brought into specialized advisor workflows with Claude. (The firm announced an initial integration in May.) The connector is designed to help advisors access and review private markets information across portfolio monitoring, investment research and related workflows.
Through the connector, advisors can bring client-specific alternative investment information into Claude for Financial Advisors, alongside the CRM, custody and portfolio reporting tools their firms already use. Advisors can review fund eligibility and capital call details when preparing for a client meeting, private investment positions and unfunded commitments during a portfolio review and repurchase windows when checking investor eligibility. The goal is to make relevant private markets information easier to access and review as part of an advisor’s broader portfolio analysis and decision-making process.
“Through our previous release, we’ve already worked with several clients to integrate CAISey into their platforms to bring insights and meet advisors where they work,” CAIS Chief Technology Officer Kan Kotecha said in a statement. “Expanding our work with Anthropic reflects our commitment to continued expansion of our capabilities across the alternative investment lifecycle so that advisors can focus on serving their clients.”
Conquest
In addition, Conquest, a fintech delivering a verifiable AI financial advice engine, is also bringing a new connector to Claude for Financial Advisors.
Through the Model Context Protocol connector, Conquest enables financial advisors to access its financial intelligence directly within Claude. According to Conquest, using this connector, instead of going to multiple apps, advisors can access live financial intelligence within Claude.
Results are powered by Conquest’s Strategic Advice Manager, a deterministic calculation engine and proprietary AI expert system, which users can interact with through conversations with Claude. Advisors get explainable, repeatable calculations in real-time. SAM ranks, prioritizes and verifies each strategy against best practices and jurisdictional compliance across multiple regulatory frameworks, including CRM2/CRM3 (Canada), Best Interest and DOL fiduciary standards (U.S.) and FCA Targeted Support (U.K.).
“Conquest was built to show up wherever advisors and their clients already are, rather than asking them to log into yet another system,” Conquest Chief Revenue Officer Rob Crnkovic said in a statement. “Connecting Conquest data with Claude will empower advisors to respond to clients in real time, and every projection can be verified. That’s why Conquest is at the forefront in democratizing quality financial advice.”
FINNY
FINNY, an AI growth engine for financial advisors, also launched an MCP connector that brings the fintech’s AI-powered growth intelligence and prospecting tools directly into Claude, enabling advisors to access campaign insights, manage prospect data and leverage its resources within existing AI workflows.
FINNY’s platform combines a proprietary database comprising millions of records with its predictive F-Score matching engine and automated outreach tools to help advisors identify, prioritize and engage high-intent wealth management prospects. Through the MCP connector, advisors can access FINNY’s data and tools directly within Claude.
“Advisors are increasingly turning to AI, and we want FINNY to be available in Claude, where they're already operating,” Eden Ovadia, co-founder and CEO of FINNY, said in a statement. “Our goal has always been to make organic growth easier and more accessible, and adding FINNY to Claude allows advisors to incorporate prospecting data, campaign insights and growth opportunities into their existing workflow without adding friction.”
Invesco
Global asset manager Invesco is bringing its data to support portfolio intelligence and practice management available through Claude for Financial Advisors. Invesco’s new connector will provide a toolkit that helps advisors strengthen their business and client relationships. Existing advisors and clients using Invesco's model portfolios will be able to access portfolio information directly via Claude. Invesco’s portfolio optimization resources combine analytical tools, market and economic insights, and investment expertise to help advisors construct portfolios.
MSCI
Lastly, the MSCI Connector integrates MSCI’s data into Claude for Financial Advisors, enabling advisors to analyze portfolios across markets. Wealth management firms can use the MSCI connector to access MSCI private assets data, indexes and analytics. The connector supports Index performance and analytics, private markets benchmarking, multi-asset portfolio transparency, real assets index intelligence, sustainability and climate insights and portfolio risk and factor insights.
The MCPs from all firms are available in Claude’s connector directory.
