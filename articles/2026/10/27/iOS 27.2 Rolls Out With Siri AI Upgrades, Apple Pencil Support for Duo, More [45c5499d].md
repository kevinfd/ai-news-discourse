# iOS 27.2 Rolls Out With Siri AI Upgrades, Apple Pencil Support for Duo, More

PCMag · Jibin Joseph · 2026-10-27

After weeks of testing, Apple released the iOS 27.2 update on Monday with Siri AI improvements, a new Suggestions feature for Messages, and Apple Pencil support for the iPhone Duo, alongside plenty of other incremental upgrades and bug fixes.
On iPhone 15 Pro and later, Siri AI can now remember past conversations, so you can ask it to recall something it suggested previously. For improved answers, Siri AI can now tap photo attachments in Messages, Calendar event attendees, and voicemails. It can also pull better results from your Safari history and Reading List, Apple says.
Other Siri AI improvements include granular text selection to copy and paste parts of its responses and an Excluded Apps list to control which apps Siri, Search, and suggestions can access. With these enhancements, Apple is also expanding the tool’s language support beyond English to include French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish.
Siri AI is still in beta and is accessible only on iOS 27 or later on compatible devices. It is still not available in the EU due to local regulations, and Apple reiterates that it “is working constructively with the EU in an attempt to find a path forward.”
What Else Is New in iOS 27.2?
Plenty of other Apple apps receive minor refinements with iOS 27.2. In Messages, a Suggestions feature uses the context of your conversations to suggest actions you can take, such as adding a dinner invite to your Calendar.
In Photos, Shared Albums now lets you edit captions and comments to help avoid duplicates. The feature can also display reminders before an album expires so participants can save their favorite photos.
Speaking of photos, images you generate with Apple’s Image Playground tool will carry an invisible Synth ID watermark. Google rolled out a dedicated Synth ID detector earlier this month to help identify images, audio, or video generated by AI tools from OpenAI, Google, Nvidia, and Kakao. It now extends to Apple’s AI tools.
Other notable improvements include new black-and-white app logo for Apple Music, Simple Profiles in Apple TV to let everyone in the household have a dedicated profile, support for third-party sign-language interpreters on FaceTime calls, two separate sections for New Episodes and Continue Playing on the homepage of the Podcasts app, and an update to the Friends tab in the Games app to view friend suggestions and games they recently played.
The iOS 27.2 update also adds support for Apple Pencil on the iPhone Duo, 9to5Mac reports. The Duo is Apple’s first foldable phone, and the company had announced plans to add Apple Pencil support by the end of the year. However, Apple Pencil (USB-C) is the only stylus supported by the Duo’s display, which is getting some heat for its durability.
Apart from new features, the software update also fixes several bugs in Health, Shortcuts, app tracking, and other areas.
How to Install iOS 27.2
You can install iOS 27.2 on iPhone 11 or newer and iPhone SE 2 or newer by going to Settings > General > Software Update.
