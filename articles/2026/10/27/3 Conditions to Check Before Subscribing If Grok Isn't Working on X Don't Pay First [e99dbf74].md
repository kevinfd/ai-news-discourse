# 3 Conditions to Check Before Subscribing If Grok Isn't Working on X | Don't Pay First

note · 黒狼, SNS攻略の裏技師 · 2026-10-27

3 Conditions to Check Before Subscribing If Grok Isn't Working on X | Don't Pay First
Where to look first when Grok isn't working on X
If you feel like you can't use Grok on X, it's dangerous to proceed to a subscription first.
The cause isn't just "because you aren't a paid subscriber."
The logged-in account,connection status with X,and usage limitscan get mixed up, causing you to get stuck in the same place even after paying.
When I incorporate AI into my X operations, I separate whether I "want to use it on X" or if "Grok alone is sufficient" before looking at the number of features.
According to official xAI, Grok is provided on Grok.com, iOS/Android apps, and the X platform.
In other words, not being able to open it within X is not synonymous with not being able to use Grok itself.
Even in regions where free usage is available, there are limits on usage volume.
If you see a screen but have reached the usage limit, it's easy to jump to the conclusion that your subscription is insufficient.
First, please check the displayed guidance and the entry point you are trying to use.
3 conditions to check before subscribing
1. Do you want "Grok within X" or standalone use?
Separate whether you want to use it while looking at your X timeline or if you want to write text or conduct research in a browser.
If tasks can be handled by Grok.com or the app, you can try it out with the free tier before changing your X plan.
Conversely, if you are aiming for features tied to an X account, check the X subscription screen and the target features.
2. Is the account you are connecting a personal account?
As of October 2026, onlypersonal X accountscan connect to The X Subscription.
Accounts managed by organizations or affiliate accounts cannot be connected.
If you use multiple accounts, review the account you selected when logging in. Logging in and connecting an account are separate operations.
3. Does the desired usage volume match the subscription plan?
The free tier and paid plan usage limits for Grok are not the same.
With The X Subscription, Grok and Cursor share a weekly usage quota.
If you also use Cursor, do not base your subscription decision solely on the intention to use Grok.
Even if you have an old X Premium contract, switching is optional, and you can check your current plan details on the subscription screen.
Stop at this table before you pay
Entry Point
- Where to check first: Within X, Grok.com, or the app
- Criteria: Is it essential to use it within X?
Account
- Where to check first: Your logged-in X account
- Criteria: Is it a personal account?
Usage Volume
- Where to check first: Plan screen/Usage status
- Criteria: Have you used up your shared quota?
Contract Change
- Where to check first: Billing/Subscriptions
- Criteria: Do you need to switch your current contract?
If you cannot fill in these four lines, you do not need to subscribe yet.
For example, if you can achieve your goal on Grok.com, there is little reason to choose a higher-tier plan just for X.
Conversely, if you need to use it within X and require a high usage volume, read the applicable features and limits shown on the subscription screen before deciding.
The issue of it not working isn't always solved by paying.
Check the entry point, account, and limits in that order to reduce the chance of feeling like it's "not what I expected" after subscribing.
