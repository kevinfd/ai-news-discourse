# Can AI Help Ray Kurzweil Cheat Death?

The Information · Amy Dockser Marcus · 2026-10-27

Ray Kurzweil does not want to die, but at the age of 78, he has been thinking more about it, a preoccupation that he describes as a “major motivating force of human beings.”
