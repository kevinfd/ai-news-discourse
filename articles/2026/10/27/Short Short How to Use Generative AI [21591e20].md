# Short Short: How to Use Generative AI

note · もも · 2026-10-27

Short Short: How to Use Generative AI
Takahiro was known as an expert in generative AI.
People who cannot use generative AI will be left behind in the coming era.
Whether at lectures or corporate training sessions, Takahiro would make that point.
There is a difference between people who can use generative AI and people who can use generative AI to drive their work forward. What is important is not simply asking questions and getting answers. It is about giving appropriate instructions, evaluating the output, and connecting it to work results.
Takahiro's explanations were easy to understand.
His beginner-level usage classes were full every time. Requests for training from companies followed one after another. Eventually, he published a book explaining how to utilize generative AI and began appearing on television programs as an expert.
Let's acquire the skills to master generative AI.
Takahiro repeated those words many times.
His work was going well. During busy periods, there were many days a week when he could only get three hours of sleep. Even so, he felt he was being useful to the world. As generative AI became more widespread, he believed his own work would continue to grow.
In fact, generative AI was rapidly permeating people's lives.
Companies proceeded with implementation into their operations, and individuals began using it daily. People who took Takahiro's courses utilized generative AI in their workplaces and shared its convenience with their colleagues.
It seemed that Takahiro's work would only continue to increase.
The turning point was a casual remark by a popular influencer.
During a stream, a viewer asked, I want to utilize generative AI for my work, but where should I start studying?
The influencer thought for just a moment before answering.
Huh? If you don't know how to use it, why don't you just ask the generative AI?
That was all.
It was neither a special discovery nor the announcement of a new technology. Many of the people watching the stream realized that they had never even thought of that.
What can it do for my work? How should I ask it questions? What kind of tasks can I make more efficient?
First, you should just try asking the generative AI.
That way of thinking spread in the blink of an eye.
Of course, Takahiro's courses didn't disappear immediately. There were still people who wanted to learn specialized applications, and companies that wanted to acquire knowledge systematically.
However, the courses for beginners gradually saw a decrease in students.
"If it's just the basic usage of generative AI, asking the generative AI itself was enough."
Such comments began to stand out in student surveys.
Book sales also dropped. Requests for corporate training decreased. He stopped getting calls from television programs.
Before he knew it, Takahiro had lost the places where he could talk about generative AI.
To make a living, Takahiro started working as a delivery driver.
The man who once gave lectures in corporate conference rooms and received applause from attendees was now picking up food at restaurants and delivering it to designated addresses.
One night, after finishing his deliveries and returning home, Takahiro opened his computer for the first time in a while.
On the screen was the input field for the generative AI he used to use frequently for work.
After thinking for a while, Takahiro typed in some text.
"How can I make a comeback from here?"
The generative AI outputted immediately.
"There is no such convenient way. Let's work steadily. Please be careful of dark part-time jobs."
